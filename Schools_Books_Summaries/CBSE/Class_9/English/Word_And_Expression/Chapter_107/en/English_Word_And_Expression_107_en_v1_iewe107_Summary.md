## 📘 Key Learnings

**1. Reading Comprehension & Analysis:**
*   **Text I (Leander Paes):** Understand the significance of Paes' 1996 Olympic medal as a major individual achievement for India after 44 years. Analyse his qualities: patriotism ("When I am playing for my country I don’t expect anything"), determination, mental strength, and gratitude towards his team/coach. Compare his individual medal with his father's team medal.
*   **Text II (Tagore's Poem):** Interpret Rabindranath Tagore's "Where the Mind is Without Fear". Evaluate his vision for an ideal India characterized by fearlessness, free knowledge, unity (absence of "narrow domestic walls"), truthfulness, rationality ("clear stream of reason"), and progressive thought, free from "dead habits". Understand its historical context (pre-independence) and enduring relevance.

**2. Vocabulary Enhancement:**
*   Learn words related to achievement and character from *Beehive* ("Reach for the Top"): *contentment, customary, affirmative, endurance, culmination, affluent, prevails, apology, positive, expedition*.
*   Understand and use idiomatic expressions: *eager beaver, cash cow, kangaroo court, bull in a china shop, hold your horses, pigeon-holed*.
    *   *Example:* "Pigeon-holed" means being unfairly categorized. Maria Sharapova couldn't be 'pigeon-holed' due to her multifaceted talent and drive.

**3. Grammar: Time Clauses:**
*   Learn to connect two actions or events using conjunctions that indicate time relationships:
    *   **When:** Indicates one action happening at the same time or immediately after another. (e.g., *It was raining heavily **when** I got home.*)
    *   **While:** Indicates two actions happening simultaneously, often one longer action interrupted by a shorter one. (e.g., *The salesman came **while** Rita was eating her breakfast.*)
    *   **Before:** Indicates one action happening earlier than another. (e.g., *Let’s go home **before** it gets dark.*)
    *   **After:** Indicates one action happening later than another. (e.g., *I will come to your house **after** I do the cooking.*)
    *   **Until:** Indicates an action continuing up to a certain point in time. (e.g., *I won’t enter the room **until** she cleans it.*)

**4. Editing Skills:**
*   Practice identifying and correcting errors in sentences, focusing on:
    *   **Word Choice/Usage:** Adding or removing words for grammatical correctness. (e.g., "Maria Sharapova likes dancing, singing and fashion." *Corrected from: likes to be dancing...*)
    *   **Spelling:** Correcting commonly misspelled words (e.g., *possession, receive, separate, supersede, threshold, unforeseen, calendar*).
    *   **Punctuation:** Using commas, apostrophes, capital letters correctly, especially in biographical texts like the one on Savitribai Phule.
