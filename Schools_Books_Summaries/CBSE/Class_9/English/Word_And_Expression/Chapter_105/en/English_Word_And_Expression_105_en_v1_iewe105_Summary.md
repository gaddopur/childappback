## 📘 Key Learnings

This unit equips learners with practical language tools and encourages critical thinking through various texts and exercises.

1.  **Reading Comprehension Strategies:**
    *   **Skimming and Scanning:** Quickly reading to grasp the main idea or find specific information (e.g., answering questions about "The Miraculous Escape").
    *   **Inferential Reading:** Understanding implied meanings, character feelings (e.g., narrator's fear, boredom, desperation), and motivations (e.g., why the narrator went on a voyage).
    *   **Vocabulary in Context:** Deducing the meaning of unfamiliar words (e.g., `tempest`, `startled`, `penetrate`, `fortress`) from the surrounding text.

2.  **Vocabulary Enhancement:**
    *   **Synonyms:** Finding words with similar meanings (e.g., wealthy = rich, gliding = moved, exhausted = tired, terror stricken = scared, instinct = urge).
    *   **Antonyms:** Finding words with opposite meanings (e.g., poor vs. wealthy, weak vs. strong, relieve vs. enforce, securely vs. insecurely, defensive vs. offensive).
    *   **Word Families:** Expanding vocabulary related to a theme (e.g., Fear: `terrific`, `dreaded`, `startled`, `frightened`, `terror stricken`; Getting Tired: `exhausted`, `tired`, `half dead`, `suffocated`).
    *   **Suffixes:** Understanding how suffixes change word class or meaning (e.g., `inform` (v) -> `information` (n); `ill` (adj) -> `illness` (n); `fear` -> `fearsome`, `fearful`, `fearless`).

3.  **Grammar - Reported Speech (Questions):**
    *   **Structure:** Reporting verb (asked, inquired) + conjunction (if/whether for Yes/No; Wh-word for Wh-questions) + subject + verb (tense shifted back).
    *   **Example (Wh-Question):**
        *   Direct: The old man asked, “Who do you think are the most intelligent people?”
        *   Indirect: The old man asked the young boy who he thought were the most intelligent people.
    *   **Example (Yes/No Question):**
        *   Direct: Romesh asked Lata, "Can you go with me for a picnic tomorrow?"
        *   Indirect: Romesh asked Lata whether/if she could go with him for a picnic the next day.
    *   **Changes:** Pronouns (you -> he/she/I), time/place expressions (tomorrow -> the next day), verb tenses (are -> were, can -> could).

4.  **Grammar - Prepositions:**
    *   Understanding the correct use of prepositions to indicate time, place, direction, relationship.
    *   **Common Errors & Corrections (from text):**
        *   `searching about food` -> `searching for food`
        *   `picked it out` -> `picked it up`
        *   `ran out into the woods` -> `ran off into the woods`
        *   `take it over` -> `take it away`
        *   `trotted around the footbridge` -> `trotted onto/across the footbridge`
        *   `glance into the water` -> `glance down into the water`
        *   `scowled towards it` -> `scowled at it`
        *   `image on the water` -> `image in the water`
        *   `went over of sight` -> `went out of sight`
        *   `bite` -> `bark` (contextual correction based on action)

5.  **Writing Coherently:**
    *   **Story Writing:** Sequencing events logically using visual cues, using descriptive language, providing a suitable title.
    *   **Descriptive Paragraphs:** Researching information and presenting it clearly and concisely (National Bravery Award winners).
