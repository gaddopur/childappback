# [Class 9] Ict - Chapter 06: Getting Connected: Internet

## 🌟 Core Concepts

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts required to navigate and utilize the Internet effectively and safely.

📊 **Concept Hierarchy:**

1.  **Internet:** A global network enabling connection and communication.
    *   **Uses:** Information gathering, communication (email, messaging), learning (online courses), sharing (blogs, photos, videos).
2.  **World Wide Web (WWW):** An information space on the Internet containing documents and resources.
    *   **Webpage:** A single document on the WWW containing multimedia information (text, images, videos).
    *   **Website:** A collection of related webpages.
        *   **Home Page:** The initial page displayed when visiting a website.
    *   **Hyperlink:** Text or image that links to another webpage or resource.
    *   **URL (Uniform Resource Locator):** The unique address of a resource on the web, typed into the address bar.
3.  **Web Browser:** Software used to access information on the WWW (e.g., Mozilla Firefox, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge).
    *   **Address Bar:** Area in the browser to type URLs.
    *   **Plug-in:** Additional software enhancing browser functionality (e.g., displaying specific content).
4.  **Search Engine:** A tool to search for information on the WWW using keywords (e.g., Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo).
    *   **Keywords:** Specific words or phrases used for searching.
    *   **Search Operators:** Symbols or words (AND, OR, " ", ...) used to refine search results.
    *   **Filtering Results:** Categorizing search results (All, Images, Videos, etc.).
5.  **Information Evaluation:** Critically assessing the reliability and quality of online resources.
6.  **Bookmarking:** Saving URLs for quick future access.
7.  **Electronic Mail (Email):** A method for sending and receiving messages electronically over the Internet.
    *   **Email Account:** Required for sending/receiving emails (e.g., username@serviceprovider.com).
    *   **Email Service Provider:** Companies offering email services (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo Mail, Outlook).
    *   **Email Components:** To, Subject, Cc, Bcc, Body, Attachment.
    *   **Inbox:** Folder storing received emails.
    *   **Uploading:** Attaching and sending files via email.
    *   **Downloading:** Saving files from the Internet/email to a device.
8.  **Internet Safety & Security:** Practices for safe online activity.
    *   **HTTPS:** Protocol indicating a secure website connection.
    *   **Safe Search:** Browser setting to filter potentially inappropriate content.
    *   **Strong Passwords:** Creating complex and unique passwords.
    *   **Virus:** Malicious software harmful to computers.
    *   **Anti-virus Software:** Programs to detect and remove viruses (e.g., Norton, Kaspersky, McAfee).
    *   **Cyber World:** The virtual environment of the Internet.

## 📘 Key Learnings

**1. Accessing the World Wide Web:**
*   The Internet allows access to a vast repository of information known as the World Wide Web (WWW).
*   To access the WWW, you need an electronic device (computer, smartphone) with an Internet connection and a **Web Browser** software.
*   You navigate the web by typing a specific address (**URL**) into the browser's **Address Bar** or by clicking on **Hyperlinks**.
*   A **Website** is composed of multiple **Webpages**; the first one you usually see is the **Home Page**.

**2. Searching for Information:**
*   When you don't know the specific URL, **Search Engines** help find information using **Keywords**.
*   **Effective Searching:**
    *   **Plan:** Define what information (facts, opinions, images, videos) you need.
    *   **Keywords:** Choose precise words related to your topic, avoiding common words. Refine keywords if initial results aren't helpful.
    *   **Filter:** Use search engine filters (e.g., Images, Videos) to narrow results by type.
    *   **Advanced Search:** Use **Search Operators** (See Table 6.2 below) for more specific results.
        *   `AND`: Includes pages with *all* keywords (e.g., `youth AND nation`).
        *   `OR`: Includes pages with *at least one* keyword (e.g., `youth OR nation`).
        *   `" "`: Searches for the *exact phrase* (e.g., `"Role of youth in the development of nation"`).
        *   `...`: Searches within a *number range* (e.g., `"Role of youth" 1990...2018`).
*   **Citing Sources:** Always acknowledge the source of information or resources obtained from the web to give credit to the creator.

**3. Evaluating Online Information:**
*   Not all information online is accurate or reliable. Anyone can publish content.
*   **Evaluation Criteria:**
    *   **Authority:** Who created the website? Can you contact them?
    *   **Purpose:** Is the site commercial, educational, biased? Who is the intended audience (students, teachers)?
    *   **Reliability:** Cross-check information with other reputable sources (other websites, books, experts).
    *   **Cost:** Is the information free or paid?

**4. Using Email for Communication:**
*   **Email (Electronic Mail)** allows sending/receiving messages and files over the Internet.
*   **Creating an Account:** Requires choosing an **Email Service Provider** (like Gmail, Yahoo), selecting a unique username, and setting a **strong password** (mix of upper/lowercase letters, numbers, symbols; 6-8+ characters; unique per account; changed frequently).
*   **Composing & Sending:**
    *   Log in, click 'Compose'.
    *   Enter recipient(s) in 'To', 'Cc' (Carbon Copy - visible recipients), or 'Bcc' (Blind Carbon Copy - hidden recipients).
    *   Write a clear 'Subject'.
    *   Write the message body.
    *   **Attach** files if needed (uploading).
    *   Click 'Send'.
*   **Receiving & Downloading:**
    *   Received emails appear in the **Inbox**.
    *   Be cautious when **downloading attachments**. Verify the sender is trusted, the file format is expected, and scan with **anti-virus software** before opening to avoid **viruses**.

**5. Safe Internet Practices:**
*   **Secure Websites:** Prefer websites with `https` in their URL.
*   **Browser Settings:** Ensure 'Safe Search' is enabled.
*   **Clicking Links:** Think before clicking, as links can lead to inappropriate or malicious sites.
*   **Passwords:** Use strong, unique passwords and change them regularly.
*   **Anti-virus:** Keep anti-virus software updated and scan downloads.
*   **Responsible Behaviour:** Behave online as responsibly as you would in the real world.
