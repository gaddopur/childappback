# [Class 9] History - Chapter 305: Pastoralists in the Modern World (Ghumantu Pashupalak aur Adhunik Duniya)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter *nomadic pastoralists* (ghumantu pashupalak) ke baare mein hai - woh log jo ek jagah nahi rehte, balki apne *janwaron* (animals) ke liye *charagah* (pastures) ki talash mein ghoomte rehte hain.

1.  **Pastoral Nomadism (Ghumantu Pashupalan): Ek Jeevan Shailee**
    *   **Definition:** Aise log jo apni *rozi-roti* (livelihood) ke liye apne *pashuon* (livestock - bhed, bakri, gaye, oont) ke saath ek jagah se doosri jagah jaate hain.
    *   **Movement Patterns (Ghumaav Ke Tareeke):**
        *   **Mountains (Pahadi Ilaake):** Seasonal movement (mausami ghumaav) - Garmiyon mein upar *bugyals* (high meadows) mein, sardiyon mein neeche *Siwalik* ya *bhabar* (low hills/dry forests) mein. Ex: Gujjar Bakarwals, Gaddis.
        *   **Plateaus/Plains/Deserts (Pathaar/Maidan/Registan):** Monsoon aur dry season ke hisaab se movement. Ex: Dhangars (Maharashtra), Raikas (Rajasthan).
    *   **Economic Importance (Arthik Mahatva):** Doodh, ghee, oon (wool), pashu bechna; kheti karne wale kisanon ke saath *symbiotic relationship* (sahjeevi sambandh) - khet ko *khaad* (manure) dena, badle mein anaaj lena.
    *   **Social Organisation (Samajik Sangathan):** Saath mein safar karna (*kafila*), communities ke apne rules aur traditions.

2.  **Colonial Rule ka Impact (Aupniveshik Shasan Ka Prabhav)**
    *   **Shrinking Pastures (Charagahon Ka Kam Hona):**
        *   *Waste Land Rules:* Angrez sarkar ne *charagahon* ko 'bekaar zameen' maana aur use *kheti* (cultivation) ke liye badal diya taaki *revenue* (lagan) badhe.
        *   *Forest Acts:* Jangalon ko 'Reserved' ya 'Protected' ghoshit kar diya, jisse pashupalakon ka *access* (pahunch) seemit ho gaya. Unhe *permits* lene padte the.
    *   **Restricted Movement (Ghumaav Par Rok):**
        *   *Criminal Tribes Act (1871):* Kai ghumantu samuhon ko 'criminal' ghoshit kar diya gaya, unke movement par sakht *nigrani* (surveillance) rakhi gayi.
        *   Boundaries & Reserves: Unhe ek *fixed area* mein rehne par majboor kiya gaya (khaas kar Africa mein Maasai ke saath).
    *   **Increased Taxation (Kar Ka Bojh):**
        *   *Grazing Tax:* Har pashu par tax lagaya gaya, jisse unki *aamdani* (income) kam ho gayi. Tax collection ke liye *contractors* rakhe gaye jo zyada paisa vasoolte the.
    *   **Trade Disruption (Vyapar Mein Badha):** Unke traditional trade routes aur markets mein jaane par *restrictions* lagaye gaye.

3.  **Adaptation and Survival (Anukoolan Aur Jeevan Sangharsh)**
    *   **Coping Strategies (Samna Karne Ke Tareeke):**
        *   Apne *pashuon ki sankhya* (number of animals) kam kar di.
        *   Naye *charagah* aur *raste* (routes) dhundhe. (Ex: Raikas ka Haryana jaana post-partition).
        *   Kuch *settle* ho gaye - zameen kharid kar kheti karne lage ya *vyapar* (trade) karne lage.
        *   Garib pashupalak *mazdoor* (labourers) ban gaye.
    *   **Modern Relevance (Adhunik Prasangikta):** Aaj bhi, dry aur hilly regions mein pastoralism ek *ecologically viable* (paryavaran ke anukool) tareeka hai jeene ka.

4.  **Case Studies (Vishesh Adhyayan)**
    *   **India:** Gujjar Bakarwals, Gaddis, Dhangars, Raikas, Banjaras.
    *   **Africa:** Maasai (Kenya, Tanzania). Unka *land loss* (zameen ka nuksan), movement restrictions, drought ka asar, aur social changes (elders/warriors vs. chiefs/poor).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh)

1.  **Pastoralists ka Movement Kyun Zaruri Hai? (Why is Movement Essential?)**
    *   *Charagah* (pastures) ek jagah par *exhaust* (khatm) ho jaate hain. Movement se *pastures ko recover* hone ka time milta hai.
    *   *Mausam* (weather) ke badlav se bachne ke liye (jaise pahadon par barf se bachne ke liye neeche aana).
    *   *Pani* (water) ki talash mein.
    *   *Diagram Idea (Conceptual Flowchart):*
        ```mermaid
        graph TD
            A[Pasture Available] --> B{Graze Animals};
            B --> C{Pasture Exhausted / Season Change};
            C --> D[Move to New Area];
            D --> A;
            D --> E[Allows Old Pasture to Recover];
        ```

2.  **Colonial Policies ne Pastoralists ki Life Kaise Badli? (How Colonial Policies Changed Lives?)**
    *   **Waste Land Rules:** Grazing land kam hua -> Animals ke liye bhojan ki kami.
    *   **Forest Acts:** Traditional grazing grounds mein entry band/restricted -> Movement limited, forage ki kami. Permits = Bureaucratic control.
    *   **Criminal Tribes Act:** Stigma (kalank), constant police *nigrani*, freedom khatm.
    *   **Grazing Tax:** Economic burden badha -> Poverty, *indebtedness* (karz).
    *   *Diagram Idea (Cause-Effect):*
        ```mermaid
        graph LR
            subgraph Colonial Policies
                A[Waste Land Rules]
                B[Forest Acts]
                C[Criminal Tribes Act]
                D[Grazing Tax]
            end
            subgraph Impact on Pastoralists
                E[Shrinking Pastures]
                F[Restricted Movement]
                G[Social Stigma / Loss of Freedom]
                H[Economic Hardship]
            end
            A --> E;
            B --> E;
            B --> F;
            C --> F;
            C --> G;
            D --> H;
        ```

3.  **Maasai Community ka Experience (Africa):**
    *   Unki *traditional land* (paramparik zameen) ka bada hissa chhin gaya (colonial boundaries, white settlement, game reserves like Serengeti).
    *   Unhe *arid* (sookhe) ilakon mein dhakel diya gaya.
    *   Movement par *restrictions* se drought ke time unke *cattle* (maveshi) badi sankhya mein marne lage.
    *   Colonial rule ne *chiefs* appoint kiye jisse traditional *elders* aur *warriors* ka power kam hua aur *amir-garib* (rich-poor) ka farak badh gaya.

4.  **Pastoralists ne Badlav ka Saamna Kaise Kiya? (How Pastoralists Coped?)**
    *   Woh *passive victims* nahi the. Unhone *adapt* kiya.
    *   *Flexibility* dikhai - routes badle, herd size adjust kiya.
    *   *Income sources diversify* kiye (kheti, trade, labour).
    *   Apne *rights* (adhikar) ke liye awaaz uthai (political pressure).

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Context)

*   **Diverse Communities:** Bharat mein pastoralism ki ek rich diversity hai. Chapter mein mentioned kuch pramukh samuh hain:
    *   **Himalayas:** Gujjar Bakarwals (J&K), Gaddis (HP), Bhotiyas, Sherpas, Kinnauris (Uttarakhand, HP). Inka movement *altitude* aur *snowfall* par depend karta hai. Jagah: *Siwalik, Pir Panjal, Bugyals, Bhabar*.
    *   **Plateaus & Plains:** Dhangars (Maharashtra - plateau se Konkan coast), Gollas, Kurumas, Kurubas (Karnataka/AP - dry plateau se coastal areas). Inka movement *monsoon* aur *dry season* par depend karta hai.
    *   **Deserts:** Raikas (Rajasthan - Thar desert). *Maru Raikas* oont (camels) paalte hain. Barish kam hone ki vajah se *cultivation* ke saath *pastoralism* combine karte hain.
    *   **Across States:** Banjaras (UP, Punjab, Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra) - long distance traders aur cattle herders.
*   **Economic Interdependence:** *Dhangars* ka Konkani kisanon ke saath *exchange* (khaad ke badle chawal) ek classic example hai ki kaise pastoralists *agricultural economy* ka hissa the.
*   **Colonial Impact:** Waste Land Rules, Forest Acts, Criminal Tribes Act, Grazing Tax - in sabka *Bharatiya* pashupalakon par bahut gehra aur *negative* asar pada. Unki *mobility* aur *livelihood* khatre mein aa gayi.
*   **Post-Independence Challenges:** Partition ne *Raikas* jaise border communities ke movement ko affect kiya. Aaj bhi *development projects* aur *environmental regulations* unke liye nayi chunautiyan (challenges) laate hain. Lekin yeh communities *adapt* kar rahi hain aur unki *ecological knowledge* aaj bhi relevant hai. 📊
