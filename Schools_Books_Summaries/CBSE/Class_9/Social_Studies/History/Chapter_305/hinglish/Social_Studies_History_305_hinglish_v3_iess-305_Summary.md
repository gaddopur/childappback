# [Class 9] History - Chapter 305: Pastoralists in the Modern World (घुमंतू चरवाहे और उनकी दुनिया)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ) 📊

1.  **Nomadic Pastoralism (घुमंतू चरवाहा जीवन):**
    *   Definition & Lifestyle (परिभाषा और जीवनशैली): Log jo ek jagah nahi rehte, apne pashuon (animals) ke liye charagah (pastures) aur paani ki talaash mein ghoomte hain. Unki livelihood animals par depend karti hai.
    *   Seasonal Movements (मौसमी आवाजाही):
        *   Mountains (पहाड़): Sardi mein neeche ke ilakon mein, Garmi mein upar pahadon par (e.g., Gujjar Bakarwals, Gaddis). Yeh movement *bugyals* (high meadows) aur *bhabar* (dry forests) ke beech hota hai.
        *   Plateaus/Plains/Deserts (पठार/मैदान/रेगिस्तान): Monsoon aur dry season ke hisaab se movement (e.g., Dhangars in Maharashtra, Raikas in Rajasthan).
    *   Symbiotic Relationships (किसानों के साथ सहजीवी संबंध): Pastoralists ke जानवर khet ko manure (khaad) dete hain, badle mein unhe anaaj ya chara milta hai (e.g., Dhangars aur Konkani peasants).
    *   Survival Strategies (जीवित रहने की रणनीतियाँ): Movement ka time, rasta, paani/pasture ki availability calculate karna; alag-alag activities combine karna (herding, cultivation, trade).

2.  **Colonial Impact (औपनिवेशिक शासन का प्रभाव):**
    *   Shrinking Pastures (चरागाहों का सिकुड़ना): Angrez sarkar ne grazing lands ko cultivated farms mein badla (revenue badhane ke liye). Waste Land Rules laagu kiye. 📉
    *   Forest Acts (वन कानून): Forests ko 'Reserved' ya 'Protected' declare kiya gaya. Pastoralists ka access bandh ya restrict ho gaya. Permits zaroori ho gaye. 🌲🚫
    *   Movement Restrictions (आवाजाही पर पाबंदी): Nomadic life ko shaq se dekha gaya. Criminal Tribes Act (1871) se kuch communities ko 'criminal' declare kiya gaya aur unke movement par control badha diya gaya. 📜
    *   Taxation (कर लगाना): Har animal par Grazing Tax lagaya gaya, jisse un par financial burden badha. 💰

3.  **Coping Mechanisms & Adaptation (बदलावों का सामना और अनुकूलन):**
    *   Reduced Herd Size (पशु संख्या घटाना): Kam charagah hone se animals kam kiye.
    *   New Pastures & Routes (नए चरागाह और रास्ते): Purane raste band hone par naye routes dhundhe (e.g., Raikas ka Haryana jaana after Partition).
    *   Settlement & Diversification (बस जाना और अन्य काम): Kuch ameer pastoralists ne zameen kharid kar kheti shuru ki ya trade mein lage. Gareeb log mazdoor ban gaye.
    *   Adaptation (अनुकूलन): Pastoralists ne modern world ke changes ko adapt kiya, apni strategies badli.

4.  **African Pastoralism (Case: Maasai) (अफ्रीकी चरवाहे: मसाई का उदाहरण):**
    *   Land Loss & Confinement (ज़मीन छिनना और सिमटना): Colonial powers ne Africa ko baanta. Maasai ki zameen ka bada hissa (approx 60%) chhin gaya, unhe arid areas mein confine kiya gaya. Game reserves (e.g., Serengeti) banaye gaye jahan grazing mana thi.
    *   Restricted Movement (आवाजाही पर पाबंदी): Borders aur reserves ne unki mobility khatam kar di. Permits zaroori ho gaye.
    *   Impact of Drought (सूखे का प्रभाव): Confinement ke kaaran, sukhe (drought) ke time woh apne animals ko bachane ke liye move nahi kar paate the, jisse bade paimane par pashu mar jaate the. 😥
    *   Social Changes (सामाजिक बदलाव): Colonial administration ne chiefs appoint kiye, jisse traditional elders aur warriors ki authority kam hui. Ameer aur gareeb pastoralists ke beech farak badha.

5.  **Modern Relevance (आधुनिक दुनिया में प्रासंगिकता):**
    *   Pastoralism aaj bhi dry aur hilly regions ke liye ek ecologically viable (पर्यावरणीय रूप से व्यवहार्य) form of life hai.
    *   Pastoralists अतीत के अवशेष (relics of the past) nahi hain, balki modern world ka hissa hain jo badalte samay ke saath adapt kar rahe hain.

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख) 📈

*   **Why Pastoralists Move (चरवाहे क्यों घूमते हैं?):** Pastoralists ka movement unke survival ke liye zaroori hai. Woh mausam (weather), charagah (pasture) ki availability, aur paani ke sources ke hisaab se move karte hain.
    *   *Example (India - Mountains):* Jammu & Kashmir ke **Gujjar Bakarwals** sardiyon mein Siwalik ki lower hills mein rehte hain aur garmiyon mein Kashmir valley ke upar *bugyals* mein chale jaate hain. Unka group journey *kafila* kehlata hai. Himachal ke **Gaddi** shepherds ka bhi similar pattern hai. Uttarakhand ke **Gujjar** cattle herders winter mein *bhabar* aur summer mein *bugyals* jaate hain. 🏔️❄️☀️
    *   *Example (India - Plateau):* Maharashtra ke **Dhangars** monsoon mein central plateau par rehte hain (jahan bajra ugate hain) aur October mein Konkan coast ki taraf move karte hain. Wahan woh *kharif* harvest ke baad khet mein apne animals charate hain (stubble) aur fields ko manure karte hain. Badle mein Konkani peasants unhe chawal dete hain. Monsoon shuru hone par wapas plateau laut jaate hain. 🌾🐑➡️🍚
*   **Colonial Policies ka Asar (Impact of Colonial Policies):** Angrezi hukumat ne pastoralists ki zindagi mushkil bana di.
    *   **Waste Land Rules:** Uncultivated land ko 'waste' mana gaya aur use cultivation ke under laaya gaya. Isse grazing land kam ho gaya. 📉
    *   **Forest Acts:** Forests mein entry restrict hone se chara (forage) milna mushkil ho gaya. Permits ne unki movement ko control kiya. 📜🌲
    *   **Criminal Tribes Act (1871):** Kai nomadic groups ko 'criminal by birth' declare kiya gaya, un par police ki nigrani badh gayi aur unka movement restrict ho gaya. Isse unka social stigma badha.
    *   **Grazing Tax:** Har janwar par tax dene se unki kamayi par bojh pada. Contractors tax وصولی mein sakhti karte the. 💸
*   **Adaptation is Key (अनुकूलन महत्वपूर्ण है):** Mushkilon ke bawajood, pastoralists ne haar nahi maani. Unhone:
    *   Apne routes badle (e.g., Partition ke baad **Raikas** Sindh nahi ja paaye toh Haryana jaane lage).
    *   Animals ki sankhya kam ki.
    *   Naye kaam dhundhe (kheti, trade, labour).
    *   Apne rights ke liye awaaz uthai. 💪
*   **India vs Africa (Maasai):** Dono jagah colonial policies ka pattern similar tha:
    *   Grazing lands ka loss (cultivation/settlements/reserves ke liye). 🌍
    *   Movement par restrictions (borders, permits, reserves). 🛂
    *   Traditional livelihood aur social structures par negative impact.
    *   Droughts ka devastating effect badh gaya due to confinement. 🏜️
    *   Maasai society mein colonial chiefs ke aane se wealth ka difference badha, jaisa India mein bhi kuch had tak hua.
*   **Pastoralism Today (आज का चरवाहा जीवन):** Environmentalists aur economists maante hain ki pastoral nomadism dry/hilly areas ke liye sustainable hai. Yeh communities modern world ka hissa hain aur unhe support ki zaroorat hai. ✅

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ) 🇮🇳

*   **Diverse Communities (विविध समुदाय):** India mein kai alag-alag pastoral communities hain jaise **Gujjar Bakarwals** (J&K), **Gaddis** (HP), **Bhotiyas, Sherpas, Kinnauris** (Himalayas), **Dhangars** (Maharashtra), **Gollas, Kurumas, Kurubas** (Karnataka, AP), **Raikas** (Rajasthan), **Banjaras** (multiple states).
*   **Geographical Adaptation (भौगोलिक अनुकूलन):** In communities ne India ke diverse landscapes - Himalayas ke *bugyals* se lekar Deccan plateau ke semi-arid regions aur Thar desert tak - ke anusaar apni life adapt ki hai. Unke movement patterns (seasonal, monsoon-based) iska proof hain.
*   **Colonial Laws ka Impact (औपनिवेशिक कानूनों का प्रभाव):** British laws jaise **Waste Land Rules, Forest Acts, Criminal Tribes Act (1871), Grazing Tax** ne Indian pastoralists ki life par gehra asar daala. Unki mobility kam hui, resources chhin gaye, aur economic hardship badhi.
*   **Post-Independence Scenario (स्वतंत्रता के बाद का परिदृश्य):** Partition ne **Raikas** jaise border communities ke movement ko affect kiya, unhe naye pastures (like in **Haryana**) dhundhne pade. Aaj bhi land use changes, development projects, aur conservation policies pastoral communities ke liye challenges create karte hain.
*   **Economic & Social Data (आर्थिक और सामाजिक डेटा):** Pastoral communities ka Indian economy mein contribution (milk, wool, meat, manure for agriculture) aksar underestimate kiya jaata hai. Unki social status, access to education aur healthcare jaise mudde bhi important hain jin par national data insights de sakta hai. Unke traditional knowledge ko recognise karna bhi zaroori hai. 📊🇮🇳
