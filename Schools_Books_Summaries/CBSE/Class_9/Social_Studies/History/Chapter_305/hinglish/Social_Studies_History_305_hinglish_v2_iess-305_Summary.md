# [Class 9] History - Chapter 5: Pastoralists in the Modern World

## 🌟 Core Concepts
*   **खानाबदोश चरवाहे (Nomadic Pastoralists):** Log jo rozi-roti ke liye ek jagah se doosri jagah ghoomte hain apne जानवर (animals) ke saath. Yeh movement unke survival aur environment ke liye zaroori hai.
*   **भारत में चरवाहा समुदाय (Pastoral Communities in India):**
    *   **पहाड़ी इलाके (Mountains):**
        *   Gujjar Bakarwals (Jammu & Kashmir): Bakri (goats) aur bhed (sheep) paalte hain; seasonal migration (sardi mein Siwalik hills, garmi mein Kashmir valley). `Kafila` mein travel karte hain.
        *   Gaddi (Himachal Pradesh): Bhed-bakri paalte hain; similar seasonal cycle (sardi mein Siwalik, garmi mein Lahul/Spiti).
        *   Gujjar cattle herders, Bhotiyas, Sherpas, Kinnauris (Garhwal/Kumaon): Seasonal movement between `bhabar` (winter) and `bugyals` (summer).
    *   **पठार, मैदान और रेगिस्तान (Plateaus, Plains & Deserts):**
        *   Dhangars (Maharashtra): Bhed, kambal banane wale, bhains (buffalo) herders. Monsoon mein central plateau, phir Konkan jaate hain (`kharif` ke baad fields manure karte, `rabi` ke liye). Farmers se rice lete hain.
        *   Gollas (Karnataka/AP): Cattle herders.
        *   Kurumas/Kurubas (Karnataka/AP): Bhed-bakri paalte, kambal bechte. Monsoon/dry season ke hisab se move karte.
        *   Banjara (UP, Punjab, Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra): Lambi doori tak travel karte, cattle aur samaan bechte.
        *   Raikas (Rajasthan): Oont (camels) ya bhed-bakri paalte. Monsoon mein gaon mein, October tak bahar nikal jaate pasture ki talaash mein. `Maru Raikas` camel herders hain.
*   **औपनिवेशिक शासन और चरवाहा जीवन (Colonial Rule and Pastoral Life):** British rule ne pastoralists ki life par gehra asar daala.
    *   **सरकारी नीतियां (Government Policies):**
        *   Waste Land Rules: Grazing lands ko kheti ke liye 'waste' mana gaya aur cultivate karne ko diya gaya.
        *   Forest Acts: Jungalon ko 'Reserved' ya 'Protected' banaya gaya, pastoralists ka access restrict kiya gaya. Permits zaroori ho gaye.
        *   Criminal Tribes Act (1871): Nomadic groups ko 'criminal by birth' declare kiya gaya, unke movement ko control kiya gaya.
        *   Grazing Tax: Har जानवर par tax lagaya gaya, jo time ke saath badhta gaya.
    *   **प्रभाव (Impact):**
        *   `चारागाहों की कमी (Shrinking Pastures)`
        *   `आवाजाही पर रोक (Restricted Movement)`
        *   `आर्थिक बोझ (Economic Burden)`
        *   `जानवरों की संख्या में गिरावट (Decline in Livestock)`
*   **बदलावों का सामना (Coping with Changes - India):**
    *   Janwaron ki sankhya kam ki.
    *   Naye pastures dhunde (e.g., Raikas Haryana jaane lage).
    *   Kuch log zameen khareed kar settle ho gaye, kheti ya trade karne lage.
    *   Gareeb log mazdoor ban gaye.
*   **अफ्रीका में चरवाहा जीवन (Pastoralism in Africa):**
    *   Focus on Maasai (Kenya/Tanzania). Duniya ki ~50% pastoral population Africa mein hai.
    *   **Colonial Impact on Maasai:**
        *   Land Loss: Maasailand ka बंटवारा (Kenya/Tanganyika), best grazing land white settlement aur game reserves (Maasai Mara, Serengeti) ke liye le liya gaya. Unhe arid areas mein dhakel diya gaya.
        *   Restricted Movement: Reserves ki boundaries fix kar di gayi, movement ke liye permit zaroori.
        *   Drought Impact: Movement par rok se sookhe (drought) ka asar badh gaya, bahut saare cattle mar gaye (e.g., 1933-34 mein half se zyada).
        *   Social Changes: Elders aur warriors ka traditional authority kam hua. British ne chiefs appoint kiye jo ameer ho gaye, rich vs poor ka naya division bana.
*   **अनुकूलन और आधुनिक प्रासंगिकता (Adaptation and Modern Relevance):** Pastoralists ne naye halaat ke hisab se khud ko adapt kiya hai aur aaj bhi dry/hilly regions ke liye unka lifestyle ecologically important mana jaata hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings

1.  **Nomadic Pastoralism Kya Hai? (What is Nomadic Pastoralism?)** 🐑🐪
    *   Nomads woh log hain jo ek jagah tik kar nahi rehte. Pastoralists nomads hote hain jo apne animals (cattle, sheep, goats, camels) ke liye paani aur charagah (pasture) ki talaash mein ghoomte rehte hain.
    *   Unka movement planned hota hai, seasonal changes ke according. Yeh movement zaroori hai taaki ek hi jagah ke resources overuse na hon aur pastures ko recover hone ka time mile. 🌿

2.  **Bharat ke Pramukh Pastoral Groups aur Unke Movements (Major Indian Pastoral Groups & Movements)** 🇮🇳
    *   **Himalayas:**
        *   *Gujjar Bakarwals (J&K):* Sardi mein neeche Siwalik ki pahadiyon mein rehte hain (dry scrub forests mein grazing). April end mein, `kafila` banakar, Pir Panjal pass cross karke Kashmir valley jaate hain summer grazing ke liye. September end mein wapas neeche aate hain.
        *   *Gaddi (HP):* Similar pattern. Sardi mein Siwalik, April mein north (Lahul/Spiti). Kuch aur upar high meadows tak jaate hain. September mein wapsi, beech mein Lahul/Spiti mein apni fasal kaat te aur sardi ki fasal bote hain.
        *   *Gujjar Cattle Herders (Garhwal/Kumaon):* Sardi mein `bhabar` (dry forests), garmi mein `bugyals` (high meadows).
        *   **Diagram Concept:** Ek simple cycle diagram show kar sakta hai summer (high mountains) aur winter (low hills/plains) locations aur movement ka direction. 📈
            ```
            [ Summer Pastures (High Altitude - Bugyals/Valleys) ]
                      ^ (Apr-May: Upward March)
                      |
                      v (Sep-Oct: Downward March)
            [ Winter Pastures (Low Hills - Siwaliks/Bhabar) ]
            ```
    *   **Plateaus, Plains, Deserts:**
        *   *Dhangars (Maharashtra):* Monsoon mein central plateau (semi-arid) par rehte hain, bajra ugate hain. October mein bajra harvest karke West (Konkan) jaate hain. Wahan `kharif` harvest ke baad khet khali hote hain; Dhangar ke sheep fields ko manure karte hain aur stubble khate hain. Konkani peasants badle mein rice dete hain. Monsoon shuru hone par wapas plateau aa jaate hain.
        *   *Raikas (Rajasthan):* Thar desert mein rehte hain. Barish kam aur uncertain hoti hai. Cultivation ke saath pastoralism karte hain. Monsoon mein gaon mein rehte hain jab pasture available hota hai. October tak jab grazing grounds sookh jaate hain, woh paani aur pasture ki talaash mein nikal jaate hain. Agle monsoon mein wapas aate hain. Maru Raikas camels paalte hain.

3.  **Colonial Rule ka Asar (Impact of Colonial Rule)** 🇬🇧➡️🇮🇳
    *   Angrez sarkar ne India mein pastoralists ki life ko bahut badla:
        *   **Waste Land Rules:** Uncultivated land (jo grazing ke liye use hoti thi) ko 'waste' mana gaya aur kheti ke liye individuals ko de diya gaya. Isse pastures kam ho gaye.
        *   **Forest Acts:** Forests ko 'Reserved' (koi entry nahi) ya 'Protected' (restricted entry with permit) declare kiya. Isse traditional grazing grounds unke liye band ho gaye. Movement regulate ho gaya (time limit, specific periods).
        *   **Criminal Tribes Act (1871):** Kayi nomadic communities (pastoralists, traders, craftsmen) ko 'Criminal Tribes' classify kiya gaya. Unhe notified villages mein rehna padta tha aur bina permit move karna allowed nahi tha. Police un par nazar rakhti thi.
        *   **Grazing Tax:** Har animal par tax lagaya gaya. Pehle contractors tax collect karte the (jo zyada se zyada vasoolne ki koshish karte the), baad mein government directly collect karne lagi via passes. Isse un par financial burden badh gaya.
    *   **Overall Impact:** Grazing lands shrink ho gaye, movement restrict ho gaya, un par suspicion kiya gaya, aur unhe tax dena pada. Isse unke livestock aur livelihood par bura asar pada. 📉

4.  **Pastoralists ne Kaise Cope Kiya? (How Pastoralists Coped?)** 💪
    *   Unhone apne herds ka size kam kiya.
    *   Jab purane raaste band hue toh naye pastures dhunde (jaise Raikas ka Sindh na jaa kar Haryana jaana after 1947 partition).
    *   Kuch ameer pastoralists ne zameen khareed li aur kheti karne lage ya trade mein lag gaye.
    *   Bahut se gareeb pastoralists ko karz lena pada, aur kai baar apne janwar kho kar woh labourers ban gaye.
    *   Phir bhi, pastoralism khatam nahi hua. Unhone adapt kiya, apne movement ke patterns badle, aur survive kiya.

5.  **Africa ke Pastoralists: The Maasai Example** 🌍🇰🇪🇹🇿
    *   Africa mein bhi pastoralists par colonial rule ka gehra asar hua. Maasai (East Africa - Kenya, Tanzania) ek major example hain.
    *   **Land Loss:** Colonial powers ne Africa ko baant diya. 1885 mein Maasailand British Kenya aur German Tanganyika ke beech divide ho gaya. Unki best grazing lands white settlement, kheti, aur game reserves (like Serengeti National Park) ke liye le li gayi. Unhe 60% se zyada zameen khoni padi aur ek chhote, dry area mein confine kar diya gaya.
    *   **Restricted Movement:** Unhe specific reserves mein rehne par majboor kiya gaya. Bina permit ke reserve se bahar jaana mana tha. Market access bhi restrict tha.
    *   **Drought ka Badhta Asar:** Jab movement restrict ho gaya, toh drought ke time woh apne cattle ko behtar pastures mein nahi le jaa sakte the. Isse 1933-34 jaise severe droughts mein unke lakho cattle mar gaye.
    *   **Social Structure mein Badlav:**
        *   Traditional division (elders vs warriors) disturb hua. British ne raiding band karwa di.
        *   British ne chiefs appoint kiye jo powerful aur wealthy ho gaye. Isse Maasai society mein ameer aur gareeb ka fark badh gaya. Jo chiefs the, woh drought aur war jhel gaye kyunki unke paas non-pastoral income bhi thi. Gareeb pastoralists, jo sirf livestock par depend the, unhone sab kuch kho diya aur work dhundne shehron mein chale gaye.

6.  **Conclusion: Adaptation and Importance Today** ✅
    *   Duniya bhar mein pastoral communities ne modern world ke changes (new laws, borders, market pressures) ko face kiya hai.
    *   Unhone adapt kiya hai - apne routes badle, herd size manage kiya, political pressure banaya, aur alternative income sources dhunde.
    *   Pastoralism koi 'past' ki cheez nahi hai. Environmentalists aur economists maante hain ki yeh dry aur hilly regions ke liye ek sustainable (viable) form of life hai.
