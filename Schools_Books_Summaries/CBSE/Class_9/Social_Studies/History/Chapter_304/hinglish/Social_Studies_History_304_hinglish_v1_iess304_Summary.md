# [Class 9] History - Chapter 304: Forest Society and Colonialism (Van Samaj aur Upniveshvaad)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Deforestation (Vanon ka Vinash)**
    *   Causes (Kaaran):
        *   Land Improvement (Zameen Sudhar): Krishi ka vistar (commercial crops - jute, sugar, wheat, cotton), population growth, revenue generation.
        *   Railways (Rail Pटरियाँ): Sleepers ki zaroorat, fuel (indhan) ki zaroorat.
        *   Shipbuilding (Jahaaz Nirman): England mein oak ki kami, India se timber ka export.
        *   Plantations (Bagan): Chai (Tea), coffee, rubber ke liye jungle saaf karna.
        *   Colonial Policies (Upniveshi Neetiyan): Forests ko 'unproductive' maanna.
2.  **Rise of Commercial Forestry (Vyavsayik Vaniki ka Uday)**
    *   State Control (Sarkari Niyantran): Forests par sarkari kabza.
    *   Dietrich Brandis: Pehle Inspector General of Forests, systematic approach.
    *   Scientific Forestry (Vaigyanik Vaniki): Natural forests ko kaatkar ek hi prakaar ke ped (teak, sal) seedhi line mein lagana (plantation), timber production par focus.
    *   Forest Laws (Van Kanoon): Indian Forest Act (1865, 1878, 1927), forests ka classification (Reserved, Protected, Village), logon ke adhikar seemit karna.
    *   Institutions: Indian Forest Service (1864), Imperial Forest Research Institute (Dehradun, 1906).
3.  **Impact on People's Lives (Logon ke Jeevan par Prabhav)**
    *   Loss of Livelihoods (Ajeevika ka Nuksan): Traditional practices (lakdi kaatna, pashu charana, phal-phool ikattha karna, shikar karna) gair-kanooni ho gaye.
    *   Shifting Cultivation (Jhum Kheti par Rok): Ban laga diya gaya (harmful for timber, tax calculation mushkil). Communities ka displacement.
    *   Hunting Restrictions (Shikar par Pabandi): Customary hunting band, bade janwaron ka shikar angrezon aur elite ka sport bana.
    *   Hardship and Exploitation (Kathinai aur Shoshan): Forest guards dwara pareshani, rishwatkhori, 'begar' (free labour), plantation workers ki kharab sthiti (e.g., Assam mein Santhals, Gonds).
    *   New Trades (Naye Vyapar): Kuch communities ne forest products ka trade shuru kiya, parantu yeh bhi government regulated tha, European firms ko fayda. Kuch communities ko 'Criminal Tribes' ghoshit kiya gaya.
4.  **Rebellion in the Forest (Jangal mein Vidroh)**
    *   Resistance (Pratirodh): Forest communities ne naye kanoonon ka virodh kiya.
    *   Bastar Rebellion (1910): Chhattisgarh mein, reservation, badhte rent, begar, akaal ke khilaf. Gunda Dhur ka netratva. British dwara kathorta se dabaya gaya.
    *   Java (Indonesia): Dutch colonialism, Kalang community ka virodh, Samin's Challenge (Surontiko Samin dwara state ownership par sawal).
5.  **Global Connections (Vaishvik Sambandh)**
    *   Colonialism's Impact: India aur Indonesia mein forest management mein similarities.
    *   World Wars' Effect: Dono World Wars mein war needs ke liye forests ki andhadhundh katai. Dutch 'scorched earth' policy Java mein.
6.  **New Developments (Naye Vikas)**
    *   Shift towards Conservation (Sanrakshan ki Taraf Badlav): 1980s se, timber collection se zyada conservation par zor.
    *   Community Involvement (Samudayik Bhagidari): Forest management mein local communities ko shamil karne ki zaroorat mehsoos hui. Sacred groves ka mahatva.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

1.  **Deforestation Kyun Hua? (Why Deforestation?)**
    *   Angrezon ne India ke forests ko 'unproductive wilderness' (bekaar ka jangal) samjha jise kheti ke neeche laana zaroori tha. Unhone commercial crops jaise **jute, sugar, wheat, cotton** ko badhava diya jinki Europe mein demand thi.
    *   *Railway ka Vistar*: 1850s se railway line bichhane ke liye **sleepers** (lakdi ke takhte) ki bhari maang thi. Ek mile track ke liye 1760 se 2000 sleepers lagte the! Iske liye laakhon ped kaate gaye, khaaskar **Sal aur Teak**. *Socho, ek average ped se agar 3-5 sleeper bante hain, toh ek mile ke liye kitne ped kate honge?* 🌳➡️🛤️
    *   *Shahi Navy ki Zaroorat*: England mein oak forests kam hone par Royal Navy ke liye मजबूत lakdi (timber) ki supply India se ki gayi.
    *   *Plantations*: Natural forests saaf karke **Chai (Tea), Coffee, Rubber** ke bagan lagaye gaye, jo European planters ko saste daamo par diye gaye.

    *Diagram Idea: Ek flowchart jo dikhata hai ki kaise Railways, Ships, Agriculture expansion aur Plantations ne milkar deforestation ko badhaya.*

2.  **'Scientific' Forestry ka Matlab Kya Tha? (What did 'Scientific' Forestry Mean?)**
    *   British ko laga ki local log aur traders forests ko barbaad kar denge. Isliye unhone German expert **Dietrich Brandis** ko bulaya.
    *   Brandis ne **Indian Forest Service (1864)** aur **Indian Forest Act (1865)** banwane mein madad ki. Dehradun mein **Imperial Forest Research Institute (1906)** bhi sthapit hua.
    *   Is system mein, natural mixed forests (jahan kai tarah ke ped the) ko kaat kar, ek hi type ke commercially valuable ped (jaise **Teak, Sal**) seedhi rows mein lagaye gaye. Ise **plantation** kehte hain.
    *   Forest Act (1878) ne forests ko teen categories mein baanta:
        *   **Reserved Forests (Aarakshit Van)**: Sabse acche forest, villagers ka pravesh varjit.
        *   **Protected Forests (Sanrakshit Van)**: Kuch adhikar diye gaye, parantu restrictions ke saath.
        *   **Village Forests (Gram Van)**: Gaon wale yahan se fuel wood, etc. le sakte the.
    *   *Par kya yeh sach mein 'scientific' tha?* Ecologists maante hain ki isse biodiversity (jaiv vividhta) ka bahut nuksan hua.

    *Diagram Idea: Ek table jo Natural Forest aur Scientific Forestry Plantation ke beech antar batata hai (Species diversity, Usage, Appearance).*

3.  **Forest Laws ka Aam Logon par Kya Asar Hua? (Impact of Forest Laws on Common People?)**
    *   Villagers forests se apni zaroorat ki cheezein - fuel, fodder, leaves, fruits, roots, herbs, wood for implements (hal, yoke), bamboo - lete the. Mahua ke phool khane aur tel nikalne ke kaam aate the. Tendu patta se Bidi banti thi.
    *   Naye kanoonon ke baad yeh sab **illegal** ho gaya. Logon ko lakdi churani padti thi, pakde jaane par forest guards ko **bribe** deni padti thi. Mahilaon ko fuelwood collect karne mein khaas pareshani hui.
    *   **Shifting Cultivation (Jhum, Podu, Bewar etc.)** par ban lag gaya. Angrezon ko lagta tha isse timber ka nuksan hota hai aur tax vasoolna mushkil hai. Kai communities ko apne ghar chhodne pade.
    *   **Hunting (Shikar)**: Aam logon ka traditional shikar karna 'poaching' (avaidh shikar) ban gaya, jabki Angrezon aur Rajas ke liye bade janwaron (Tiger, Leopard) ka shikar ek **sport** ban gaya. 1875-1925 ke beech 80,000+ tigers maare gaye! Example: Maharaja of Sarguja ne 1157 tigers maare.
    *   Kuch logon ko naye kaam mile (e.g., forest products ka trade), lekin yeh bhi government control mein tha. Kai nomadic communities (jaise **Korava, Karacha, Yerukula** Madras Presidency mein) ki rozi-roti chhin gayi aur unhe **'Criminal Tribes'** kaha gaya. Assam ke tea plantations mein **Santhals, Oraons, Gonds** jaise communities se logon ko recruit kiya gaya jahan conditions bahut kharab thi.

    *Diagram Idea: Mind map showing impacts on different groups: Villagers, Shifting Cultivators, Pastoralists, Hunters, Women, Traders.*

4.  **Logon ne Vidroh Kyun Kiya? (Why did People Rebel?)**
    *   Colonial forest policies ne logon ki zindagi mushkil kar di thi. Unke traditional rights chheen liye gaye the.
    *   **Bastar (Chhattisgarh) mein 1910 ka Vidroh**:
        *   *Kaaran*: Forest reservation ka proposal (1905), shifting cultivation/hunting/collection par ban, badhte land rent, free labour (begar) ki maang, aur 1899-1900 & 1907-1908 ke bhayanak akaal (famines).
        *   *Netratva aur Ghatnayein*: Initiative Dhurwas (Kanger forest) ne liya. **Gunda Dhur** (Nethanar village) ko important leader maana jaata hai. Gaon-gaon mein **mango boughs, lump of earth, chillies, arrows** (aam ki tehni, mitti ka dhela, mirch, teer) bhejkar vidroh ka sandesh diya gaya. Bazaars loote gaye, officials/traders ke ghar, schools, police stations jalaye gaye.
        *   *Daman*: British ne sena bhejkar vidroh ko brutally suppress kiya. Gunda Dhur kabhi pakde nahi gaye.
        *   *Parinam*: Reservation ka kaam temporarily roka gaya, area kam kiya gaya.
    *   **Java (Indonesia) mein Pratirodh**:
        *   **Kalangs**: Skilled forest cutters the, Dutch ne unpar control karna chaha toh unhone 1770 mein vidroh kiya (dabaya gaya).
        *   **Samin's Challenge (c. 1890)**: **Surontiko Samin** ne state ownership par sawal uthaya - "Hawa, paani, zameen, lakdi state ne nahi banayi, toh woh iska malik kaise ho sakta hai?" Unke followers ne tax/fine dene se, labour karne se mana kar diya, survey ke time zameen par let gaye.

    *Diagram Idea: Timeline showing key events of the Bastar Rebellion.*

5.  **War ka Forests par Asar aur Naye Trends (Impact of War and New Trends)**
    *   First aur Second World War ke time British war needs ko poora karne ke liye India mein forests ki jamkar katai hui.
    *   Java mein, Japanese kabze se pehle Dutch ne 'scorched earth' policy apnai - sawmills, teak ke logs jala diye taaki Japanese ke haath na lage. Japanese ne bhi forests ka exploitation kiya.
    *   **1980s ke baad badlav**: Governments ne samjha ki communities ko forests se door rakhne se conflicts badhte hain. Ab **Conservation (Sanrakshan)** par zyada focus hai. Yeh maana gaya ki forests ke paas rehne wale logon ko forest management mein **shamil karna** zaroori hai.
    *   Bharat mein kai jagah (Mizoram se Kerala tak) ghane jangal isliye bache hain kyunki gaon walon ne unhe **Sacred Groves** (पवित्र उपवन - jaise *Sarnas, Devarakudu, Kan, Rai*) ke roop mein protect kiya hai. Kuch gaon khud patrolling karte hain.

    *Diagram Idea: Simple graphic showing a shift in focus: Exploitation (Timber) ---> Conservation (Ecology + Community Involvement).*
