# [Class 9] History - Chapter 301: The French Revolution (फ्रांसीसी क्रांति)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter French Revolution ke important ideas aur events ko explore karta hai. Mukhya concepts hain:

1.  **France ka Purana Shashan (Old Regime): 1789 se pehle ka France**
    *   **Samaj ka Batwara (Social Structure):** Teen Estates mein banta hua samaj.
        *   *First Estate:* Clergy (Church ke log) - Privileged, tax nahi dete the.
        *   *Second Estate:* Nobility (Kulin varg/Amir log) - Privileged, tax nahi dete the, feudal rights the.
        *   *Third Estate:* Baaki sab log (90% population) - Bade businessmen, merchants, lawyers se lekar kisan, artisans, mazdoor tak. Sirf yahi log tax dete the.
    *   **Arthik Samasyaein (Economic Problems):** Khali khazana (empty treasury), war ka kharcha, extravagant court, karz (debt) aur badhte interest rates.
    *   **Subsistence Crisis (जीविका संकट):** Population badhne se foodgrains ki demand badhi, prices badhe, wages kam the, aur drought/hail se fasal kharab hone par halat aur bigad jaati thi.

2.  **Revolution ke Kaaran (Causes of Revolution)**
    *   **Social Inequality (Samajik Asamantata):** Third Estate par sara bojh, First/Second Estate ko privileges.
    *   **Economic Hardship (Arthik Pareshani):** Taxes ka bojh, badhti keematें (especially bread), subsistence crisis.
    *   **Middle Class ka Uday (Rise of Middle Class):** Third Estate mein पढ़े-लिखे aur prosperous log (merchants, lawyers) jo birth-based privileges ke khilaf the aur merit par based society chahte the.
    *   **Enlightenment Thinkers ke Vichar (Ideas of Philosophers):**
        *   *John Locke:* Government ke divine/absolute right ko reject kiya.
        *   *Jean Jacques Rousseau:* Social contract (logon aur representatives ke beech) par based government ka idea diya. Har member ko vote ka adhikar.
        *   *Montesquieu:* Power ka division (Legislative, Executive, Judiciary) ka idea diya (USA mein implement hua).
    *   **Immediate Cause:** King Louis XVI ka naye taxes lagane ka plan.

3.  **Revolution ki Shuruat aur Mukhya Ghatnayein (Outbreak and Key Events)**
    *   **Estates General ki Meeting (May 1789):** Tax badhane ke liye bulai gayi. Third Estate ne 'one person, one vote' ki maang ki, jo reject ho gayi.
    *   **National Assembly ka Gathan aur Tennis Court Oath (June 1789):** Third Estate ke representatives ne khud ko National Assembly ghoshit kiya aur constitution banane tak disperse na hone ki shapath li (Tennis Court Oath).
    *   **Bastille ka Patan (Storming of the Bastille - 14 July 1789):** Paris mein logon ne hathiyar dhundne ke liye Bastille qile (jo raja ki despotic power ka symbol tha) par hamla kiya aur use tod diya. Yeh revolution ka ek bada symbol bana.
    *   **The Great Fear (Gramin Vidroh):** Gaon mein rumours phaile ki lords ne fasal barbaad karne ke liye log bheje hain. Kisano ne dar ke maare chateaux (mahals) par hamla kiya, anaaj loota, aur records jala diye.

4.  **Revolution ke Alag Alag Phases (Different Phases of Revolution)**
    *   **Constitutional Monarchy (1791-1792):**
        *   National Assembly ne 1791 mein Constitution banaya. Raja ki powers limit kar di gayi aur powers ko Legislature, Executive, Judiciary mein baant diya gaya.
        *   *Declaration of the Rights of Man and Citizen:* Natural rights (life, freedom of speech/opinion, equality before law) declare kiye gaye.
        *   Voting rights sabko nahi mile: Sirf 'Active Citizens' (25+ age ke पुरुष jo tax dete the) vote kar sakte the. Mahilayon aur baaki पुरुषों ko 'Passive Citizens' mana gaya.
    *   **France becomes a Republic (1792-1794):**
        *   War against Prussia & Austria (1792).
        *   Jacobin Clubs ka influence badha (leader: Robespierre). Inke members kam prosperous sections se the (shopkeepers, artisans, workers). Yeh log *sans-culottes* kehlaye (bina knee breeches wale).
        *   August 1792: Tuileries Palace par hamla, Raja ko bandi banaya gaya.
        *   *Convention* (newly elected assembly) ne Monarchy abolish karke France ko Republic ghoshit kiya (Sep 1792). Sabhi 21+ पुरुषों ko vote ka adhikar mila (regardless of wealth).
        *   King Louis XVI ko treason ke charge mein execute kiya gaya (Jan 1793), baad mein Queen Marie Antoinette ko bhi.
    *   **The Reign of Terror (आतंक का राज - 1793-1794):**
        *   Robespierre ki policy of severe control and punishment. Republic ke 'enemies' (ex-nobles, clergy, other party members, even his own disagreeing members) ko arrest karke guillotine (sir kaatne ki machine) se saza di gayi.
        *   Maximum ceiling on wages/prices, rationing, 'equality bread' ka use, Citoyen/Citoyenne address forms. Churches band.
        *   July 1794: Robespierre ko bhi convict karke guillotine kiya gaya.
    *   **Directory Rule (1795-1799):**
        *   Wealthier middle classes ne power li. Naya constitution (vote sirf property walon ko).
        *   Executive mein 5 Directors the (power concentration rokne ke liye).
        *   Lekin Directors aur Legislative Councils mein clashes hote the, leading to political instability.
    *   **Napoleon Bonaparte ka Uday (Rise of Napoleon):** Directory ki instability ne military dictator Napoleon Bonaparte ke liye raasta banaya. 1804 mein woh Emperor bana.

5.  **Mahilaon ki Bhumika (Role of Women)**
    *   Shuru se active participants thi. Apne clubs aur newspapers shuru kiye (e.g., Society of Revolutionary and Republican Women).
    *   Political rights (vote, election ladna, office hold karna) ki maang ki. 1791 Constitution se disappointed thi (passive citizens).
    *   Revolutionary government ne kuch sudhar kiye (schooling compulsory, divorce legal, job training).
    *   Reign of Terror mein women's clubs band kar diye gaye, activities ban hui.
    *   *Olympe de Gouges:* Politically active mahila, 'Declaration of the Rights of Woman and Citizen' (1791) likha, Jacobins ko criticise kiya, treason ke charge mein execute hui.
    *   France mein mahilaon ko vote ka adhikar finally 1946 mein mila.

6.  **Gulami ka Ant (Abolition of Slavery)**
    *   French colonies (Caribbean) mein plantations par labour shortage thi, jise triangular slave trade (Europe-Africa-Americas) se poora kiya jaata tha. Bordeaux aur Nantes jaise port cities slave trade se prosperous hue.
    *   Convention ne 1794 mein French colonies mein slavery abolish ki.
    *   Napoleon ne 1804 mein slavery phir se shuru kar di.
    *   Finally, French colonies mein slavery 1848 mein abolish hui.

7.  **Revolution aur Rozmarra ki Zindagi (Revolution and Everyday Life)**
    *   1789 ke baad censorship abolish hui. Newspapers, books, plays etc. bina king ke approval ke publish ho sakte थे.
    *   Freedom of speech and expression natural right bana. Ideas tezi se phaile.
    *   Plays, songs, festive processions se liberty/justice jaise ideas aam logon tak pahuche. Political symbols ka use badha (Box 1).

8.  **Revolution ki Virasat (Legacy of the French Revolution)**
    *   **Ideas:** Liberty (स्वतंत्रता), Equality (समानता), Fraternity (बंधुत्व) aur democratic rights ka idea France se baaki Europe aur duniya mein phaila.
    *   **Impact on Europe:** Feudal systems abolish hue. Nation-state ka concept strong hua.
    *   **Napoleon:** Reforms (private property protection, uniform weights/measures - decimal system) kiye, modern laws laaye. Lekin uski armies invading force bhi bani. Defeated at Waterloo (1815).
    *   **Impact on Colonised Peoples (like India):** Freedom from bondage ka idea movements ko inspire kiya. Tipu Sultan aur Raja Rammohan Roy jaise Indians ne revolutionary France ke ideas par react kiya.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh)

Is chapter se hum seekhte hain ki kaise France mein社會िक (social), आर्थिक (economic), aur राजनीतिक (political) conditions ne ek bade badlav (revolution) ko janm diya.

1.  **Old Regime ki Problems:** Teen estates ka system bahut unequal tha. Third Estate par saara tax ka bojh tha, jabki First aur Second Estate ko privileges mile hue the. Khali khazana aur badhti keematon ne aam logon ki zindagi mushkil kar di thi (Subsistence Crisis).
    *   *Diagram Idea 📈:* Ek simple pyramid structure showing 3 Estates, highlighting population % and tax burden.
        *   Top: Clergy (Small %, No Tax)
        *   Middle: Nobility (Small %, No Tax, Feudal Dues)
        *   Bottom: Third Estate (Large 90%+, All Taxes - Taille, Tithe, Indirect)

2.  **Ideas ka Role:** Enlightenment philosophers ke liberty, equality, aur popular sovereignty ke ideas ne logon ko inspire kiya, especially middle class ko. In ideas ne Old Regime ke legitimacy par sawal uthaye.

3.  **Revolution ke Stages:** Revolution ek single event nahi tha, balki ek process tha jismein kai phases aaye:
    *   *Phase 1:* Absolute Monarchy -> Constitutional Monarchy (National Assembly, Declaration of Rights, Limited Suffrage)
    *   *Phase 2:* Monarchy Abolished -> Republic (Convention, Universal Male Suffrage, Jacobins, Reign of Terror)
    *   *Phase 3:* Reign of Terror Ends -> Directory Rule (Wealthier Class Power, Instability)
    *   *Phase 4:* Directory Overthrown -> Napoleon's Rule (Consolidation, Expansion, Reforms)

4.  **Rights ki Ladai:** Revolution ne 'Rights of Man' declare kiye, lekin yeh universal nahi the. Mahilaon ko political rights nahi mile, aur shuru mein vote ka adhikar bhi limited tha. Slavery bhi pehle abolish hui, phir reinstate hui, aur baad mein finally abolish hui. Isse pata chalta hai ki liberty aur equality ke ideas ko practice mein laana complex tha.

5.  **Symbols ka Mahatva:** Revolution mein symbols (like Broken chain, Red Phrygian cap, Eye within triangle, Bundle of rods, National colours Blue-White-Red - Box 1) ka use important ideas ko communicate karne ke liye kiya gaya, especially un logon ke liye jo padh-likh nahi sakte the.

6.  **Legacy:** French Revolution modern world ke liye ek turning point tha. Iske liberty, equality, fraternity aur democratic rights ke ideas ne duniya bhar mein political movements ko inspire kiya, including India's freedom struggle.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ) 🇮🇳📊

French Revolution ke ideas ka asar sirf Europe tak seemit nahi raha, balki Bharat jaise desh par bhi pada.

1.  **Inspiration for Indian Thinkers:** 19th century mein, Bharat ke kuch progressive thinkers jaise **Raja Rammohan Roy** French Revolution aur uske liberty, equality, fraternity ke ideas se bahut prabhavit the. Woh Europe mein ho rahe badlavon mein gehri dilchaspi rakhte the (Box 2). South India mein, **Tipu Sultan** bhi revolutionary France ke ideas mein interest rakhte the aur unhone French se madad lene ki koshish bhi ki thi.

2.  **Impact on Freedom Struggle:** French Revolution se nikle 'nation-state' (राष्ट्र-राज्य) aur 'sovereignty of the people' (जनता की संप्रभुता) ke ideas ne Bharat ke freedom fighters ko inspire kiya. Unhone bhi British Raj se 'freedom from bondage' (गुलामी से आज़ादी) aur ek sovereign nation banane ka sapna dekha. Liberty (आज़ादी) aur Equality (समानता) jaise concepts hamare national movement ke core values bane.

3.  **Influence on Indian Constitution:** Hamare Samvidhan (Constitution) ki Preamble (प्रस्तावना) mein liberty, equality, aur fraternity ke ideals shaamil hain. Fundamental Rights (मौलिक अधिकार) jo har Bharatiya citizen ko diye gaye hain, woh bhi kahin na kahin French 'Declaration of the Rights of Man and Citizen' se prerit hain. Yeh dikhata hai ki French Revolution ke ideas ka global aur long-lasting impact raha hai.

(Note: While direct economic data comparison isn't the focus here, the *ideas* of challenging unfair economic burdens (like heavy taxes on the Third Estate) resonated with Indians facing exploitative colonial economic policies.)
