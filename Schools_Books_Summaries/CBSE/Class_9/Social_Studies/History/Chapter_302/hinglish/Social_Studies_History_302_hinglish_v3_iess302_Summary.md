# [Class 9] History - Chapter 302: Europe mein Samajvaad aur Russian Kranti (Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter Europe mein social aur political badlav (changes) ke daur ko explore karta hai, khaas kar French Kranti ke baad, jisse naye vichar jaise Liberalism, Radicalism, aur Conservatism ubhre. Iske baad Industrial Revolution aur uske social impacts par focus kiya gaya hai, jisne Socialism (Samajvaad) ke uday ke liye zameen taiyar ki. Phir, chapter Russia par shift hota hai, 1917 ki Kranti se pehle ke halaat, Kranti ke mukhya events (February aur October Revolutions), Bolsheviks ka power mein aana, aur uske baad ke badlav (Civil War, Stalinism, Collectivisation) ko detail mein batata hai. Aakhir mein, Russian Kranti ke global influence, khaas kar Bharat par, ko discuss kiya gaya hai.

**Concept Hierarchy:**

1.  **French Kranti ka Asar (Impact of French Revolution)**
    *   Freedom (Azaadi) aur Equality (Samanata) ke ideas ka phailna.
    *   Samajik sanrachna (social structure) badalne ki possibility.
2.  **Nayi Rajneetik Vichardharayein (New Political Ideologies)**
    *   **Liberals (Udarwadi):** Tolerant nation, individual rights ke paksh mein, elected government, lekin universal suffrage ke khilaf (khaas kar property-less men aur women ke liye).
    *   **Radicals (Amool Parivartanwadi):** Majority-based government, women's suffrage ke supporter, private property ke concentration ke khilaf.
    *   **Conservatives (Rudhiwadi):** Dheere-dheere badlav ke paksh mein, parampara (tradition) ka samman.
3.  **Industrial Society aur Samajik Badlav (Industrial Society and Social Change)**
    *   Naye shehron ka vikas, factories ka aana.
    *   Workers ki kharab sthiti (long hours, poor wages, unemployment, poor housing).
    *   Liberals aur Radicals dwara solutions ki khoj.
4.  **Socialism ka Uday (Coming of Socialism)**
    *   Private property (niji sampatti) ka virodh - ise sabhi social problems ki jad maana gaya.
    *   Collective social interests (samuhik samajik hit) par zor.
    *   **Alag-alag Visions:**
        *   Cooperatives (Sahakari Samitiyan) - Robert Owen, Louis Blanc.
        *   Marxism (Karl Marx & Friedrich Engels): Capitalism (Poonjivad) ka analysis, workers dwara exploitation (shoshan), communist society ka idea jahan property socially controlled ho.
    *   **Support for Socialism:** Second International ka formation, Trade Unions, Social Democratic Party (Germany), Labour Party (Britain), Socialist Party (France).
5.  **Russian Kranti (The Russian Revolution)**
    *   **Russia 1914 mein:** Tsarist autocracy (Tsar Nicholas II ka shasan), vast empire, Orthodox Christianity major religion, diverse population.
    *   **Economy & Society:** Primarily agricultural (85% log kheti se jude), industry pockets mein (St Petersburg, Moscow), workers divided (skill, village links), peasants ki halat (land hunger, nobility se nafrat, commune system - *mir*).
    *   **Socialism in Russia:** Political parties illegal (before 1914), Russian Social Democratic Workers Party (1898 - Marxists), Socialist Revolutionary Party (1900 - peasants' rights), Lenin aur Bolsheviks (disciplined party) vs Mensheviks (open party).
    *   **1905 ki Kranti:** Turbulent time, Bloody Sunday (Khooni Ravivar), strikes, demands for constitution, Duma (elected parliament) ka creation (lekin Tsar ne powers limit kar di).
    *   **Pehla Vishwa Yudh (First World War):** Initial support for Tsar, baad mein unpopularity (defeats, casualties, Tsarina & Rasputin ka influence), industry par bura asar, food shortages.
    *   **February Kranti (1917):** Petrograd mein food shortages, strikes (International Women's Day), soldiers ka विद्रोह (mutiny), Tsar ka abdication (gaddi chhodna), Provisional Government (Antarim Sarkar) ka formation.
    *   **October Kranti (1917):** Lenin ki wapsi, April Theses (war end, land transfer, banks nationalised), Bolshevik influence badha, Provisional Government ke khilaf uprising, Bolsheviks dwara power seizure (satta par kabza), All Russian Congress of Soviets dwara approval.
6.  **October ke Baad Kya Badla? (What Changed after October?)**
    *   **Bolshevik Policies:** Private property ka विरोध, Industry/Banks ka Nationalisation (Rashtriyakaran), Land declared social property (peasants ko seizure ki anumati), Old titles banned, New uniforms, Bolshevik Party renamed Communist Party.
    *   **Civil War (1918-20):** Bolsheviks ('Reds') vs Non-Bolshevik socialists ('Greens') aur Pro-Tsarists ('Whites'), foreign intervention (France, Britain, USA, Japan), Bolshevik victory.
    *   **Making a Socialist Society:** Centralised Planning, Five-Year Plans (Panchvarshiya Yojnaayein) for industrial growth, new factory cities, poor working conditions, extended schooling, crèches, healthcare (limited resources).
7.  **Stalinism aur Collectivisation (Stalinism and Collectivisation)**
    *   Grain supplies ki problem.
    *   Stalin ka power mein aana (Lenin ki death ke baad).
    *   **Collectivisation Programme (1929 se):** Forced peasants to cultivate in collective farms (*kolkhoz*), elimination of 'kulaks' (well-to-do peasants), peasant resistance (livestock destruction), severe punishment (deportation, exile), production initially nahi badhi, devastating famine (1930-33, 4 million+ deaths).
    *   **Criticism and Repression:** Party ke andar criticism, Stalin dwara critics par conspiracy ka आरोप, purges (safai abhiyan), lakhs log prisons/labour camps mein, forced confessions, executions.
8.  **Global Influence aur USSR (Global Influence and the USSR)**
    *   Workers' state ke idea ne duniya bhar ke logon ko inspire kiya.
    *   Communist parties ka formation (e.g., Great Britain, India).
    *   Comintern (Communist International).
    *   USSR ka ek global power banna.
    *   **Criticism:** Lack of freedom, repressive policies.
    *   USSR ka decline, lekin socialist ideals ka respect barkarar.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh) 📈

1.  **Ideologies ka Clash:** French Kranti ke baad Europe mein teen mukhya political groups ubhre:
    *   **Liberals:** Badlav chahte the, lekin dheere-dheere aur sabko vote ka adhikar (universal suffrage) nahi dena chahte the, khaaskar jinke paas property na ho ya mahilayein. Ve individual rights aur elected parliament ke paksh mein the.
    *   **Radicals:** Tez badlav chahte the, government majority population par based ho, aur mahilaon ko bhi vote ka adhikar mile. Ve private property ke concentration ke khilaf the.
    *   **Conservatives:** Purani vyavastha ko banaye rakhna chahte the ya bahut dheere badlav chahte the. French Kranti ke baad unhone bhi kuch badlav ki zaroorat mehsoos ki.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek comparison table banayein jo Liberals, Radicals, aur Conservatives ke views ko property, voting rights, aur change ki speed par compare kare.

2.  **Industrialisation ka Asar aur Socialism ka Janm:**
    *   Industrial Revolution ne production badhaya lekin workers ki life bahut kathin bana di (lambi duty, kam salary, begharipan). Isse social inequality badhi.
    *   Socialists ne is inequality ka karan private property ko mana. Unka manna tha ki agar means of production (factories, land etc.) par sabka control ho (collective ownership), toh society behtar hogi.
    *   Karl Marx ne kaha ki workers ko capitalism (poonjivad) ko ukhad phenk kar ek communist society banani hogi jahan sab kuch socially controlled ho.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek flowchart banayein jo dikhaye: Industrial Revolution -> Poor Working Conditions / Inequality -> Rise of Socialist Ideas -> Different Socialist Approaches (Cooperatives, Marxism).

3.  **Russian Kranti ke Karan (Causes of Russian Revolution):**
    *   **Political:** Tsar Nicholas II ki autocracy (tanashahi), logo ka participation na hona, Duma ki kamzor powers.
    *   **Social:** Workers ki kharab conditions, peasants mein zameen ki bhookh aur nobility se nafrat, society mein divisions.
    *   **Economic:** Backward economy, WWI ke karan food shortages aur industry par bura asar, high prices.
    *   **WWI ka Impact:** Bhari haar, massive casualties, soldiers ka demoralisation, government mein logon ka vishwas khatm hona.

4.  **1917 ki Do Krantiyan (The Two Revolutions of 1917):**
    *   **February Revolution:** Spontaneous uprising (khud se shuru hui), food riots aur strikes se shuru hui (khaas kar mahila workers ne lead kiya), soldiers ne bhi join kiya, Tsar ko gaddi chhodni padi, Provisional Government bani. Yeh largely monarchy ke khilaf thi.
    *   **October Revolution:** Planned takeover by Bolsheviks under Lenin. Unhone 'Peace, Land, and Bread' ka nara diya. Provisional Government ko hata kar power seize ki gayi. Yeh socialist revolution thi.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek timeline banayein jo 1917 ke mukhya events (Feb Rev start, Tsar abdicates, Lenin returns, July Days, Kornilov Affair (not explicitly detailed but relevant context), Oct Rev) ko dikhaye.

5.  **Bolsheviks ke Shuruati Kadam aur Civil War:**
    *   Power mein aate hi Bolsheviks ne bade badlav kiye: Industries/banks nationalise kiye, zameen ko social property declare kiya (kisano ko kabza karne diya), purane titles khatm kiye.
    *   In badlavon ka virodh hua, jisse Civil War shuru hui (Reds vs Whites/Greens). Foreign powers ne bhi Whites ka saath diya. Bolsheviks jeet gaye, partly due to land policies jo peasants ko attract karti thi aur non-Russian nationalities ke cooperation se.

6.  **Stalin ka Daur - Industrialisation aur Atyachar (Stalin's Era - Industrialisation and Repression):**
    *   Stalin ne tezi se industrialisation ke liye Five-Year Plans shuru kiye. Isse industrial production badha, naye sheher bane.
    *   Lekin iske saath working conditions kharab hui, aur logon ko bahut hardship jhelni padi.
    *   **Collectivisation:** Kheti ko modernise karne aur grain shortage door karne ke naam par, kisano se zabardasti unki zameen lekar collective farms (kolkhoz) banaye gaye. Iska bhari virodh hua, jisne famine (akaal) ko janm diya aur लाखों log maare gaye. Kulaks ko 'class enemy' maan kar khatm kiya gaya.
    *   Stalin ne kisi bhi tarah ke opposition ko brutally crush kiya (purges, labour camps - Gulags, executions).

7.  **Global Impact - Ek Nayi Ummeed aur Ek Warning:**
    *   Russian Kranti ne duniya bhar ke workers aur colonised logon ko inspire kiya. Socialism ek powerful global idea ban gaya. Bharat mein bhi iska asar hua (CPI ka formation, Nehru/Tagore ke vichar).
    *   Lekin, USSR mein democracy ki kami aur state dwara kiye gaye अत्याचार (atrocities) ne yeh bhi dikhaya ki socialist ideals ko galat tareeke se implement karne ke kya khatre ho sakte hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Context)

*   **Ideas ka Prabhav (Influence of Ideas):** French Kranti ke ideals (freedom, equality) ne 19th century mein Raja Rammohan Roy aur Derozio jaise Bharatiya thinkers ko prabhavit kiya. Italy ke nationalist Giuseppe Mazzini ke writings ne bhi Bharat ke nationalists ko inspire kiya.
*   **Russian Kranti se Prerna (Inspiration from Russian Revolution):**
    *   20th century mein, Russian Kranti ne kai Bharatiya revolutionaries aur nationalists ko inspire kiya. Unhe laga ki agar Russia mein workers aur peasants state bana sakte hain, toh Bharat bhi British rule se azaad ho sakta hai.
    *   **Communist Movement:** Kai Indians ne USSR ki 'Communist University of the Workers of the East' mein training li. 1920s mein Communist Party of India (CPI) ka formation hua, jiske Soviet Communist Party se links the.
    *   **Leaders ke Vichar:** Jawaharlal Nehru aur Rabindranath Tagore jaise figures ne Soviet Union visit kiya. Tagore ne wahan ke education system aur social upliftment (khaas kar peasants aur workers ka) ki tareef ki, halanki unhone freedom ki kami par bhi chinta jatayi. Nehru Soviet planning se kafi prabhavit the, jiska asar azaad Bharat ki Five-Year Plans par dikhta hai.
    *   **Perception:** Shaukat Usmani jaise Indians ne Soviet Russia mein equality aur lack of racial discrimination dekha (Source F), jo unke liye impressive tha, khaas kar colonial India ke context mein.
*   **Economic Data Context:** Jab hum Russia ke 85% population ke agriculture par dependent hone ki baat karte hain (pre-revolution), hum ise British India ki predominantly agrarian economy se compare kar sakte hain, jahan kisano ki halat bhi exploitation aur poverty se ग्रस्त (grasst - afflicted) thi. Socialism aur land reform ke ideas ne Bharat mein bhi land redistribution aur zamindari abolition jaise muddo par behas ko badhawa diya. Soviet Union ke Five-Year Plans ne Bharat mein bhi centralised economic planning ke model ko influence kiya.
