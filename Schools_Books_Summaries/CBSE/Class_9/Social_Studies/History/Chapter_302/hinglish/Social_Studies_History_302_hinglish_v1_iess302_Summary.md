# [Class 9] History - Chapter 302: Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution / Europe mein Samajwad evam Russi Kranti

## 🌟 Core Concepts
Detailed concept hierarchy 📊

1.  **Europe mein Social Change ke Ideas (Ideas of Social Change in Europe)**
    *   **French Revolution ka Asar:** Freedom (azadi) aur equality (samanta) ke ideas ka phailna. Society ke structure ko badalne ki possibility.
    *   **Teen Pramukh Vichardharayein (Three Main Ideologies):**
        *   **Liberals (Udarwadi):** Sabhi religions ko tolerate karne wala nation chahte the. Individual rights ke paksh mein the, lekin universal adult franchise (sabhi ko vote ka adhikar) ke khilaf the (property wale men ko vote). Women's vote ke bhi khilaf the. Elected parliamentary government chahte the.
        *   **Radicals (Amool Parivartanwadi):** Majority population par based government chahte the. Women's suffragette movements ko support karte the. Bade landowners aur wealthy factory owners ke privileges ke khilaf the. Private property ke khilaf nahi, par concentration of property ke khilaf the.
        *   **Conservatives (Rudhiwadi):** Badlav ke khilaf the, par French Revolution ke baad dheere-dheere badlav (gradual change) ki zaroorat accept ki. Past ki respect par zor dete the.
    *   **Industrial Society aur Social Change (Industrial Society and Social Change):**
        *   Industrial Revolution ka impact: New cities, factories, railways.
        *   Workers ki समस्याएं: Lambe working hours, kam wages (vetan), unemployment, poor housing & sanitation.
        *   Liberals aur Radicals dwara solutions ki khoj.
    *   **Socialism ka Uday (Rise of Socialism):**
        *   Private property ko social problems ka root maana gaya.
        *   **Early Socialists:**
            *   Robert Owen: Cooperative communities (New Harmony).
            *   Louis Blanc: Government encouraged cooperatives.
        *   **Karl Marx & Friedrich Engels:**
            *   Industrial society = Capitalist society.
            *   Capitalists own capital, profit workers se aata hai.
            *   Workers ko capitalism overthrow karna hoga.
            *   Communist society (sabhi property socially controlled) future hai.
        *   **Support for Socialism:** Second International (1870s), Workers' associations (Germany - SPD, Britain - Labour Party, France - Socialist Party).

2.  **Russian Revolution (Russi Kranti)**
    *   **Kranti se Pehle ka Russia (Pre-Revolutionary Russia - 1914 tak):**
        *   **Political System:** Autocracy under Tsar Nicholas II (Tsar ki tanashahi, koi parliament ke prati jawabdeh nahi).
        *   **Empire:** Vast, diverse (Finland, Poland ke hisse, Central Asia, etc.). Majority religion: Russian Orthodox Christianity.
        *   **Economy & Society:**
            *   ~85% population agriculturists. Russia = major grain exporter.
            *   Industry pockets mein (St Petersburg, Moscow). Large factories & craft workshops saath-saath.
            *   Workers: Divided social group (skill, village links). Poor working conditions (10-15 hours), kam wages. Women workers (31% by 1914) ko aur kam vetan. Strikes hote the (1896-97, 1902).
            *   Peasants: Land cultivate karte the, par nobility, crown, Church ke paas large properties thi. Nobles ke liye respect nahi thi. Land redistribution chahte थे. Commune (mir) system - land pooling & periodic division.
    *   **Socialism in Russia:**
        *   Political parties illegal thi (before 1914).
        *   Russian Social Democratic Workers Party (RSDWP) - 1898 (Marxist). Illegal organisation.
        *   Socialist Revolutionary Party - 1900 (Peasants' rights, land transfer).
        *   **Lenin & Bolsheviks vs Mensheviks:** RSDWP mein division. Lenin (Bolshevik leader) disciplined party chahte the. Mensheviks open party chahte the. Lenin ko lagta tha peasants united nahi hain.
    *   **1905 Kranti (1905 Revolution):**
        *   **Causes:** Autocracy, poor worker conditions (real wages decline), Russo-Japanese War mein haar (not explicitly mentioned in this section but contextually relevant).
        *   **Bloody Sunday (Khooni Ravivar):** Workers' procession par attack (Jan 1905). >100 killed.
        *   **Effects:** Strikes across country, universities band, Union of Unions bani (constituent assembly ki demand). Tsar ne **Duma** (elected consultative Parliament) banane ki permission di. Pehli do Dumas jaldi dismiss kar di gayi. Voting laws change karke Third Duma mein conservatives bhar diye gaye.
    *   **First World War ka Asar (Impact of WWI):**
        *   Initially popular, baad mein support ghata (Tsar ne Duma se consult nahi kiya).
        *   Anti-German sentiments (St Petersburg -> Petrograd). Tsarina & Rasputin se autocracy unpopular hui.
        *   Heavy losses (7 million casualties by 1917). Army ne crops/buildings destroy kiye -> 3 million refugees.
        *   Severe impact on industry (Baltic Sea route cut off). Labour shortages, grain shortage -> bread riots (winter 1916).
    *   **February Revolution (Farvari Kranti - 1917):**
        *   **Context:** Petrograd mein grim conditions, food shortages, cold winter. Duma ko dissolve karne ke Tsar ke desire ke khilaf Parliamentarians.
        *   **Events:**
            *   22 Feb: Factory lockout.
            *   23 Feb: Sympathy strikes (50 factories), women led many (International Women's Day). Workers cross to city centre. Curfew.
            *   24-25 Feb: Demonstrations continue. Govt calls cavalry.
            *   25 Feb: Duma suspended.
            *   26 Feb: Demonstrators return.
            *   27 Feb: Police HQ ransacked. Slogans for bread, wages, democracy. Cavalry refuses to fire. Regiments mutiny. Soldiers & workers form **Petrograd Soviet**.
            *   2 March: Tsar abdicates (gaddi chhod deta hai).
        *   **Outcome:** **Provisional Government** bani (Soviet leaders + Duma leaders). Future Constituent Assembly decide karegi (universal adult suffrage basis par). Monarchy khatm.
    *   **October Revolution (October Kranti - 1917):**
        *   **Context:** Lenin returns (April 1917), issues **April Theses** (War end, Land to peasants, Banks nationalised). Bolshevik Party renamed Communist Party. Provisional Government ki power reduce, Bolshevik influence badha. Peasants seize land (July-Sep).
        *   **Events:**
            *   Lenin ko dar tha Provisional Govt dictatorship layegi. Uprising ki planning. Military Revolutionary Committee (under Trotskii) bani.
            *   24 Oct: Uprising begins. Govt buildings seized, ministers arrest kiye gaye. Winter Palace par shelling (Aurora ship). City control mein.
            *   All Russian Congress of Soviets approves Bolshevik action. Moscow mein fighting.
        *   **Outcome:** Bolsheviks control Moscow-Petrograd area by December. Power seized.

3.  **Kranti ke Baad (After the Revolution)**
    *   **Bolshevik Changes (Immediate):**
        *   Private property ke opposed. Industry & banks nationalised (Nov 1917).
        *   Land declared social property, peasants ko nobility ki land seize karne ki permission.
        *   Cities mein large houses partition kiye gaye. Old titles banned. New uniforms (Soviet hat - *budeonovka*).
        *   Constituent Assembly elections mein majority nahi mili -> Lenin dismisses Assembly (Jan 1918). All Russian Congress of Soviets ko zyada democratic mana.
        *   Peace with Germany (Brest Litovsk, March 1918).
        *   Russia becomes one-party state. Trade unions under party control. Secret police (Cheka -> OGPU -> NKVD). Censorship.
    *   **Civil War (1918-20):**
        *   **Sides:** Bolsheviks ('reds') vs 'greens' (Socialist Revolutionaries) & 'whites' (pro-Tsarists). Whites ko French, American, British, Japanese troops ka support.
        *   **Reasons:** Land redistribution order se army break-up, opposition to Bolshevik uprising.
        *   **Impact:** Looting, banditry, famine. Whites ke harsh steps se popular support loss.
        *   **Outcome:** Bolsheviks win by Jan 1920. Cooperation with non-Russian nationalities & Muslim jadidists helped. USSR (Soviet Union) formed (Dec 1922). Political autonomy di gayi, par unpopular policies (like nomadism discouragement) se success limited rahi.
    *   **Socialist Society ka Nirman (Building a Socialist Society):**
        *   **Centralised Planning:** Five Year Plans introduce kiye gaye (First two: 1927-32, 1933-38). Govt fixed prices.
        *   **Economic Growth:** Industrial production badha (Oil, coal, steel 100% increase in 1929-33). New factory cities (Magnitogorsk).
        *   **Problems:** Rapid construction -> Poor working conditions (Magnitogorsk mein hard lives, stoppages). Poor living quarters.
        *   **Social Changes:** Extended schooling, universities mein workers/peasants ke liye arrangements. Crèches in factories. Cheap public healthcare. Model living quarters. Effect uneven tha (limited resources).
    *   **Stalinism & Collectivisation:**
        *   **Context:** Grain supplies ki problem (1927-28). Peasants fixed price par grain nahi bech rahe the. Stalin (Lenin ke baad party head) ne emergency measures liye. Rich peasants ('kulaks') par hoarding ka आरोप.
        *   **Collectivisation Programme (1929 onwards):** Small farms ko modernise karna mushkil mana gaya. Solution: Eliminate kulaks, land chheen kar state-controlled large farms (**kolkhoz**) banana.
        *   **Implementation:** Peasants ko kolkhoz mein cultivate karne ko force kiya gaya. Land & implements transfer hue. Kolkhoz profit shared hota tha.
        *   **Resistance:** Peasants ne resist kiya, livestock destroy kiye (cattle fell by 1/3rd between 1929-31). Resistors ko severely punish kiya gaya (deported, exiled). Peasants ka argument: woh rich nahi the, socialism ke khilaf nahi the, bas kolkhoz mein kaam nahi karna chahte the.
        *   **Impact:** Production immediately nahi badha. Bad harvests (1930-33) -> Devastating famine (over 4 million died).
        *   **Criticism & Purges:** Party ke andar bhi criticism hui. Stalin ne critics par conspiracy ka आरोप lagaya. By 1939, over 2 million log prisons/labour camps mein the (many innocent, forced confessions, executed).

4.  **Global Influence & USSR (Vaishvik Prabhav aur USSR)**
    *   **Initial Reaction:** European socialist parties Bolshevik takeover ke tareeke se poori tarah sehmat nahi the.
    *   **Inspiration:** Workers' state ki possibility ne duniya bhar ke logon ko inspire kiya. Communist parties bani (e.g., Great Britain).
    *   **Comintern:** Bolsheviks ne colonial logon ko unka experiment follow karne ko encourage kiya. Conference of the Peoples of the East (1920), Comintern (pro-Bolshevik socialist parties ka international union). USSR's Communist University mein education.
    *   **WWII tak:** USSR ne socialism ko global face diya.
    *   **Later Acknowledgement (1950s onwards):** USSR mein government style revolution ke ideals ke mutabik nahi tha. World socialist movement mein bhi problems recognise ki gayi. Backward country great power bani, industry/agriculture develop hui, poor ko khana mila, BUT essential freedoms deny ki gayi, repressive policies se development hua.
    *   **End of 20th Century:** USSR ki international reputation as socialist country decline hui, par socialist ideals ki respect logon mein bani rahi. Har desh mein socialism ke ideas alag-alag tareekon se re-think kiye gaye.

## 📘 Key Learnings
Detailed explanations with diagrams 📈

1.  **Europe mein Vicharon ka Sangharsh (Clash of Ideas in Europe):**
    *   French Revolution ne 'status quo' ko challenge kiya. Purane system (aristocracy/church control) ko badalne ki hope jaagi.
    *   **Liberals vs Radicals vs Conservatives:** Inke beech society ke future ko lekar deep differences the.
        *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek spectrum (line) dikhayein jiske ek end par Conservatives (slow/no change), beech mein Liberals (gradual change, limited democracy), aur doosre end par Radicals (fast change, majority rule, wider rights) ho.
    *   Ye terms time aur context ke saath badalte hain.

2.  **Industrialisation ka Double-Edged Sword (Two Sides of Industrialisation):**
    *   **Progress:** New technologies, factories, cities, railways. Wealth creation (mostly for owners).
    *   **Problems:** Workers ke liye exploitation (lamba kaam, kam paisa, khatarnak conditions), unemployment, shehron mein bheed aur gandagi.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Factory -> Produces Goods & Profits (for owners) -> BUT also leads to Poor Worker Conditions & Urban Problems -> Leads to Calls for Change (Liberal/Radical/Socialist ideas).
    *   Liberals/Radicals (jo khud often property owners the) मानते थे ki individual effort aur freedom se society develop hogi, agar workers healthy aur educated hon.

3.  **Socialism: Ek Naya Vision (Socialism: A New Vision):**
    *   **Core Idea:** Private property samajik buraiyon ki jad hai. Property par individual control ki jagah collective (samuhik) control hona chahiye, jisse social interests par dhyan diya ja sake.
    *   **Different Approaches:**
        *   Cooperatives (Owen, Blanc): Log milkar produce karein aur profit baantein.
        *   Marxism (Communism): Capitalism inherently exploitative hai. Workers ko revolution karke power seize karni hogi aur ek classless, stateless communist society banani hogi jahan sab property socially owned ho.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Capitalism (Private Property -> Worker Exploitation -> Profit for Capitalist) --- [Marxist Solution: Revolution] ---> Socialism/Communism (Social Property -> No Exploitation -> Needs Met Collectively).

4.  **Russia ka Unique Context (Russia's Unique Context):**
    *   Europe ke comparison mein kam industrialised. Zyada population kheti par nirbhar.
    *   **Tsarist Autocracy:** Raja ki unlimited power, koi effective parliament nahi.
    *   **Peasantry:** Land redistribution ki strong desire. Nobles ke liye respect nahi. *Mir* (commune) system unique tha (land pooling). Isse kuch socialists ko laga ki Russia mein peasants revolution ka main force honge.
    *   **Workers:** Conditions kharab, strikes hote the, par workers divided the.

5.  **Russi Kranti ke Stages (Stages of the Russian Revolution):**
    *   **1905 Revolution:** 'Dress rehearsal'. Bloody Sunday ne widespread protest trigger kiya. Duma bani par Tsar ne power kam nahi ki.
    *   **WWI ka Catalyst Role:** War ne Russia ki weaknesses expose ki. Army mein dissatisfaction, economy collapse, food shortages ne Tsar ke khilaf gussa badhaya.
    *   **February Revolution (1917):** Spontaneous uprising (Bread riots + worker strikes + soldier mutiny). Tsar ka शासन khatm. Provisional Government bani (liberal/moderate socialist).
    *   **October Revolution (1917):** Planned Bolshevik takeover. Lenin ke leadership mein soviets ne power seize ki. Provisional Government ko overthrow kiya.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Timeline: 1904-05 (Russo-Jap War, Bloody Sunday, 1905 Rev, Duma) -> 1914 (WWI Starts) -> 1916 (Food Riots) -> Feb 1917 (Tsar Abdicates, Prov. Govt) -> April 1917 (Lenin Returns, April Theses) -> Oct 1917 (Bolsheviks Seize Power) -> 1918-20 (Civil War) -> 1922 (USSR Formed).

6.  **Bolshevik Power mein: Sapne aur Haqeeqat (Bolsheviks in Power: Dreams vs Reality):**
    *   **Immediate Changes:** Radical reforms (land, industry, banks, titles). One-party state bana. Peace treaty ki.
    *   **Civil War:** Brutal conflict jisne desh ko aur barbaad kiya. Bolsheviks jeete.
    *   **Building Socialism:** Five Year Plans se rapid industrialisation hua, par human cost bohot zyada tha (poor conditions, forced labour). Social welfare measures (education, health, crèches) bhi liye gaye, par resources limited the.
    *   **Stalinism & Collectivisation:** Agriculture ko force se collectivise kiya gaya. Kulaks ko eliminate kiya gaya. Bhayankar famine (akaal) aaya. Political opposition ko brutally suppress kiya gaya (Purges).
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Collectivisation: Small Peasant Farms -> Forced into Kolkhoz (Collective Farms) -> State Control over Agriculture -> Peasant Resistance / Livestock Destruction -> Lower Production Initially / Famine -> Elimination of Kulaks / Deportations.

7.  **Global Impact:**
    *   Russian Revolution ne duniya bhar mein hope aur dar dono paida kiye. Socialism ek major global force bana.
    *   USSR ek superpower bana, par uske methods (repression, lack of freedom) ki criticism hui.
    *   India jaise desh mein independence movements aur social reformers par iska asar pada.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context
National economic/social data 📊 (Focus on Influence & Connections)

*   **Early Connections:** French Revolution ke ideas (freedom, equality) ne India mein bhi thinkers ko prabhavit kiya. Raja Rammohan Roy aur Derozio ne iske significance par baat ki. Italian nationalist Giuseppe Mazzini ke writings ko Indian nationalists ne bhi padha.
*   **Russian Revolution ka Prabhav (Impact of Russian Revolution):**
    *   **Inspiration:** Revolution ne kai Indians ko inspire kiya. Ek workers' state ki possibility ne imagination ko capture kiya.
    *   **Communist Movement:** Kai Indians ne USSR ki Communist University mein training li. Mid-1920s tak India mein Communist Party (CPI) form ho gayi. CPI ke members Soviet Communist Party ke touch mein rehte the. M.N. Roy ek prominent Indian revolutionary the jo Comintern leader bane.
    *   **Visits and Writings:** Important Indian figures jaise Jawaharlal Nehru aur Rabindranath Tagore ne Soviet Russia visit kiya aur Soviet Socialism par likha.
        *   Tagore (Source G) ne workers ki improved condition aur ignorance/helplessness ko kam karne ke efforts ko appreciate kiya ("It all seemed like the work of the Genii in the Arabian Nights").
        *   Shaukat Usmani (Source F) ne Europeans aur Asians ke beech equality aur logon mein confidence/fearlessness dekha ("We had come to a land of real equality").
    *   **Literature:** Soviet Russia par India mein kaafi likha gaya. Hindi mein R.S. Avasthi (Russian Revolution, Lenin), S.D. Vidyalankar (The Rebirth of Russia). Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu mein bhi likha gaya. (Fig. 20 shows Indo-Soviet Journal).
*   **Economic/Social Data Comparison (Conceptual):** While direct comparable data isn't in the text, the *idea* of challenging vast inequality (like land ownership by nobility in Russia) resonated in India, where similar zamindari systems existed. The Soviet focus on planned industrial development and literacy campaigns also influenced India's post-independence planning approach, although India chose a democratic, mixed-economy path unlike the Soviet one-party state model. The Soviet experiment offered a powerful, though flawed, alternative model for rapid development and social change for newly independent nations.
