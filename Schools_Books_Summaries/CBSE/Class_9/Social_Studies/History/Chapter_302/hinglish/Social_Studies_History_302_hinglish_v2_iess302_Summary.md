# [Class 9] History - Chapter 302: Europe mein Samajwad evam Russi Kranti (Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter Europe mein social aur political badlavon par focus karta hai, khaas kar 19th aur 20th century mein, jiska climax Russian Revolution tha.

1.  **Samajik Parivartan ka Yug (The Age of Social Change)**
    *   French Kranti ke baad Europe mein badlav ki leher.
    *   Teen mukhya political vichardharayein (ideologies):
        *   **Liberals (Udarwadi):** Tolerant towards all religions, individual rights ke paksh mein, elected parliamentary government chahte the, lekin universal adult franchise (sabhi ko vote ka adhikar) ke khilaf the, property owners ko vote dena chahte the.
        *   **Radicals (Amool Parivartanwadi):** Majority population par based government chahte the, women's suffrage (mahilaon ka vote ka adhikar) ko support karte the, private property ke existence ke khilaf nahi the, parantu property ka concentration kuch haathon mein hone ke khilaf the.
        *   **Conservatives (Rudhiwadi):** Badlav ke khilaf the, lekin French Revolution ke baad kuch badlav ko zaroori maana, par slow process se aur past ko respect karte hue.
2.  **Audyogik Samaj aur Samajik Parivartan (Industrial Society and Social Change)**
    *   Industrial Revolution ka prabhav: Naye sheher, factories, railways.
    *   Workers ki samasyaayein: Lambe working hours, kam wages (vetan), unemployment, kharab housing aur sanitation.
    *   Liberals aur Radicals in issues ke solutions dhoondh rahe the.
3.  **Europe mein Samajwad ka Aana (The Coming of Socialism to Europe)**
    *   **Samajwad (Socialism):** Private property ke khilaf, ise sabhi social problems ki jad maante the. Collective social interests par zor.
    *   **Early Socialists:**
        *   Robert Owen: Cooperative communities (like New Harmony) ka idea.
        *   Louis Blanc: Government ko cooperatives encourage karna chahiye.
    *   **Marxism:**
        *   Karl Marx & Friedrich Engels: Industrial society 'capitalist' hai. Capitalists workers dwara produce kiye gaye profit ko accumulate karte hain.
        *   Workers ko capitalism overthrow karke ek 'communist' society banani hogi jahan saari property socially controlled ho.
4.  **Samajwad ke liye Samarthan (Support for Socialism)**
    *   1870s tak socialist ideas Europe mein faile.
    *   Second International: Socialists ka international coordination body.
    *   Workers' associations bani (e.g., Germany mein SPD, Britain mein Labour Party).
    *   1914 tak koi socialist government nahi bani, par unke ideas ne legislation ko shape kiya.
5.  **Russi Kranti (The Russian Revolution)**
    *   **1914 ka Russian Empire:** Tsar Nicholas II ka autocracy (nirankush shasan). Vast empire, diverse population (Russian Orthodox majority, plus Catholics, Protestants, Muslims, Buddhists).
    *   **Economy & Society:** Primarily agrarian (85% population). Industry kuch pockets mein (St Petersburg, Moscow). Workers divided the (skill, village links), conditions kharab thi. Peasants bhi divided the, nobility ke liye respect nahi tha, land redistribution chahte the. Commune (mir) system.
    *   **Russia mein Samajwad:** Political parties illegal thi. Russian Social Democratic Workers Party (1898) - Marxists. Socialist Revolutionary Party (1900) - Peasants' rights par focus. SDs mein division: Bolsheviks (Lenin - disciplined party) aur Mensheviks (sabke liye open party).
    *   **1905 ki Kranti:**
        *   Karan: Autocracy, workers ki kharab conditions, Russo-Japanese war mein haar.
        *   Bloody Sunday (Khooni Ravivar): Workers ke procession par attack, widespread strikes aur protests.
        *   Parinam: Tsar ne Duma (elected consultative parliament) banane ki anumati di, lekin baad mein use control kiya.
    *   **Pehla Vishwa Yudh aur Russian Empire (First World War):**
        *   Shuru mein popular support, baad mein war fatigue, heavy casualties (7 million by 1917).
        *   Economic impact: Industries par bura asar, labour shortage, food scarcity, riots. Tsar ki unpopularity badhi.
    *   **February Kranti (1917):**
        *   Petrograd (St Petersburg) mein food shortages, strikes (International Women's Day se shuru).
        *   Sena ne protestors par goli chalane se mana kiya, mutiny hui.
        *   Petrograd Soviet ka gathan.
        *   Tsar ne abdicate kiya (2 March). Provisional Government bani.
    *   **October Kranti (1917):**
        *   Lenin ka exile se wapas aana, April Theses (War band karo, land peasants ko do, banks nationalise karo).
        *   Bolshevik influence badha, Provisional Government kamzor hui.
        *   Bolsheviks ne Military Revolutionary Committee ke under power seize kiya (24 October). Winter Palace par kabza.
        *   All Russian Congress of Soviets ne Bolshevik action approve kiya. Moscow-Petrograd area par control.
6.  **October ke Baad kya Badla? (What Changed after October?)**
    *   Bolshevik policies: Private property ke khilaf. Industry/banks nationalised. Land social property declare, peasants ko nobility ki land seize karne ki anumati. Titles ban kiye gaye.
    *   Russian Communist Party (Bolshevik) naam rakha gaya. One-party state bana. Secret police (Cheka).
    *   Civil War (Greh Yudh) (1918-20): Bolsheviks ('Reds') vs. anti-Bolsheviks ('Greens' - SRs, 'Whites' - Pro-Tsarists, backed by foreign powers). Bolsheviks jeete.
    *   USSR ka gathan (1922).
7.  **Samajwadi Samaj ka Nirman (Making a Socialist Society)**
    *   Centralised Planning: Five Year Plans (Panchvarshiya Yojnaayein) shuru ki gayi (pehli do 1927-32, 1933-38). Focus on industrial growth.
    *   Economic growth hua, naye factory cities bane.
    *   Parantu: Poor working conditions, limited resources, uneven development.
    *   Social changes: Extended schooling, crèches, cheap healthcare, model living quarters.
8.  **Stalinism aur Samuhikaran (Stalinism and Collectivisation)**
    *   Lenin ke baad Stalin party head bane.
    *   Grain supplies ki problem solve karne ke liye:
        *   Emergency measures, 'kulaks' (well-to-do peasants) par raid.
        *   Collectivisation (Samuhikaran): Forced peasants to cultivate in collective farms (kolkhoz). State-controlled large farms.
        *   Peasants ka virodh, livestock destroy kiya. Severe punishment, deportation, exile.
        *   Result: Production immediately nahi badha. Bad harvests (1930-33) se devastating famine (4 million+ deaths).
    *   Party ke andar criticism ko dabaya gaya. Purges (safai abhiyan), accusations, labour camps, executions (1939 tak 2 million+).
9.  **Russi Kranti aur USSR ka Vaishvik Prabhav (Global Influence)**
    *   Workers' state ki possibility ne duniya bhar ke logon ko inspire kiya. Communist parties bani (e.g., Great Britain, India).
    *   Comintern (Communist International) ka gathan.
    *   USSR ek global power bana, socialism ko global face mila.
    *   Lekin: 1950s tak realise hua ki USSR mein government ka style revolution ke ideals ke anuroop nahi tha (lack of freedom, repressive policies).
    *   20th century ke end tak international reputation decline hui, par socialist ideals ka respect bana raha.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh)

1.  **Ideologies ka Sangharsh (Clash of Ideologies):** French Revolution ke baad Europe mein alag-alag political ideas (Liberalism, Radicalism, Conservatism) ubhre jo samaj ko shape karne ke liye compete kar rahe the. Yeh samjhna zaroori hai ki in terms ka meaning time aur context ke saath badalta hai.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek spectrum dikhaya ja sakta hai jisme ek taraf Conservatives (slow change/no change), beech mein Liberals (gradual change, limited democracy), aur doosri taraf Radicals (faster change, wider democracy) ho. 📊
2.  **Industrialisation ke Parinam (Consequences of Industrialisation):** Audyogikaran ne economic growth toh laaya, lekin saath hi workers ke liye nayi problems bhi create ki jaise exploitation, poverty, aur poor living conditions. Inhi conditions ne socialist ideas ke liye zameen taiyar ki.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek simple flow chart: Industrialisation -> Urbanisation + Factory System -> Poor Worker Conditions -> Rise of Socialist Ideas. 🏭➡️🏘️➡️😟➡️💡
3.  **Socialism ka Uday (Rise of Socialism):** Socialism ek powerful idea ke roop mein ubhra jo private property ko social problems ki jad maanta tha aur collective ownership/control ki baat karta tha. Marx aur Engels ne ismein class struggle aur communist revolution ka concept joda.
4.  **Russian Revolution ke Karan (Causes of Russian Revolution):** Russia mein क्रांति ke kai kaaran the: Tsarist autocracy ka oppressive nature, kisaano aur mazdooron ki dayaniye sthiti, 1905 ki Kranti ka failure, WWI mein Russia ki haar aur uske disastrous consequences.
5.  **February vs. October Revolution:** Dono revolutions alag the.
    *   **February Revolution:** Spontaneous thi, food shortages aur war weariness se trigger hui, jiske result mein Tsar ka shasan khatm hua aur ek Provisional Government bani. Ismein workers, soldiers, aur middle-class leaders shaamil the.
    *   **October Revolution:** Ek planned event tha, jise Bolshevik party ne Lenin ke leadership mein organise kiya. Iska aim Provisional Government ko hatakar socialist state (Soviet power) establish karna tha. Ismein Bolshevik party members, soldiers, aur workers active the.
6.  **Bolsheviks ka Power Consolidation:** October Revolution ke baad Bolsheviks ne turant radical changes kiye (land, banks, industry par control). Lekin unhe Civil War ka saamna karna pada aur power consolidate karne ke liye unhone one-party rule aur secret police ka istemal kiya.
7.  **Stalin ka Daur (Stalin's Era):** Stalin ke under USSR ne rapid industrialisation achieve kiya (Five Year Plans), lekin iski bhaari keemat chukani padi – forced collectivisation, jisne famine ko janm diya, aur political opponents ka brutal suppression (Purges). Yeh dikhata hai ki kaise socialist ideals ko authoritarian tareeke se implement kiya gaya.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek balance scale: Ek taraf Industrial Growth/Social Programs, doosri taraf Famine/Repression/Lack of Freedom. ⚖️
8.  **Global Impact:** Russian Revolution ne duniya bhar mein socialist aur communist movements ko inspire kiya, colonial countries mein independence struggles ko bhi prabhavit kiya. Lekin USSR ke internal problems ne baad mein iski image ko dhundhla kiya.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Context)

*   **Early Connections:** French Revolution ke ideas ne Raja Rammohan Roy aur Derozio jaise Indian thinkers ko prabhavit kiya tha, jo societal change par vichaar kar rahe the.
*   **Inspiration from Russia:** Russi Kranti ne kai Bharatiya nationalists aur intellectuals ko inspire kiya.
    *   **Jawaharlal Nehru aur Rabindranath Tagore** ne Soviet Russia visit kiya aur wahan ke experiment (Soviet Socialism) ke baare mein likha. Tagore ne wahan ke logo mein aaye confidence aur illiteracy/helplessness ko door karne ke prayason ki tareef ki (Source F).
    *   **Shaukat Usmani** jaise revolutionaries ne Soviet Russia mein equality aur freedom ko dekha (Source G).
    *   **M.N. Roy** ek prominent Indian revolutionary the jo Comintern ke leader bane aur Central Asia mein active the.
*   **Communist Movement in India:** Russi Kranti ki inspiration se mid-1920s mein **Communist Party of India** ka gathan hua. Yeh party Soviet Communist Party ke touch mein rehti thi. Kayi Indians ne USSR ki Communist University mein education bhi prapt ki.
*   **Data Comparison (Tulna):** Jaise Russia mein 20th century ke shuru mein ~85% log agriculture par nirbhar the, us samay Bharat mein bhi ek badi sankhya kheti par depend karti thi. Dono deshon mein land ownership ek bada mudda tha (Russia mein Nobles vs. Peasants, India mein Zamindars/British vs. Peasants). Isse hum samajik-arthik conditions ki tulna kar sakte hain. 📊
