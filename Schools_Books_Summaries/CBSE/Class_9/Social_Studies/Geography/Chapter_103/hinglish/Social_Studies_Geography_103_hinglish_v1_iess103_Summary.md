# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 103: Drainage (अपवाह)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ) 📊

Yeh chapter Bharat ke river systems (नदी तंत्र) yaani 'Drainage' ke baare mein hai. Ismein hum dekhenge ki nadiyan kaise banti hain, kahan se nikalti hain, aur Bharat mein kitne prakar ke drainage systems hain.

1.  **Drainage (अपवाह)**
    *   Definition: Kisi area ka river system (नदी तंत्र).
    *   Drainage Basin (अपवाह द्रोणी): Ek single river system dwara drain kiya gaya area.
    *   Water Divide (जल विभाजक): Koi bhi uncha area (mountain/upland) jo do drainage basins ko alag karta hai (Jaise Ambala, Indus aur Ganga system ke beech).
2.  **Drainage Systems in India (भारत में अपवाह तंत्र)**
    *   Based on Relief Features (उच्चावच लक्षणों पर आधारित).
    *   Two Major Groups (दो मुख्य वर्ग):
        *   The Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ)
        *   The Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)
3.  **Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ)**
    *   Characteristics: Perennial (बारहमासी - paani saal bhar), Source: Rain + Snowmelt (वर्षा + हिम), Long courses (लंबे रास्ते), Gorges banati hain, Intensive erosion (ऊपरी भाग में तीव्र अपरदन), Depositional features (मध्य और निचले भाग में निक्षेपण आकृतियाँ - meanders, oxbow lakes, deltas).
    *   Major Systems (मुख्य तंत्र):
        *   Indus River System (सिंधु नदी तंत्र)
        *   Ganga River System (गंगा नदी तंत्र)
        *   Brahmaputra River System (ब्रह्मपुत्र नदी तंत्र)
4.  **Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)**
    *   Characteristics: Mostly Seasonal (मौसमी - rainfall par nirbhar), Shorter & Shallower courses (छोटे और उथले रास्ते), Originate: Western Ghats (पश्चिमी घाट) / Central Highlands (मध्य उच्चभूमि).
    *   Flow Direction (प्रवाह दिशा):
        *   Eastward (पूर्व की ओर - Bay of Bengal mein): Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri (Deltas banati hain).
        *   Westward (पश्चिम की ओर - Arabian Sea mein): Narmada, Tapi (Estuaries बनाती हैं), Sabarmati, Mahi, etc.
5.  **Lakes (झीलें)**
    *   Importance: Tourism, Regulate river flow (नदी प्रवाह नियंत्रण), Hydel power (जलविद्युत), Moderate climate (जलवायु नियंत्रण), Aquatic ecosystem (जलीय पारितंत्र), Recreation (मनोरंजन).
    *   Types in India (भारत में प्रकार): Freshwater (मीठे पानी की - Wular, Dal), Saltwater (खारे पानी की - Sambhar, Chilika), Glacial (हिमानी निर्मित), Tectonic (विवर्तनिक), Ox-bow (गोखुर), Lagoons (लैगून), Man-made (मानव निर्मित - Guru Gobind Sagar).
6.  **Role of Rivers in Economy (अर्थव्यवस्था में नदियों की भूमिका)**
    *   Fundamental Importance (मौलिक महत्व): Water source (जल स्रोत), Settlements (बस्तियाँ), Irrigation (सिंचाई), Navigation (नौसंचालन), Hydro-power (जलविद्युत). Agriculture ke liye vishesh mahatva.
7.  **River Pollution (नदी प्रदूषण)**
    *   Causes: Growing demand (बढ़ती मांग - domestic, industrial, agricultural), Untreated sewage & effluents (अनुपचारित वाहित मल और औद्योगिक बहिःस्राव).
    *   Effects: Reduced volume (जल आयतन में कमी), Poor water quality (जल गुणवत्ता में गिरावट), Loss of self-cleansing capacity (स्वतः स्वच्छीकरण क्षमता में कमी).
    *   Conservation Efforts (संरक्षण प्रयास): Ganga Action Plan (गंगा कार्य योजना - GAP, 1985), National River Conservation Plan (राष्ट्रीय नदी संरक्षण योजना - NRCP, 1995), Namami Gange (नमामि गंगे).

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख) 📈

**1. Drainage Basin aur Water Divide (अपवाह द्रोणी और जल विभाजक):**
Imagine karo ki choti-choti streams (धाराएँ) alag-alag dishaon se aakar ek main river (मुख्य नदी) banati hain, jo finally kisi lake (झील) ya sea (समुद्र) mein milti hai. Jis poore area ka paani yeh ek river system collect karta hai, use **Drainage Basin** kehte hain. Aur koi bhi uncha sthan, jaise pahad ya pathar, jo do aise basins ke paani ko alag karta hai, use **Water Divide** kehte hain. (Dekho Figure 3.1). Bharat mein Ganga river ka basin sabse bada hai.

**2. Himalayan vs Peninsular Rivers (हिमालयी बनाम प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ):**

| Feature (विशेषता)         | Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ)                                   | Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)                                     |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Origin (उद्गम)**       | Himalayas (Glaciers + Rain)                                          | Peninsular Plateau, Central Highlands (Mostly Rain)                        |
| **Nature (प्रकृति)**     | Perennial (बारहमासी)                                                  | Mostly Seasonal (अधिकतर मौसमी)                                             |
| **Course (रास्ता)**      | Long, Deep Valleys, Gorges (लंबा, गहरी घाटियाँ, गॉर्ज)                 | Shorter, Shallower Valleys (छोटा, उथली घाटियाँ)                             |
| **Erosion (अपरदन)**      | High in upper course (ऊपरी भाग में अधिक)                              | Comparatively Less (तुलनात्मक रूप से कम)                                   |
| **Features (आकृतियाँ)** | Meanders, Oxbow lakes, Deltas (विसर्प, गोखुर झील, डेल्टा)              | Deltas (East flowing), Estuaries (West flowing) (डेल्टा/एस्चुएरी)          |
| **Examples (उदाहरण)**    | Indus (सिंधु), Ganga (गंगा), Brahmaputra (ब्रह्मपुत्र)                | Narmada, Tapi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi                         |

**3. Major River Systems (प्रमुख नदी तंत्र):**

*   **Indus System (सिंधु तंत्र):** Tibet mein Mansarovar lake ke paas se nikalti hai, Ladakh se India mein enter karti hai. Iski main tributaries hain - Zaskar, Nubra, Shyok, Satluj, Beas, Ravi, Chenab, Jhelum. Mostly Pakistan mein behti hai aur Arabian Sea mein girti hai. **Indus Water Treaty (1960)** ke anusar, India iske paani ka sirf 20% use kar sakta hai.
*   **Ganga System (गंगा तंत्र):** Headwaters 'Bhagirathi' (Gangotri Glacier) aur Alaknanda Devaprayag mein milkar Ganga banati hain. Haridwar mein plains mein aati hai. Tributaries: Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi (North se); Chambal, Betwa, Son (South se). West Bengal mein Farakka par divide hoti hai (Hooghly aur main stream jo Bangladesh jaati hai). Brahmaputra ke saath milkar duniya ka sabse bada delta - **Sundarban Delta** banati hai. Length: >2500 km. **Namami Gange Programme** iske conservation ke liye hai.
*   **Brahmaputra System (ब्रह्मपुत्र तंत्र):** Tibet mein Mansarovar ke paas se nikalti hai (wahan Tsang Po kehte hain). Eastward behti hai, Namcha Barwa se 'U' turn lekar Arunachal Pradesh mein enter karti hai (wahan Dihang). Assam mein ise Brahmaputra kehte hain. Yahan high rainfall ke kaaran ismein paani aur silt bahut hota hai. **Braided channel** (गुंफित वाहिका) aur **riverine islands** (नदी द्वीप - Majuli, duniya ka sabse bada) banati hai. Assam aur Bangladesh mein floods laati hai. Bangladesh mein ise Jamuna kehte hain.

**4. Peninsular Rivers Details (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियों का विवरण):**

*   **Narmada & Tapi:** Dono Central Highlands se nikalti hain aur **rift valleys** (भ्रंश घाटी) mein west ki taraf behti hain, Arabian Sea mein **estuaries** banati hain. Narmada Amarkantak hills (MP) se, Tapi Satpura ranges (MP) se nikalti hai. Narmada par Dhuadhar falls aur Marble rocks famous hain. MP Govt. ne **Namami Devi Narmade** conservation mission shuru kiya hai.
*   **Godavari:** Sabse lambi Peninsular river (~1500 km), Western Ghats (Nasik, Maharashtra) se nikalti hai. Iska basin bhi sabse bada hai (Maharashtra, MP, Odisha, AP). Ise **Dakshin Ganga** bhi kehte hain. Bay of Bengal mein girti hai.
*   **Mahanadi:** Chhattisgarh ke highlands se nikalti hai, Odisha se hote hue Bay of Bengal mein. Length ~860 km.
*   **Krishna:** Mahabaleshwar (Maharashtra) ke paas se nikalti hai, Bay of Bengal mein girti hai. Length ~1400 km. Tributaries: Tungabhadra, Koyana, Bhima, Musi.
*   **Kaveri:** Western Ghats ki Brahmagiri range (Karnataka) se nikalti hai, Tamil Nadu mein Bay of Bengal mein girti hai. Length ~760 km. Yeh India ka doosra sabse bada waterfall, **Shivasamudram Falls**, banati hai.

**5. Lakes & Their Importance (झीलें और उनका महत्व):**
Lakes jaise Kashmir ki Dal Lake ya Nainital ki lake, tourism ke liye important hain. Iske alawa, yeh floods rokne mein, dry season mein paani dene mein, hydro-power banane mein, climate ko moderate karne mein, aur aquatic life ko support karne mein madad karti hain. India mein Wular Lake (J&K) sabse badi freshwater lake hai, jo tectonic activity se bani hai. Chilika (Odisha), Pulicat (AP/TN), Kolleru (AP) lagoons hain. Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) ek salt water lake hai. Guru Gobind Sagar (Bhakra Nangal Project) ek man-made lake hai.

**6. River Pollution - Ek Gambhir Samasya (नदी प्रदूषण - एक गंभीर समस्या):**
Nadiyon par badhta domestic, industrial, aur agricultural pressure unki water quality ko kharab kar raha hai. Sewage aur industrial waste bina treat kiye nadiyon mein daalne se pollution badh raha hai aur nadiyon ki self-cleansing capacity kam ho rahi hai. Ganga jaisi nadi bhi bade shehron ke paas pollution ko dilute nahi kar paa rahi hai. Isliye, **GAP** aur **NRCP** jaise river cleaning programs shuru kiye gaye hain. Saaf paani ke bina human life mushkil hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

*   **National Data & Examples (राष्ट्रीय आँकड़े और उदाहरण) 📊:**
    *   **Largest River Basin:** Ganga River basin Bharat ka sabse bada drainage basin hai.
    *   **Longest Peninsular River:** Godavari (~1500 km), jise 'Dakshin Ganga' bhi kehte hain.
    *   **West Flowing Rivers:** Narmada aur Tapi do pramukh nadiyan hain jo rift valleys se hokar west mein behti hain.
    *   **Water Treaty:** Indus Water Treaty (1960) ke tahat Bharat, Indus system ke total water ka 20% hi istemal kar sakta hai (mainly Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan mein irrigation ke liye).
    *   **Major Waterfalls:** Kaveri nadi par Shivasamudram Falls (Bharat ka doosra sabse bada).
    *   **Largest Freshwater Lake:** Wular Lake (Jammu & Kashmir).
    *   **Largest Brackish Water Lagoon:** Chilika Lake (Odisha).
    *   **Conservation Programs:**
        *   **Namami Gange:** Ganga nadi ko clean aur rejuvenate karne ka flagship program (June 2014).
        *   **Namami Devi Narmade:** Madhya Pradesh sarkar dwara Narmada conservation ke liye shuru ki gayi scheme.
        *   **NRCP (1995):** Desh ki major rivers ki water quality improve karne ka national plan.
    *   **Economic Importance:** Bharat mein agriculture majority population ka livelihood source hai, jiske liye nadiyan irrigation ka main source hain. Hydro-power generation bhi important hai. Kai bade shehar nadiyon ke kinare base hain.
