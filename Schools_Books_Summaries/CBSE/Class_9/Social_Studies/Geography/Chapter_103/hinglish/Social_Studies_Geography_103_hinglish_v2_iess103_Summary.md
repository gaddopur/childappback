# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 3: Drainage (अपवाह)

*(Note: NCERT Chapter number is 3, not 103)*

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Drainage (अपवाह)**
    *   Meaning: Kisi area ka river system (नदी तंत्र).
    *   **Drainage Basin (अपवाह द्रोणी)**: Ek single river system dwara drain kiya gaya area.
    *   **Water Divide (जल विभाजक)**: Koi bhi uncha area (like mountain ya upland) jo do drainage basins ko alag karta hai. (Example: Ambala - Indus aur Ganga systems ke beech).

2.  **Drainage Systems in India (भारत में अपवाह तंत्र)**
    *   Based on Relief Features (स्थलाकृति पर आधारित).
    *   **Two Major Groups:**
        *   Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ)
        *   Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)

3.  **Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ)**
    *   **Characteristics:**
        *   Perennial (बारहमासी): Saal bhar paani (rain + snowmelt).
        *   Long Courses (लंबे रास्ते).
        *   Source: North of mountain ranges (Indus, Brahmaputra).
        *   Features: Gorges (गॉर्ज), Intensive Erosion (ऊपरी भाग में तीव्र अपरदन), Meanders (विसर्प), Oxbow Lakes (गोखुर झील), Deltas (डेल्टा) (lower course mein).
    *   **Major Systems:**
        *   Indus River System (सिंधु नदी तंत्र)
        *   Ganga River System (गंगा नदी तंत्र)
        *   Brahmaputra River System (ब्रह्मपुत्र नदी तंत्र)

4.  **Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)**
    *   **Characteristics:**
        *   Seasonal (मौसमी): Flow depends on rainfall. Dry season mein paani kam.
        *   Shorter & Shallower Courses (छोटे और उथले रास्ते) compared to Himalayan rivers.
        *   Origin: Mostly Western Ghats (पश्चिमी घाट) (flow east to Bay of Bengal) or Central Highlands (मध्य उच्चभूमि) (कुछ नदियाँ पश्चिम की ओर बहती हैं).
        *   Features: Form Deltas (पूर्व की ओर बहने वाली) or Estuaries (एस्चुएरी) (पश्चिम की ओर बहने वाली - Narmada, Tapi).
    *   **Major Rivers:** Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri (East flowing); Narmada, Tapi (West flowing).

5.  **Lakes (झीलें)**
    *   Types: Freshwater (मीठे पानी की), Saltwater (खारे पानी की), Natural (प्राकृतिक), Man-made (मानव निर्मित), Glacial (हिमानी निर्मित), Tectonic (विवर्तनिक), Ox-bow, Lagoons (लैगून).
    *   Importance: Tourism, regulate river flow, hydel power, moderate climate, aquatic ecosystem.

6.  **Role of Rivers in Economy (अर्थव्यवस्था में नदियों की भूमिका)**
    *   Basic natural resource.
    *   Used for: Irrigation (सिंचाई), Navigation (नौसंचालन), Hydropower (जलविद्युत).
    *   Support agriculture (कृषि).
    *   Ancient settlements se lekar modern cities tak river banks par base hain.

7.  **River Pollution (नदी प्रदूषण)**
    *   Causes: Domestic waste (घरेलू कचरा), Municipal waste (नगरपालिका का कचरा), Industrial effluents (औद्योगिक कचरा), Agricultural runoff (कृषि रसायन).
    *   Effects: Reduces water quality, harms aquatic life, affects human health, reduces self-cleansing capacity.
    *   Conservation Efforts: Ganga Action Plan (GAP), National River Conservation Plan (NRCP), Namami Gange, Namami Devi Narmade.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Drainage Basin vs Water Divide:** Ek nadi aur uski tributaries jitna area cover karti hain, woh uska **Drainage Basin** kehlata hai. Jaise Ganga basin India ka sabse bada basin hai. Do basins ke beech ka uncha hissa (pahaad ya pathaar) **Water Divide** kehlata hai. (Dekho Figure 3.1).
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Simple sketch showing two slopes separated by a ridge (water divide), with streams flowing down each slope.
        ```
           / \     <-- Water Divide (Upland)
          /   \
        Stream A   Stream B
         /          \
        /            \
       River System 1  River System 2
        (Basin 1)      (Basin 2)
        ```

*   **Himalayan vs Peninsular Rivers:**
    | Feature          | Himalayan Rivers                  | Peninsular Rivers                     |
    | :--------------- | :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
    | Origin (उद्गम)   | Himalayas (Glaciers + Rain)     | Peninsular Plateau, Central Highlands |
    | Nature (प्रकृति) | Perennial (बारहमासी)             | Seasonal (मौसमी - mostly)             |
    | Course (रास्ता)  | Long, Deep Valleys, Gorges      | Shorter, Shallower Valleys            |
    | Erosion (अपरदन) | High in upper course            | Comparatively less                    |
    | Features (आकृतियाँ)| Meanders, Oxbows, Large Deltas | Smaller Deltas, Estuaries (West)      |
    | Examples         | Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra         | Godavari, Krishna, Narmada, Kaveri    |

*   **River Features (नदी द्वारा निर्मित आकृतियाँ):** (Dekho Figure 3.3)
    *   **Upper Course:** V-shaped valleys, Gorges (e.g., Indus gorge in Ladakh, Narmada near Jabalpur - Marble Rocks). Yahan erosion zyada hota hai.
    *   **Middle Course:** Meanders (नदी के मोड़), Oxbow lakes (जब meander cut off ho jaata hai). Yahan transportation aur deposition shuru hota hai.
    *   **Lower Course:** Floodplains (बाढ़ के मैदान), Deltas (नदी ke mouth par sediment deposit hone se bana triangular landform, e.g., Sundarban Delta). Yahan deposition dominant hota hai.
    *   **Estuary (एस्चुएरी):** Jahan nadi samudra se milti hai aur tide ka asar hota hai, wahan delta ki jagah funnel-shaped opening banti hai. West-flowing rivers like Narmada, Tapi estuaries banati hain.

*   **Rift Valley Rivers (भ्रंश घाटी नदियाँ):** Narmada aur Tapi rivers faulting se bani rift valleys mein behti hain. Isliye inka path seedha hai aur tributaries kam hain jo right angles par milti hain.

*   **Lakes - Formation & Types:**
    *   **Ox-bow Lakes:** Meandering river se bante hain.
    *   **Lagoons:** Coastal areas mein spits/bars se bante hain (e.g., Chilika, Pulicat, Kolleru).
    *   **Glacial Lakes:** Glacier ke dwara khode gaye basin mein paani bharne se (e.g., Nainital, Bhimtal).
    *   **Tectonic Lakes:** Earth movements se bante hain (e.g., Wular Lake - India's largest freshwater lake).
    *   **Salt Water Lakes:** Arid/Semi-arid regions mein (e.g., Sambhar Lake in Rajasthan - salt production).
    *   **Man-made Lakes:** Dam banane se (e.g., Guru Gobind Sagar - Bhakra Nangal).

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

*   **National Economic/Social Data (राष्ट्रीय आर्थिक/सामाजिक डेटा) 📊:**
    *   **Indus Water Treaty (1960):** Is treaty ke according, India Indus river system ke total water ka sirf 20% use kar sakta hai. Yeh paani Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan mein irrigation ke liye use hota hai.
    *   **Ganga Basin:** India ka sabse bada river basin, jo desh ke large part ko cover karta hai aur crores logon ki lifeline hai. Agriculture ke liye bahut important hai.
    *   **Godavari Basin:** Largest Peninsular river basin, ise 'Dakshin Ganga' bhi kehte hain. Maharashtra, MP, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh ke hisson ko cover karta hai.
    *   **River Interlinking Projects:** Bharat mein paani ke distribution ko balance karne ke liye rivers ko jodne ke proposals hain, jinke apne fayde aur nuksan hain. (Yeh extra information hai, NCERT mein direct nahi hai par relevant hai).
    *   **Major Lakes & Importance:**
        *   **Wular Lake (J&K):** India's largest freshwater lake (tectonic origin).
        *   **Chilika Lake (Odisha):** India's largest brackish water lagoon. Important for biodiversity.
        *   **Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan):** India's largest inland salt lake. Salt production ke liye important.
        *   **Loktak Lake (Manipur):** Famous for 'Phumdis' (floating islands). (Figure 3.6).
    *   **Conservation Missions:**
        *   **Namami Gange:** Ganga ko clean aur rejuvenate karne ka flagship programme (June 2014).
        *   **Namami Devi Narmade:** Madhya Pradesh government ka Narmada river conservation mission.
    *   **Economic Role:** India mein agriculture abhi bhi major livelihood source hai, aur iske liye rivers irrigation ka main source hain. Hydropower generation bhi important hai (e.g., Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud, Nagarjuna Sagar, Shivasamudram falls on Kaveri). Ancient cities like Haridwar, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Patna Ganga ke kinare base hain.
