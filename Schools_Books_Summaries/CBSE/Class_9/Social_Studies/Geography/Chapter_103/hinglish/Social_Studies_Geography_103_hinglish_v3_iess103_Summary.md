# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 103: Drainage (अपवाह)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ)

Yeh concept hierarchy drainage system ko samajhne mein madad karti hai:

1.  **Drainage (अपवाह)**
    *   Definition: Ek area ka river system (नदी तंत्र).
    *   Components: Small streams (छोटी धाराएँ) joining the main river.
    *   Destination: Lake (झील), Sea (समुद्र), or Ocean (महासागर).
2.  **Drainage Basin (अपवाह द्रोणी)**
    *   Definition: Area drained by a single river system (एक नदी तंत्र द्वारा अपवाहित क्षेत्र).
3.  **Water Divide (जल विभाजक)**
    *   Definition: Elevated area (mountain/upland) separating two drainage basins (कोई भी ऊँचा क्षेत्र जो दो अपवाह द्रोणियों को अलग करता है). (Jaise Figure 3.1 mein dikhaya gaya hai).
4.  **Drainage Systems in India (भारत में अपवाह तंत्र)**
    *   Basis of Division: Relief features (उच्चावच).
    *   **Major Groups:**
        *   **Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ)**
            *   Origin: Himalayas.
            *   Nature: Perennial (बारहमासी - saal bhar paani), fed by rain and snowmelt.
            *   Course: Long, intensive erosion in upper course, depositional features (meanders, oxbow lakes, deltas) in lower course.
            *   Examples: Indus (सिंधु), Ganga (गंगा), Brahmaputra (ब्रह्मपुत्र).
            *   Features: Gorges (गॉर्ज) formation.
        *   **Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)**
            *   Origin: Peninsular plateau, Central Highlands, Western Ghats.
            *   Nature: Seasonal (मौसमी - dependent on rainfall), reduced flow in dry season.
            *   Course: Shorter and shallower (छोटी और छिछली).
            *   Flow Direction: Mostly Eastward (Bay of Bengal), some Westward (Arabian Sea).
            *   Examples: Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri (Eastward); Narmada, Tapi (Westward).
            *   Features: Deltas (पूर्व की ओर बहने वाली), Estuaries (पश्चिम की ओर बहने वाली).
5.  **River Features (नदी द्वारा निर्मित आकृतियाँ)** (Jaise Figure 3.3 mein)
    *   Upper Course (ऊपरी भाग): Erosion (अपरदन), Gorges.
    *   Middle Course (मध्य भाग): Meanders (विसर्प), Oxbow Lakes (गोखुर झील).
    *   Lower Course (निचला भाग): Floodplains (बाढ़ के मैदान), Deltas (डेल्टा).
6.  **Lakes (झीलें)**
    *   Formation: Glacial action, wind, river action, human activities (damming), tectonic activity, spits and bars (coastal lagoons).
    *   Types: Freshwater (मीठे पानी की - Wular, Dal), Saltwater (खारे पानी की - Sambhar, Chilika), Seasonal, Permanent, Natural, Artificial (मानव निर्मित - Guru Gobind Sagar).
    *   Importance: Regulate river flow, prevent flooding, hydel power, moderate climate, tourism, recreation.
7.  **Role of Rivers in Economy (अर्थव्यवस्था में नदियों का महत्व)**
    *   Basic natural resource.
    *   Supports agriculture (irrigation - सिंचाई), navigation (नौसंचालन), hydro-power generation (जलविद्युत उत्पादन).
    *   Historical importance: Settlements (बस्तियाँ) developed into cities.
8.  **River Pollution (नदी प्रदूषण)**
    *   Causes: Domestic, municipal, industrial, agricultural demand; untreated sewage (अनुपचारित वाहित मल), industrial effluents (औद्योगिक बहिःस्राव).
    *   Effects: Reduced water volume, poor water quality, loss of self-cleansing capacity.
    *   Solutions: River action plans (नदी कार्य योजनाएँ) like Ganga Action Plan (GAP), National River Conservation Plan (NRCP), Namami Gange.

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख)

**Drainage System Basics (अपवाह तंत्र की मूल बातें):**
*   'Drainage' ka matlab hai kisi area ka river network. Chhoti streams milkar main nadi banati hain jo finally samudra ya jheel mein milti hai.
*   Ek single river system jitne area ka paani bahata hai, use 'Drainage Basin' kehte hain. Duniya ka sabse bada drainage basin Amazon nadi ka hai. Bharat mein, Ganga nadi ka basin sabse bada hai.
*   Koi bhi uncha area jaise pahad ya upland jo do drainage basins ko alag karta hai, use 'Water Divide' (जल विभाजक) kehte hain. (Figure 3.1 ka concept).

**India ke Drainage Systems (भारत के अपवाह तंत्र):**
Bharat ki नदियों ko do main groups mein baanta gaya hai: Himalayan aur Peninsular rivers.

1.  **Himalayan Rivers (हिमालय की नदियाँ):**
    *   Yeh nadiyaan Hamesha behti rehti hain (Perennial) kyunki inhe baarish aur barf pighalne (snowmelt) dono se paani milta hai.
    *   Inka source se samudra tak ka raasta bahut lamba (long courses) hota hai.
    *   Apne upper course mein yeh tezi se kaatav (intensive erosion) karti hain aur bahut saara silt aur sand lekar chalti hain. Example: Indus aur Brahmaputra mountains ko kaat kar Gorges (गॉर्ज) banati hain.
    *   Middle aur lower course mein yeh Meanders (विसर्प), Oxbow lakes (गोखुर झील) banati hain aur apne floodplains mein depositional features chhodti hain. Yeh well-developed Deltas (डेल्टा) bhi banati hain. (Figure 3.3 ke features).
    *   **Major Systems:**
        *   **Indus River System (सिंधु नदी तंत्र):** Tibet mein Mansarovar Lake ke paas se nikalti hai, Ladakh mein enter karti hai. Tributaries: Zaskar, Nubra, Shyok, Hunza, Satluj, Beas, Ravi, Chenab, Jhelum. Length: 2900 km. Zyada hissa Pakistan mein hai. *Indus Water Treaty (1960)* ke anusar, India iske paani ka sirf 20% use kar sakta hai.
        *   **Ganga River System (गंगा नदी तंत्र):** Headwaters 'Bhagirathi' (Gangotri Glacier se) aur 'Alaknanda' Devaprayag mein milkar Ganga banati hain. Haridwar mein plains mein aati hai. Tributaries from Himalayas: Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi. Tributaries from Peninsular uplands: Chambal, Betwa, Son. Length: 2500 km se zyada. Farakka (West Bengal) mein bifurcate hoti hai (Bhagirathi-Hooghly aur mainstream jo Bangladesh jaati hai). Brahmaputra ke saath milkar Bay of Bengal mein duniya ka sabse bada delta, *Sundarban Delta*, banati hai. *Namami Gange Programme* iski safai aur conservation ke liye hai.
        *   **Brahmaputra River System (ब्रह्मपुत्र नदी तंत्र):** Tibet mein Mansarovar ke paas se nikalti hai (wahan Tsang Po kehte hain). Indus se thodi lambi hai, zyada course India ke bahar hai. Namcha Barwa ke paas 'U' turn lekar Arunachal Pradesh mein enter karti hai (wahan Dihang kehte hain). Assam mein Dibang, Lohit etc. tributaries milne par Brahmaputra banti hai. Tibet mein kam paani/silt hota hai (cold/dry area), India mein high rainfall region se guzarti hai toh paani/silt zyada hota hai. Assam mein braided channel aur riverine islands (Majuli - world's largest) banati hai. Har saal Assam aur Bangladesh mein floods laati hai. Riverbed mein silt deposit hone se bed upar uth jaata hai aur channel shift hota rehta hai. Bangladesh mein ise Jamuna kehte hain.

2.  **Peninsular Rivers (प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ):**
    *   Yeh nadiyaan mostly seasonal (मौसमी) hoti hain, baarish par depend karti hain. Dry season mein paani kam ho jaata hai.
    *   Inke courses Himalayan rivers ke comparison mein shorter aur shallower hote hain.
    *   Main water divide Western Ghats banata hai.
    *   Zyadatar badi nadiyaan (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri) Western Ghats se nikalkar East ki taraf Bay of Bengal mein girti hain aur Deltas banati hain.
    *   Kuch nadiyaan Central highlands se nikalti hain aur West ki taraf behti hain. Sirf Narmada aur Tapi lambi nadiyaan hain jo West mein behkar Arabian Sea mein girti hain aur Estuaries (ज्वारनदमुख) banati hain.
    *   **Major Systems:**
        *   **Narmada Basin (नर्मदा द्रोणी):** Amarkantak hills (MP) se nikalti hai. Rift valley (भ्रंश घाटी) mein West ki taraf behti hai. Marble Rocks (Jabalpur), Dhuadhar falls jaise picturesque locations banati hai. Tributaries chhoti hain. MP aur Gujarat ko cover karti hai. *Namami Devi Narmade* conservation mission MP govt. ne shuru kiya hai.
        *   **Tapi Basin (तापी द्रोणी):** Satpura ranges (Betul district, MP) se nikalti hai. Narmada ke parallel rift valley mein behti hai, par chhoti hai. MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra ko cover karti hai.
        *   **Godavari Basin (गोदावरी द्रोणी):** Sabse badi Peninsular river (Largest). Western Ghats ke slopes (Nasik, Maharashtra) se nikalti hai. Length: 1500 km. Bay of Bengal mein girti hai. Iska drainage basin bhi Peninsular rivers mein sabse bada hai (50% Maharashtra mein). Tributaries: Purna, Wardha, Pranhita, Manjra, Wainganga, Penganga. Ise 'Dakshin Ganga' bhi kehte hain.
        *   **Mahanadi Basin (महानदी द्रोणी):** Chhattisgarh ke highlands se nikalti hai. Odisha se hote hue Bay of Bengal mein girti hai. Length: 860 km. Basin shared by Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha.
        *   **Krishna Basin (कृष्णा द्रोणी):** Mahabaleshwar ke paas spring se nikalti hai. Length: 1400 km. Bay of Bengal mein girti hai. Tributaries: Tungabhadra, Koyana, Ghatprabha, Musi, Bhima. Basin shared by Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh.
        *   **Kaveri Basin (कावेरी द्रोणी):** Western Ghats ki Brahmagiri range se nikalti hai. Tamil Nadu mein Bay of Bengal mein girti hai. Length: 760 km. Tributaries: Amravati, Bhavani, Hemavati, Kabini. Basin drains parts of Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu. Yeh India ka second biggest waterfall, *Shivasamudram Falls*, banati hai.

**Lakes (झीलें):**
*   India mein bahut si lakes hain, size aur characteristics mein alag alag. Kuch permanent, kuch seasonal (jaise semi-arid regions ki).
*   Formation ke reasons: Glaciers (e.g., most Himalayan lakes), wind, river action (Ox-bow lakes), human activities (dams - Guru Gobind Sagar), tectonic activity (Wular Lake - India's largest freshwater lake), coastal processes (Lagoons like Chilika, Pulicat, Kolleru).
*   Saltwater lake example: Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) - salt production ke liye use hoti hai.
*   Freshwater lake examples: Wular, Dal, Bhimtal, Nainital, Loktak, Barapani (mostly Himalayan region mein, glacial origin).
*   Lakes bahut valuable hain: River flow regulate karti hain, floods rokti hain, dry season mein paani deti hain, hydel power generation mein help karti hain, climate moderate karti hain, tourism badhati hain.

**Rivers ka Economy mein Role (अर्थव्यवस्था में नदियों की भूमिका):**
*   Rivers hamesha se fundamental importance ki rahi hain. Yeh basic natural resource hain.
*   Purane zamane se log nadi kinare baste aaye hain, jo ab bade sheher ban gaye hain.
*   Irrigation (सिंचाई), navigation (नौसंचालन), hydro-power generation (जलविद्युत उत्पादन) ke liye bahut zaroori hain, khaas kar India jaise desh mein jahan agriculture major livelihood hai.

**River Pollution (नदी प्रदूषण):**
*   Paani ki badhti domestic, municipal, industrial, agricultural demand se water quality affect ho rahi hai.
*   Nadiyon se zyada paani nikalne se unka volume kam ho raha hai.
*   Untreated sewage aur industrial waste nadiyon mein daalne se pollution badh raha hai aur nadi ki self-cleansing capacity kam ho rahi hai. Example: Ganga bade shehron ke 20 km ke andar pollution ko dilute kar sakti thi, par badhte urbanisation/industrialisation ke karan ab nahi kar paati.
*   Is badhte pollution ko rokne ke liye river cleaning action plans launch kiye gaye hain, jaise Ganga Action Plan (1985) aur National River Conservation Plan (NRCP, 1995). *Namami Gange* ek current flagship programme hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

*   **National Economic/Social Data 📊:**
    *   **Largest River Basin:** Ganga basin Bharat ka sabse bada river basin hai, jo desh ke bade hisse ko drain karta hai aur crores logon ko support karta hai.
    *   **Water Resources:** Himalayan rivers perennial hain aur North India ke liye vital water source hain (agriculture, drinking water). Peninsular rivers seasonal hain, in par dependency rainfall par zyada hai, jo kabhi-kabhi water scarcity ka reason banta hai.
    *   **Agriculture Dependency:** India ki majority population agriculture par dependent hai, aur rivers irrigation ka main source hain. Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan mein Indus system ka paani irrigation ke liye crucial hai. Ganga plains desh ke sabse fertile agricultural regions mein se ek hain.
    *   **Hydro-power:** Rivers par bane dams jaise Bhakra Nangal (Satluj), Hirakud (Mahanadi), Nagarjuna Sagar (Krishna), Sardar Sarovar (Narmada) hydro-power generation ke important sources hain. Kaveri par Shivasamudram Falls se generate hui power Mysuru, Bengaluru ko supply hoti hai.
    *   **Cultural Significance:** Nadiyan jaise Ganga, Yamuna, Narmada, Kaveri ka Bharat mein gehra cultural aur religious significance hai. Inke kinare kai teerth sthal hain.
    *   **Government Initiatives:** Bharat Sarkar ne nadiyon ki safai aur conservation ke liye specific programmes launch kiye hain jaise *Namami Gange* (Ganga ke liye) aur *Namami Devi Narmade* (Narmada ke liye MP Govt. dwara), jo National River Conservation Plan (NRCP) ka hissa hain. Yeh initiatives pollution abatement (प्रदूषण नियंत्रण) par focus karte hain.
    *   **Specific Lakes:** Wular Lake (J&K) Bharat ki sabse badi freshwater lake hai (tectonic origin). Chilika Lake (Odisha) ek important lagoon hai. Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) salt production ka source hai. Loktak Lake (Manipur) apne floating islands (phumdis) ke liye famous hai.
