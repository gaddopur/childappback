# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 6: Jansankhya (Population)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
Yeh chapter population yaani jansankhya ke important concepts ko detail mein samjhata hai. Neeche diye gaye points is chapter ke core ideas hain:

1.  **Jansankhya ka Mahatva (Importance of Population) 🧑‍🤝‍🧑**
    *   Log hi resources (sansadhan) ko istemaal karte hain aur banate hain.
    *   Society aur economy ko develop karne mein logon ka role central hai.
    *   Population social studies ka pivotal element (kendriya tatva) hai, jiske reference mein hi 'resources', 'calamities', 'disasters' ka matlab samajh aata hai.

2.  **Population Size aur Distribution (Aakar aur Vitran) 🗺️**
    *   **India ki Jansankhya:** Kitne log hain (Size).
    *   **Distribution:** Log desh mein kahan aur kaise faile hue hain (Vitran).
        *   Uneven Distribution (Asamaan Vitran): Kahin zyada log, kahin bahut kam.
    *   **Population Density (Jansankhya Ghanatva):** Per unit area mein kitne log rehte hain.
        *   Factors Influencing Density: Zameen (terrain), climate (jalvayu), mitti (soil), baarish (rainfall) jaise kaarak density par asar daalte hain.

3.  **Population Growth aur Change (Jansankhya Vriddhi aur Parivartan) 📈📉**
    *   **Growth:** Ek specific time mein population mein kitna badlav aaya.
        *   Absolute Increase: Kitne log badhe (kul sankhya mein).
        *   Growth Rate: Kis raftaar se badhe (percentage mein).
    *   **Processes of Change (Parivartan ki Prakriyaayein):** Population change ke teen main kaaran:
        *   Birth Rate (Janm Dar): Har 1000 logon par ek saal mein kitne bachhe paida hue.
        *   Death Rate (Mrityu Dar): Har 1000 logon par ek saal mein kitne logon ki death hui.
        *   Migration (Pravasan): Logon ka ek jagah se doosri jagah jaana (internal ya international).

4.  **Population Characteristics/Composition (Jansankhya ki Visheshtayein/Sanrachna) 👥**
    *   **Adolescent Population (Kishor Jansankhya):** 10-19 saal ke log, desh ka future resource. Unki needs aur challenges.

5.  **National Population Policy (Rashtriya Jansankhya Niti) 📜**
    *   Sarkaar ki policy population ko manage karne aur logon ki well-being improve karne ke liye.

## 📘 Key Learnings

1.  **Log hi Sab Kuch Hain!** 💡
    *   Imagine karo bina insaano ki duniya? Kaun resources use karta? Kaun society banata? Log hi economy aur society ko develop karte hain.
    *   Koyla (Coal) sirf ek pathar ka tukda tha jab tak insaan ne use nikalne aur istemaal karne ki technology nahi banayi. Flood ya Tsunami 'disaster' tabhi bante hain jab woh kisi populated area ko affect karte hain. Isliye, population sabse important hai.

2.  **Bharat ki Jansankhya: Kitni aur Kahan?** 🇮🇳
    *   **Size:** March 2011 Census ke anusaar, India ki population 1210.6 million (ya 1.21 billion) thi. Yeh duniya ki population ka 17.5% hai! 🤯
    *   **Area:** Hamara desh duniya ke area ka sirf 2.4% hissa hai, lekin itne saare log yahan rehte hain. (Dekho Figure 6.1 - World Area vs Population mein India ka share).
    *   **Uneven Distribution:** Population desh mein barabar nahi faili hai.
        *   Uttar Pradesh (UP) sabse zyada populous state hai (199 million, 2011), jo desh ki 16% population hai.
        *   Wahin Sikkim mein sirf 0.6 million aur Lakshadweep mein lagbhag 64,000 log hi hain.
        *   Aadhi se zyada population sirf 5 states mein rehti hai: UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, West Bengal, aur Andhra Pradesh (ab Telangana alag ho gaya hai).
        *   Rajasthan area mein sabse bada state hai, lekin wahan desh ki sirf 5.5% population rehti hai. (Dekho Figure 6.2 - State-wise population distribution).
    *   *Diagram Description (Figure 6.1):* Yeh pie chart dikhata hai ki duniya ke land area mein India ka hissa (2.4%) kitna chhota hai, jabki world population mein hamara hissa (17.5%) kitna bada hai.
    *   *Diagram Description (Figure 6.2):* Yeh bar chart dikhata hai ki India ki total population ka kitna percentage kis state mein rehta hai, highlighting the dominance of a few states like UP.

3.  **Population Density: Kahan Kitni Bheed?** 🚶‍♂️🚶‍♀️🚶
    *   **Definition:** Population density ka matlab hai 'number of persons per unit area' (jaise per square km). Yeh uneven distribution ko behtar samajhne mein madad karta hai.
    *   **India's Density:** 2011 mein India ki average density 382 persons per sq km thi, jo duniya mein kaafi high hai. (Sirf Bangladesh aur Japan ki average density humse zyada hai).
    *   **Variations:** Density har jagah same nahi hai.
        *   Bihar mein density sabse zyada (1,102 persons/sq km) hai.
        *   Arunachal Pradesh mein sabse kam (sirf 17 persons/sq km) hai.
    *   **Kyun hai Aisa Fark? (Factors):**
        *   **Low Density Areas (e.g., Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, mountainous regions):** Yahan rugged terrain (unchi-neechi zameen), unfavourable climate (kathin mausam), aur mushkil halaat ki wajah se kam log rehte hain. (States with density below 250 persons/sq km).
        *   **Moderate Density Areas (e.g., Assam, Peninsular states):** Hilly/rocky terrain, moderate/low rainfall, kam upjau mitti (less fertile soils) in areas mein moderate density ka kaaran hain.
        *   **High Density Areas (e.g., Northern Plains - UP, Bihar, WB; Kerala):** Yahan flat plains (samatl maidan), fertile soils (upjau mitti), aur achhi rainfall (abundant rainfall) ki wajah se bahut log rehte hain.
    *   *Diagram Description (Figure 6.3):* Yeh map India ke alag-alag states mein population density ko colours se dikhata hai. Darker shades matlab higher density, lighter shades matlab lower density. Isse saaf pata chalta hai ki Northern Plains aur coastal areas mein density zyada hai jabki mountainous aur desert regions mein kam.

4.  **Population Growth: Badhti Aabadi ka Sach** 👶📈
    *   **Definition:** Population growth matlab ek time period mein (jaise 10 saal mein) kisi jagah ke logon ki sankhya mein badlav.
    *   **Do Tarah se Napna:**
        *   **Absolute Increase:** Kitne log badhe (e.g., 2011 population - 2001 population).
        *   **Annual Growth Rate (%):** Har saal kitne percent population badhi (e.g., 2% per annum ka matlab har 100 logon par 1 saal mein 2 log badhe).
    *   **India ka Trend:**
        *   1951 (361 million) se 2011 (1210.6 million) tak population lagatar badhi hai.
        *   1951 se 1981 tak annual growth rate bhi tezi se badh raha tha (Table 6.1 dekho). Isliye population bahut tezi se badhi.
        *   **Since 1981:** Growth *rate* dheere-dheere kam hona shuru hua hai. Lekin, kyunki hamari total population (base population) itni zyada hai, isliye kam rate par bhi har saal/decade judne wale logon ki *absolute number* bahut badi hoti hai. (Jaise 1990s mein 182 million log jude!).
        *   Yeh declining growth rate birth control efforts ka positive sign hai, lekin badi population ke karan resource conservation par pressure bana rehta hai.
    *   *Diagram Description (Figure 6.4a & 6.4b):* Graph (a) 1951 se 2011 tak har decade mein population growth rate (%) dikhata hai, jisme 1981 ke baad गिरावट (decline) dikhti hai. Graph (b) isi period mein total population (in millions) ko dikhata hai, jo lagatar upar ja raha hai.

5.  **Population Change ke Kaaran: Birth, Death, Migration** 🔄
    *   Teen main processes population ko badalte hain:
        *   **Birth Rate (Janm Dar):** Number of live births per 1000 persons in a year. India mein growth ka bada kaaran yeh hai ki birth rate hamesha death rate se zyada raha hai.
        *   **Death Rate (Mrityu Dar):** Number of deaths per 1000 persons in a year. India mein population growth tezi se isliye hui kyunki death rates mein tezi se kami aayi (behtar health facilities etc.).
        *   **Natural Increase:** Birth Rate - Death Rate.
        *   **Trend:** 1980 tak high birth rate aur declining death rate se population growth high thi. 1981 ke baad birth rates bhi dheere-dheere kam hone lage, jisse growth rate mein kami aayi.
        *   **Migration (Pravasan):** Logon ka ek region se doosre region mein move karna.
            *   **Internal Migration:** Desh ke andar (e.g., gaon se sheher). Yeh desh ki total population nahi badalta, lekin distribution badal deta hai.
            *   **International Migration:** Ek desh se doosre desh.
            *   **Push Factors (Gaon se Dhakka):** Garibi, berozgari (unemployment) jaise kaaran logon ko gaon chhodne par majboor karte hain.
            *   **Pull Factors (Sheher ka Khinchav):** Shehron mein zyada employment opportunities aur better living conditions logon ko attract karti hain.
            *   **Impact:** Rural-urban migration se shehron (urban areas) ki population ka percentage badh raha hai (1951 mein 17.29% se 2011 mein 31.80%). 'Million plus cities' ki sankhya bhi badhi hai (2001 mein 35 se 2011 mein 53).

6.  **Adolescent Population: Desh ka Bhavishya** 🧑‍🎓👩‍🎓
    *   India ki population ka ek significant feature hai yahan ki badi adolescent population (10-19 years age group). Yeh total population ka 1/5th hissa hain!
    *   Yeh hamare future ke liye sabse important resource hain.
    *   **Needs & Challenges:** Inki nutritional requirements (poshan ki zaroorat) bachhon aur adults se zyada hoti hai. Lekin India mein unhe milne wali diet aksar inadequate hoti hai. Bahut si adolescent girls anaemia (khoon ki kami) se suffer karti hain. Unki problems par abhi tak utna dhyan nahi diya gaya hai.
    *   **Solution:** Literacy aur education ke zariye unmein awareness badhana zaroori hai.

7.  **National Population Policy (NPP) 2000** 🇮🇳📜
    *   Yeh policy Government of India ne family planning ko promote karne aur individual health & welfare ko improve karne ke liye banayi (Family Planning Programme 1952 mein shuru hua tha).
    *   **Key Objectives of NPP 2000:**
        *   14 saal tak ke bachhon ke liye free aur compulsory school education.
        *   Infant mortality rate (shishu mrityu dar) ko 30 per 1000 live births se kam karna.
        *   Sabhi bachhon ka universal immunisation (teekakaran) vaccine-preventable diseases ke khilaf.
        *   Ladkiyon ki shaadi mein deri (delayed marriage) ko promote karna.
        *   Family welfare ko people-centred programme banana.
