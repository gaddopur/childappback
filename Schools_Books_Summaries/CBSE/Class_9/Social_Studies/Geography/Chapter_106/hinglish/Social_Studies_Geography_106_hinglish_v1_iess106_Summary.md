# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 106: Jansankhya (Population)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Jansankhya ka Mahatva (Importance of Population)**
    *   Log hi resources ka istemaal karte hain aur society/economy develop karte hain.
    *   Resources, calamities, disasters - sab human beings ke reference mein hi meaningful hain.
    *   Population: Social Studies ka pivotal element (kendriya tatva).

2.  **Jansankhya ka Akaar aur Vitran (Population Size and Distribution)**
    *   **Size (Akaar):**
        *   India ki population (March 2011): 1,210.6 million (121 Crore+) - World ka 17.5%.
        *   India ka area: 3.28 million sq km - World ka 2.4%.
    *   **Distribution (Vitran):**
        *   Bahut hi uneven (asamaan).
        *   Sabse zyada populous state: Uttar Pradesh (199 million, India ka ~16%).
        *   Sabse kam population wale states: Sikkim (~0.6 million), Lakshadweep (~64,000).
        *   Lagbhag आधी population sirf 5 states mein: UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh.
        *   Area mein sabse bada state Rajasthan, par population sirf 5.5%.

3.  **Jansankhya Ghanatva (Population Density)**
    *   **Definition:** Per unit area mein rehne wale logon ki average sankhya (Number of persons per sq km).
    *   **India ka Density (2011):** 382 persons per sq km (World ke most densely populated countries mein se ek).
    *   **Variations:**
        *   Highest: Bihar (1,102 persons/sq km).
        *   Lowest: Arunachal Pradesh (17 persons/sq km).
    *   **Factors Influencing Density (Prabhavit Karne Wale Kaarak):**
        *   High Density Areas (Northern Plains, Kerala): Flat plains, fertile soils, abundant rainfall.
        *   Moderate Density Areas (Assam, Peninsular States): Hilly/rocky terrain, moderate rainfall, less fertile soil.
        *   Low Density Areas (Arunachal, Sikkim): Rugged terrain (pahadi ilaake), unfavourable climate.

4.  **Jansankhya Vriddhi aur Parivartan ki Prakriyaayein (Population Growth and Processes of Population Change)**
    *   **Population Growth (Jansankhya Vriddhi):** Ek specific time period mein population mein hone wala change.
        *   **Absolute Increase (Kul Badhotri):** Total number of people added.
        *   **Annual Growth Rate (Varshik Vriddhi Dar):** Percentage increase per year.
    *   **India ka Trend:**
        *   1951 (361 million) se 2011 (1210 million) tak lagatar badhi hai.
        *   1951-1981: Growth rate steadily badha.
        *   Since 1981: Growth rate dheere-dheere kam ho raha hai (positive sign), lekin kyunki base population bahut badi hai, absolute increase abhi bhi bahut zyada hai (e.g., 1990s mein 182 million log add hue).
    *   **Processes of Change (Parivartan ki Prakriyaayein):**
        *   **Birth Rate (Janm Dar):** Per 1000 persons per year live births ki sankhya. India mein hamesha death rate se zyada rahi hai.
        *   **Death Rate (Mrityu Dar):** Per 1000 persons per year deaths ki sankhya. India mein death rate mein tezi se गिरावट aayi hai, jo population growth ka main reason tha.
        *   **Migration (Pravasan):** Logon ka ek region/territory se doosre mein move karna.
            *   Internal (Desh ke andar): Population size change nahi karta, par distribution badal deta hai (e.g., rural to urban).
            *   International (Deshon ke beech).
            *   Push Factors (Gaon mein): Poverty, unemployment.
            *   Pull Factors (Sheher mein): Better employment, living conditions.

5.  **Jansankhya ki Visheshtayein (Characteristics of Population)**
    *   **Adolescent Population (Kishor Jansankhya):**
        *   Age group: 10-19 years.
        *   India ki total population ka 1/5th (one-fifth) hissa.
        *   Future ke liye sabse important resource.
        *   Nutritional needs zyada hoti hain.
        *   Challenges: Poor nutrition, anaemia (khaaskar adolescent girls mein). Need for awareness and education.
    *   **Literacy (Saksharta):** (Mentioned in Exercise/Glossary context) Census ke anusaar, 7 saal ya usse upar ka vyakti jo kisi bhi language ko samajh ke saath padh aur likh sakta hai.

6.  **Rashtriya Jansankhya Niti (National Population Policy - NPP)**
    *   **NPP 2000:** Planned efforts ka result.
    *   **Key Objectives:**
        *   14 saal tak ke bachhon ke liye free aur compulsory education.
        *   Infant Mortality Rate (Shishu Mrityu Dar) ko 30 per 1000 live births se kam karna.
        *   Sabhi vaccine-preventable diseases ke khilaf universal immunization.
        *   Ladkiyon ke liye delayed marriage promote karna.
        *   Family welfare ko people-centred programme banana.

7.  **Census (Janganana)**
    *   Definition: Population ki official enumeration (ganana) jo periodically hoti hai.
    *   India mein pehla census 1872 mein, pehla complete census 1881 mein. Tab se har 10 saal par hota hai.
    *   Importance: Demographic, social, economic data ka sabse comprehensive source.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh) 📈

*   **Population Kyun Important Hai?** Log hi resources ko banate aur istemaal karte hain. Koyla (Coal) sirf ek pathar ka tukda tha jab tak logon ne use nikalne aur istemaal karne ki technology nahi banayi. Flood ya Tsunami 'disaster' tabhi bante hain jab woh kisi populated area ko affect karte hain. Isliye, population (unki sankhya, distribution, growth, characteristics) environment ke sabhi aspects ko samajhne ka base hai.

*   **India ki Jansankhya - Ek Vishal Chitra:** 2011 mein, hum 121 crore se zyada the! Yeh duniya ki आबादी ka 17.5% hai, jabki zameen sirf 2.4% hai (Figure 6.1). Yeh log desh mein ek samaan nahi phaile hain (uneven distribution). Uttar Pradesh mein sabse zyada log rehte hain, toh Sikkim jaise state mein bahut kam. Rajasthan area mein sabse bada hai, par population density kam hai (Figure 6.2). Aisa kyun? Iske peeche geographical factors hain.

*   **Density ka Khel:** Population Density (Jansankhya Ghanatva) uneven distribution ko behtar samajhne mein madad karti hai. India ki average density (382/sq km in 2011) kaafi high hai. Lekin states mein bahut farak hai - Bihar (1102) mein log bahut paas-paas rehte hain, jabki Arunachal Pradesh (17) mein bahut door-door (Figure 6.3). Maidaani ilake (Northern Plains) jahan zameen upjau hai aur baarish achhi hoti hai, wahan density high hai. Pahadi (hilly/rugged) ilakon mein, jahan kheti mushkil hai aur climate kathin, wahan density kam hai.

*   **Badhti Jansankhya - Gati aur Prakriya:** Population growth ko do tarah se dekhte hain - kitne log badhe (absolute increase) aur kis rate se badhe (growth rate). India ki population 1951 se lagatar badhi hai (Table 6.1, Figure 6.4a/b). 1981 tak growth rate bhi badhta gaya, lekin uske baad rate kam hone laga hai. Par, kyunki hamari total population itni zyada hai, kam rate par bhi har saal judne wale logon ki sankhya bahut badi hoti hai. Isse resources aur environment par दबाव padta hai. Population change ke teen main process hain:
    1.  **Birth Rate (Janm Dar):** India mein yeh traditionally high rahi hai.
    2.  **Death Rate (Mrityu Dar):** Medical facilities behtar hone se ismein tezi se kami aayi hai. Shuru mein high birth rate aur declining death rate se population tezi se badhi.
    3.  **Migration (Pravasan):** Log kaam aur behtar zindagi ki talaash mein gaon se sheher (rural to urban) ja rahe hain ('push' & 'pull' factors). Isse shehron ki population badh rahi hai aur composition change ho raha hai.

*   **Adolescents - Hamara Bhavishya:** Desh ki population ka ek bada hissa (1/5th) kishoravastha (10-19 years) mein hai. Yeh desh ka future hain. Inki health aur education par dhyaan dena bahut zaroori hai. Khaaskar, adolescent girls mein anaemia (khoon ki kami) ek badi samasya hai, jiske liye awareness aur sahi nutrition zaroori hai.

*   **Sarkari Prayas - NPP 2000:** Sarkar ne population ko manage karne ke liye policies banayi hain. National Population Policy 2000 ka maqsad health, education, aur overall well-being improve karna hai, jaise bachhon ko padhana, shishu mrityu dar kam karna, vaccination, aur family planning ko promote karna.
