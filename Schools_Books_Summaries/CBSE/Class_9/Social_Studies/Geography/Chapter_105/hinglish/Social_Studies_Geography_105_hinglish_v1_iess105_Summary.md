# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 5: Prakritik Vanaspati tatha Vanya Prani (Natural Vegetation and Wildlife)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Bharat ki Jaiv Vividhta (India's Biodiversity):**
    *   India duniya ki 12 mega biodiversity countries mein se ek hai.
    *   **Flora (वनस्पति):** Lagbhag 47,000 plant species (World mein 10th, Asia mein 4th). Includes 15,000 flowering plants (World ka 6%). Non-flowering plants jaise ferns, algae, fungi bhi hain.
    *   **Fauna (प्राणीजात):** Lagbhag 90,000 animal species, 2,000 bird species (World ka 13%), 2,546 fish species (World ka 12%).
2.  **Prakritik Vanaspati (Natural Vegetation):**
    *   Woh plant community jo naturally grow karti hai bina human interference ke. Ise **Virgin Vegetation** (अक्षत वनस्पति) bhi kehte hain.
    *   **Endemic/Indigenous Species (देशज प्रजातियां):** Jo purely Indian hain.
    *   **Exotic Species (विदेशज प्रजातियां):** Jo bahar se aayi hain.
3.  **Vanaspati ke Prakar (Types of Vegetation):** Factors influencing vegetation: Relief (Land, Soil) and Climate (Temperature, Sunlight, Precipitation/Rainfall). Major types hain:
    *   Tropical Evergreen Forests (उष्णकटिबंधीय सदाबहार वन)
    *   Tropical Deciduous Forests (उष्णकटिबंधीय पर्णपाती वन)
    *   Tropical Thorn Forests and Scrubs (उष्णकटिबंधीय कंटीले वन तथा झाड़ियाँ)
    *   Montane Forests (पर्वतीय वन)
    *   Mangrove Forests (मैंग्रोव वन)
4.  **Vanya Jeevan (Wildlife):** Bharat mein vividh prakar ke animals paye jaate hain, unke specific habitats ke saath.
5.  **Sanrakshan (Conservation):** Flora aur Fauna ko bachane ki zaroorat aur sarkari prayas (efforts).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

**1. Tropical Evergreen Forests (उष्णकटिबंधीय सदाबहार वन):**
    *   **Rainfall:** > 200 cm. Short dry season.
    *   **Areas:** Western Ghats ke heavy rainfall wale parts, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Upper Assam, Tamil Nadu coast.
    *   **Characteristics:** Bahut ghane (dense), trees ki height 60m tak ya usse bhi upar. Multi-layered structure (trees, shrubs, creepers). Koi definite time nahi hota leaves shed karne ka, isliye **saal bhar hare-bhare** (green all year round) rehte hain.
    *   **Species:** Ebony, Mahogany, Rosewood, Rubber, Cinchona.
    *   **Fauna:** Elephant, Monkey, Lemur, Deer, One-horned Rhino (Assam/WB jungles mein), birds, bats, sloth, scorpions.
    *   *Diagram Concept:* Ek ghana jungle jismein alag-alag height ke ped, lataayein (creepers) dikhayi gayi ho, sab hara bhara. 🌳🌿

**2. Tropical Deciduous Forests (उष्णकटिबंधीय पर्णपाती वन):**
    *   **Rainfall:** 70 cm to 200 cm.
    *   **Areas:** India ke sabse bade area mein phaile hue hain. Inhe **Monsoon Forests** (मानसूनी वन) bhi kehte hain.
    *   **Characteristics:** Dry summer mein 6-8 weeks ke liye apne **patte gira dete hain** (shed their leaves). Paani ki availability ke basis par do types:
        *   **Moist Deciduous (आर्द्र पर्णपाती):** 100-200 cm rainfall. Eastern India, NE states, Himalayas ke foothills, Jharkhand, West Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Eastern slopes of Western Ghats. *Species:* Teak (dominant), Bamboo, Sal, Shisham, Sandalwood, Khair, Kusum, Arjun, Mulberry.
        *   **Dry Deciduous (शुष्क पर्णपाती):** 70-100 cm rainfall. Peninsular plateau ke rainier parts, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh plains. *Species:* Teak, Sal, Peepal, Neem. Bade hisse ko kheti (cultivation) ya charai (grazing) ke liye saaf kar diya gaya hai.
    *   **Fauna:** Lion, Tiger, Pig, Deer, Elephant, birds, lizards, snakes, tortoises.
    *   *Diagram Concept:* Ped jinke patjhad (autumn) mein patte gir rahe hain. 🍂

**3. Tropical Thorn Forests and Scrubs (उष्णकटिबंधीय कंटीले वन तथा झाड़ियाँ):**
    *   **Rainfall:** < 70 cm.
    *   **Areas:** North-western parts including semi-arid areas of Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana.
    *   **Characteristics:** Kantele ped (thorny trees) aur jhaadiyan. Ped scattered hote hain. **Lambi jadein** (long roots) jo paani ke liye gehrai tak jaati hain. Stems succulent hote hain (paani conserve karne ke liye). Leaves moti aur chhoti hoti hain (evaporation kam karne ke liye).
    *   **Species:** Acacias, Palms, Euphorbias, Cacti.
    *   **Fauna:** Rats, Mice, Rabbit, Fox, Wolf, Tiger, Lion, Wild Ass, Horses, Camels.
    *   *Diagram Concept:* Registan jaisa ilaka, door-door kantele ped aur jhaadiyan. 🌵🐫

**4. Montane Forests (पर्वतीय वन):**
    *   **Areas:** Mountainous areas.
    *   **Characteristics:** Temperature **unchai (altitude) ke saath kam** hota hai, jisse vegetation change hoti hai.
        *   1000-2000m: Wet Temperate Forests (Oaks, Chestnuts).
        *   1500-3000m: Temperate Forests (Coniferous trees - Pine, Deodar, Silver Fir, Spruce, Cedar). Southern slopes of Himalayas, Southern & NE India ke high altitude areas.
        *   >3600m: Alpine Vegetation (Silver Fir, Junipers, Pines, Birches). Jaise-jaise snow-line ke paas jaate hain, ped chhote hote jaate hain (stunted). Finally Alpine grasslands, mosses, lichens (Tundra vegetation). Nomadic tribes like Gujjars, Bakarwals yahan grazing karte hain.
    *   **Fauna:** Kashmir Stag, Spotted Deer, Wild Sheep, Jack Rabbit, Tibetan Antelope, Yak, Snow Leopard, Squirrels, Wild Ibex, Bear, Red Panda.
    *   *Diagram Concept:* Ek pahad ka cross-section, jismein alag-alag unchai par alag-alag vanaspati belts dikhaye gaye hon. ⛰️🌲

**5. Mangrove Forests (मैंग्रोव वन):**
    *   **Areas:** Coastal areas jahan tides ka influence hota hai (Tidal forests). Ganga, Mahanadi, Krishna, Godavari, Kaveri ke deltas.
    *   **Characteristics:** Mud aur silt jama ho jaata hai. Dense mangroves hote hain jinki **roots paani ke neeche submerge** rehti hain.
    *   **Species:** Sundari trees (Ganga-Brahmaputra delta - Sundarbans, provide durable hard timber), Palm, Coconut, Keora, Agar.
    *   **Fauna:** Royal Bengal Tiger (famous), Turtles, Crocodiles, Gharials, Snakes.
    *   *Diagram Concept:* Ped jinki jadein paani mein doobi hui hain, coastal area mein. 🌊🌳

**6. Medicinal Plants (औषधीय पौधे):**
    *   India ancient times se herbs aur spices ke liye jaana jaata hai. Ayurveda mein lagbhag 2000 plants ka वर्णन hai.
    *   **Examples:**
        *   **Sarpagandha:** Blood pressure ke liye, sirf India mein milta hai.
        *   **Jamun:** Vinegar banane mein use hota hai (digestive properties), seed powder diabetes control ke liye.
        *   **Arjun:** Leaves ka juice earache ke liye, blood pressure regulate karta hai.
        *   **Babool:** Leaves eye sores ke liye, gum tonic hai.
        *   **Neem:** High antibiotic aur antibacterial properties.
        *   **Tulsi:** Cough aur cold ke liye.
        *   **Kachnar:** Asthma aur ulcers ke liye, digestive problems mein bhi helpful.

**7. Wildlife Conservation (वन्यजीव संरक्षण):**
    *   **Threats (Khatre):** Hunting (shikar), Pollution (pradushan), Deforestation (jangalon ki katai), Habitat loss. Inse ecosystem disturb hua hai. Lagbhag 1300 plant species endangered hain, 20 extinct ho chuki hain. Kayi animal species bhi endangered ya extinct hain.
    *   **Government Steps (Sarkari Kadam):**
        *   **Wildlife Protection Act 1972** implement kiya gaya.
        *   **18 Biosphere Reserves** set up kiye gaye hain (e.g., Sundarbans, Nanda Devi, Nilgiri, Gulf of Mannar, Panna). 12 inmein se World Network of Biosphere Reserves mein shamil hain.
        *   **106 National Parks, 573 Wildlife Sanctuaries** (Data as per text, may vary slightly with updates).
        *   **Projects:** Project Tiger, Project Rhino, Project Great Indian Bustard etc. shuru kiye gaye hain.
        *   Botanical Gardens ko financial/technical help di jaati hai.
