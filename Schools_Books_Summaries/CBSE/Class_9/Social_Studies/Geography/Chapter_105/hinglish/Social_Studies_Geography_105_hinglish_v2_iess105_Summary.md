# [Class 9] Geography - Chapter 5: Prakritik Vanaspati tatha Vanya Prani (Natural Vegetation and Wildlife)

*(Note: NCERT Class 9 Geography Chapter 5 deals with this topic. The request mentioned Chapter 105, which seems to be a placeholder.)*

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter Bharat ke natural vegetation (prakritik vanaspati) aur wildlife (vanya prani) ke baare mein hai. Mukhya concepts hain:

1.  **Jaiv-Vividhata (Biodiversity):**
    *   Bharat duniya ki 12 mega-biodiversity countries mein se ek hai.
    *   Flora (Vanaspati): Lagbhag 47,000 plant species. Tenth in world, fourth in Asia. Includes flowering (15,000) and non-flowering plants (ferns, algae, fungi).
    *   Fauna (Vanya Prani): Lagbhag 90,000 animal species, 2,000 bird species, 2,546 fish species.
2.  **Prakritik Vanaspati (Natural Vegetation):**
    *   Definition: Plant community jo naturally grow hui ho, bina human interference ke (Virgin Vegetation).
    *   Endemic (Desi) Species: Jo mool roop se Bharatiya hain.
    *   Exotic (Videshi) Species: Jo bahar se laaye gaye hain.
3.  **Vanaspati ke Prakar (Types of Vegetation in India) 📊:**
    *   Ushnakatibandhiya Sadabahar Van (Tropical Evergreen Forests) 🌳
    *   Ushnakatibandhiya Parnapati Van (Tropical Deciduous Forests) 🍂
        *   Moist Deciduous (Aadr Parnapati)
        *   Dry Deciduous (Shushk Parnapati)
    *   Kanteele Van tatha Jhaadiyaan (Tropical Thorn Forests and Scrubs) 🌵
    *   Parvatiya Van (Montane Forests) 🏔️
    *   Mangrove Van (Mangrove Forests) 🌊
4.  **Vanya Prani (Wildlife):**
    *   Distribution: Alag-alag vegetation zones mein specific animals ka paya jaana.
    *   Mahatva (Importance): Ecosystem balance, resources (milk, meat, transport), pollination.
5.  **Sanrakshan (Conservation):**
    *   Threats: Shikar (hunting), pollution, deforestation, alien species introduction.
    *   Zarurat: Ecosystem ko bachana, endangered species ko protect karna.
    *   Sarkari Kadam (Government Steps): Wildlife Protection Act (1972), Biosphere Reserves, National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Projects (Tiger, Rhino etc.).

## 📘 Key Learnings

Yahan hum har type ki vegetation aur wildlife ko detail mein samjhenge:

1.  **Tropical Evergreen Forests (Ushnakatibandhiya Sadabahar Van) 🌳:**
    *   **Kahan milte hain?** Heavy rainfall areas (>200 cm) - Western Ghats, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar, Upper Assam, Tamil Nadu coast.
    *   **Visheshtayein:** Saal bhar hare-bhare (no definite leaf shedding time), ped bahut lambe (up to 60m), multi-layered structure (trees, shrubs, creepers). Warm aur wet climate.
    *   **Mukhya Ped:** Ebony, Mahogany, Rosewood, Rubber, Cinchona (Commercially important).
    *   **Jeev-Jantu:** Elephant, Monkey, Lemur, Deer, One-horned Rhino (Assam/WB jungles), birds, bats, sloth.

2.  **Tropical Deciduous Forests (Ushnakatibandhiya Parnapati Van) 🍂:**
    *   **Kahan milte hain?** India ke sabse bade area mein फैले hue hain. Rainfall 70-200 cm. Inhe 'Monsoon Forests' bhi kehte hain.
    *   **Visheshtayein:** Dry summer mein 6-8 weeks ke liye patte girate hain. Paani ki availability ke basis par do types:
        *   **Moist (Aadr):** Rainfall 100-200 cm. Eastern India, NE states, Himalayan foothills, Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Eastern slopes of Western Ghats. **Ped:** Teak (dominant), Bamboo, Sal, Shisham, Sandalwood, Khair, Arjun, Mulberry.
        *   **Dry (Shushk):** Rainfall 70-100 cm. Peninsular plateau ke rainier parts, Bihar, UP plains. **Ped:** Teak, Sal, Peepal, Neem. Bade hisse kheti ya grazing ke liye clear kar diye gaye hain.
    *   **Jeev-Jantu:** Lion, Tiger, Pig, Deer, Elephant, birds, lizards, snakes, tortoises.

3.  **Thorn Forests and Scrubs (Kanteele Van tatha Jhaadiyaan) 🌵:**
    *   **Kahan milte hain?** Less than 70 cm rainfall wale regions - North-western India (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, UP, Haryana).
    *   **Visheshtayein:** Ped scattered, roots lambi (paani ki talaash mein), stems succulent (paani conserve karne ke liye), leaves moti/choti/kaante wali (evaporation kam karne ke liye).
    *   **Mukhya Plants:** Acacias, Palms, Euphorbias, Cacti.
    *   **Jeev-Jantu:** Rats, Mice, Rabbit, Fox, Wolf, Tiger, Lion, Wild Ass, Camels.

4.  **Montane Forests (Parvatiya Van) 🏔️:**
    *   **Kahan milte hain?** Mountainous areas mein. Altitude badhne par temperature kam hota hai, vegetation change hoti hai.
    *   **Visheshtayein & Zones:**
        *   **1000-2000m:** Wet Temperate Forests (Evergreen broad-leaf trees like Oaks, Chestnuts).
        *   **1500-3000m:** Temperate Forests (Coniferous trees like Pine, Deodar, Silver Fir, Spruce, Cedar). Southern slopes of Himalayas, Southern/NE India high altitude areas.
        *   **Higher elevations:** Temperate Grasslands.
        *   **>3600m:** Alpine Vegetation (Silver Fir, Junipers, Pines, Birches). Ped stunted ho jaate hain near snow-line. Phir Alpine Grasslands (used by nomadic tribes like Gujjars, Bakarwals for grazing).
        *   **Highest altitudes:** Tundra Vegetation (Mosses, Lichens).
    *   **Jeev-Jantu:** Kashmir Stag, Spotted Deer, Wild Sheep, Jack Rabbit, Tibetan Antelope, Yak, Snow Leopard, Squirrels, Ibex, Bear, Red Panda.

5.  **Mangrove Forests 🌊:**
    *   **Kahan milte hain?** Coastal areas jahan tides ka influence hota hai. Ganga, Mahanadi, Krishna, Godavari, Kaveri ke deltas mein. Mud aur silt accumulate hota hai.
    *   **Visheshtayein:** Dense mangroves, plants ki roots paani mein doobi rehti hain. Survive in salt water.
    *   **Mukhya Ped:** Sundari trees (Ganga-Brahmaputra delta - provide durable hard timber), Palm, Coconut, Keora, Agar.
    *   **Jeev-Jantu:** Royal Bengal Tiger (famous), Turtles, Crocodiles, Gharials, Snakes.

6.  **Wildlife (Vanya Prani) 🐅🐘:**
    *   India mein rich fauna hai. Elephants (hot wet forests), One-horned Rhinos (swampy lands), Wild Ass & Camels (arid areas), Indian Bison, Nilgai, Deer, Monkeys.
    *   **Unique:** India duniya ka eklauta desh hai jahan Tigers aur Lions dono hain. Lions sirf Gir Forest (Gujarat) mein. Tigers MP, Sundarbans, Himalayas mein.
    *   **Himalayan Fauna:** Yak, Tibetan Antelope, Bharal (Blue Sheep), Wild Sheep, Kiang (Tibetan Wild Ass), Ibex, Snow Leopard, Red Panda.
    *   **Birds:** Peacocks, Pheasants, Ducks, Cranes, Pigeons. Migratory birds (like Siberian Crane) winter mein aate hain (e.g., Rann of Kachchh).

7.  **Medicinal Plants (Aushadhiya Paudhe) 🌿:**
    *   Bharat ancient times se herbs/spices ke liye jana jata hai. Ayurveda mein 2000+ plants ka zikr hai.
    *   **Examples:**
        *   **Sarpagandha:** Blood pressure ke liye (Only in India).
        *   **Jamun:** Vinegar, digestive, diabetes control (seed powder).
        *   **Arjun:** Earache, blood pressure regulation.
        *   **Babool:** Eye sores, tonic (gum).
        *   **Neem:** High antibiotic & antibacterial properties.
        *   **Tulsi:** Cough & cold.
        *   **Kachnar:** Asthma, ulcers, digestive problems.

8.  **Conservation (Sanrakshan) 🛡️:**
    *   **Kyun Zaruri Hai?** Har species ka ecosystem mein role hota hai. Human activities (over-exploitation) se ecosystem disturb hua hai. Approx 1300 plant species endangered, 20 extinct. Kayi animal species bhi endangered/extinct.
    *   **Mukhya Khatre (Threats):** Hunting (shikar), Pollution (chemical/industrial waste, acid deposits), Alien species ka introduction, Deforestation (kheti/rehne ke liye).
    *   **Sarkar ke Kadam (Govt. Steps):**
        *   **Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.**
        *   **18 Biosphere Reserves:** Flora/Fauna protect karne ke liye (12 world network mein hain - e.g., Sundarbans, Nanda Devi, Nilgiri, Panna).
        *   **Financial/Technical Assistance:** Botanical gardens ko (since 1992).
        *   **Projects:** Project Tiger (1973), Project Rhino, Project Great Indian Bustard etc.
        *   **Protected Areas:** 106 National Parks, 573 Wildlife Sanctuaries, Zoological Gardens.
    *   **Hamari Zimmedari:** Natural ecosystem ki importance ko samajhna aur uske indiscriminate destruction ko rokna.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

Bharat ka natural heritage bahut rich aur diverse hai:

1.  **National Economic/Social Data 📊:**
    *   Bharat duniya ki **12 mega-biodiversity nations** mein shamil hai.
    *   Yahan **47,000+ plant species** (world ka 10th, Asia ka 4th) aur **90,000+ animal species** hain.
    *   Forests se lakdi (timber), medicinal plants, aur dusre resources milte hain jo economy aur local livelihoods ke liye important hain. Tourism (wildlife tourism) bhi ek important economic activity hai.
2.  **India-Specific Examples 🇮🇳:**
    *   **Western Ghats & Eastern Himalayas:** Biodiversity hotspots hain, rich in endemic species.
    *   **Medicinal Plants:** Ayurveda aur traditional medicine mein **Tulsi, Neem, Ashwagandha, Sarpagandha** jaise plants ka hazaron saalon se istemal ho raha hai.
    *   **Sacred Groves:** Bharat ke kayi hisson mein community dwara protect kiye gaye forest patches (Devrai/Kavu) jo biodiversity conservation ka traditional tareeka hain.
    *   **Tribal Communities:** Adivasi samuday jaise **Gujjars, Bakarwals (Himalayas), Gonds (Central India)** ka forests aur wildlife ke saath gehra sambandh hai aur unke traditional knowledge conservation mein helpful ho sakte hain.
3.  **Government Initiatives 🛡️:**
    *   **Project Tiger (1973):** Tiger conservation ke liye ek successful initiative.
    *   **Project Elephant (1992):** Elephant corridors aur habitat protection ke liye.
    *   **National Biodiversity Authority (NBA):** Biological resources ke access aur benefit sharing ko regulate karta hai under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.
    *   **Biosphere Reserves Program:** Conservation, research, aur sustainable development ko integrate karta hai (e.g., Nilgiri, Nanda Devi).

Yeh sabhi aspects Bharat ke unique natural heritage ko highlight karte hain aur iske conservation ki importance ko emphasize karte hain.
