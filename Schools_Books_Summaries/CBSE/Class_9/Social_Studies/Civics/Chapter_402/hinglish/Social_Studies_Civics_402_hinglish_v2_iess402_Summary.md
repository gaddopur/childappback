# [Class 9] Civics - Chapter 402: Constitutional Design (Samvidhan Nirman)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Samvidhan Kya Hai? (What is a Constitution?)**
    *   Desh ka supreme law (sarvoch kanoon).
    *   Basic rules ka set jo citizens aur government dono follow karte hain.
    *   Determines: Citizens ke rights, government ki powers, aur government kaise function karegi.

2.  **Humein Samvidhan Kyon Chahiye? (Why Do We Need a Constitution?)**
    *   **Trust & Coordination:** Alag-alag groups ke beech bharosa aur talmel banana.
    *   **Government Formation:** Specify karta hai ki sarkar kaise banegi aur decisions kaun lega.
    *   **Limits on Government Power:** Sarkar ki shaktiyon par seema lagata hai.
    *   **Citizens' Rights:** Nagrikon ke adhikar batata hai.
    *   **Aspirations:** Ek achhe samaj (good society) banane ki logon ki ichhayein express karta hai.

3.  **South Africa Ka Udaharan (The South African Example)**
    *   **Apartheid:** Racial discrimination (nasli bhedbhav) ka system (1948-1994). Whites dwara non-whites (Blacks, Coloureds, Indians) par atyachar.
    *   **Struggle:** Nelson Mandela aur African National Congress (ANC) ka lamba sangharsh. Mandela ko 27 saal jail (Robben Island).
    *   **Transition to Democracy:** Negotiations ke baad peaceful transition. Naya 'Rainbow Nation' bana.
    *   **New Constitution:** Sabhi races ke liye equality, extensive rights, forgiveness aur saath milkar kaam karne par focus. Duniya ke liye ek misaal (model).

4.  **Bharat Ka Samvidhan Banana (Making of the Indian Constitution)**
    *   **Mushkil Halat (Difficult Circumstances):**
        *   Partition (Vibhajan) ka dard aur violence.
        *   Princely states (Riyasaton) ka integration.
        *   Huge diversity (vividhta) wala desh.
    *   **Fayde (Advantages):**
        *   Freedom struggle se nikla consensus (aam sehmati).
        *   Colonial rule ke institutions ka experience.
        *   Dusre deshon ke samvidhano se seekhne ki kshamta (French Revolution, British Parliament, US Bill of Rights, Russian Revolution).
    *   **The Path to Constitution:**
        *   1928: Motilal Nehru Report (Pehla draft).
        *   1931: Karachi Session Resolution (Values like universal adult franchise, equality, minority rights).

5.  **Samvidhan Sabha (The Constituent Assembly)**
    *   Elected representatives ki assembly jisne Samvidhan likha.
    *   Indirectly elected (Provincial Legislatures dwara).
    *   Diverse representation (regions, languages, castes, classes, religions). Dominated by Congress, par alag vichardhara wale members bhi the.
    *   Working Style: Systematic, open, consensual. Bahut discussion aur debate (114 din, 3 saal). Drafting Committee ke Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.
    *   Adopted: 26 November 1949. Enforced: 26 January 1950 (Republic Day).

6.  **Samvidhan Ke Margdarshak Mulya (Guiding Values of the Constitution)**
    *   **Leaders' Vision:**
        *   Mahatma Gandhi: Inequality khatam ho, sabhi communities harmony mein rahein, poorest feel karein ki yeh unka desh hai.
        *   Dr. B.R. Ambedkar: Political equality ke saath social aur economic equality bhi zaroori hai, varna democracy khatre mein padegi.
        *   Jawaharlal Nehru ('Tryst with Destiny'): Freedom ke saath responsibility aati hai - poverty, ignorance, disease, inequality ko khatam karna.
    *   **Preamble (Prastavna):** Samvidhan ki philosophy/aatma (soul).
        *   **WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA:** Samvidhan logon ne banaya hai, kisi raja ya bahari shakti ne nahi diya.
        *   **SOVEREIGN (Samprabhu):** Bharat apne internal aur external decisions lene ke liye free hai.
        *   **SOCIALIST (Samajwadi):** Wealth socially generate hoti hai aur equally share honi chahiye. Socio-economic inequalities kam karna. (Added in 1976)
        *   **SECULAR (Panthnirpeksh):** Sabhi religions ko follow karne ki freedom. Koi official religion nahi. Sarkar sabhi dharmon ka samaan adar karti hai. (Added in 1976)
        *   **DEMOCRATIC (Loktantrik):** Sarkar logon dwara chuni jaati hai, log accountable rakhte hain. Equal political rights.
        *   **REPUBLIC (Ganrajya):** Head of State (President) elected hota hai, hereditary nahi.
        *   **JUSTICE (Nyay):** Social, economic, political nyay. Discrimination nahi hoga.
        *   **LIBERTY (Swatantrata):** Sochne, express karne, action lene ki freedom (reasonable restrictions ke saath).
        *   **EQUALITY (Samanta):** Kanoon ke saamne sab barabar. Social inequalities khatam karna. Equal opportunity.
        *   **FRATERNITY (Bandhutva):** Sabhi citizens ek family ke members ki tarah behave karein. Bhai-chara.

7.  **Institutional Design (Sansthagat Swaroop)**
    *   Values ko institutions mein badalna.
    *   Procedure for choosing government.
    *   Powers ka distribution.
    *   Limits on government (Citizen rights).
    *   Amendments (Sanshodhan): Samay ke saath badlav ki gunjaish.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Samvidhan ki Zaroorat Kyon?** Ek society ko smoothly chalane ke liye basic rules chahiye. South Africa ka example dikhata hai ki kaise dushman bhi saath rehne ke liye common rules par agree ho sakte hain. Ye rules **trust** banate hain, **sarkar ki power define** karte hain, **citizens ke rights protect** karte hain, aur **behtar future ka vision** dete hain.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Flowchart showing -> Diverse People -> Need for Rules -> Constitution -> Trust, Coordination, Govt Structure, Rights, Aspirations.
*   **South Africa ka Sabak:** Apartheid (nasli bhedbhav) ek behad anyaypurn system tha. Lambe sangharsh ke baad, Nelson Mandela ke leadership mein, South Africa ne ek naya samvidhan banaya jo **forgiveness (maafi), inclusion (sabko shaamil karna), aur equality (barabari)** par adharit tha. Yeh 'Rainbow Nation' ka concept duniya bhar ke democrats ko inspire karta hai.
*   **Bharat ka Samvidhan Nirman:** Yeh aasaan nahi tha. Partition ka trauma, riyasaton ka mudda, aur desh ki vividhta badi chunautiyan thi. Lekin, freedom struggle se nikli **aam sehmati (consensus)**, colonial institutions ka anubhav, aur doosre deshon se seekhne ki ichha ne madad ki.
*   **Samvidhan Sabha ka Role:** Is assembly ne, jismein alag-alag background ke log the (halanki directly sabhi ne nahi chuna tha), **open discussion aur debate** ke zariye samvidhan banaya. Dr. Ambedkar ka Drafting Committee ke head ke roop mein role crucial tha. Iske kaam karne ka tareeka (systematic, open, consensual) samvidhan ko **legitimacy (vaidhyata)** deta hai.
*   **Preamble - Samvidhan ki Aatma:** Preamble ek short introductory statement hai jo samvidhan ke basic values (SOVEREIGN, SOCIALIST, SECULAR, DEMOCRATIC, REPUBLIC, JUSTICE, LIBERTY, EQUALITY, FRATERNITY) batata hai. Yeh poore samvidhan ka **foundation (neenv)** hai aur kisi bhi kanoon ya sarkari action ko judge karne ka **standard (maanak)** hai.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Mind map with 'Preamble' in the center and branches for each key term (Sovereign, Socialist, etc.) with brief Hinglish explanations.
*   **Values aur Institutions:** Samvidhan sirf values ka statement nahi hai, balki un values ko **institutional arrangements (sansthagat vyavasthaon)** mein badalta hai – jaise sarkar kaise chunenge, kiske paas kitni power hogi, etc. Samvidhan **amendable (sanshodhaniya)** hai, yaani zaroorat ke hisab se badla ja sakta hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Rashtriya Sandarbh) 📊

*   **National Economic/Social Data:**
    *   **Diversity:** Bharat ki zabardast vividhta (anek Bhashayein, Dharm, Jatiyan) samvidhan nirmataon ke liye ek badi chunauti thi, jise samvidhan ne address karne ki koshish ki (e.g., minority rights, secularism).
    *   **Partition (1947):** Lagbhag 10 lakh logon ki maut ka anubhav samvidhan mein security, unity aur minority rights ke provisions par asar daala.
    *   **Constituent Assembly Data:** 299 members (after partition), lagbhag 3 saal (114 din meeting) lage samvidhan banane mein.
    *   **Adoption & Enforcement:** Samvidhan 26 November 1949 ko **adopt (apnaya)** gaya (jise ab **Constitution Day / Samvidhan Diwas** ke roop mein manaya jaata hai) aur 26 January 1950 ko **laagu (enforce)** hua (jise hum **Republic Day / Gantantra Diwas** ke roop mein manate hain).
*   **Key Indian Figures:** Mahatma Gandhi (inspiration, though not a member), Jawaharlal Nehru (first PM, 'Tryst with Destiny'), Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (princely states integration), Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Chairman, Drafting Committee), Dr. Rajendra Prasad (President, Constituent Assembly), Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, Sarojini Naidu, aur kai anya leaders ka yogdaan mahatvapurna tha.
*   **Indian Preamble:** Yeh Bharat ke logon ki aspirations ko reflect karta hai aur desh ke basic principles (Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic, Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity) ko sthapit karta hai. 'Socialist' aur 'Secular' shabd 1976 mein 42nd Amendment dwara jode gaye.
