# [Class 9] Civics - Chapter 2: Constitutional Design (Samvidhan Nirman)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Samvidhan (Constitution): Kya aur Kyun?**
    *   Definition: Desh ka supreme law (sarvoch kanoon); basic rules ka set jo citizens aur government dono follow karte hain.
    *   Zaroorat (Need):
        *   Trust aur coordination banana (alag-alag groups ke beech).
        *   Government kaise banegi, kiske paas power hogi - yeh specify karna.
        *   Government ki powers par limits lagana aur citizens ke rights batana.
        *   Ek achhi society banane ke logon ke aspirations (akankshayein) express karna.

2.  **South Africa ka Udaharan (Example):**
    *   Apartheid (Rangbhed): System of racial discrimination (nasli bhedbhav).
    *   Sangharsh (Struggle): Nelson Mandela aur African National Congress (ANC) ka role. Lambi ladaai aur imprisonment.
    *   Transition to Democracy: Negotiations (baat-cheet), compromise (samjhauta) white minority aur black majority ke beech.
    *   Naya Samvidhan: Sabhi races ke liye equality, social justice, human rights par based. Ek "Rainbow Nation" ka concept. Duniya ke liye ek misaal (example).

3.  **Bharat ka Samvidhan Nirman (Making of the Indian Constitution):**
    *   Mushkil Circumstances: Partition (vibhajan) ka trauma, princely states (riyasaton) ka integration, diversity ki challenge.
    *   Advantages: Freedom struggle se nikla consensus (aam sehmati), colonial rule ke institutions ki familiarity, doosre deshon se seekhne ki willingness (lekin apni terms par).
    *   Constituent Assembly (Samvidhan Sabha):
        *   Formation: Elected representatives (chune hue pratinidhi), July 1946 mein elections, December 1946 mein first meeting. Partition ke baad divide hui. Indian Constituent Assembly mein 299 members the.
        *   Kaam karne ka Tarika: Systematic, open, consensual. Basic principles decide hue -> Drafting Committee (Prarup Samiti) ne Dr. B.R. Ambedkar ki chairmanship mein draft banaya -> Detailed discussions (clause-by-clause), amendments (sanshodhan) consider kiye gaye (approx. 2000+). 114 din meeting hui (3 saal mein).
        *   Representation: Alag-alag regions, language groups, castes, classes, religions, occupations ke members. Congress ka dominance tha, par usmein bhi variety thi. Non-Congress members bhi the.
    *   Adoption & Enforcement: 26 November 1949 ko adopt kiya gaya, 26 January 1950 ko लागू (laagu) hua (Republic Day).

4.  **Samvidhan ke Mool Darshan (Guiding Values):**
    *   Freedom Struggle ke Ideals: Democracy, Equality, Justice, Liberty, Fraternity.
    *   Leaders ke Views: Mahatma Gandhi (poorest feel it's their country, no high/low class, harmony, women's rights), Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (political equality vs social/economic inequality ka contradiction), Jawaharlal Nehru ('Tryst with Destiny' speech - service of India means ending poverty, ignorance, disease, inequality).
    *   Preamble (Prastavna): Samvidhan ki 'atma' (soul) ya philosophy. Basic values ka short statement.
        *   Keywords: WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, SOVEREIGN (Samprabhu), SOCIALIST (Samajwadi), SECULAR (Panthnirpeksh), DEMOCRATIC (Loktantrik), REPUBLIC (Ganrajya), JUSTICE (Nyay), LIBERTY (Swatantrata), EQUALITY (Samta), FRATERNITY (Bandhutva). (*Socialist aur Secular 42nd Amendment, 1976 mein add kiye gaye*).

5.  **Institutional Design (Sansthagat Swaroop):**
    *   Values ko institutions mein badalna.
    *   Procedure for choosing government.
    *   Powers ka distribution (kiske paas kitni power).
    *   Government par limits (through citizen rights).
    *   Constitutional Amendments (Samvidhan Sanshodhan): Samay ke saath badlav ki zaroorat aur process. Ek living document.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Samvidhan Kyun Zaroori Hai?** Imagine karo ek football match bina rules ke! Koi bhi kuch bhi karega, chaos hoga. Waise hi, ek desh ko chalane ke liye basic rules chahiye jin par sab agree karein. South Africa mein, jahan itni nafrat thi (whites vs blacks), wahan bhi saath rehne ke liye common rules banane pade. Ye rules trust banate hain, batate hain ki sarkar kaise chunegi (how government will be elected), sarkar kya kar sakti hai aur kya nahi (limits on power), aur citizens ke kya adhikar (rights) hain.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Flowchart showing: Diverse People -> Need for Rules -> Constitution -> Trust, Coordination, Govt Structure, Limits, Rights, Aspirations.

*   **Bharat ka Samvidhan Kaise Bana?** Yeh aasaan nahi tha. Desh ka batwara (partition) hua tha, dange hue the (violence), 10 lakh log maare gaye the. Chhoti-chhoti riyasaton (princely states) ko Bharat mein shaamil karna tha. Lekin, hamare leaders ke paas ek advantage tha - aazadi ki ladai (freedom struggle) ke time se hi kai basic ideas par sab sehmat the (like democracy, universal adult franchise, equality, minority rights). Humne British rule ke time ke kuch administrative details (like Government of India Act 1935) bhi adapt kiye aur doosre deshon (France, Britain, US, Russia) ke ideas se bhi seekha.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Timeline showing: 1928 Motilal Nehru Report -> 1931 Karachi Resolution -> 1937 Provincial Elections -> 1946 Constituent Assembly Elections -> 1947 Partition/Independence -> 1949 Constitution Adopted -> 1950 Constitution Enforced.

*   **Constituent Assembly - Ek Mini-India:** Yeh assembly poore desh ko represent karti thi. Alag-alag ilakon (regions), bhashaon (languages), jaatiyon (castes), dharmo (religions) ke log the. Bhale hi sab log direct vote se nahi chun kar aaye the, par iski composition kaafi diverse thi. Sabne milkar, khuli بحث (discussion) karke, 3 saal tak mehnat karke samvidhan banaya. Har baat record ki gayi ('Constituent Assembly Debates').

*   **Preamble - Samvidhan ka Aaina (Mirror):** Yeh ek short introduction hai jo poore samvidhan ka essence batata hai.
    *   `WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA`: Power logon ke haath mein hai.
    *   `SOVEREIGN`: Bharat apne faisle khud lega (internal/external), koi bahari power dictate nahi karegi.
    *   `SOCIALIST`: Wealth society mein equally share honi chahiye, sarkar inequality kam karegi.
    *   `SECULAR`: Sabhi dharmo ka samman, koi official religion nahi. Citizens ko apna dharm maanne ki aazadi.
    *   `DEMOCRATIC`: Log apni sarkar chunte hain, sabko equal political rights.
    *   `REPUBLIC`: Desh ka head (President) elected hoga, hereditary nahi (jaise Raja/Rani).
    *   `JUSTICE`: Social, economic, political nyay. Bhedbhav (discrimination) nahi hoga.
    *   `LIBERTY`: Sochne, bolne, express karne ki aazadi (reasonable restrictions ke saath).
    *   `EQUALITY`: Kanoon ke saamne sab barabar. Opportunity ki equality. Social inequalities khatam karni hongi.
    *   `FRATERNITY`: Sabhi citizens bhai-chare (brotherhood) se rahenge, ek doosre ko inferior na samjhein.

*   **Samvidhan sirf Kitaab Nahi, Ek Zinda Document Hai:** Samvidhan mein badlav (amendments) kiye ja sakte hain taaki woh badalte time aur zarooraton ke hisaab se relevant rahe.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Rashtriya Sandarbh) 📊

*   **Historical Roots:** Hamara samvidhan achanak nahi bana. Iski roots hamare freedom struggle mein hain. 1928 ki Motilal Nehru Report aur 1931 ke Karachi Congress session mein hi Universal Adult Franchise, Freedom, Equality, Minority Rights jaise ideas discuss ho chuke the.
*   **Influence of GoI Act, 1935:** British India ka yeh act kaafi detailed tha. Hamare samvidhan ne isse kai administrative details aur institutional structures liye.
*   **Key Indian Leaders & Their Vision:**
    *   **Mahatma Gandhi:** (Assembly member nahi the, par unka influence tha) - Unka sapna tha ek aisa India jahan gareeb se gareeb bhi feel kare ki yeh uska desh hai, koi unch-neech na ho, sab harmony mein rahein.
    *   **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar:** (Drafting Committee Chairman) - Political equality (one man, one vote) ke saath social aur economic equality par zor diya. Unhone chetavni di ki agar social/economic inequality bani rahi toh political democracy khatre mein pad jayegi. Unka role Dalit rights aur social justice ke liye crucial tha.
    *   **Jawaharlal Nehru:** (First PM) - Unki 'Tryst with Destiny' speech ne aazad India ke goals set kiye - poverty, ignorance, disease, inequality ko khatam karna. Socialism aur democracy ke advocate the.
    *   **Sardar Patel:** (Home Minister) - Princely states ko India mein integrate karne mein decisive role nibhaya.
*   **Diversity & Unity:** India ki zabardast diversity (languages, religions, cultures) ko dekhte hue, ek aisa samvidhan banana zaroori tha jo sabko saath lekar chale aur national unity ko promote kare. Preamble ka 'Fraternity' value iska prateek hai.
*   **Data Point:** Partition ke time lagbhag 10 lakh logon ki jaanein gayi, yeh violence samvidhan nirmataon ke dimaag mein thi jab woh minority rights aur secularism jaise mudde discuss kar rahe the. Constituent Assembly ke 299 members ne is vishal desh ke liye samvidhan banaya.
