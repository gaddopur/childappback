# [Class 9] Civics - Chapter 4: Working of Institutions (संस्थाओं का कामकाज)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ) 📊

Yeh chapter samjhata hai ki democracy mein sarkar kaise kaam karti hai, khaas kar Bharat ke sandarbh mein. Sarkar sirf chunav jeetne wale netaon se nahi chalti, balki institutions (संस्थाओं) ke ek set se chalti hai jo rules aur procedures follow karte hain.

*   **Need for Institutions (संस्थाओं की ज़रूरत):**
    *   Democracy mein kaam karne ke liye arrangements zaroori hain (like rules, committees, routines).
    *   Yeh institutions ensure karte hain ki decisions soch-samajh kar liye jaayein aur sabko consultation ka mauka mile.
    *   Power kisi ek vyakti ke haath mein na rahe.
*   **Major Policy Decisions (प्रमुख नीतिगत निर्णय):**
    *   Bade faisle kaise liye jaate hain? Example: Mandal Commission ki report par reservation ka faisla (Office Memorandum).
    *   Is process mein kai log aur sansthayein shaamil hoti hain.
*   **Key Institutions in India (भारत की प्रमुख संस्थाएँ):**
    *   **Legislature (विधायिका - Parliament/Sansad):** Kanoon banana (Law-making), sarkar par control rakhna, paise (budget) par control. Iske do Houses hain: Lok Sabha aur Rajya Sabha.
    *   **Executive (कार्यपालिका):** Kanoon ko laagu karna (Implementation). Ismein do tarah ke log hote hain:
        *   *Political Executive:* Neta jo chune jaate hain (PM, Ministers). Bade policy decisions lete hain.
        *   *Permanent Executive:* Sarkari afsar (Civil Servants). Rozmarra ka administration dekhte hain aur ministers ki madad karte hain.
    *   **Judiciary (न्यायपालिका):** Vivadon (disputes) ko suljhana, kanoon ki vyakhya karna (interpret), aur nagrikon ke अधिकारों (rights) ki raksha karna. Ismein Supreme Court, High Courts, etc., aate hain.

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य बातें) 📈

**1. How are Major Policy Decisions Taken? (प्रमुख नीतिगत निर्णय कैसे लिए जाते हैं?) - The Mandal Commission Example**

*   **The Order (Office Memorandum):** 13 August 1990 ko Government of India ne ek Office Memorandum (O.M. No. 36012/31/90-Estt(SCT)) jaari kiya. Isne announce kiya ki Central Government ki civil posts aur services mein 27% vacancies Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBCs) ke liye reserved hongi. Yeh SC/ST reservation ke alawa tha.
*   **The Decision Makers (निर्णय लेने वाले):**
    *   Yeh faisla kisi ek officer ne nahi liya tha. Yeh ek lambi process ka result tha.
    *   **Mandal Commission:** 1979 mein bani Second Backward Classes Commission (headed by B.P. Mandal) ne 1980 mein report di, jismein 27% reservation ki sifarish (recommendation) thi.
    *   **Parliament:** Report par Parliament mein discussion hui. Kai saalon tak MPs aur parties ise laagu karne ki maang karte rahe.
    *   **Election & Government:** 1989 Lok Sabha election mein Janata Dal ne apne manifesto mein isse laagu karne ka vaada kiya. Jeetne ke baad V.P. Singh PM bane.
    *   **Cabinet Decision:** 6 August 1990 ko Union Cabinet ne isse laagu karne ka formal decision liya.
    *   **PM's Announcement:** Agle din PM ne Parliament mein is decision ki ghoshna ki.
    *   **Implementation:** Cabinet ke decision ko Department of Personnel and Training bheja gaya. Senior officers ne O.M. draft kiya, Minister ka approval liya, aur ek officer ne sign karke use jaari kar diya.
*   **The Controversy & Resolution (विवाद और समाधान):**
    *   Is decision par desh bhar mein bahut bahas (debate) hui, protests hue, kuch violent bhi the. Kuch log iske पक्ष (favour) mein the (social inequality kam hogi), kuch विपक्ष (against) mein (equality of opportunity khatam hogi, casteism badhega).
    *   **Judiciary's Role:** Kai logon aur associations ne is order ke khilaf courts mein case file kiye. Supreme Court ne sab cases ko ek saath suna ('Indira Sawhney & others Vs Union of India case').
    *   **Supreme Court Verdict (1992):** Court ne order ko valid قرار diya, lekin kaha ki backward classes mein jo 'well-to-do' (creamy layer) hain, unhe reservation ka benefit nahi milna chahiye.
    *   **Modification:** Government ne SC ke निर्देशानुसार order ko modify kiya (8 Sept 1993 ko naya O.M. jaari hua). Is tarah yeh dispute khatam hua.

**Conceptual Flow of the O.M. Decision:**
`Mandal Commission Report -> Parliament Discussion -> Election Promise -> New Government -> Cabinet Decision -> PM informs Parliament -> O.M. Drafted (Civil Servants) -> O.M. Issued -> Public Debate/Protests -> Court Cases -> Supreme Court Verdict -> Modified O.M. Issued -> Policy Implemented`

**2. Parliament (Sansad - विधायिका)**

*   **Why do we need Parliament?** Law banane ki supreme authority, sarkar chalane walon par control, government ke paison par control, national issues par discussion aur debate ka sabse bada forum.
*   **Two Houses (दो सदन):**
    *   **Lok Sabha (House of the People):** Directly elected by people. Zyada powerful hai, especially money matters mein aur government banane mein (Council of Ministers Lok Sabha ke prati responsible hai). Agar Lok Sabha majority 'no confidence' pass kar de, toh PM sahit sabhi ministers ko resign karna padta hai. Term: 5 years (dissolve ho sakti hai). Total Members: 543 elected + 2 nominated (Anglo-Indian provision ab abolish ho gaya hai, NCERT text purana ho sakta hai is point par, but based on provided text).
    *   **Rajya Sabha (Council of States):** Mostly indirectly elected by state MLAs. States ke interests ko represent karti hai. Permanent House (dissolve nahi hoti), members ka term 6 years hota hai, one-third members har 2 saal retire hote hain. Total Members: 238 elected + 12 nominated by President. Kuch special powers hain states se related matters par.
*   **Law Making:** Ordinary laws dono Houses se pass hone zaroori hain. Difference hone par joint session hota hai (Lok Sabha ka view usually prevail karta hai due to larger number). Money Bills sirf Lok Sabha mein introduce ho sakte hain, Rajya Sabha unhe sirf 14 din delay kar sakti hai ya suggestions de sakti hai (jo Lok Sabha maan bhi sakti hai, nahi bhi).

**3. Political Executive (राजनीतिक कार्यपालिका)**

*   **Political vs. Permanent Executive:**
    *   *Political Executive:* Elected leaders (PM, Ministers). Specific period ke liye hote hain. Bade policy decisions lete hain. People ko answerable hote hain. Minister apne field ka expert ho yeh zaroori nahi, par woh final decision leta hai kyunki woh logon ka representative hai.
    *   *Permanent Executive (Civil Services):* Appointed officials (IAS, IPS, etc.). Long-term basis par hote hain. Ruling party change hone par bhi office mein rehte hain. Political executive ke under kaam karte hain, decisions implement karne mein help karte hain, technical advice dete hain.
*   **Prime Minister (PM - प्रधान मंत्री):**
    *   Head of the Government. Sabse important political institution.
    *   *Appointment:* President appoint karte hain - us leader ko jo Lok Sabha mein majority party ya coalition ka leader ho. Agar kisi ko majority nahi milti, toh President apne discretion se use appoint karte hain jo majority support haasil kar sakta hai.
    *   *Powers:* Cabinet meetings chair karte hain, different ministries ke kaam ko coordinate karte hain, ministers ko kaam distribute/redistribute karte hain, ministers ko dismiss kar sakte hain. Agar PM resign karta hai, toh puri ministry resign karti hai.
    *   *Coalition Politics:* Coalition government mein PM ki powers par constraints (rukawat) aa jaati hain, use alliance partners ko saath lekar chalna padta hai.
*   **Council of Ministers (मंत्रिपरिषद):** Body jismein sabhi ministers hote hain (usually 60-80).
    *   *Cabinet Ministers:* Top leaders, major ministries ke in-charge (approx 25). Cabinet hi Council of Ministers ke naam par decisions leti hai. Isliye ise 'Cabinet form of government' bhi kehte hain.
    *   *Ministers of State (Independent Charge):* Smaller ministries ke in-charge. Cabinet meetings mein special invitation par hi aate hain.
    *   *Ministers of State:* Cabinet Ministers ko assist karte hain.
    *   Cabinet ek team ki tarah kaam karti hai, collective responsibility hoti hai.

**4. The President (राष्ट्रपति)**

*   Head of the State. Highest formal authority.
*   *Election:* Directly logon dwara nahi chune jaate. Elected MPs aur MLAs unhe chunte hain (indirect election).
*   *Powers:* Mostly nominal/ceremonial powers (like Queen of Britain). Saare governmental activities, laws, major appointments (Chief Justice of India, Supreme/High Court Judges, Governors, Election Commissioners, etc.), international treaties President ke naam par hote hain. Supreme Commander of Defence Forces.
*   **Real vs. Nominal Power:** President apni powers Council of Ministers (headed by PM) ki advice par hi use karte hain. Woh advice ko reconsider karne ko keh sakte hain, par agar wahi advice dobara milti hai, toh unhe maanni padti hai. Bill par assent (manzoori) dete hain, chahe toh kuch der delay kar sakte hain ya reconsider ke liye bhej sakte hain, par agar Parliament dobara pass kar de, toh sign karna padta hai.
*   **Discretionary Power:** Ek important power jo woh khud use karte hain - PM ko appoint karna jab kisi party/coalition ko Lok Sabha mein clear majority na ho.

**5. The Judiciary (न्यायपालिका)**

*   **Independence (स्वतंत्रता):** Judiciary legislature ya executive ke control mein nahi hoti. Judges government ke direction par kaam nahi karte. Yeh democracy ke liye essential hai.
    *   *Appointment:* Judges (Supreme Court/High Courts) ko President appoint karte hain (PM aur Chief Justice of India ki salah par, in consultation with senior judges). Ab practice mein senior judges hi naye judges ko select karte hain, political interference kam hai.
    *   *Removal:* Judges ko hatana bahut mushkil hai (impeachment process, jo Parliament ke dono Houses mein two-thirds majority se pass ho). Aaj tak India mein aisa nahi hua hai.
*   **Structure:** Integrated judiciary - Supreme Court sabse upar, uske decisions sabhi courts par binding hote hain. Phir High Courts (states mein), District Courts, aur local level courts.
*   **Powers & Functions:**
    *   *Dispute Resolution:* Citizens ke beech, citizen aur government ke beech, state governments ke beech, Union aur State government ke beech ke vivadon ko suljhana.
    *   *Interpretation:* Constitution ko interpret karna.
    *   *Judicial Review:* Parliament ke banaye kisi law ya executive ke action ko invalid declare kar sakti hai agar woh Constitution ke against ho. Supreme Court ne kaha hai ki Parliament Constitution ke basic principles ko change nahi kar sakti.
    *   *Guardian of Fundamental Rights:* Citizens apne rights ke violation par courts ja sakte hain.
    *   *Public Interest Litigation (PIL):* Koi bhi vyakti public interest mein court ja sakta hai agar government actions se nuksan ho raha ho. Courts ne PIL ke through public interest aur human rights protect karne ke liye kai judgements diye hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ) 📊

Yeh chapter poori tarah se Indian context par focused hai. Kuch khaas Bharatiya examples hain:

*   **Mandal Commission & SEBC Reservation:** Yeh India ke social structure aur affirmative action policies ka ek bada example hai. Isne Indian politics ko kaafi prabhavit kiya ('Mandalisation of politics').
*   **Parliamentary System:** India ne UK se inspire hokar Parliamentary system apnaya hai, jahan Parliament supreme hai aur Executive (government) Parliament (specifically Lok Sabha) ke prati accountable hai.
*   **Role of President vs. PM:** India mein President (Head of State) aur PM (Head of Government) ke roles alag-alag hain, jo Presidential system (like USA) se different hai.
*   **Independent Judiciary:** Indian Constitution judiciary ki independence ensure karta hai. Supreme Court ka role (jaise Indira Sawhney case mein), Judicial Review, aur PIL jaise concepts Indian democracy ke important features hain.
*   **Coalition Governments:** Pichle kuch dashakon mein India mein coalition governments (गठबंधन सरकारें) kaafi aam ho gayi hain, jisse PM ki powers par kuch constraints aaye hain aur regional parties ka importance badha hai.
