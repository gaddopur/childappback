# [Class 9] Civics - Chapter 4: Working of Institutions (संस्थाओं का कामकाज)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein)

Democracy mein sirf chunav se kaam nahi chalta. Rulers ko kuch rules aur procedures follow karne hote hain, aur yeh kaam Institutions (संस्थाओं) ke through hota hai. Yeh chapter inhi institutions ke kaamkaaj ko samjhata hai.

📊 **Concept Hierarchy:**

1.  **Rajneetik Sansthaon ki Zaroorat (Need for Political Institutions):**
    *   Kyun rules, regulations, aur institutions zaroori hain? (Why are rules, regulations, and institutions necessary?)
    *   Decisions mein consultation (salaah-mashvira) ka importance.
    *   Stability aur continuity provide karna. (Providing stability and continuity).

2.  **Major Policy Decision Kaise Liya Jaata Hai? (How is a Major Policy Decision Taken?)**
    *   Example: Mandal Commission aur Office Memorandum (O.M.) for SEBC reservations.
    *   Decision-making process mein alag-alag functionaries ka role.

3.  **Mukhya Sansthayein (Major Institutions):**
    *   **Vidhayika (Legislature) - Parliament (Sansad):**
        *   Role & Functions (Bhumika aur Karya): Law-making (kanoon banana), Executive par control, Financial control (vittiya niyantran), Discussion/Debate ka forum.
        *   Do Sadan (Two Houses): Lok Sabha (House of the People) aur Rajya Sabha (Council of States) - Unki powers aur comparison.
    *   **Karyapalika (Executive):**
        *   Do Tarah ki Executive (Two types): Political Executive (Chune hue neta - Elected leaders like Ministers) aur Permanent Executive (Civil Servants/Bureaucrats - Sthayi karmachari).
        *   Pradhan Mantri (Prime Minister - PM) aur Mantri Parishad (Council of Ministers - CoM): Appointment (Niyukti), Powers (Shaktiyan), Cabinet ka role.
        *   Rashtrapati (President): Head of State (Rajya ka pramukh), Election (Chunav), Powers (Nominal vs. Real), Discretionary powers (Vivekadhin shaktiyan).
    *   **Nyaypalika (Judiciary):**
        *   Role & Functions: Dispute resolution (vivaad suljhana), Constitution ki vyakhya karna (interpreting the Constitution), Fundamental Rights ki raksha karna (protecting FRs), Judicial Review (Nyayik samiksha).
        *   Structure (Sanrachna): Integrated Judiciary (Supreme Court, High Courts, District Courts).
        *   Independence (Swatantrata): Kyun zaroori hai aur kaise ensure ki jaati hai (Why necessary and how ensured - appointment, removal).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

**1. Decision Making Process - The Mandal Commission Example:**

Ek bada sarkari faisla (government decision) kaise liya jaata hai, iska best example hai Mandal Commission ki report par based Office Memorandum (O.M.).

*   **Background:** 1979 mein Second Backward Classes Commission (Mandal Commission) bani. Report 1980 mein aayi, jisme SEBCs (Socially and Educationally Backward Classes) ke liye 27% government jobs mein reservation ki sifarish thi.
*   **Political Promise:** 1989 ke election mein Janata Dal ne isse implement karne ka vaada kiya.
*   **Cabinet Decision:** Janata Dal ki sarkar bani, V.P. Singh PM bane. August 1990 mein Union Cabinet ne reservation implement karne ka formal decision liya.
*   **PM's Announcement:** PM ne Parliament ke dono Houses mein is decision ki ghoshna ki.
*   **Implementation (O.M.):** Decision ko Department of Personnel and Training bheja gaya. Officers ne O.M. draft kiya, Minister ka approval liya, aur ek Joint Secretary ne 13 August 1990 ko O.M. No. 36012/31/90-Est (SCT) sign kiya.
*   **Controversy & Protests:** Is decision par desh bhar mein behas (debate) aur protests hue. Kuch log iske favour mein the (samajik nyay ke liye - for social justice), kuch log khilaaf (equality of opportunity ke khilaaf - against equal opportunity).
*   **Judicial Intervention:** Mamla Supreme Court pahuncha ('Indira Sawhney & others Vs Union of India case'). 1992 mein SC ne order ko valid قرار diya, lekin 'creamy layer' (well-to-do persons among backward classes) ko reservation se bahar rakhne ko kaha.
*   **Final Outcome:** Sarkar ne SC ke निर्देशानुसार order modify kiya (8 Sept 1993), aur tab se yeh policy lagu hai.

📈 **Diagram Idea:** Flowchart showing the journey of the Mandal reservation decision from Commission formation to final implementation after SC verdict.

**2. Parliament (Sansad) - Why We Need It?**

Parliament logon ki taraf se supreme political authority exercise karti hai. Iske mukhya kaam hain:

*   **Kanoon Banana (Law Making):** Desh ke liye naye kanoon banana, purane badalna ya khatm karna. Isliye ise Legislature (Vidhayika) bhi kehte hain.
*   **Sarkar par Control (Control over Executive):** Sarkar tabhi tak kaam kar sakti hai jab tak use Lok Sabha mein majority support ho. Parliament questions pooch sakti hai, debates kar sakti hai.
*   **Paise par Control (Financial Control):** Sarkar ka saara paisa (public money) Parliament ki manzoori (sanction) se hi kharch hota hai. Budget Parliament pass karti hai.
*   **Behas ka Manch (Forum for Discussion):** National issues aur policies par debate aur discussion ka sabse bada platform hai.

**Lok Sabha vs. Rajya Sabha:**

| Feature           | Lok Sabha (House of the People)             | Rajya Sabha (Council of States)                 |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------- |
| **Election**      | Directly elected by people (Logon dwara seedhe chune gaye) | Indirectly elected by state MLAs (Rajyon ke MLAs dwara chune gaye) + 12 Nominated by President |
| **Term**          | 5 years (can be dissolved earlier)          | Permanent House (Members have 6-year term, 1/3 retire every 2 years) |
| **Total Members** | Max 552 (Currently 543 elected + 2 nominated Anglo-Indians provision removed) | Max 250 (Currently 245: 233 elected + 12 nominated) |
| **Power**         | **More Powerful:** Money Bills sirf yahan introduce hote hain. Govt formation/removal mein decisive role. Joint session mein larger number prevails. | **Less Powerful (generally):** Ordinary bills mein equal power (but LS view prevails in deadlock). Money bills ko sirf 14 din delay kar sakti hai ya suggestions de sakti hai. Special powers regarding states' list subjects. |

📈 **Diagram Idea:** Venn Diagram showing unique and shared powers of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.

**3. Executive (Karyapalika) - Implementing Laws**

Executive ka kaam hai Parliament dwara banaye gaye laws aur policies ko लागू karna (implement karna).

*   **Political Executive:** Elected leaders (PM, Ministers). Short term ke liye hote hain, policy decisions lete hain. Log inke kaam ke liye inhe accountable thehrate hain. *Ye log policy ka overall framework aur objectives decide karte hain.*
*   **Permanent Executive (Civil Services/Bureaucracy):** Appointed officials (IAS, IPS, etc.). Long term ke liye hote hain, party badalne par bhi rehte hain. Political executive ko assist karte hain, day-to-day administration dekhte hain. *Ye experts hote hain aur ministers ko technical advice dete hain.*

**Minister zyada powerful kyun? (Why is the Minister more powerful?)**
Democracy mein logon ki ichha (will of the people) sabse upar hai. Minister logon ka chuna hua representative hota hai, isliye final decisions wahi leta hai aur apne decisions ke liye logon ko jawabdeh (answerable) hota hai. Civil servants expert advice dete hain, par antim faisla minister ka hota hai.

**Pradhan Mantri (PM) aur Mantri Parishad (Council of Ministers - CoM):**

*   **PM:** Head of the Government. President us leader ko PM appoint karte hain jise Lok Sabha mein majority support ho (ya jo majority juta sake). PM Cabinet meetings chair karte hain, ministries ke beech coordination karte hain, ministers ko kaam distribute/redistribute karte hain, aur dismiss bhi kar sakte hain. PM ke resign karne par poori ministry resign karti hai. *Coalition politics (gathbandhan ki rajneeti) mein PM ki powers par kuch constraints aa jate hain.*
*   **Council of Ministers (Mantri Parishad):** Body jisme sabhi ministers hote hain (60-80).
    *   **Cabinet Ministers:** Top leaders, major ministries ke in-charge (approx 25). Asli decision-making Cabinet meetings mein hoti hai. Ise 'inner ring' kehte hain.
    *   **Ministers of State (Independent Charge):** Smaller ministries ke in-charge. Cabinet meetings mein special invitation par aate hain.
    *   **Ministers of State:** Cabinet Ministers ko assist karte hain.
*   **Collective Responsibility:** Poori Council of Ministers ek team ki tarah kaam karti hai. Har minister ko Cabinet ke har decision ko support karna hota hai, bhale hi woh personally agree na kare.

📈 **Diagram Idea:** Organogram showing President -> PM -> Council of Ministers (Cabinet, MoS-Ind, MoS) -> Civil Servants.

**Rashtrapati (President):**

*   **Head of the State:** Desh ka formal head, par powers mostly nominal (naamमात्र ki) hain, like the Queen of Britain. Functions largely ceremonial hain.
*   **Election:** Indirectly elected by MPs and MLAs.
*   **Powers:** Sabhi governmental activities President ke naam par hoti hain (laws, appointments - CJI, Judges, Governors, ECs, Ambassadors; treaties). Supreme Commander of Defence Forces.
    *   **Real Power?** Lekin yeh sab powers President, Council of Ministers (headed by PM) ki advice par hi exercise karte hain. President advice ko reconsider karne ko keh sakte hain, par agar wahi advice dobara mile toh manna padega. Bill ko kuch time delay kar sakte hain ya Parliament ko reconsider karne bhej sakte hain, par dobara pass hone par assent dena padega.
    *   **Discretionary Power:** Ek important power jo President apne विवेक (discretion) se use karte hain - Jab kisi party ya coalition ko Lok Sabha mein clear majority na mile, tab President us leader ko PM appoint karte hain jo unki opinion mein majority support haasil kar sakta hai.

**4. Judiciary (Nyaypalika) - Guardian of the Constitution**

Judiciary ka kaam hai kanoon ka shasan (rule of law) ensure karna aur vivadon ko suljhana.

*   **Integrated Judiciary:** Ek hierarchical structure hai - Supreme Court (poore desh ke liye), High Courts (states ke liye), District Courts, aur lower courts. Supreme Court sabse upar hai aur uske decisions sabhi courts par binding hote hain.
*   **Dispute Resolution:** Citizens ke beech, citizen aur government ke beech, state governments ke beech, aur Union aur State government ke beech ke disputes ko solve karti hai.
*   **Independence (Swatantrata):** Judiciary, Legislature aur Executive se independent hai. Judges bina kisi pressure ke kaam karte hain.
    *   *Kaise Ensure Hoti Hai?* Judges ki appointment mein political interference kam hai (senior judges ka role important). Unhe hatana (impeachment) bahut mushkil hai (needs special majority in both Houses).
*   **Powers:**
    *   **Interpreting the Constitution:** Samvidhan ki vyakhya karne ka adhikar.
    *   **Judicial Review (Nyayik Samiksha):** Parliament ya Executive ke kisi bhi kanoon ya action ko invalid declare kar sakti hai agar woh Constitution ke khilaaf ho. SC ne kaha hai ki Parliament Constitution ke basic structure ko change nahi kar sakti.
    *   **Guardian of Fundamental Rights:** Agar kisi citizen ke Fundamental Rights ka violation hota hai, toh woh courts ja sakta hai.
    *   **Public Interest Litigation (PIL - Janhit Yachika):** Koi bhi vyakti public interest mein court ja sakta hai agar government actions se public interest hurt ho raha ho. Courts ne PILs ke through public interest aur human rights protect karne ke liye important decisions diye hain.

*   **Judicial Activism & PIL:** India mein Judiciary ne Public Interest Litigation (PIL) ke through social justice aur human rights ke liye important interventions kiye hain (e.g., environmental protection, rights of under-trials). Yeh Indian democracy ka ek unique feature hai.
