# [Class 9] Civics - Chapter 404: Working of Institutions (Sansthanon ki Kaamkaj)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein)

Yeh chapter samjhata hai ki democracy mein sarkar kaise kaam karti hai, khaas kar ke important decisions kaise liye jaate hain aur lagu kiye jaate hain. Hum teen main institutions par focus karenge:

1.  **Legislature (Vidhayika) 🏛️ - Yani Parliament (Sansad)**
    *   Kaam: Kanoon banana (Law-making), Sarkar par control rakhna (Controlling the government), Paiso ka hisaab rakhna (Controlling finances), Desh ke muddo par behes karna (Forum for discussion).
    *   Do Hisse (Two Houses):
        *   **Lok Sabha (House of the People):** Log direct chunte hain. Zyada powerful hai.
        *   **Rajya Sabha (Council of States):** Indirectly chune jaate hain, States ko represent karti hai.
2.  **Executive (Karyapalika) 🧑‍💼 - Yani Sarkar chalane wale**
    *   Kaam: Policy decisions lena (Taking policy decisions), Kanoono ko lagu karna (Implementing laws).
    *   Do Tarah ke Hote Hain (Two Types):
        *   **Political Executive (Rajnetik Karyapalika):** Neta log jo election jeet kar aate hain (PM, Ministers). Bade faisle lete hain. Janta ko jawabdeh hote hain.
        *   **Permanent Executive (Sthayi Karyapalika):** Sarkari officers (Civil Servants/Bureaucrats). Lambe time ke liye appoint hote hain. Ministers ki help karte hain kaam karne mein, expertise dete hain.
3.  **Judiciary (Nyayapalika) ⚖️ - Yani Adalat System**
    *   Kaam: Vivadon ko suljhana (Resolving disputes - citizens ke beech, citizen aur sarkar ke beech, state governments ke beech), Samvidhan ki raksha karna (Protecting the Constitution), Fundamental Rights ko bachana (Guarding Fundamental Rights).
    *   Structure: Supreme Court (Sabse upar), High Courts (States mein), District Courts.
    *   Independence (Swatantrata): Judiciary ko Legislature aur Executive se independent hona chahiye taaki woh bina pressure ke faisle de sake.

📊 **Concept Hierarchy:**
Sarkar (Government) -> Institutions (Sansthan)
    -> Legislature (Parliament: Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha + President) -> Makes Laws
    -> Executive (Political: PM + Council of Ministers; Permanent: Civil Servants) -> Implements Laws
    -> Judiciary (Supreme Court, High Courts, etc.) -> Interprets Laws & Resolves Disputes

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

**1. Ek Bada Policy Decision Kaise Liya Jata Hai? (Example: Mandal Commission Reservation Order)**

*   **The Order (Sarkari Aadesh):** 1990 mein ek Office Memorandum (O.M.) aaya. Isne Central Government jobs mein 27% reservation Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBCs) ke liye fix kar diya. Yeh SC/ST reservation ke alawa tha.
*   **Decision Makers (Faisla Lene Wale):** Yeh order sign karne wale officer ne nahi, balki political executive ne liya tha.
    *   **Mandal Commission:** 1979 mein bani, 1980 mein report di, jisme 27% reservation suggest kiya.
    *   **Political Promise:** Janata Dal ne 1989 election mein isko lagu karne ka vaada kiya.
    *   **Government Action:** V.P. Singh PM bane. President ne Parliament mein intention bataya. Union Cabinet ne formal decision liya (Aug 6, 1990). PM ne Parliament ko inform kiya. Order issue hua (Aug 13, 1990).
*   **Controversy & Resolution (Vivad aur Samadhan):** Bahut protests hue. Mamla Supreme Court gaya ('Indira Sawhney case'). 1992 mein SC ne order ko valid kaha, par 'creamy layer' (backward classes ke well-to-do log) ko reservation se bahar rakhne ko kaha. Sarkar ne order modify kiya.
*   **Isse Kya Seekha?** Bade decisions mein kai institutions involved hote hain - Commission, Political Parties, President, Cabinet, PM, Parliament (discussion hui thi), Civil Servants (order draft kiya), aur Judiciary (dispute resolve kiya).

📈 **Decision Flow Diagram (Mandal Example):**
[Commission Report] -> [Political Promise] -> [Govt Formation] -> [Cabinet Decision] -> [Parliament Informed] -> [Order Issued (O.M.)] -> [Public Protests] -> [Court Case (Indira Sawhney)] -> [Supreme Court Verdict (Modified)] -> [Final Implementation]

**2. Parliament (Sansad) - Kanoon Banane ki Jagah**

*   **Kyun Zaroori Hai?** Final authority hai kanoon banane ki, badalne ki ya hatane ki. Sarkar ko control karti hai (No-confidence motion se hata sakti hai). Sarkar ke kharchon (budget) ko approve karti hai. Public issues par discussion ka sabse bada manch hai.
*   **Do Sadan (Two Houses): Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha**

    | Feature          | Lok Sabha                     | Rajya Sabha                      |
    | :--------------- | :---------------------------- | :------------------------------- |
    | **Election**     | Directly by people            | Indirectly by MLAs             |
    | **Members**      | Max 552 (Currently 543 elected) | Max 250 (Currently 245)        |
    | **Term**         | 5 years (can be dissolved)    | Permanent (Members 6 yrs term) |
    | **Money Bills**  | More Power (RS can only delay) | Less Power                     |
    | **Govt Control** | Controls Council of Ministers | No direct control              |
    | **Joint Session**| Usually prevails (more members)| Less influence                 |

*   **Lok Sabha zyada powerful kyun hai?** Money matters mein, Sarkar banane/girane mein, aur joint session mein Lok Sabha ki baat zyada maani jaati hai.

**3. Executive (Karyapalika) - Faisle Lagu Karne Wale**

*   **Political vs Permanent Executive:**
    *   **Political (Neta):** PM, Ministers. Janta dwara chune gaye. Bade policy decisions lete hain. Janta ko jawabdeh hain. Unko subject expert hona zaroori nahi, woh experts (civil servants) ki salah lete hain. Final decision minister ka hota hai kyunki woh logo ka representative hai.
    *   **Permanent (Officers):** Civil Servants (IAS, IPS etc.). Lambe time ke liye appointed. Rules ke hisaab se kaam karte hain. Ministers ko administration chalane mein madad karte hain, technical advice dete hain. Sarkar badalne par bhi yeh rehte hain.
*   **Prime Minister (Pradhan Mantri - PM):** Head of the Government. Sabse important political institution.
    *   **Appointment:** President us leader ko PM appoint karte hain jiske paas Lok Sabha mein majority ho (ya coalition ki majority ho).
    *   **Powers:** Cabinet meetings head karna, Ministries ke beech coordination, Ministers ko kaam distribute karna/dismiss karna, Govt ka main face. Agar PM resign kare toh poori ministry resign karti hai. Coalition politics mein PM ki power thodi kam ho sakti hai.
*   **Council of Ministers (Mantriparishad):** PM ki team.
    *   **Cabinet Ministers:** Top leaders, major ministries ke head. Core group jo CAbinet meetings mein decision leta hai (approx 25).
    *   **Ministers of State (Independent Charge):** Chhote ministries ke head.
    *   **Ministers of State:** Cabinet Ministers ko assist karte hain.
    *   **Collective Responsibility:** Poori Council of Ministers ek team ki tarah kaam karti hai. Ek minister ka decision sabka decision mana jata hai.
*   **President (Rashtrapati):** Head of the State.
    *   **Powers:** Nominal (Naam ke). Saare kaam President ke naam par hote hain (laws, appointments, treaties). Supreme Commander of Defence Forces.
    *   **Role:** Acts on the advice of Council of Ministers (PM + Cabinet). Bill ko ek baar reconsider karne ke liye bhej sakte hain, par dobara pass hone par sign karna padta hai.
    *   **Discretionary Power:** Jab kisi party/coalition ko Lok Sabha mein clear majority na mile, tab President apne विवेक (discretion) se PM appoint karte hain jo majority prove kar sake.

**4. Judiciary (Nyayapalika) - Insaf ka Mandir**

*   **Role:** Disputes resolve karna, Constitution aur Fundamental Rights ko protect karna.
*   **Independence (Swatantrata):** Bahut zaroori hai taaki fairness bani rahe. Judges ki appointment (President dwara, CJI aur senior judges ki salah par - Collegium system) aur removal (Impeachment - bahut difficult process) se independence ensure hoti hai.
*   **Powers:**
    *   **Judicial Review:** Parliament ke banaye kanoon ya Executive ke action ko check kar sakti hai ki woh Constitution ke khilaf toh nahi. Agar hai, toh usey invalid declare kar sakti hai.
    *   **Guardian of Fundamental Rights:** Agar kisi citizen ke Fundamental Rights violate hote hain, woh courts ja sakta hai.
    *   **Public Interest Litigation (PIL):** Koi bhi person public interest ke mudde par court ja sakta hai. Isse courts ne environment, human rights jaise issues par important decisions diye hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Sandarbh)

*   **National Economic/Social Data Link 📊:** Sarkari institutions jo faisle lete hain (jaise reservation, budget allocation, welfare schemes), unka asar desh ke social aur economic data mein dikhta hai.
    *   **Example 1 (Reservation Impact):** Mandal Commission ke baad, Central Government jobs mein OBCs ka representation badha hai. Iska data Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) ya National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC) ki reports mein milta hai. Yeh data batata hai ki policy kitni effective rahi.
    *   **Example 2 (Economic Policies):** Parliament har saal Budget pass karti hai. Ismein education, health, defence, infrastructure par kitna paisa kharch hoga, yeh decide hota hai. Iska asar desh ki GDP growth, employment, aur poverty levels par padta hai, jiska data National Statistical Office (NSO) surveys aur Economic Survey mein aata hai.
    *   **Example 3 (Judiciary & Social Justice):** Supreme Court ke kai faislon (jaise Right to Food par PILs, environmental protection orders) ne sarkari policies ko shape kiya hai. Inka impact related ministry ki performance reports (jaise Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution ya Ministry of Environment) aur social indicators mein dekha ja sakta hai. Yeh data batata hai ki institutions janta ke liye kaise kaam kar rahe hain.
