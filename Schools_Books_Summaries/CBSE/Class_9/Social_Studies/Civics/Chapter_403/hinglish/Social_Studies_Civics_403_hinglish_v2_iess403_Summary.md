# [Class 9] Nagrik Shastra (Civics) - Adhyay 3: Chunavi Rajneeti (Electoral Politics)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Chunav Kyon Zaroori Hain? (Why Elections?)**
    *   Loktantra (Democracy) mein logon ka shasan (Rule of the people).
    *   Direct democracy bade deshon mein possible nahin.
    *   Isliye, log apne pratinidhi (representatives) chunte hain.
    *   Chunav ek mechanism hai jisse log representatives ko chunte hain aur badal bhi sakte hain.
    *   Voters chunav mein important choices karte hain:
        *   Kaun kanoon (laws) banayega?
        *   Kaun sarkar (government) banayega aur bade faisle (decisions) lega?
        *   Kis party ki neetiyan (policies) sarkar aur kanoon banane mein guide karengi?

2.  **Ek Chunav ko Loktantrik (Democratic) Kya Banata Hai?**
    *   Minimum Conditions:
        *   **Universal Adult Franchise (Sarvabhaumik Vayask Matadhikar):** Har adult citizen ko vote ka adhikar, har vote ki value barabar (One vote, one value).
        *   **Choice:** Parties aur candidates ko चुनाव ladne ki azadi ho, voters ke paas asli vikalp (real choice) ho.
        *   **Regular Intervals:** Chunav niyamit antral (regular intervals) par hone chahiye (Jaise India mein har 5 saal).
        *   **People's Preference:** Jo candidate logon ko pasand ho, wahi jeetna chahiye.
        *   **Free and Fair Manner:** Chunav swatantra (free) aur nishpaksh (fair) tareeke se hone chahiye, jahan log apni marzi se vote de sakein.

3.  **Rajneetik Pratiyogita (Political Competition)**
    *   **Fayde (Pros):** Parties ko logon ki behtari ke liye kaam karne ka incentive milta hai; agar kaam na karein toh agle chunav mein haarne ka dar rehta hai. Logon ki issues uthane par majboor karta hai.
    *   **Nuksan (Cons):** Disunity aur factionalism (gutbaazi) paida kar sakta hai; 'dirty tricks' ka istemal ho sakta hai; long-term policies banane mein rukawat aa sakti hai.

4.  **Bharat ki Chunav Pranali (India's Election System)**
    *   **Chunav Kshetra (Electoral Constituencies):**
        *   Desh ko alag-alag areas mein baanta jaata hai (Lok Sabha ke liye 543 constituencies).
        *   Har constituency se ek representative chuna jaata hai (MP/MLA).
        *   Har constituency mein lagbhag (roughly) equal population hoti hai.
        *   Local bodies (Panchayat/Municipal) ke liye bhi wards hote hain.
    *   **Aarakshit Nirvachan Kshetra (Reserved Constituencies):**
        *   Weaker sections (SC/ST) ko representation dene ke liye.
        *   SC/ST reserved seat se sirf usi community ka vyakti chunav lad sakta hai.
        *   Lok Sabha mein SC ke liye 84, ST ke liye 47 seats reserved hain (as per text, Jan 2019 data).
        *   Local bodies mein OBC aur Mahilaon (Women) ke liye bhi reservation hai (Mahilaon ke liye 1/3rd seats). *Note: Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, 2023 Parliament/Assemblies mein mahila reservation ki baat karta hai.*
    *   **Matdata Suchi (Voters' List / Electoral Roll):**
        *   Eligible voters ki list chunav se pehle taiyar ki jaati hai.
        *   India mein 18 saal ya usse upar ke sabhi citizens ko vote ka adhikar hai (Universal Adult Franchise).
        *   List har 5 saal mein revise hoti hai.
        *   Election Photo Identity Card (EPIC) introduce kiya gaya hai, par voting ke liye compulsory nahin (other IDs bhi chalte hain).
    *   **Umeedwaron ka Naamankan (Nomination of Candidates):**
        *   Koi bhi voter (jo 25 saal ka ho) candidate ban sakta hai (kuch exceptions ke saath).
        *   Parties apne candidates nominate karti hain ('ticket' deti hain).
        *   Har candidate ko nomination form bharna hota hai aur security deposit dena hota hai.
        *   Supreme Court ke direction par ab candidates ko apni assets, liabilities, criminal cases, aur educational qualifications declare karni padti hain.
    *   **Chunav Abhiyan (Election Campaign):**
        *   Candidates aur parties voters se contact karte hain, mudde uthate hain.
        *   Campaign period usually final list aur polling date ke beech 2 hafte ka hota hai.
        *   **Regulations:** Koi party/candidate voters ko rishwat (bribe) ya dhamki nahin de sakta; caste/religion ke naam par vote nahin maang sakta; government resources ka use nahin kar sakta; spending limits (Lok Sabha `₹ 25 lakh`, Assembly `₹ 10 lakh` - *as per text*) cross nahin kar sakta.
        *   **Model Code of Conduct (Aadarsh Aachar Sanhita):** Parties dwara agreed guidelines jise Election Commission enforce karta hai (e.g., dharmik sthalon ka use na karna, government vehicles ka use na karna, announcements na karna).
    *   **Matdan aur Matganana (Polling and Counting):**
        *   Voters polling booth par jaakar vote 'cast' karte hain.
        *   Pehle Ballot Paper use hote the, ab Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) use hoti hain.
        *   Polling ke baad EVMs seal hokar secure jagah le jaayi jaati hain.
        *   Fixed date par sabhi candidates ke agents ke saamne votes count hote hain.
        *   Sabse zyada vote paane wala candidate jeet jaata hai (declared elected).

5.  **Bharat mein Chunav Loktantrik Kyon Hain? (Why Indian Elections are Democratic?)**
    *   **Swatantra Chunav Aayog (Independent Election Commission - ECI):**
        *   Bahut powerful aur independent body hai (like judiciary).
        *   CEC ko President appoint karte hain, par hatana mushkil hai.
        *   EC chunav conduct karne se lekar results declare karne tak sab decisions leta hai.
        *   Code of Conduct लागू karta hai, violate karne walon ko punish karta hai.
        *   Government ko guidelines follow karne ka order de sakta hai, officials transfer kar sakta hai.
        *   EC ke paas wide powers hain.
    *   **Jan Bhagidari (Popular Participation):**
        *   India mein voter turnout (vote daalne walon ka percentage) stable raha hai ya badha hai (unlike many Western countries).
        *   Gareeb, illiterate, underprivileged log zyada proportion mein vote karte hain.
        *   Aam log elections ko importance dete hain, unhe lagta hai unke vote se farak padta hai.
        *   Log election-related activities mein participate karte hain.
    *   **Chunavi Parinamon ki Sweekriti (Acceptance of Election Outcome):**
        *   Haarne wali party usually results ko 'people's verdict' maan kar accept kar leti hai.
        *   Ruling parties (satta mein jo party hai) aksar chunav haarti hain (national aur state level dono par).
        *   Lagbhag aadhe sitting MPs/MLAs chunav haar jaate hain.

6.  **Swatantra aur Nishpaksh Chunav ki Chunautiyan (Challenges to Free and Fair Elections)**
    *   **Money Power:** Ameer candidates aur badi parties ko unfair advantage milta hai.
    *   **Criminal Connections:** Kuch candidates criminal background ke saath chunav jeet jaate hain ya ticket pa lete hain.
    *   **Dynastic Succession (Parivarvaad):** Kuch families political parties ko dominate karti hain, relatives ko tickets milte hain.
    *   **Lack of Meaningful Choice:** Kabhi kabhi major parties ki policies aur practices mein zyada antar nahin hota.
    *   **Disadvantage for Smaller Parties:** Chhoti parties aur independent candidates ko bade parties ke muqable nuksan hota hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Representative Democracy ki Need:** Hum elections ke through apne representatives chunte hain jo hamari taraf se decisions lete hain. Example: Haryana 1987 election mein Devi Lal ki Lok Dal party ne Congress ko harakar sarkar banayi, jisse policy change hui (loans maaf kiye gaye).
*   **Democratic Elections ki Shartein:** Yaad rakhein 5 conditions - Universal Adult Franchise, Real Choice, Regular Intervals, People's Preference, Free & Fair Process. Inke bina election democratic nahin kehla sakta.
*   **India ka System:** Constituencies (like your own Lok Sabha/Vidhan Sabha area), Reserved Constituencies (SC/ST/OBC/Women ke liye representation ensure karna), Voters' List (aapka naam hai ya nahin check karna zaroori hai!), Nomination (candidates ki details jaanna), Campaign (slogans jaise 'Garibi Hatao', Model Code of Conduct), Polling (EVMs ka use), Counting (sabse zyada vote = winner).
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Apne Lok Sabha constituency ka map dekhein (NCERT mein Gulbarga ka example hai) aur usmein kitni Vidhan Sabha constituencies hain, yeh samjhein.
*   **ECI ka Role:** Election Commission ek referee ki tarah hai jo ensure karta hai ki chunav fair ho. Iski independence aur powers Indian democracy ke liye bahut important hain.
*   **Participation Matters:** India mein logon ka elections mein vishwas aur participation (khaas kar gareeb aur underprivileged sections ka) democracy ki strength dikhata hai.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* NCERT ke Voter Turnout charts (India vs UK/US, different social groups in India) ko analyze karein.
*   **Challenges ko Pehchanein:** Election system perfect nahin hai. Money power, criminalization, parivarvaad jaise issues real hain aur in par reforms ki zaroorat hai.
