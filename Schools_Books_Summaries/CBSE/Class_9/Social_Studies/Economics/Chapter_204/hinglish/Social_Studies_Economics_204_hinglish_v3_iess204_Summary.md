# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 204

(Based on NCERT Economics Class 9, Chapter 4: Food Security in India)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Food Security (खाद्य सुरक्षा):**
    *   Matlab: Sabhi logon ke liye, har samay, bhojan ki availability (उपलब्धता), accessibility (पहुंच), aur affordability (सामर्थ्य) ka hona.
    *   **Dimensions (आयाम):**
        *   **Availability (उपलब्धता):** Desh mein food production (अनाज उत्पादन), imports (आयात), aur sarkari godowns mein pichle saal ka stock.
        *   **Accessibility (पहुंच):** Bhojan har vyakti ki pahunch mein ho. Koi rukavat (barrier) na ho.
        *   **Affordability (सामर्थ्य):** Vyakti ke paas paryapt paise (enough money) ho ki woh sufficient (पर्याप्त), safe (सुरक्षित), aur nutritious (पौष्टिक) bhojan khareed sake.

2.  **Need for Food Security (खाद्य सुरक्षा की ज़रुरत):**
    *   Gareeb parivar (poor households) hamesha food insecure ho sakte hain.
    *   Natural Calamities (प्राकृतिक आपदाएं) jaise earthquake (भूकंप), drought (सूखा), flood (बाढ़), tsunami, famine (अकाल) ke time par production kam ho jaata hai, prices badh jaate hain, aur log bhukhmari (starvation) ka shikar ho sakte hain. (Example: Bengal Famine 1943).
    *   COVID-19 jaise pandemics bhi food security ko prabhavit karte hain.

3.  **Food-Insecure Groups (खाद्य-असुरक्षित समूह):**
    *   **Rural Areas (ग्रामीण क्षेत्र):** Landless labourers (भूमिहीन मजदूर), small farmers (छोटे किसान), traditional artisans (पारंपरिक कारीगर), petty self-employed workers, destitutes (निराश्रित), beggars (भिखारी). (Example: Ramu - seasonal unemployment).
    *   **Urban Areas (शहरी क्षेत्र):** Families jinke members ill-paid occupations (कम वेतन वाले काम) aur casual labour market (अनियमित मजदूरी) mein kaam karte hain. (Example: Ahmad - rickshaw puller).
    *   **Social Composition (सामाजिक संरचना):** SCs, STs, kuch OBC sections (jinke paas kam zameen ya low productivity hai).
    *   **Other Groups:** Natural disaster victims, migrants (प्रवासी), women (especially pregnant and nursing mothers - गर्भवती और स्तनपान कराने वाली महिलाएं), children under 5 years.
    *   **Regions (क्षेत्र):** Economically backward states (आर्थिक रूप से पिछड़े राज्य) jaise Uttar Pradesh (eastern/south-eastern), Bihar, Jharkhand, Orissa, West Bengal, Chhattisgarh, parts of MP & Maharashtra.

4.  **Hunger (भुखमरी):**
    *   **Chronic Hunger (दीर्घकालिक भुखमरी):** Lagatar kam quantity ya quality wala bhojan milna, usually due to very low income.
    *   **Seasonal Hunger (मौसमी भुखमरी):** Saal ke kuch specific periods mein kaam na milne ke karan bhojan ki kami hona (related to agriculture cycles or casual labour like construction).

5.  **India's Strategy (भारत की रणनीति):**
    *   **Self-Sufficiency (आत्मनिर्भरता):** Green Revolution (हरित क्रांति) ke through foodgrain production (especially wheat and rice) mein badhotri.
    *   **Food Security System (खाद्य सुरक्षा प्रणाली):**
        *   **Buffer Stock (बफर स्टॉक):** Sarkar dwara FCI (Food Corporation of India - भारतीय खाद्य निगम) ke through gehun (wheat) aur chawal (rice) ka stock jama karna. Farmers se MSP (Minimum Support Price - न्यूनतम समर्थन मूल्य) par khareeda jaata hai. Purpose: Deficit areas mein supply karna, calamity ke time use karna.
        *   **Public Distribution System (PDS - सार्वजनिक वितरण प्रणाली):** Sarkari regulated ration shops (Fair Price Shops - उचित मूल्य की दुकानें) ke through gareeb logon ko subsidized rates (कम कीमत) par anaaj, chini, kerosene baantna.

6.  **Evolution of PDS & Key Schemes (PDS का विकास और मुख्य योजनाएं):**
    *   **Early PDS:** Universal (sabke liye).
    *   **RPDS (1992):** Remote aur backward areas ke liye.
    *   **TPDS (1997):** Targeted - Poor aur Non-poor ke liye alag price policy.
    *   **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana - 2000):** Poorest of the poor ke liye highly subsidized foodgrains (₹2/kg wheat, ₹3/kg rice).
    *   **APS (Annapurna Scheme - 2000):** Indigent senior citizens ke liye free foodgrains.
    *   **NFSA (National Food Security Act - राष्ट्रीय खाद्य सुरक्षा अधिनियम, 2013):** Legal right to food. 75% rural & 50% urban population covered. Priority households ko 5kg foodgrains per person per month at subsidized rates (Rice ₹3/kg, Wheat ₹2/kg, Coarse grains ₹1/kg).

7.  **Challenges in Food Security System (खाद्य सुरक्षा प्रणाली में चुनौतियां):**
    *   **FCI Godowns:** Overflowing stocks, wastage (अनाज का सड़ना, चूहे खाना), high storage & carrying costs.
    *   **MSP Issues:** Pressure from surplus states (Punjab, Haryana), shift away from coarse grains, environmental degradation (water level depletion).
    *   **PDS Malpractices:** Ration dealers diverting grains to open market, selling poor quality grains, irregular shop opening.
    *   **APL (Above Poverty Line) Families:** Unke liye PDS prices market price ke kareeb, isliye kam incentive.

8.  **Role of Cooperatives (सहकारी समितियों की भूमिका):**
    *   Food security mein important role, especially in Southern/Western India.
    *   Low priced goods bechne ke liye shops kholna. (Example: Tamil Nadu mein 94% Fair Price Shops cooperatives chalate hain).
    *   **Examples:** Mother Dairy (Delhi - milk, vegetables), Amul (Gujarat - milk products, White Revolution), ADS Grain Banks (Maharashtra).

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख) 📈

1.  **Food Security ke Teen Stambh (Pillars):**
    *   **Availability (उपलब्धता):** Desh mein kitna anaaj paida hota hai, kitna import hota hai, aur kitna sarkari stock hai. Green Revolution ne isme madad ki.
    *   **Accessibility (पहुंच):** Kya har insaan tak anaaj pahunch sakta hai? Ration shops (PDS) iska ek zariya hain. Geographic location, social barriers isme rukavat daal sakte hain.
    *   **Affordability (सामर्थ्य):** Kya logon ke paas anaaj khareedne ke liye paise hain? Gareebi (Poverty) iska sabse bada challenge hai. Sarkari subsidies (like in PDS, NFSA) affordability badhati hain.
    *   `[Diagram Idea: Ek triangle jiske teen kone Availability, Accessibility, Affordability hain, aur beech mein Food Security likha ho]`

2.  **Calamities aur Food Security ka Rishta:**
    *   Jab koi natural calamity (like drought) aati hai -> Foodgrain production ghat jaata hai -> Affected areas mein shortage ho jaati hai -> Prices badh jaate hain -> Gareeb log khareed nahi paate -> Starvation (भुखमरी) ho sakti hai -> Agar yeh bade scale par ho toh Famine (अकाल) ban sakta hai.
    *   **Example:** Bengal Famine 1943 mein starvation aur epidemics se 30 lakh log mare the. Table 4.1 dikhata hai ki production kam hua tha, par availability mein drastic decline nahi tha, suggesting distribution/access issues bhi the.

3.  **Kaun Hain Food Insecure?**
    *   **Ramu ki Kahani:** Gaon mein khetiहर mazdoor, seasonal kaam milta hai, 4 mahine berozgaar rehta hai. Is time par uske parivar ko mushkil hoti hai, kabhi kabhi bachchon ko bhookha sona padta hai. Yeh seasonal hunger ka example hai.
    *   **Ahmad ki Kahani:** Shahar mein rickshaw puller, daily earnings fix nahi hain. Lekin uske paas Yellow Card (BPL PDS card) hai, jisse use saste daam par anaaj, chini, kerosene mil jaata hai, jo uske parivar ke survival mein madad karta hai.
    *   Mahilayein aur bachche malnutrition (कुपोषण) ke zyada shikar hote hain (NHFS data: approx 11 crore).

4.  **Green Revolution ka Asar:**
    *   India foodgrains mein self-sufficient (आत्मनिर्भर) bana. Famines ko avoid kiya gaya.
    *   Lekin fayda disproportionate tha. Kuch states (UP, Punjab, Haryana, MP) mein production zyada badha.
    *   Focus mainly wheat aur rice par raha.

5.  **Sarkari System Kaise Kaam Karta Hai:**
    *   **MSP & Buffer Stock:** Sarkar (FCI) kisaano se MSP par anaaj khareedti hai (incentive for production). Isse Buffer Stock banaya jaata hai.
    *   `[Flowchart Idea: Farmer -> Sells at MSP -> FCI Procures -> Stores (Buffer Stock) -> Distributes via PDS at Issue Price / Uses in Calamity]`
    *   **PDS & Ration Shops:** Buffer stock se anaaj ration shops ko diya jaata hai. Alag-alag cards (Antyodaya, BPL, APL) ke hisab se logon ko subsidized rates par milta hai. NFSA, 2013 ne isse legal right bana diya hai.

6.  **PDS ki Safalta aur Chunautiyan:**
    *   **Safalta (Success):** Prices ko stabilize kiya, consumers ko affordable food diya, hunger/famine ko roka, farmers ko income security di (kuch regions mein).
    *   **Chunautiyan (Challenges):** Godowns mein anaaj ka overflow aur wastage, high costs, MSP badhne se sarkari kharch badhna, PDS dealers ki gadbadiyan (malpractices), APL families ka kam interest.

7.  **Cooperatives ka Yogdaan:**
    *   Yeh societies bhi food security mein madad karti hain, especially saste daam par zaroori cheezein bech kar. Amul ne doodh ke production mein kranti laayi (White Revolution). ADS ne Maharashtra mein Grain Banks shuru kiye.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ) 📊

*   **National Economic/Social Data:**
    *   **Bengal Famine (1943):** Ek devastating historical event jisne food security ki importance ko highlight kiya (30 lakh deaths).
    *   **Green Revolution (हरित क्रांति):** India ko foodgrains mein self-sufficient banaya (especially wheat & rice). Key states: Punjab, Haryana, Western UP. Production figures (e.g., 2020-21 mein total foodgrain 310 Million Tonnes).
    *   **FCI (भारतीय खाद्य निगम):** Buffer stock maintain karne wali primary agency.
    *   **MSP (न्यूनतम समर्थन मूल्य):** Farmers ko diya jaane wala pre-announced price. Iska level aur impact ek important policy issue hai.
    *   **PDS Network:** Lagbhag 5.5 lakh ration shops across India. Different card types (Antyodaya, BPL, APL).
    *   **NFSA, 2013 (राष्ट्रीय खाद्य सुरक्षा अधिनियम):** Ek landmark legislation jo food ko legal right banata hai. Specific coverage (75% rural, 50% urban) aur subsidized prices (₹3/2/1 per kg).

    *   **Buffer Stock Levels (Graph 4.2):** Often higher than minimum norms, leading to storage issues. (e.g., 2022 mein stock norm se kaafi zyada tha).
    *   **Regional Disparities:** Kuch states (Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, etc.) mein food insecurity zyada hai.
    *   **Cooperatives Success Stories:** Amul (Gujarat), Mother Dairy (Delhi), ADS Grain Banks (Maharashtra).
    *   **NHFS Data:** Malnutrition among women and children (approx 11 crore).
