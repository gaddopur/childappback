# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 204: Food Security in India (Bharat mein Khadya Suraksha)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter inn important ideas par focus karta hai:

1.  **Food Security (Khadya Suraksha)**: Iska matlab hai sabhi logon ke liye, har samay bhojan ki availability (uplabdhata), accessibility (pahunch), aur affordability (samarthya).
    *   **Availability (Uplabdhata)**: Desh mein food production, imports, aur sarkari godamon (granaries) mein pichhle saal ka stock.
    *   **Accessibility (Pahunch)**: Bhojan har vyakti ki pahunch mein ho, koi rukavat (barrier) na ho.
    *   **Affordability (Samarthya)**: Vyakti ke paas sufficient, safe, aur nutritious bhojan khareedne ke liye paryapt paisa ho.
2.  **Need for Food Security (Khadya Suraksha ki Zaroorat)**:
    *   Garib pariwar (poor households) hamesha food insecure ho sakte hain.
    *   Natural calamities (prakritik aapdayein) jaise earthquake, drought, flood, tsunami, famine ke time par sabhi log food insecure ho sakte hain. (Example: Bengal Famine 1943).
    *   Pandemics jaise Covid-19 bhi food security ko impact karti hain.
3.  **Food Insecure Groups (Khadya Asurakshit Varg)**:
    *   Rural Areas mein: Landless labourers, small farmers, traditional artisans, petty self-employed workers, destitutes (nirashrit).
    *   Urban Areas mein: Casual labourers in ill-paid occupations, seasonal workers.
    *   Social Groups: SCs, STs, kuch OBC sections.
    *   Others: Natural disaster victims, migrants, women (especially pregnant/nursing), children under 5.
    *   Regions: Economically backward states (UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, Orissa, WB, Chhattisgarh, MP, Maharashtra ke kuch hisse).
4.  **Hunger (Bhukhmari)**: Food insecurity ka ek pehlu.
    *   **Chronic Hunger (Deerkalik Bhukhmari)**: Lagatar kam quantity ya quality wala diet milna (due to low income).
    *   **Seasonal Hunger (Mausami Bhukhmari)**: Krishi ke seasonal nature ya casual labour mein kaam na milne ke karan saal ke kuch mahino mein bhukha rehna.
5.  **India's Food Security System (Bharat ki Khadya Suraksha Pranali)**:
    *   **Buffer Stock**: Sarkar dwara FCI (Food Corporation of India) ke through gehu aur chawal ka stock jama karna. Farmers ko MSP (Minimum Support Price) par khareeda jaata hai. Yeh stock calamity ke time ya garibon mein baantne ke kaam aata hai (at Issue Price).
    *   **Public Distribution System (PDS - Sarvajanik Vitran Pranali)**: Sarkari ration shops (Fair Price Shops) ke network se garib logon tak subsidized rates par anaaj (wheat, rice), sugar, kerosene pahunchana. Alag-alag ration cards (Antyodaya, BPL, APL).
6.  **Evolution of PDS**: Universal PDS -> RPDS (1992) -> TPDS (1997) -> AAY (2000), APS (2000) -> National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013.
7.  **Challenges (Chunautiyan)**: PDS mein malpractices (diversion, poor quality), overflowing FCI godowns, high carrying costs, MSP ka pressure, environmental issues (water depletion).
8.  **Role of Cooperatives (Sahakari Samitiyon ki Bhumika)**: Food security mein madad karna (e.g., Tamil Nadu mein ration shops, Mother Dairy Delhi, Amul Gujarat, ADS Maharashtra - Grain Banks).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Food Security ke 3 'A's**: Humne samjha ki Khadya Suraksha sirf pet bharna nahi hai. Iske liye **Availability** (desh mein paryapt anaaj ho), **Accessibility** (logon tak anaaj pahunch sake), aur **Affordability** (log use khareed sakein) teeno zaroori hain. Agar ek bhi dimension kamzor hai, toh food security nahi ho sakti.
*   **Aapda aur Khadya Suraksha**: Jab koi natural calamity (jaise drought ya flood) aati hai, toh food production ghat jaata hai (availability kam). Isse shortage hoti hai, prices badh jaate hain (affordability kam). Garib log khareed nahi paate, jisse starvation (bhukhmari) aur extreme cases mein famine (akaal) ho sakta hai. **Bengal Famine (1943)** ek bhayavah udaharan hai jisme lagbhag 30 lakh log maare gaye the (Table 4.1 production data par discussion).
*   **Kaun Hain Food Insecure?**: Bharat mein ek bada hissa food insecure hai. Khaas kar **Ramu** jaise khet mazdoor (jo seasonal unemployment jhelte hain) aur **Ahmad** jaise shehri casual workers (jinki income roz fluctuate hoti hai). Socially backward groups (SC/ST/OBC), mahilayein aur bachhe (NHFS data: ~11 crore), aur kuch pichhde rajyon ke log zyada vulnerable hain.
*   **Bhukhmari ke Prakar**: **Chronic hunger** un logon ko hoti hai jinki income itni kam hai ki woh pet bharne layak bhojan bhi nahi khareed paate. **Seasonal hunger** gaon mein kheti ke season par aur shehron mein casual kaam (like construction) ke off-season par nirbhar karti hai (Table 4.2 data shows decline over years).
*   **Green Revolution aur Self-Sufficiency**: Azadi ke baad, India ne 'Green Revolution' (Harit Kranti) ke zariye, khaas kar wheat aur rice production mein, self-sufficiency (aatmanirbharta) haasil ki. **Graph 4.1** foodgrain production mein badhotri dikhata hai (2021-22 mein 315 million tonnes). Lekin iska fayda sabhi regions ko barabar nahi mila (UP, Punjab, MP mein zyada growth).
*   **Sarkari System: Buffer Stock aur PDS**: Sarkar **FCI** ke through kisaano se **MSP** (Minimum Support Price) par anaaj khareed kar **Buffer Stock** banati hai. Yeh stock zaroorat padne par (calamity ya garibon ke liye) **Issue Price** (market price se kam) par **PDS** ke zariye **Ration Shops** (Fair Price Shops) se baanta jaata hai.
    *   **PDS ka Vikas**: Pehle sabke liye tha, phir RPDS (remote areas), TPDS (garibon par focus, differential pricing), AAY (poorest of the poor), APS (indigent senior citizens) jaise targeted schemes aaye.
    *   **NFSA 2013**: Ek important Act jo 75% rural aur 50% urban population ko subsidized foodgrains ka legal right deta hai (Priority Households ko 5kg per person per month @ Rs 2/kg wheat, Rs 3/kg rice, Rs 1/kg coarse grains). (Table 4.3 details various schemes).
*   **System ki Samasyayein**:
    *   FCI godowns mein anaaj ka overflow hona (Graph 4.2 shows stock vs buffer norms), jisse storage cost badhti hai aur anaaj waste (rotting, eaten by rats) hota hai.
    *   MSP badhne se sarkari kharch badhta hai aur farmers (khaas kar Punjab, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh) coarse grains ki jagah wheat/rice ugane lagte hain, jo garibon ka staple food hai.
    *   Rice cultivation mein zyada paani use hone se environmental degradation (pani ka level girna) ho raha hai.
    *   Ration shops mein malpractices: anaaj ko open market mein bechna, kharab quality dena, dukan time par na kholna.
    *   TPDS mein APL (Above Poverty Line) families ke liye price market ke kareeb hone se unka PDS se khareedne ka incentive kam ho gaya hai.
*   **Sahakari Samitiyon ka Yogdan**: Cooperatives bhi food security mein important role play kar rahi hain. Jaise Tamil Nadu mein 94% ration shops cooperatives chalati hain. Delhi mein **Mother Dairy** (milk/vegetables) aur Gujarat mein **Amul** (milk products - White Revolution) successful examples hain. Maharashtra mein **ADS** (Academy of Development Science) NGOs ke network se **Grain Banks** chala rahi hai.
