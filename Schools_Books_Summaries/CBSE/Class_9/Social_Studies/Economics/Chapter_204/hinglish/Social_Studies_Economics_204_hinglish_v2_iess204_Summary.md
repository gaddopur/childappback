# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 204: Food Security in India (Bharat mein Khadya Suraksha)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

*   **Food Security (Khadya Suraksha):** Matlab sabhi logon ke liye har samay bhojan ki **availability** (uplabdhata), **accessibility** (pahunch), aur **affordability** (khareedne ki kshamata/samarthya) hona.
    *   **Availability (Uplabdhata):** Desh mein food production, imports, aur government granaries (sarkari anaj bhandaron) mein pichhle saal ka stock.
    *   **Accessibility (Pahunch):** Bhojan har vyakti ki pahunch mein ho, koi rukavat (barrier) na ho.
    *   **Affordability (Samarthya):** Vyakti ke paas sufficient, safe, aur nutritious (poshtik) bhojan khareedne ke liye पर्याप्त paisa ho.
*   **Need for Food Security (Khadya Suraksha ki Zaroorat):**
    *   Gareeb parivar (poor households) hamesha food insecure ho sakte hain.
    *   Natural calamities (prakritik aapdayein) jaise earthquake (bhukamp), drought (sukha), flood (baadh), tsunami, famine (akaal) ke time par sabhi log prabhavit ho sakte hain.
    *   Calmities ke dauran production ghatta hai, prices badhte hain, jisse starvation (bhukhmari) aur famine (akaal) ki sthiti ban sakti hai (Example: Bengal Famine 1943).
*   **Food-Insecure Groups (Khadya Asurakshit Varg):**
    *   **Rural Areas (Gramin Kshetra):** Landless labourers (bhoomiheen mazdoor), small farmers, traditional artisans (paramparik karigar), petty self-employed workers, destitutes (niraashrit), beggars (bhikshuk).
    *   **Urban Areas (Shahri Kshetra):** Casual labourers (aniyamit mazdoor) in ill-paid occupations (kam vetan wale kaam), seasonal workers.
    *   **Social Groups:** SCs, STs, kuch OBC sections (jinke paas kam zameen ya kam upajau zameen hai).
    *   **Other Vulnerable Groups:** Natural disaster victims, migrants, women (especially pregnant and nursing mothers), children under 5 years.
    *   **Regions:** Economically backward states (arthik roop se pichhde rajya) jaise UP (eastern/south-eastern), Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal, Chhattisgarh, MP, Maharashtra ke kuch hisse.
*   **Hunger (Bhukh):** Food insecurity ka ek pehlu.
    *   **Chronic Hunger (Deerghkalik Bhukh):** Lagatar kam matra ya quality wala bhojan milne se. Yeh gareebi aur kam income ke karan hota hai.
    *   **Seasonal Hunger (Mausami Bhukh):** Krishi ke seasonal nature ya casual labour mein kaam na milne ke karan (e.g., rainy season mein construction workers).
*   **India's Food Security System (Bharat ki Khadya Suraksha Pranali):**
    *   **Buffer Stock:** Sarkar dwara FCI (Food Corporation of India) ke madhyam se gehun (wheat) aur chawal (rice) ka stock. Farmers se MSP (Minimum Support Price - Nyuntam Samarthan Mulya) par khareeda jaata hai. Yeh stock kami wale ilakon (deficit areas) aur gareeb logon mein Issue Price (nirgam mulya) par baanta jaata hai, aur aapda ke samay kaam aata hai.
    *   **Public Distribution System (PDS - Sarvajanik Vitran Pranali):** Sarkari regulated ration shops (uchit mulya ki dukaanen/Fair Price Shops) ke network se gareeb logon tak anaj pahunchana.
        *   **Ration Cards:** Antyodaya (poorest of the poor), BPL (Below Poverty Line), APL (Above Poverty Line).
*   **Key Schemes (Mukhya Yojanayein):** PDS ka vikas - RPDS (1992), TPDS (1997), AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana - 2000), APS (Annapurna Scheme - 2000), National Food Security Act (NFSA) - 2013.
*   **Role of Cooperatives (Sahakari Samitiyon ki Bhumika):** Food security mein madad karna, especially southern aur western India mein (e.g., Tamil Nadu ration shops, Mother Dairy, Amul).
*   **Challenges (Chunautiyan):** FCI godowns mein anaj ka overflow hona, wastage, high storage costs, MSP ka badhna, PDS mein malpractices (dhandhli).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

1.  **Food Security ke Dimensions:** Khadya suraksha sirf 'do waqt ki roti' nahi hai. Iske teen mukhya pillars hain: **Uplabdhata** (desh mein anaj ka hona), **Pahunch** (logon tak anaj ka pahunchna), aur **Samarthya** (logon ki khareedne ki shakti). Jab ye teeno conditions poori hoti hain, tabhi desh mein food security ensure hoti hai.
2.  **Vulnerability aur Calamities ka Asar:** Gareeb log aksar khadya asurakshit rehte hain, lekin prakritik aapda (natural calamity) jaise sukha, baadh, ya pandemic (jaise Covid-19) ke time par, production kam ho jaata hai, keematein badh jaati hain, aur ameir log bhi food insecure ho sakte hain. Isse bhukhmari (starvation) aur akaal (famine) ki sthiti aa sakti hai. **Bengal Famine (1943)** iska ek bhayanak udaharan hai, jisme lagbhag 30 lakh log maare gaye the. (Dekhein Table 4.1 - Rice production data).
3.  **Pehchan: Kaun Hain Food-Insecure?:** Bharat mein ek bada hissa food and nutrition insecurity se जूझ raha hai. Gramin ilakon mein bhoomiheen khet mazdoor, chhote kisan, aur paramparik kaam karne wale log zyada vulnerable hain. Shahri ilakon mein kam salary wale aniyamit mazdoor (casual labourers) ismein aate hain. Socially, SCs, STs, aur kuch OBCs; geographically, pichhde rajya (Bihar, Odisha, etc.); aur demographically, mahilayein aur 5 saal se kam umar ke bachche zyada food insecure hain (NHFS data ke anusar lagbhag 11 crore). Ramu (casual labourer) aur Ahmad (rickshaw puller) ki stories is vulnerability ko darshati hain.
4.  **Hunger ke Prakar:** Bhukh do tarah ki hoti hai:
    *   **Chronic (Deerghkalik):** Lagatar aparyapt bhojan milna, mainly kam income ke karan.
    *   **Seasonal (Mausami):** Saal ke kuch mahino mein kaam na milne se bhojan ki kami, jaise khetiहर mazdooron ko fasal ke beech ke samay mein. (Dekhein Table 4.2 - Hunger trends in India).
5.  **Green Revolution aur Self-Sufficiency:** Azadi ke baad, Bharat ne 'Green Revolution' (Harit Kranti) ke zariye, khaas kar gehun aur chawal ke production mein, self-sufficiency (aatmanirbharta) haasil karne ki koshish ki. Isse desh ne akaal ko kaafi had tak taal diya hai. Production badha hai (Dekhein Graph 4.1 - Foodgrain Production trends), lekin yeh growth asamaan (disproportionate) rahi hai, kuch rajyon (UP, MP, Punjab, Haryana) aur faslon (wheat, rice) mein zyada hui hai.
6.  **Buffer Stock aur MSP:** Sarkar Food Corporation of India (FCI) ke through kisano se unki surplus fasal (mainly wheat & rice) ek pehle se ghoshit keemat par khareedti hai, jise **Minimum Support Price (MSP)** kehte hain. Yeh MSP sowing season se pehle declare hota hai taaki kisano ko production badhane ka incentive mile. Is khareede gaye anaj ke stock ko **Buffer Stock** kehte hain. Iska uddeshya anaj ki kami wale ilakon mein aur gareeb logon ko saste daam (Issue Price) par anaj uplabdh karana hai, aur aapda ke samay supply maintain karna hai.
7.  **Public Distribution System (PDS):** FCI dwara khareeda gaya anaj sarkari control wali **Ration Shops** (Fair Price Shops) ke zariye desh bhar mein (lagbhag 5.5 lakh shops) gareeb logon ko market price se kam keemat par distribute kiya jaata hai. Alag-alag parivaron ke liye alag ration cards hain (Antyodaya, BPL, APL) jinke basis par unhe har mahine ek nishchit matra mein anaj, cheeni, kerosene milta hai.
8.  **PDS ka Vikas aur NFSA 2013:** Shuru mein PDS universal tha. Baad mein ise zyada targeted banane ke liye badla gaya:
    *   **RPDS (1992):** Remote aur backward areas ke liye.
    *   **TPDS (1997):** Gareebon ko target kiya gaya, alag-alag price policy (BPL/APL) shuru hui.
    *   **AAY (2000):** 'Poorest of the poor' ke liye, highly subsidised rates par anaj.
    *   **APS (2000):** 'Indigent senior citizens' ke liye free anaj.
    *   **National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013:** Is Act ne food ko legal right banaya. Iske tehat, 75% rural aur 50% urban population ko subsidized anaj (Rice Rs 3/kg, Wheat Rs 2/kg, Coarse grains Rs 1/kg) milne ka adhikar hai. (Dekhein Table 4.3 - PDS Schemes Features).
9.  **System ki Chunautiyan (Challenges):**
    *   **Overflowing Granaries:** FCI ke godaam aksar buffer norms se kahin zyada anaj se bhare rehte hain (Dekhein Graph 4.2). Isse storage cost badhti hai, anaj sadta (rotting) hai ya choohe kha jaate hain.
    *   **High Costs:** Badhte MSP, procurement, transportation, aur storage costs se sarkari kharcha badh raha hai (Subsidy+).
    *   **Regional Imbalance:** Procurement kuch hi rajyon (Punjab, Haryana, AP, West UP) aur 2 faslon (wheat, rice) par concentrated hai. Isse coarse grains (gareebon ka staple food) ka production kam hua hai.
    *   **Environmental Issues:** Rice cultivation mein zyada pani ke istemal se environmental degradation aur water level neeche ja raha hai.
    *   **PDS Malpractices:** Ration dealers dwara anaj ko open market mein bechna, kharab quality ka anaj dena, dukaan time par na kholna jaisi samasyayein hain. TPDS ke baad, APL parivar ke liye PDS rates market rates ke kareeb hone se unka incentive kam ho gaya hai.
10. **Sahakari Samitiyon (Cooperatives) ka Role:** Desh ke kai hisson, khaas kar southern aur western parts mein, cooperatives food security mein important role play kar rahi hain.
    *   **Tamil Nadu:** Lagbhag 94% fair price shops cooperatives chala rahi hain.
    *   **Delhi:** Mother Dairy controlled rates par milk aur vegetables provide kar rahi hai.
    *   **Gujarat:** Amul ne milk production mein 'White Revolution' laya hai.
    *   **Maharashtra:** Academy of Development Science (ADS) ne NGOs ke network ke zariye 'Grain Banks' sthapit kiye hain, jo ek successful food security intervention maana jaata hai.
