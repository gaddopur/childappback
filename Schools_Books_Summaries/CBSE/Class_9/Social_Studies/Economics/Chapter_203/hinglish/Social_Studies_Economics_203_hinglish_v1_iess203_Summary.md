# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 203: Garibi: Ek Chunauti (Poverty as a Challenge)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter Bharat ki sabse badi chunautiyon mein se ek – **Garibi (Poverty)** – par focus karta hai.

1.  **Garibi Kya Hai? (What is Poverty?)**
    *   Basic needs (buniyadi zarooratein) jaise roti, kapda, makaan, swasthya (health), shiksha (education) ka na hona.
    *   Helplessness (lachari), lack of regular job, kam income (low income).
    *   Examples: Landless labourers (bhumiheen mazdoor), slum dwellers (jhuggi-jhopdi wale), daily wage workers (dihadi mazdoor), child labourers (baal mazdoor).

2.  **Garibi ka Maapan (Measuring Poverty)**
    *   **Poverty Line (Garibi Rekha):** Ek imaginary line jo minimum income ya consumption level ko darshata hai basic needs poori karne ke liye.
        *   **Aadhar (Basis):** Calorie Requirement (Rural: 2400 cal/person/day, Urban: 2100 cal/person/day) aur inko khareedne ke liye zaroori paisa (monetary value).
        *   **2011-12 Data (Tendulkar Methodology):** Rural - ₹816/person/month; Urban - ₹1000/person/month. (Ek 5-member rural family ke liye approx. ₹4080/month se kam, urban family ke liye ₹5000/month se kam).
        *   **Estimation:** National Sample Survey Organisation (NSSO) dwara har 5 saal mein sample surveys se.
        *   **International Poverty Line:** World Bank dwara (e.g., $2.15 per person per day, 2017 PPP).
    *   **Social Indicators (Samajik Sanketak):** Illiteracy (niraksharta), malnutrition (kuposhan), lack of healthcare access, job opportunities, safe drinking water, sanitation.

3.  **Social Science Perspective (Samajik Vigyan ka Nazariya)**
    *   **Social Exclusion (Samajik Bahishkar):** Garib logon ka behtar mahaul aur suvidhaon se alag-thalag rehna. Example: Bharat mein caste system ke karan kuch groups ko equal opportunities se vanchit rakhna. Yeh garibi ka karan bhi hai aur parinaam bhi.
    *   **Vulnerability (Asuraksha):** Kuch communities (jaise SC/ST) ya individuals (jaise widow, physically handicapped) ke future mein garib hone ya bane rehne ki zyada probability (sambhavna). Yeh assets, education, health, job opportunities ki kami aur natural disasters ya economic shocks jhelne ki kam kshamta par depend karta hai.

4.  **Garibi ke Trends (Poverty Trends)**
    *   **Bharat mein (In India):**
        *   Poverty Ratio mein kami aayi hai (1993-94 mein ~45% se 2011-12 mein ~22%).
        *   Number of poor bhi kam hue hain (2004-05 mein 407 million se 2011-12 mein 270 million).
        *   **Vulnerable Groups (Sabse Kamzor Samooh):** Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Rural agricultural labourers, Urban casual labourers. STs mein poverty sabse zyada (43% in 2011-12).
        *   **Inter-State Disparities (Rajyon ke beech Antar):** Kuch states mein garibi zyada (Bihar, Odisha), kuch mein kam (Kerala, Punjab, Haryana, Tamil Nadu). Alag-alag states mein kami ki speed bhi alag hai.
    *   **Global Trends (Vaishvik Trends):**
        *   Extreme poverty ($2.15/day) globally kam hui hai.
        *   Regional differences bahut hain: China aur Southeast Asia mein tezi se kami; South Asia mein bhi kami; Sub-Saharan Africa mein abhi bhi sabse zyada garibi hai.

5.  **Garibi ke Karan (Causes of Poverty in India)**
    *   **Historical:** British colonial rule ke time low economic development, traditional handicrafts ka khatma.
    *   **Economic:** Slow economic growth (1980s tak), high population growth, low per capita income growth, unemployment/underemployment. Green Revolution ka limited impact.
    *   **Social & Structural:** High income inequality (aay ki asamanta), unequal distribution of land & resources, land reforms ka sahi se implement na hona, social obligations (samajik zimmedariyan) aur religious ceremonies par kharch, indebtedness (karz mein doobna).

6.  **Garibi Nivaran Upay (Anti-Poverty Measures)**
    *   **Government Strategy:** Two main approaches:
        1.  Promotion of Economic Growth (Arthik Vikas ko badhava dena).
        2.  Targeted Anti-Poverty Programmes (Lakshit Garibi Nivaran Karyakram).
    *   **Key Programmes:**
        *   **MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005):** Rural areas mein 100 din ka guaranteed wage employment.
        *   **PMRY (Prime Minister Rozgar Yozana, 1993):** Educated unemployed youth ke liye self-employment.
        *   **REGP (Rural Employment Generation Programme, 1995):** Rural/small towns mein self-employment.
        *   **SGSY (Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana, 1999):** Self Help Groups (SHGs) ke through poor families ko poverty line se upar lana.
        *   **PMGY (Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yozana, 2000):** Basic services (health, education, water, etc.) ke liye states ko additional assistance.
        *   **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana):** Poorest of the poor ko food grains provide karna.
    *   **Challenges in Implementation:** Lack of proper implementation, right targeting, overlapping schemes.

7.  **Bhavishya ki Chunautiyan (The Challenges Ahead)**
    *   Garibi kam hui hai, par abhi bhi Bharat ki sabse badi chunauti hai.
    *   Disparities (asamanata) bani hui hain (rural/urban, states, social groups).
    *   **Human Poverty (Manav Garibi):** Sirf income nahi, balki education, health, job security, self-confidence, freedom from discrimination, gender equality, dignity bhi zaroori hain. Poverty ka concept development ke saath badalta rehta hai. Yeh ek "moving target" hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh) 📈

1.  **Poverty Line Calculation Ko Samajhna:** Bharat mein Garibi Rekha calorie needs par based hai (Rural 2400, Urban 2100). In calories ko provide karne wale food items (anaaj, daal, sabzi, etc.) ki market price ke hisab se monthly income/expenditure figure nikala jata hai (Jaise 2011-12 mein Rural ₹816, Urban ₹1000 per person per month). Yeh line samay aur jagah ke saath badalti hai.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek simple flowchart jo dikhaye: Calorie Needs -> Food Basket -> Market Price -> Poverty Line (₹).

2.  **Social Exclusion aur Vulnerability ka Matlab:** Social Exclusion ka matlab hai samaj ke kuch groups ko opportunities aur benefits se door rakhna (jaise caste basis par). Vulnerability ka matlab hai kuch groups (SC, ST, widows) ka garibi ke prati zyada risk mein hona, unke paas resources ki kami aur shocks jhelne ki kam kshamta ke karan. Ram Saran aur Lakha Singh ki stories in concepts ko illustrate karti hain.

3.  **Bharat mein Garibi ke Trends:** Table 3.1 dikhata hai ki poverty ratio (garibi anupat) 1993-94 se 2011-12 tak consistently kam hua hai (45% se 22%). Garib logon ki sankhya bhi 2004-05 ke baad kam hui hai.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek line graph jo time ke saath poverty ratio (%) mein decline dikhaye.

4.  **Vulnerable Groups ki Pehchan:** Graph 3.1 ke अनुसार, SCs (29%), STs (43%), rural agricultural labourers (34%), aur urban casual labourers (34%) mein poverty average se kahin zyada hai (Average 22% in 2011-12). Sivaraman ki story intra-family inequality (parivar ke andar asamanta) bhi dikhati hai.

5.  **Inter-State Disparities:** Graph 3.2 se pata chalta hai ki Bihar (33.7%) aur Odisha (32.6%) jaise states mein poverty levels national average se bahut upar hain, jabki Kerala, Punjab jaise states mein kaafi kam. Iske peeche alag-alag kaaran hain jaise agricultural growth (Punjab), human resource development (Kerala), land reforms (West Bengal), PDS (Tamil Nadu/Andhra Pradesh).
    *   *Diagram Idea:* India ka map jisme high poverty states (e.g., Red color) aur low poverty states (e.g., Green color) highlight kiye gaye hon.

6.  **Garibi ke Mukhya Karan:** Colonial past, slow growth, population pressure, unemployment, inequality (land distribution), social factors (kharcha on ceremonies), aur indebtedness (karz) - yeh sab milkar Bharat mein garibi ko banaye rakhte hain.

7.  **Sarkari Garibi Nivaran Karyakram:** Sarkar economic growth badhane ke saath-saath specific schemes bhi chalati hai. MGNREGA (rozgar guarantee), PMRY/REGP (self-employment), SGSY (SHGs), PMGY (basic services), AAY (food security) kuch important examples hain. Inki effectiveness implementation par depend karti hai.

8.  **Human Poverty ka Concept:** Garibi sirf paison ki kami nahi hai. Ek "reasonable" level of living ke liye education, health, security, dignity, aur equality bhi zaroori hain. Yeh 'Human Poverty' ka concept hai, jo sirf 'minimum subsistence' se aage dekhta hai.
