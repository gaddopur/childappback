# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 203: Poverty as a Challenge (Garibi: Ek Chunauti)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
Concept Hierarchy 📊

1.  **Poverty ka Matlab (Meaning of Poverty)**
    *   Basic Needs ki Kami (Lack of food, shelter, clothing)
    *   Social Dimensions (Lack of education, healthcare, sanitation, clean water, regular job)
    *   Psychological Impact (Helplessness, ill-treatment)
    *   Examples: Landless labourers, jhuggi dwellers, daily wagers, child labour, beggars.

2.  **Poverty ko Kaise Measure Karein? (Measuring Poverty)**
    *   **Poverty Line (Garibi Rekha)**
        *   Concept: Ek imaginary line jo minimum income/consumption level batati hai basic needs ke liye.
        *   India ka Method: Calorie requirement (Rural: 2400 kcal, Urban: 2100 kcal) aur essential non-food items (clothing, fuel, education, health) par based monetary value (e.g., 2011-12: Rural ₹816/person/month, Urban ₹1000/person/month).
        *   Estimation: National Sample Survey Organisation (NSSO) dwara har 5 saal mein.
        *   International Comparison: World Bank ka standard (e.g., $2.15 per person per day, 2017 PPP).
    *   **Social Exclusion (Samajik Bahishkar)**
        *   Concept: Poor logon ka better surroundings aur opportunities se alag-thalag rehna (e.g., caste system ke karan). Yeh poverty ka cause bhi hai aur consequence bhi.
    *   **Vulnerability (Asuraksha)**
        *   Concept: Kuch communities (like SC/ST) ya individuals (widows, disabled) ka future mein poor hone ya rehne ka zyada risk. Yeh assets, education, health, job options aur natural disasters/shocks se deal karne ki ability par depend karta hai.

3.  **Poverty Trends (Garibi ke Rujhan)**
    *   **India Mein:**
        *   Overall Decline: Poverty ratio kam hua hai (1993-94 mein ~45% se 2011-12 mein ~22%).
        *   Number of Poor: Bhi kam hue hain (2004-05 mein ~407 million se 2011-12 mein ~270 million).
        *   Rural vs Urban: Dono jagah decline, par differences hain.
    *   **Global Trends:**
        *   Overall Decline: Extreme poverty (World Bank definition) kam hui hai.
        *   Regional Variations: China aur Southeast Asia mein tezi se kami. South Asia mein bhi decline. Sub-Saharan Africa mein poverty abhi bhi bahut high hai aur sabse zyada poor log wahin hain. Latin America mein thoda increase.

4.  **Vulnerable Groups in India (Bharat mein Asurakshit Samuh)**
    *   Social Groups: Scheduled Castes (SC) aur Scheduled Tribes (ST) sabse zyada vulnerable hain.
    *   Economic Groups: Rural agricultural labour households aur Urban casual labour households bhi bahut vulnerable hain.
    *   Intra-family Inequality: Poor families mein bhi women, elderly, aur female infants ko kam resources milte hain.

5.  **Inter-State Disparities (Rajyon ke Beech Asamantayein)**
    *   Poverty har state mein ek jaisi nahi hai.
    *   High Poverty States: Bihar, Odisha, Assam, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh (2011-12 data).
    *   Low Poverty States / Significant Decline: Kerala, Punjab, Haryana, Gujarat, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu. Reasons alag-alag hain (agriculture growth, human resource development, land reforms, PDS).

6.  **Causes of Poverty in India (Bharat mein Garibi ke Karan)**
    *   Historical: British colonial policies ne traditional industries ko barbad kiya aur economic development kam hui.
    *   Economic: Slow economic growth (until 1980s), kam job opportunities, high population growth -> low per capita income. Green Revolution ka limited impact.
    *   Social & Structural: Unequal distribution of land & resources. Land reforms ka ineffective implementation. Social obligations/ceremonies par kharcha. Caste system.
    *   Indebtedness (Karz): Chhote kisan inputs ke liye karz lete hain, poverty ke karan chuka nahi paate -> debt trap.

7.  **Anti-Poverty Measures (Garibi Nivaran Upay)**
    *   Government Strategy ke Do Planks:
        *   1. Promotion of Economic Growth (Arthik Vikas ko Badhava Dena): Growth se opportunities badhti hain, resources milte hain. 1980s ke baad higher growth se poverty reduction mein help mili hai.
        *   2. Targeted Anti-Poverty Programmes (Lakshit Garibi Nivaran Karyakram): Direct help ke liye schemes.
            *   **MGNREGA (2005):** Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act - Har rural household ko 100 din ka guaranteed wage employment. Livelihood security aur sustainable development (drought, deforestation rokna). 1/3 jobs women ke liye reserved.
            *   **PMRY (1993):** Prime Minister Rozgar Yozana - Educated unemployed youth ko self-employment ke liye help (business/industry setup).
            *   **REGP (1995):** Rural Employment Generation Programme - Rural areas/small towns mein self-employment opportunities create karna.
            *   **SGSY (1999):** Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana - Poor families ko Self Help Groups (SHGs) mein organize karke bank credit/subsidy se poverty line se upar lana.
            *   **PMGY (2000):** Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yozana - States ko basic services (health, education, shelter, water, electricity) ke liye additional funds.
            *   **AAY:** Antyodaya Anna Yojana (details next chapter mein).
    *   Effectiveness Issues: Implementation problems, wrong targeting, schemes ka overlap.

8.  **Challenges Ahead & Human Poverty (Aage ki Chunautiyan aur Manav Garibi)**
    *   Progress ke bawajood, poverty reduction India ki sabse badi chunauti hai. Disparities (rural/urban, states, groups) abhi bhi hain.
    *   Official definition sirf 'minimum' subsistence dekhti hai, 'reasonable' living standard nahi.
    *   Concept ko broaden karna hoga -> **Human Poverty:** Sirf income nahi, balki education, health, shelter, job security, self-confidence, discrimination se freedom, child labour ka na hona bhi shamil hai.
    *   Poverty eradication ek 'moving target' hai; jaise development hogi, definition bhi badlegi. Future tasks: Sabke liye health, education, job security, gender equality, dignity.

## 📘 Key Learnings
Detailed explanations with diagrams 📈

1.  **Garibi Sirf Paise ki Kami Nahi Hai:** Humne Ram Saran (Ranchi ka daily wager) aur Lakha Singh (Meerut ka landless labourer) ki stories dekhi. Unki life dikhati hai ki poverty ka matlab hai bhookh, beghar hona, bachchon ko school na bhej pana, ilaaj na karwa pana, saaf paani/safai na hona, regular kaam na milna, aur lachari (helplessness). Yeh ek multi-dimensional samasya hai.

2.  **Poverty Line - Ek Maapdand:** India mein poverty line calorie intake (gaon mein 2400, sheher mein 2100) aur zaroori cheezon par hone wale minimum kharche (expenditure) se tay hoti hai. 2011-12 mein yeh gaon ke liye ₹816/mahina/व्यक्ति aur sheher ke liye ₹1000/mahina/व्यक्ति thi. NSSO yeh data collect karti hai. *Socho, kya yeh amount aaj ke time mein basic needs ke liye kaafi hai?*

3.  **Social Exclusion aur Vulnerability Garibi Badhate Hain:** Social Exclusion matlab samaj se bahar kar dena, jaise caste system mein kuch logon ko opportunities nahi milti. Vulnerability matlab kuch groups (SC, ST, widows) ka garib hone ka risk zyada hota hai kyunki unke paas options kam hote hain. Sivaraman (Tamil Nadu ka cobbler jo khet mazdoor hai) ki story SC community ki vulnerability dikhati hai, jahan uski betiyan school nahi jaati aur use apni behen ki shaadi ke liye paise nahi hain.

4.  **India mein Garibi Kam Hui Hai, Par Ab Bhi Bahut Hai:** Table 3.1 batata hai ki poverty ratio 1993-94 mein 45% se ghatkar 2011-12 mein 22% ho gaya. Garibon ki sankhya bhi 407 million (2004-05) se kam hokar 270 million (2011-12) hui. Lekin abhi bhi har 5th Indian gareeb hai.

5.  **Sabse Zyada Garib Kaun?** Graph 3.1 (NCERT) ke anusaar, Scheduled Tribes (43% BPL in 2011-12), Urban Casual Labourers (34%), Rural Agricultural Labourers (34%), aur Scheduled Castes (29%) mein poverty rate national average (22%) se kaafi zyada hai. Poor families mein bhi mahilayein, buzurg aur ladkiyan zyada suffer karti hain (Sivaraman sirf bete ke liye doodh khareedta hai).

6.  **Har State ki Kahani Alag Hai:** Graph 3.2 (NCERT) dikhata hai ki Bihar (33.7%) aur Odisha (32.6%) 2011-12 mein sabse poor states the, jabki Kerala, Punjab jaise states mein poverty kaafi kam hai. Iske peeche alag-alag reasons hain jaise Punjab mein Green Revolution, Kerala mein human development par focus, West Bengal mein land reforms, aur Andhra/Tamil Nadu mein PDS ka effective hona.

7.  **Duniya Bhar Mein Garibi:** World Bank ke $2.15/day ke hisab se global poverty kam hui hai, khaas kar China aur SE Asia mein. Lekin Sub-Saharan Africa mein situation bahut kharab hai (Graph 3.3/3.4 NCERT). UN ka lakshya hai 2030 tak har tarah ki poverty khatam karna.

8.  **Garibi Ke Peeche ke Karan:** Angrezon ke time se hi economic development kam thi. Azaadi ke baad shuru mein growth slow rahi, population tezi se badhi. Zameen ka bantwara theek se nahi hua. Land reforms sahi se लागू nahi hue. Log social functions par zyada kharch karte hain aur karz mein doob jaate hain.

9.  **Sarkar ki Koshishein:** Sarkar ne economic growth badhane par zor diya hai aur specific schemes chalaayi hain:
    *   **MGNREGA:** Gaon mein 100 din kaam ki guarantee. Isse employment mila hai aur wages badhe hain. Yeh ek important scheme hai.
    *   Dusri schemes jaise PMRY, REGP, SGSY, PMGY bhi self-employment aur basic services provide karne mein madad karti hain.
    *   Lekin in schemes ka implementation aur targeting behtar ho sakta hai. Kai baar benefits sahi logon tak nahi pahunchte.

10. **Challenge Sirf Income ka Nahi, Human Poverty ka Hai:** Garibi kam hui hai, par abhi bhi challenge bada hai. Humein sirf minimum income nahi, balki sabke liye acchi health, education, job security, samman (dignity), aur barabari (equality) ensure karni hogi. Yeh 'Human Poverty' ka concept hai.
