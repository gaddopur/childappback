# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 203: Garibi - Ek Chunauti (Poverty as a Challenge)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
**Garibi ki Mukhya Avdharnayein (Main Concepts of Poverty) 📊**

1.  **Garibi Ka Matlab (Meaning of Poverty):**
    *   Sirf income ki kami nahi, balki ek **multi-dimensional** samasya (problem).
    *   Includes: Bhukhmari (hunger), Ashray ki kami (lack of shelter), Swasthya suvidhaon tak pahunch na hona (lack of healthcare access), Shiksha ki kami (lack of education), Safai ki kami (lack of sanitation), Rozgar ki kami (lack of regular job), Asahayeata (helplessness).

2.  **Garibi ko Samajik Vaigyanik Kaise Dekhte Hain (Poverty as Seen by Social Scientists):**
    *   **Indicators:**
        *   Income aur Consumption levels (Aay aur Upbhog star).
        *   **Social Indicators:** Illiteracy level (niraksharta star), Malnutrition (kuposhan), Lack of access to healthcare, job opportunities, safe drinking water, sanitation.
    *   **Social Exclusion (Samajik Bahishkar):** Garib logon ka behtar mahaul aur suvidhaon se alag-thalag rehna. Example: Caste system mein exclusion. Yeh garibi ka karan (cause) aur parinam (consequence) dono ho sakta hai.
    *   **Vulnerability (Asuraksha):** Kuch communities (like backward castes, SC/ST) ya individuals (like widow, physically handicapped) ke future mein garib hone ya bane rehne ki zyada probability (sambhavna). Yeh natural disasters ya job loss jaise 'bure samay' mein zyada prabhavit hone ka risk bhi batata hai.

3.  **Poverty Line (Garibi Rekha):**
    *   Ek **imaginary line** jo basic needs (buniyadi zarooratein) poori karne ke liye zaroori minimum income ya consumption level ko darshata hai.
    *   **Measurement:** Income ya Consumption par based.
    *   **India mein Calculation:**
        *   Minimum food requirement (based on **calorie needs**: Rural - 2400 calories/person/day, Urban - 2100 calories/person/day). Rural areas mein physical work zyada hone se calorie need bhi zyada.
        *   Clothing, footwear, fuel, light, education, medical needs bhi shamil.
        *   In cheezon ko khareedne ke liye zaroori **monetary value** (paise mein keemat), jo samay-samay par prices ke badhne ke saath revise hoti hai.
        *   **2011-12 Data:** Rural - Rs 816/person/month, Urban - Rs 1000/person/month. (Urban areas mein cheezein mehengi hone ke karan amount zyada).
    *   **Estimation:** National Sample Survey Organisation (NSSO) dwara periodically (usually every 5 years) sample surveys se estimate kiya jata hai.
    *   **International Comparison:** World Bank $2.15 per person per day (2017 PPP) ka standard use karta hai (text mein $1.90 ka bhi zikr hai purane data ke liye).

4.  **Poverty Estimates (Garibi ke Aakde):**
    *   India mein poverty trends over time (samay ke saath badlav).

5.  **Vulnerable Groups (Kamzor Samuh):**
    *   Woh social aur economic groups jinmein garibi ka risk zyada hai.
    *   **Social Groups:** Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST).
    *   **Economic Groups:** Rural agricultural labour households (Grameen khetihar mazdoor parivar), Urban casual labour households (Shehri aniyat mazdoor parivar).
    *   **Intra-family Inequality:** Parivar ke andar bhi sabko barabar resources nahi milte (mahilayein, buzurg, female infants ko kam mil sakta hai).

6.  **Inter-State Disparities (Rajyon ke Beech Antar):**
    *   India ke alag-alag states mein poverty levels kaafi different hain.

7.  **Global Poverty Scenario (Vaishvik Garibi Paridrishya):**
    *   Duniya bhar mein garibi ke trends aur regional differences.

8.  **Causes of Poverty (Garibi ke Karan):**
    *   Historical (Aitihasik): British colonial rule ke time low economic development.
    *   Economic (Arthik): Slow growth rate, unemployment, income inequality, unequal distribution of land/resources.
    *   Demographic (Jansankhyaki): High population growth.
    *   Social/Cultural (Samajik/Sanskritik): Social obligations, religious ceremonies par kharch, indebtedness (karz mein doobna).

9.  **Anti-Poverty Measures (Garibi Nivaran Upay):**
    *   Sarkar dwara garibi kam karne ke liye uthaye gaye kadam.
    *   **Two Planks Strategy:**
        1.  Promotion of Economic Growth (Arthik vikas ko badhava dena).
        2.  Targeted Anti-poverty Programmes (Lakshit garibi-virodhi karyakram).

10. **Challenges Ahead (Aage ki Chunautiyan):**
    *   Garibi kam karne mein progress ke bawajood bachi hui chunautiyan.
    *   **Human Poverty (Manav Garibi):** Garibi ko sirf income se nahi, balki education, health, job security, self-confidence, discrimination se mukti jaise aspects se bhi dekhna.

## 📘 Key Learnings
**Vistar Se Samajhein (Detailed Explanations) 📈**

1.  **Garibi Ke Aayam (Dimensions of Poverty):**
    *   Chapter ki shuruaat mein **Ram Saran (Urban case)** aur **Lakha Singh (Rural case)** ki kahaniyan batati hain ki garibi sirf paise ki kami nahi hai. Yeh bhookh, beghari, school na ja pana, ilaaj na karwa pana, saaf paani/safai ki kami, regular kaam na milna aur majboori (helplessness) ka bhi naam hai. Poor people ko aksar har jagah ill-treatment sehna padta hai.
    *   **Diagram/Example:** Ram Saran ki family (6 log) Rs 1500/month mein گزارا karti hai, bachhe school nahi jate, health care nahi hai. Lakha Singh ki family (8 log) ko roz khana nahi milta, kachhe ghar mein rehte hain, pita ki TB se death ho gayi (ilaaj ki kami).

2.  **Social Exclusion & Vulnerability:**
    *   **Social Exclusion:** Kaise kuch groups ko society mein opportunities aur benefits se 'bahar' rakha jata hai. Jaise Bharat mein caste system ke karan kuch jaatiyon ko barabar mauke nahi milte. Isse income kam ho sakti hai, par iska asar usse bhi zyada bura ho sakta hai.
    *   **Vulnerability:** Yeh batata hai ki kaun se log 'risk' par hain. Jaise, ek SC/ST family ya ek vidhwa (widow) ke garib hone ya rehne ki sambhavna zyada hoti hai. Unke paas assets, education, health, job ke options kam hote hain. Natural disasters (prakritik aapda) ya economic shocks ke time unpar bura asar padne ki probability bhi zyada hoti hai.

3.  **Poverty Line Explained:**
    *   Yeh ek tool hai garibi ko measure karne ka. India mein, yeh **calorie requirement** (Rural: 2400, Urban: 2100) aur unhe khareedne ke liye zaroori **minimum monthly expenditure** par based hai.
    *   **2011-12 figures:** Rural areas mein Rs 816 per person/month (yaani 5 logon ki family ke liye approx Rs 4080/month) aur Urban areas mein Rs 1000 per person/month (yaani 5 logon ki family ke liye approx Rs 5000/month). Urban amount zyada hai kyunki shehron mein essential products mehenge hote hain.
    *   **NSSO** yeh data collect karti hai. World Bank ka international standard ($2.15/day) alag hai, isliye international comparisons mein figures different ho sakte hain.

4.  **India Mein Garibi Ke Trends (Trends in India):**
    *   **Table 3.1 (Tendulkar Methodology):** Dikhaata hai ki poverty ratio (garibi anupat) India mein kam hua hai.
        *   1993-94: ~45%
        *   2004-05: ~37.2%
        *   2011-12: ~22%
    *   Absolute number of poor (garibon ki sankhya) bhi 2004-05 (407 million) se 2011-12 (270 million) mein kam hui. Average annual decline 2.2 percentage points tha is period mein.

5.  **Sabse Kamzor Kaun? (Vulnerable Groups):**
    *   **Graph 3.1 (2011-12):** Jabki India ka average poverty ratio 22% tha:
        *   Scheduled Tribes (ST): 43% poor
        *   Scheduled Castes (SC): 29% poor
        *   Rural Agricultural Labourers: 34% poor
        *   Urban Casual Labourers: 34% poor
    *   Yeh dikhata hai ki in groups mein garibi ki samasya zyada gambhir (serious) hai.
    *   **Sivaraman ki kahani (Tamil Nadu):** Ek SC (cobbler) agricultural labourer ki family mein intra-family inequality dikhti hai – woh doodh sirf apne bete ke liye khareedta hai, betiyon ki padhai par kharch nahi karna chahta. Mahilaon ko same kaam ke liye kam paisa milta hai.

6.  **Rajyon Mein Asamaanata (Inter-State Disparities):**
    *   **Graph 3.2 (2011-12):** Poverty har state mein ek jaisi nahi hai.
        *   **Highest Poverty Ratio:** Bihar (33.7%), Odisha (32.6%). Madhya Pradesh, Assam, Uttar Pradesh mein bhi All India level se zyada poverty thi.
        *   **Significant Decline:** Kerala, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, West Bengal mein poverty kaafi kam hui hai.
        *   **Reasons for Success:** Punjab/Haryana (high agricultural growth), Kerala (human resource development), West Bengal (land reforms), Andhra/Tamil Nadu (Public Distribution System - PDS).

7.  **Duniya Mein Garibi (Global Poverty Scenario):**
    *   **World Bank Data ($2.15/day):** Extreme poverty duniya mein kam hui hai (2010 mein 16.27% se 2019 mein 9.05%).
    *   **Regional Differences:**
        *   **China & Southeast Asia:** Rapid economic growth aur human resource investment se poverty tezi se kam hui. China mein 2020 tak 0.1% reh gayi.
        *   **South Asia (India, Pakistan, etc.):** Decline hua hai (2017 mein 12.8% se 2021 mein 10.9%).
        *   **Sub-Saharan Africa:** Poverty abhi bhi bahut high hai (2019 mein 35.4%), aur future mein extreme poor ka sabse bada concentration yahan hone ka anuman hai.
        *   **Latin America & Caribbean:** Poverty thodi badhi hai (2017 mein 4.4% se 2021 mein 4.6%).
    *   **UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG):** 2030 tak har tarah ki poverty ko khatm karna.

8.  **Garibi Ke Mukhya Karan (Major Causes of Poverty in India):**
    *   **British Rule:** Colonial policies ne traditional handicrafts ko barbaad kiya aur industrial development ko roka.
    *   **Slow Economic Growth & Population Growth:** Azadi ke baad shuru ke dashakon mein growth rate kam thi aur population tezi se badhi, jisse per capita income kam rahi.
    *   **Unemployment:** Agriculture aur Industry mein itne jobs create nahi hue jitne zaroori the. Log shehron mein rickshaw pullers, vendors jaise kaam karne lage.
    *   **Inequality:** Land aur dusre resources ka unequal distribution. Land reforms theek se implement nahi hue.
    *   **Social Factors & Indebtedness:** Log social customs (shaadi, etc.) par bahut kharch karte hain. Chhote kisan inputs ke liye karz lete hain aur garibi ke karan chuka nahi paate, jisse woh karz ke jaal (debt trap) mein phans jate hain. Indebtedness garibi ka karan bhi hai aur parinam bhi.

9.  **Sarkari Upay (Anti-Poverty Measures):**
    *   **Strategy:** Economic growth ko badhana (jisse opportunities badhein) + Specific programmes jo seedhe garibon tak pahunche.
    *   **Key Programmes:**
        *   **Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) 2005:** Rural households ko saal mein 100 din ka wage employment guarantee karna. Livelihood security dena. Sustainable development (drought, deforestation rokna) bhi aim hai. 1/3rd jobs women ke liye reserved. Wages state-wise alag hain (e.g., 2018 mein Haryana mein Rs 281/day, Bihar/Jharkhand mein Rs 168/day).
        *   **Prime Minister Rozgar Yojana (PMRY) 1993:** Educated unemployed youth ko self-employment (apna kaam shuru karne) ke liye help karna.
        *   **Rural Employment Generation Programme (REGP) 1995:** Rural areas/small towns mein self-employment opportunities create karna.
        *   **Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana (SGSY) 1999:** Poor families ko Self Help Groups (SHGs) mein organize karke bank credit aur government subsidy ke mix se poverty line se upar lana.
        *   **Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yojana (PMGY) 2000:** States ko basic services (health, education, shelter, drinking water, electrification) ke liye additional central assistance dena.
        *   **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY):** Poorest of the poor ko food grains provide karna (details next chapter mein).
    *   **Effectiveness Issues:** In programmes ke results mixed rahe hain. Reasons: Lack of proper implementation, wrong targeting (sahi logon tak na pahunchna), schemes ki overlapping. Ab proper monitoring par zor diya ja raha hai.

10. **Human Poverty - Ek Badi Chunauti:**
    *   Official poverty line sirf "minimum subsistence" (bas guzara karne layak) level ko dekhti hai, "reasonable" level of living ko nahi.
    *   Scholars kehte hain ki hamein **Human Poverty** ke concept ko dekhna chahiye. Kya logon ke paas sirf khana hai, ya education, shelter, health care, job security, self-confidence bhi hai? Kya woh caste/gender discrimination se free hain? Kya child labour abhi bhi hai?
    *   Development ke saath poverty ki definition bhi badalti hai. Garibi hatana ek **moving target** hai. Agle kuch saalon mein shayad sabko minimum income mil jaye, par health, education, job security, gender equality, dignity for the poor jaise bade challenges bane rahenge.
