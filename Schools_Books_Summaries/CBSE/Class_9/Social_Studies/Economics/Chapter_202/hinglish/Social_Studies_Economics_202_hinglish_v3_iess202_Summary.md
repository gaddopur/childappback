# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 202: Log Ek Sansadhan (People as Resource)

## 🌟 Core Concepts - Mukhya Avdharnayein 📊

1.  **Log Ek Sansadhan (People as Resource):**
    *   Population ko liability (bojh) nahi, balki economy ke liye ek asset (sampatti) samajhna.
    *   Ye desh ke working people ki skills aur abilities ko refer karta hai.
2.  **Manav Punji (Human Capital):**
    *   Jab population mein education (shiksha), training (prashikshan), aur medical care (swasthya seva) ke roop mein investment (nivesh) kiya jaata hai, toh woh human capital ban jaati hai.
    *   Yeh logon mein maujood skills aur productive knowledge ka stock hai.
3.  **Manav Punji Nirman (Human Capital Formation):**
    *   Existing human resource ko education aur health ke zariye develop karna.
    *   Yeh desh ki productive power ko badhata hai, bilkul 'physical capital formation' ki tarah.
    *   **Investment ke Returns:** Higher income (uchh aay), higher productivity (uchh utpadakta).
    *   **Virtuous Cycle (Achha Chakra):** Educated/healthy parents apne bachchon par zyada invest karte hain.
    *   **Vicious Cycle (Bura Chakra):** Uneducated/unhealthy parents apne bachchon ko bhi disadvantaged state mein rakhte hain.
4.  **Arthik Gatividhiyan (Economic Activities):**
    *   **Teen Sectors:**
        *   **Primary (Prathmik):** Agriculture, forestry, fishing, mining, etc.
        *   **Secondary (Dwitiyak):** Manufacturing (Vinirman).
        *   **Tertiary (Tritiyak):** Trade, transport, banking, education, health, tourism, services, etc.
    *   **Do Prakar:**
        *   **Market Activities (Bazar Gatividhiyan):** Pay ya profit ke liye ki gayi activities (goods/services production, government service).
        *   **Non-Market Activities (Gair-Bazar Gatividhiyan):** Self-consumption (swayam-upbhog) ke liye production (e.g., ghar ka kaam).
5.  **Jansankhya ki Gunvatta (Quality of Population):**
    *   Factors par depend karti hai:
        *   **Literacy Rate (Saksharta Dar):** Padhe likhe logon ka percentage.
        *   **Health (Swasthya):** Life expectancy (jeevan pratyasha) se pata chalta hai.
        *   **Skill Formation (Kaushal Nirman):** Logon dwara prapt ki gayi skills.
    *   Desh ki growth rate (vikas dar) decide karti hai.
6.  **Berozgari (Unemployment):**
    *   Jab log current wages (mazdoori) par kaam karne ko taiyar hain, lekin unhe job nahi milti.
    *   **Prakar (Types):**
        *   **Seasonal (Mausami):** Saal ke kuch mahino mein job na milna (e.g., agriculture mein).
        *   **Disguised (Prachhann / Chhipi hui):** Log kaam karte hue dikhte hain, par unki zaroorat nahi hoti (e.g., khet mein zaroorat se zyada log).
        *   **Educated (Shikshit):** Padhe likhe logon ko job na milna (urban areas mein common).
    *   **Impacts:** Manpower resource ka wastage, nirasha, economic overload, quality of life mein giravat.

## 📘 Key Learnings - Mukhya Baatein 📈

1.  **Population as an Asset:** Chapter ka main focus hai ki population ko ek 'resource' ya 'human capital' ke roop mein dekha jaye. Jab hum logon ki education aur health mein invest karte hain, toh woh desh ki economy ke liye valuable ban jaate hain. Unki productivity badhti hai, jisse Gross National Product (GNP) mein contribution hota hai.
    *   **Example:** Sakal ki story dikhati hai ki kaise education ne use ek achhi job dilayi aur uski productivity badhai. Vilas ki story iske vipreet hai, jahan education aur health ki kami ne use kam income wale kaam mein phansa diya.

2.  **Investment in Human Capital:** Education, training, aur health par kiya gaya kharch ek investment hai, jiska return future mein milta hai – jaise higher income aur society mein greater contribution. Yeh physical capital (machines, buildings) mein investment jaisa hi hai.
    *   **Benefit:** Educated aur healthy log sirf khud hi zyada nahi kamate, balki unke fayde society ke doosre logon tak bhi pahunchte hain. Human resource, land aur capital se superior hai kyunki yeh unka istemal kar sakta hai; land aur capital khud se useful nahi ho sakte.
    *   **Example:** Japan ne natural resources na hote hue bhi human resource (education, health) mein invest karke development hasil kiya.

3.  **Economic Activities & Gender Division:** Log alag-alag kaam karte hain jo Primary, Secondary, ya Tertiary sectors mein aate hain. Market activities (paison ke liye) aur Non-market activities (khud ke liye) hoti hain.
    *   **Gender Aspect:** Aksar gharon mein kaam ka batwara hota hai - mahilayen gharelu kaam (domestic chores) dekhti hain aur purush bahar kaam karte hain. Mahilaon ke gharelu kaam ko National Income mein recognize nahi kiya jaata, halanki yeh bahut important hai. Jab mahilayen labour market mein enter karti hain, unki earning education aur skill par depend karti hai, lekin aksar unhe पुरुषों (men) se kam pay milti hai, khaaskar agar unki education/skill kam ho.

4.  **Quality of Population Matters:** Desh ka vikas wahan ki population ki quality par nirbhar karta hai – yani log kitne literate (sakshar), healthy (swasth), aur skilled (kushal) hain.
    *   **Education:** Education na sirf individual growth (Sakal ki tarah) ke liye zaroori hai, balki society ki growth, national income, cultural richness, aur governance ki efficiency bhi badhati hai. Sarkar ne Universal Elementary Education ke liye 'Sarva Siksha Abhiyan' jaise kadam uthaye hain. Mid-day meal scheme se attendance aur nutrition improve karne ki koshish ki gayi hai.
    *   **Health:** Achhi health logon ko apni potential realize karne aur illness se ladne mein madad karti hai. Healthy workers zyada productive hote hain. Sarkar ne health infrastructure improve karne aur healthcare access badhane par focus kiya hai, jisse life expectancy badhi hai aur Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) kam hui hai.

5.  **Understanding Unemployment:** Berozgari tab hoti hai jab log kaam karna chahte hain par unhe kaam nahi milta. India mein rural areas mein seasonal aur disguised unemployment common hai, jabki urban areas mein educated unemployment ek badi samasya hai.
    *   **Consequences:** Unemployment se human resource waste hota hai, logon mein hopelessness aati hai, families par financial burden badhta hai, aur overall economy par negative impact padta hai. Yeh ek depressed economy ka indicator hai.
    *   **India Context:** India mein unemployment rate statistically kam dikh sakta hai kyunki bahut se log kam income/productivity wale kaam mein lage hain (underemployed), jise disguised unemployment bhi kehte hain, khaaskar agriculture mein.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context - Rashtriya Sandarbh 📊

*   **Human Capital Investment:** India ne Green Revolution (behtar technology aur knowledge se agriculture productivity badhana) aur IT Revolution (human capital ka importance material/machinery se zyada hona) ke zariye human capital ki power dekhi hai.
*   **Education Initiatives:**
    *   **Sarva Siksha Abhiyan (SSA):** 6-14 saal ke bachchon ko elementary education provide karne ka significant step.
    *   **Mid-day Meal Scheme:** School attendance, retention aur bachchon ka nutritional status improve karne ke liye.
    *   **Navodaya Vidyalaya:** Har district mein pace-setting schools ka establishment.
    *   **Literacy Data:** Literacy rate 1951 mein 18% se badhkar 2018 mein 85% hui. Lekin abhi bhi gender gap (male literacy ~16.1% higher than female) aur regional disparities (Kerala ~94% vs Bihar ~62% in 2011) maujood hain. Education par GDP ka kharch lagbhag 3% ke aas-paas raha hai.
*   **Health Improvements:**
    *   Life expectancy 2021 mein 67.2 years tak badhi hai.
    *   Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) 1951 mein 147 se ghatkar 2020 mein 28 ho gayi hai.
    *   Health infrastructure (hospitals, doctors, nurses) mein significant growth hui hai, lekin abhi bhi kaafi challenges hain, khaaskar rural areas mein access ko lekar. Kuch states (Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu) mein medical colleges ka concentration zyada hai.
*   **Unemployment Scenario:**
    *   Agriculture abhi bhi sabse bada employer hai, lekin disguised unemployment ke kaaran is par dependency kam ho rahi hai.
    *   Educated unemployment shehri (urban) areas mein ek badi chinta ka vishay hai. Technical skills ki kami aur available jobs ke beech mismatch hai.
