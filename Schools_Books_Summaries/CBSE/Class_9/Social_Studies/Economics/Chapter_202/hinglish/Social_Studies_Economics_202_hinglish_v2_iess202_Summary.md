# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 2: Log Sansadhan Ke Roop Mein (People as Resource)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Log Sansadhan Ke Roop Mein (People as Resource):**
    *   Jansankhya (Population) ko ek *liability* (bojh) ke bajaye ek **asset** (sampatti) ke roop mein dekhna.
    *   Jab population mein *education* (shiksha), *training* (prashikshan) aur *medical care* (chikitsa dekhbhal) ke roop mein *investment* (nivesh) kiya jaata hai, toh woh **Manav Punji (Human Capital)** ban jaati hai.
    *   **Manav Punji (Human Capital):** Logon mein maujood *skill* (kaushal) aur *productive knowledge* (utpadak gyan) ka stock. Yeh desh ke kaam karne wale log hain jinki apni *productive skills* aur *abilities* hoti hain.
    *   **Manav Punji Nirman (Human Capital Formation):** Jab maujooda 'human resource' ko aur *educated* aur *healthy* banakar *develop* kiya jaata hai. Yeh desh ki *productive power* ko badhata hai, bilkul 'physical capital formation' ki tarah.

2.  **Investment aur Returns (Nivesh aur Labh):**
    *   Manav punji mein nivesh (shiksha, swasthya) se bhavishya mein *return* milta hai, jaise *physical capital* (bhautik punji) mein nivesh se milta hai.
    *   Yeh *return* seedhe taur par *higher incomes* (uchh aay) ke roop mein dikhta hai (zyada padhe-likhe, behtar trained, ya swasth logon ki *higher productivity* ke karan).
    *   Society ko bhi *indirect* fayde milte hain, kyunki ek educated ya healthy population ke fayde un tak bhi pahunchte hain jo direct educated nahi hain.
    *   **Superiority:** Manav punji hi *land* (bhumi) aur *capital* (punji) ka sahi istemal kar sakti hai. Land aur capital apne aap *useful* nahi ban sakte!

3.  **Aarthik Gatividhiyan (Economic Activities):**
    *   Log alag-alag kaam karte hain (like Vilas machli bechta tha, Sakal ko firm mein job mili).
    *   **Teen Sectors (Three Sectors):**
        *   **Prathmik (Primary):** Krishi (Agriculture), Vaniki (Forestry), Pashupalan (Animal Husbandry), Matsya Palan (Fishing), Murgi Palan (Poultry Farming), Khanan (Mining).
        *   **Dwitiyak (Secondary):** Vinirman (Manufacturing).
        *   **Tritiyak (Tertiary):** Vyapar (Trade), Parivahan (Transport), Sanchar (Communication), Banking, Shiksha (Education), Swasthya (Health), Paryatan (Tourism), Sevaen (Services), Bima (Insurance) etc.
    *   Yeh activities *goods* (vastuon) aur *services* (sevaon) ka production karti hain aur *National Income* (Rashtriya Aay) mein *value add* karti hain.
    *   **Do Prakar (Two Types):**
        *   **Market Activities (Bazar Kriyayein):** Woh kaam jinke liye *remuneration* (paarishramik) milta hai - yaani *pay* ya *profit* ke liye ki gayi activities (e.g., sarkari naukri, samaan bechna).
        *   **Non-Market Activities (Gair-Bazar Kriyayein):** *Self-consumption* (swayam-upbhog) ke liye production (e.g., apne khet ka anaj istemal karna, ghar ka kaam karna).

4.  **Jansankhya ki Gunvatta (Quality of Population):**
    *   Yeh desh ke *growth rate* (vikas dar) ko decide karti hai.
    *   Factors:
        *   **Saksharta Dar (Literacy Rate):** Padhe-likhe logon ka anupat.
        *   **Swasthya (Health):** Logon ka swasthya, jise *life expectancy* (jeevan pratyasha) aur *skill formation* (kaushal nirman) se anka jaata hai.
        *   **Kaushal Nirman (Skill Formation):** Logon dwara prapt ki gayi skills.
    *   *Literate* (sakshar) aur *healthy* (swasth) population desh ke liye ek **asset** hoti hai.

5.  **Berozgari (Unemployment):**
    *   Jab log *willing to work* (kaam karne ke ichhuk) hain *going wages* (prachalit mazdoori dar) par, lekin unhe kaam nahi milta.
    *   **Workforce Population:** 15 se 59 saal ke log.
    *   **Prakar (Types):**
        *   **Mausami (Seasonal):** Saal ke kuch mahino mein kaam na milna (khaaskar Krishi mein - sowing, harvesting ke time kaam, baaki time nahi).
        *   **Prachhann (Disguised):** Log kaam karte hue dikhte hain, par unki zaroorat nahi hoti ya unki *productivity* (utpadakta) zero hoti hai. (e.g., khet mein 5 logon ki zaroorat hai par 8 log kaam kar rahe hain, toh 3 log disguised unemployed hain).
        *   **Shikshit (Educated):** Padhe-likhe (Matric, Graduate, Post-Graduate) logon ko job na milna. Yeh shehri ilakon (urban areas) mein common hai.
    *   **Prabhav (Impact):** Manpower resource ka *wastage*, logon mein *hopelessness* (nirasha), *economic overload* (arthik bojh), *quality of life* mein giravat, health status mein kami, school dropout badhna. Yeh ek *depressed economy* ka indicator hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

1.  **Manav Punji ka Mahatva (Importance of Human Capital):**
    *   Log desh ke liye sabse important resource hain. Shiksha aur Swasthya mein nivesh karke unhe *Human Capital* mein badla ja sakta hai.
    *   **Sakal ki Kahani:** Education mein investment se Sakal ko achhi job aur salary mili. Usne firm ke liye naya software banaya, productivity badhai. Yeh ek **virtuous cycle** (achha chakra) ka example hai - educated parents apne bachhon ki education/health par invest karte hain.
    *   **Vilas ki Kahani:** Education aur health care na milne ke karan Vilas kam income wala kaam karne par majboor hua, apni maa ki tarah. Yeh ek **vicious cycle** (bura chakra) ka example hai - disadvantaged parents apne bachhon ko bhi disadvantaged state mein rakhte hain.
    *   **Diagram Idea 📈:** Ek taraf virtuous cycle (Education -> Better Job -> Higher Income -> Investment in next generation's education/health) aur doosri taraf vicious cycle (Lack of Education -> Low Income Job -> Poor Health/Nutrition -> Inability to invest in next generation).

2.  **Economic Activities aur Gender Division:**
    *   Teen sectors (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary) mein alag-alag kaam hote hain. Jaise kheti (Primary), kapda banana (Secondary), banking/teaching (Tertiary).
    *   **Market vs Non-Market:** Geeta ka machli bechna market activity hai (paise milte hain). Sheela ka ghar ka kaam karna non-market activity hai (National Income mein count nahi hota).
    *   **Gender Bias:** Aksar mahilayen ghar ka kaam dekhti hain (unpaid) aur purush bahar kaam karte hain (paid). Mahilaon ko kam education aur skill ke karan *low paid work* milta hai, job security kam hoti hai. Lekin high education aur skill wali mahilaon ko purushon ke barabar pay mil sakti hai (e.g., teaching, medicine, administrative services).

3.  **Quality of Population - Shiksha aur Swasthya:**
    *   **Shiksha (Education):** Sakal ki tarah, education logon ko behtar *economic opportunities* ka fayda uthane mein madad karti hai. Yeh *National Income*, *cultural richness*, aur *governance efficiency* badhati hai.
        *   **Bharat mein Initiatives:** *Sarva Siksha Abhiyan* (6-14 saal ke bachhon ke liye universal elementary education), *Navodaya Vidyalaya*, *Mid-day Meal Scheme*.
        *   **Data:** Literacy rate 1951 mein 18% se badhkar 2018 mein 85% hui. Male literacy (approx 16.1% higher) aur Urban literacy (approx 14.2% higher) female/rural se zyada hai (as per text). Kerala (94%) vs Bihar (62%) mein bada antar (2011 Census). Higher Education mein GER (Gross Enrolment Ratio) 18-23 age group mein 27% (2019-20).
    *   **Swasthya (Health):** Ek swasth vyakti apni *potential* ko realize kar sakta hai aur beemari se lad sakta hai. Yeh *productivity* badhata hai.
        *   **Bharat mein Progress:** *Life expectancy* 67.2 saal (2021) tak badhi hai. *Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)* 1951 mein 147 se kam hokar 28 (2020) hui hai. *Crude Birth Rate* aur *Death Rate* bhi kam hue hain. Health infrastructure (hospitals, doctors, nurses) mein badhotri hui hai, lekin abhi bhi असमानता (inequality) hai (kuch states mein zyada medical colleges).

4.  **Unemployment ki Samasya (Problem of Unemployment):**
    *   Bharat mein rural areas mein *Seasonal* aur *Disguised* unemployment zyada hai. Urban areas mein *Educated* unemployment ek badi samasya hai.
    *   **Paradox:** Ek taraf kuch categories mein *manpower surplus* hai, toh doosri taraf *technical skills* ki kami hai jo economic growth ke liye zaroori hai.
    *   Unemployment se desh ka resource waste hota hai, logon mein nirasha aati hai, aur economy par bojh badhta hai.
    *   **Employment Structure:** Dheere-dheere log Agriculture (Primary sector) se Secondary aur Tertiary sectors ki taraf move kar rahe hain. Tertiary sector mein new services jaise IT, Biotechnology mein scope badh raha hai.

5.  **Human Capital se Vikas (Development through Human Capital):**
    *   **Story of a Village:** Ek gaon ki kahani batati hai ki kaise human capital (agro-engineer, teacher, tailor) develop hone se gaon mein nayi job opportunities bani, productivity badhi aur gaon *prosperous* (samriddh) hua. Pehle jahan koi job nahi thi, wahan kai modern economic activities shuru ho gayi.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Context)

*   **National economic/social data 📊:**
    *   **Literacy Rates:** Bharat ki saksharta dar 1951 (18%) se 2018 (85%) tak badhi hai. Lekin *male* (purush) aur *female* (mahila) literacy mein antar hai (Male ~16.1% higher). Shehri (urban) aur Gramin (rural) areas mein bhi antar hai (Urban ~14.2% higher). States mein bhi farak hai, jaise *Kerala* (approx 94%) aur *Bihar* (approx 62%) (2011 Census).
    *   **Health Indicators:** *Life Expectancy* badhkar 67.2 years (2021) hui hai. *Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)* mein kaafi kami aayi hai (1951 mein 147 se 2020 mein 28). *Birth rates* aur *Death rates* bhi kam hue hain.
    *   **Govt. Initiatives:** *Sarva Siksha Abhiyan (SSA)*, *Mid-day Meal Scheme*, *Navodaya Vidyalayas* shiksha ko badhava dene ke liye. National Health Policy ka aim healthcare access badhana hai, khaaskar *underprivileged sections* ke liye.
    *   **Economic Examples:** *India's Green Revolution* (behtar technology aur knowledge se agriculture productivity badhi) aur *India's IT Revolution* (human capital ka importance material/machinery se zyada dikhaya).
    *   **Health Infrastructure:** Desh mein medical colleges (542) aur dental colleges (313) hain, lekin distribution uneven hai (kuch states jaise Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu mein zyada hain). Hospitals, dispensaries, doctors, nurses ki sankhya badhi hai, par population ke hisab se abhi bhi challenges hain.
    *   **Education Expenditure:** Shiksha par kharch GDP ka lagbhag 3% ke aas-paas raha hai (stagnant), jo 1951-52 (0.64%) se toh badha hai, par abhi bhi kam mana jaata hai.
