# [Class 9] Economics - Chapter 202: People as Resource (Log Ek Sansadhan)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter samjhata hai ki desh ki population (जनसंख्या) ek liability (बोझ) nahi, balki ek asset (संपत्ति) hai. Jab hum logon ki education (शिक्षा), health (स्वास्थ्य), aur training (प्रशिक्षण) mein invest karte hain, toh woh **Human Capital (मानव पूंजी)** ban jaate hain.

1.  **People as Resource (Log Ek Sansadhan):**
    *   Desh ke working people (कामकाजी लोग) apni skills aur abilities ke saath.
    *   Population ka positive aspect – unki Gross National Product (सकल राष्ट्रीय उत्पाद) mein contribute karne ki ability.
    *   Human Resource (मानव संसाधन) -> Investment (निवेश) -> Human Capital Formation (मानव पूंजी निर्माण).

2.  **Human Capital vs. Physical Capital (मानव पूंजी बनाम भौतिक पूंजी):**
    *   Dono mein investment se return milta hai (higher income, productivity).
    *   Human capital superior hai kyunki yeh land aur physical capital ko use kar sakta hai; land/capital khud se useful nahi ho sakte.
    *   **Example:** Japan ne natural resources na hote hue bhi human resource mein invest karke development kiya.

3.  **Economic Activities (आर्थिक गतिविधियाँ):**
    *   **Sectors (क्षेत्र):**
        *   Primary (प्राथमिक): Agriculture, fishing, mining etc. (Kheti, machli palan, khanij nikalna).
        *   Secondary (द्वितीयक): Manufacturing (Vastuen banana).
        *   Tertiary (तृतीयक): Services like transport, banking, education, health, tourism (Parivahan, banking, shiksha, swasthya, paryatan).
    *   **Types (प्रकार):**
        *   Market Activities (बाजार गतिविधियाँ): Kaam jiske liye payment ya profit milta hai (paid work).
        *   Non-Market Activities (गैर-बाजार गतिविधियाँ): Self-consumption ke liye production (khud ke istemal ke liye utpadan, jaise ghar ka kaam).

4.  **Quality of Population (जनसंख्या की गुणवत्ता):**
    *   Depends on: Literacy Rate (साक्षरता दर), Health (Life Expectancy - जीवन प्रत्याशा), Skill Formation (कौशल निर्माण).
    *   Literate aur healthy population desh ke liye asset hai.

5.  **Education (शिक्षा):**
    *   Individual aur society ki growth ke liye important.
    *   Enhances national income, cultural richness, governance efficiency.
    *   **Virtuous Cycle (अच्छा चक्र):** Educated parents apne bachchon ki education/health pe invest karte hain.
    *   **Vicious Cycle (बुरा चक्र):** Disadvantaged parents (uneducated, lack hygiene) apne bachchon ko bhi disadvantaged state mein rakhte hain.

6.  **Health (स्वास्थ्य):**
    *   Potential realise karne aur illness se ladne ki ability deta hai.
    *   Healthy workers zyada productive hote hain.
    *   National health policy ka aim: healthcare accessibility improve karna, especially for underprivileged.

7.  **Unemployment (बेरोजगारी):**
    *   Jab log willing to work at going wages (मौजूदा मजदूरी पर काम करने को तैयार) hain par job nahi mil paati.
    *   **Types in India:**
        *   Seasonal (मौसमी): Saal ke kuch mahino mein kaam na milna (e.g., agriculture).
        *   Disguised (प्रच्छन्न/छिपी हुई): Log kaam karte dikhte hain par unki zaroorat nahi hoti (e.g., khet mein zaroorat se zyada log).
        *   Educated (शिक्षित): Padhe-likhe logon ko job na milna (common in urban areas).
    *   Impact: Wastage of manpower, hopelessness, economic overload, low quality of life.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh) 📈

*   **Population as an Asset:** Log desh ke liye bojh nahi, resource hain. Agar unhe acchi education aur health mile, toh woh human capital ban jaate hain, jo desh ki economy ko badhate hain. Jaise Sakal ne padhai karke acchi job paayi aur firm ki progress mein help ki, jabki Vilas education aur health na milne ke karan kam income wala kaam hi karta raha.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Flowchart: Population -> Investment (Education, Health) -> Human Capital -> Higher Productivity -> Economic Growth.

*   **Investment in Humans Pays Off:** Jaise physical capital (machines, buildings) mein investment se return milta hai, waise hi human capital (education, health) mein investment se bhi high returns milte hain – higher income, better society.
    *   **India Example:** Green Revolution (behtar technology/knowledge se kheti ki productivity badhi) aur IT Revolution (human capital ki importance material/machine se zyada hui).

*   **Economic Activities Classification:** Hum jo bhi kaam karte hain, woh mainly 3 sectors mein aata hai:
    1.  **Primary:** Directly nature se related (kheti, fishing).
    2.  **Secondary:** Manufacturing (factory mein samaan banana).
    3.  **Tertiary:** Services (doctor, teacher, bank wale).
    *   In activities se National Income (राष्ट्रीय आय) banti hai.
    *   **Market vs Non-Market:** Market activities = paiso ke liye kaam. Non-market = apne liye kaam (ghar ka kaam). Aksar, women ka household work (ghar ka kaam) non-market hota hai aur National Income mein count nahi hota, even though it's crucial.

*   **Quality Matters:** Desh ki tarakki sirf population size par nahi, uski quality par depend karti hai. Quality matlab:
    *   **Literacy Rate:** Kitne log padhna-likhna jaante hain. India mein literacy rate badha hai (1951 mein 18% se 2018 mein 85% approx), par male-female aur urban-rural mein abhi bhi difference hai. Kerala (highest) vs Bihar (lowest) shows regional disparity.
        *   *Diagram Reference:* Graph 2.1 (Literacy Rate Trends).
    *   **Health:** Life expectancy (log kitne saal jeete hain) aur Infant Mortality Rate (IMR - shishu mrityu dar) se pata chalta hai. India mein life expectancy badhi hai aur IMR kam hua hai. Acchi health = better productivity.
        *   *Diagram Reference:* Table 2.2 (Health Infrastructure Data).
    *   **Skills:** Logon ke paas kaam karne ka hunar.

*   **Government Initiatives:** Sarkar ne education improve karne ke liye steps liye hain:
    *   *Sarva Siksha Abhiyan:* 6-14 saal ke sabhi bachchon ko elementary education dena.
    *   *Navodaya Vidyalaya:* Har district mein quality schools.
    *   *Mid-day Meal Scheme:* School attendance badhane aur nutrition improve karne ke liye.

*   **Unemployment - A Problem:** Berozgari ek badi samasya hai.
    *   **Seasonal & Disguised:** Gaon mein common, especially agriculture mein.
    *   **Educated:** Shehron mein common, jahan degree holders ko bhi job nahi milti. Yeh 'manpower resource' ka wastage hai aur social waste bhi. Isse logon mein nirasha aati hai aur economy par bojh badhta hai.

*   **Human Capital Transforms:** Story of the Village shows kaise ek simple gaon mein jab logon ne education (agro-engineer, teacher) aur skills (tailor) mein invest kiya, toh wahan nayi job opportunities bani aur gaon prosperous ho gaya. Yeh dikhata hai ki human capital development se kaise positive change aa sakta hai.
