# [Class 9] Vigyan - Adhyay 4: Parmanu ki Sanrachna (Structure of the Atom)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
Yeh section chapter ke mukhya vicharon ko ek structure mein प्रस्तुत karta hai 📊:

1.  **Parmanu ki Vibhajyata (Divisibility of Atom):**
    *   Dalton ki theory ki seemayein (limitations).
    *   Static electricity aur experiments se sanket ki atoms indivisible nahi hain.
2.  **Aveshit Kan (Charged Particles):**
    *   **Electron (e⁻):**
        *   Khoj (Discovery): J.J. Thomson.
        *   Gunn (Properties): Negative charge (-1 unit), negligible mass.
    *   **Proton (p⁺):**
        *   Khoj (Discovery): E. Goldstein (Canal Rays), naam Rutherford ne diya.
        *   Gunn (Properties): Positive charge (+1 unit), mass (1 unit, electron se ~2000 guna zyada).
    *   **Parmanu ki Udaseenta (Neutrality of Atom):** Protons aur electrons ke charges ka balance.
3.  **Parmanu ke Models (Atomic Models):**
    *   **Thomson ka Model (Plum Pudding/Watermelon Model):**
        *   Positive sphere mein embedded electrons.
        *   Udaseenta explain ki, lekin experiments ko nahi samjha paya.
    *   **Rutherford ka Model (Nuclear Model):**
        *   Alpha-particle scattering experiment (Gold Foil Experiment).
        *   **Nabhik (Nucleus):** Positive charge aur mass ka center, bahut chhota size.
        *   Electrons nucleus ke charo taraf chakkar lagate hain.
        *   **Kami (Drawback):** Atom ki stability explain nahi kar paya (accelerating electron energy radiate karega).
    *   **Bohr ka Model:**
        *   **Discrete Orbits (Energy Levels/Shells):** Electrons sirf kuch special orbits (K, L, M, N...) mein revolve karte hain bina energy radiate kiye.
        *   Atom ki stability samjhai gayi.
4.  **Neutron (n):**
    *   Khoj (Discovery): J. Chadwick (1932).
    *   Gunn (Properties): No charge, mass lagbhag proton ke barabar.
    *   Location: Nucleus mein (Hydrogen ko chhodkar).
5.  **Electron Vitran (Electron Distribution in Shells):**
    *   **Bohr-Bury Niyam (Rules):**
        *   Maximum electrons per shell = 2n² (n = shell number).
        *   Sabse bahari shell (outermost shell) mein maximum 8 electrons (Octet Rule).
        *   Shells stepwise fill hote hain (andar wale pehle).
6.  **Sanyojakta (Valency):**
    *   **Valence Electrons:** Outermost shell ke electrons.
    *   **Octet Rule:** Atoms react karke outermost shell mein 8 electrons (ya 2 for K shell) achieve karne ki koshish karte hain (by losing, gaining, or sharing electrons).
    *   **Combining Capacity:** Kitne electrons lose, gain, ya share kiye gaye hain octet complete karne ke liye.
7.  **Parmanu Sankhya (Atomic Number - Z):**
    *   Definition: Nucleus mein protons ki sankhya.
    *   Har element ke liye unique. Z = number of protons.
8.  **Dravyaman Sankhya (Mass Number - A):**
    *   Definition: Nucleus mein protons aur neutrons ka total sum (Nucleons).
    *   A = (Number of protons) + (Number of neutrons).
    *   Notation: `Mass Number` A `Symbol` Z `Atomic Number` (jaise ¹⁴₇N).
9.  **Samasthanik (Isotopes):**
    *   Definition: Ek hi element ke atoms jinka atomic number (Z) same ho, lekin mass number (A) alag ho (due to different number of neutrons).
    *   Examples: Hydrogen (¹H, ²H, ³H), Carbon (¹²C, ¹⁴C), Chlorine (³⁵Cl, ³⁷Cl).
    *   Properties: Chemical properties similar, physical properties different.
    *   **Average Atomic Mass:** Isotopes ke natural abundance ke basis par calculate kiya jata hai (e.g., Chlorine ka 35.5 u).
    *   **Upyog (Applications):** Nuclear fuel (Uranium), Cancer treatment (Cobalt), Goitre treatment (Iodine).
10. **Sambharik (Isobars):**
    *   Definition: Alag elements ke atoms jinka mass number (A) same ho, lekin atomic number (Z) alag ho.
    *   Example: Argon (⁴⁰₁₈Ar) aur Calcium (⁴⁰₂₀Ca).

## 📘 Key Learnings
Yahan core concepts ko vistar se samjhaya gaya hai, diagrams ke saath 📈:

1.  **Sub-atomic Particles ki Khoj:**
    *   Experiments jaise static electricity aur gas discharge tubes ne ishara diya ki atom divisible hai.
    *   **Electron (e⁻):** J.J. Thomson ne cathode rays experiment se discover kiya. Ye negatively charged hote hain aur inka mass bahut kam hota hai.
    *   **Proton (p⁺):** E. Goldstein ne 'canal rays' (positively charged radiations) discover ki gas discharge mein. Baad mein inhe protons kaha gaya. Ye positively charged hote hain aur inka mass electron se lagbhag 2000 guna zyada hota hai. Atom ke nucleus mein paye jaate hain.
    *   **Neutron (n):** J. Chadwick ne discover kiya. In par koi charge nahi hota aur mass lagbhag proton ke barabar hota hai. Ye bhi nucleus mein hote hain (except Hydrogen).

2.  **Atomic Models ka Vikas:**
    *   **Thomson Model (1904):** Ek "tarbooz" (watermelon) jaisa model, jisme positive charge red part ki tarah faila hua hai aur electrons beej (seeds) ki tarah embedded hain. Isne atom ki neutrality samjhai par Rutherford ke experiment ke results nahi. (Dekhein Fig. 4.1).
    *   **Rutherford Model (1911):** Alpha particle scattering experiment ( सोने की पन्नी - gold foil par alpha particles ki bombardment) ke adhar par:
        *   *Observations:* Zyadaatar alpha particles seedhe nikal gaye, kuch small angles se deflect hue, bahut kam (1 in 12000) wapas aa gaye (rebound).
        *   *Conclusions:* Atom ka zyadaatar hissa khali (empty) hai. Positive charge aur mass ek bahut chhote volume mein concentrated hai jise **nucleus** (नाभिक) kehte hain. Electrons nucleus ke charo taraf chakkar lagate hain. (Dekhein Fig. 4.2).
        *   *Limitation:* Stable nahi ho sakta kyunki circular motion mein electron accelerate karega aur energy radiate karke nucleus mein gir jayega.
    *   **Bohr Model (1913):** Rutherford model ki kami ko door kiya:
        *   Electrons sirf kuch **fixed energy levels** ya **shells** (K, L, M, N...) mein hi revolve kar sakte hain.
        *   In orbits mein revolve karte hue electrons energy radiate **nahi** karte.
        *   Ye model atom ki stability ko explain karta hai. (Dekhein Fig. 4.3).

3.  **Electrons ka Shells mein Arrangement (Bohr-Bury Scheme):**
    *   **Rule 1:** Kisi shell 'n' mein maximum electrons = **2n²**.
        *   K-shell (n=1): max 2 electrons.
        *   L-shell (n=2): max 8 electrons.
        *   M-shell (n=3): max 18 electrons.
        *   N-shell (n=4): max 32 electrons.
    *   **Rule 2:** Outermost shell mein maximum **8 electrons** accommodate ho sakte hain (Octet rule).
    *   **Rule 3:** Electrons pehle andar ki shells ko bharte hain, phir bahar ki shells mein jaate hain (step-wise filling).
    *   Pehle 18 elements ka electronic configuration Fig. 4.4 mein dikhaya gaya hai.

4.  **Valency (Sanyojakta):**
    *   Kisi atom ki **combining capacity** ko valency kehte hain.
    *   Ye atom ke outermost shell (valence shell) mein electrons par depend karti hai.
    *   Atoms stable hone ke liye (octet complete karne ke liye) electrons **lose** karte hain (agar valence electrons kam hain, jaise Na: 2,8,1 -> loses 1e⁻, valency=1), **gain** karte hain (agar valence electrons zyada hain, jaise Cl: 2,8,7 -> gains 1e⁻, valency=1 derived from 8-7), ya **share** karte hain (jaise Carbon: 2,4 -> shares 4e⁻, valency=4).
    *   Jin elements ka outermost shell completely filled hota hai (He: 2, Ne: 2,8, Ar: 2,8,8), unki valency zero hoti hai aur wo chemically inert hote hain.

5.  **Atomic Number (Z) aur Mass Number (A):**
    *   **Atomic Number (Z):** Number of protons in the nucleus. Ye element ki identity define karta hai. Ek neutral atom mein, Z = number of electrons bhi hota hai.
    *   **Mass Number (A):** Total number of protons and neutrons in the nucleus (A = p⁺ + n). Atom ka mass mainly nucleus ke kaaran hota hai.
    *   **Notation:** Element ko `ᴬ<0xE2><0x82><0x93>X` se represent karte hain. Example: Carbon-12 is ¹²₆C (6 protons, 6 neutrons). Nitrogen-14 is ¹⁴₇N (7 protons, 7 neutrons).

6.  **Isotopes (Samasthanik) aur Isobars (Sambharik):**
    *   **Isotopes:** Same element (same Z), different mass number (A). Example: Protium (¹₁H), Deuterium (²₁H), Tritium (³₁H). Inke chemical properties same hote hain.
    *   **Average Atomic Mass:** Naturally occurring isotopes ke mass aur percentage abundance ka weighted average hota hai. Jaise Chlorine ka average mass 35.5 u hai (³⁵Cl aur ³⁷Cl ke 3:1 ratio ke kaaran).
    *   **Isobars:** Different elements (different Z), same mass number (A). Example: ⁴⁰₁₈Ar aur ⁴⁰₂₀Ca.
