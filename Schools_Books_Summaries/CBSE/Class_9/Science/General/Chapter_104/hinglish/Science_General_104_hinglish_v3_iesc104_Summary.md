# [Class 9] General - Chapter 104: Parmanu ki Sanrachna (Structure of the Atom)

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh section atom ke structure ke mool concepts ko ek hierarchy mein प्रस्तुत karta hai:

1.  **Padarth (Matter)**
    *   Fundamental building blocks: **Parmanu (Atoms)** aur **Anu (Molecules)** (Chapter 3 se yaad karein).
2.  **Parmanu ki Avibhajyata ka Sawal (Question of Atom's Indivisibility)**
    *   Dalton ki theory: Atom indivisible tha.
    *   Lekin experiments ne sabit kiya ki atom divisible hai.
3.  **Parmanu ke Mool Kan (Sub-atomic Particles)**
    *   **Electron (e⁻)**
        *   Khoj (Discovery): J.J. Thomson
        *   Aavesh (Charge): Rinatmak (Negative, -1 unit)
        *   Dravyaman (Mass): Lagbhag naganya (Negligible)
    *   **Proton (p⁺)**
        *   Khoj (Discovery): E. Goldstein (Canal rays), named Proton later.
        *   Aavesh (Charge): Dhanatmak (Positive, +1 unit)
        *   Dravyaman (Mass): Electron se ~2000 guna, lagbhag 1 unit mana gaya.
        *   Sthiti (Location): Kendrak (Nucleus) ke andar.
    *   **Neutron (n)**
        *   Khoj (Discovery): J. Chadwick (1932)
        *   Aavesh (Charge): Koi aavesh nahi (No charge, neutral)
        *   Dravyaman (Mass): Lagbhag proton ke barabar (Approx. 1 unit)
        *   Sthiti (Location): Kendrak (Nucleus) mein (Hydrogen ko chhodkar).
4.  **Parmanu ke Models (Atomic Models)**
    *   **Thomson ka Model (Plum Pudding Model)**
        *   Description: Positive sphere mein electrons embedded (jaise tarbooz mein beej).
        *   Key Point: Atom electrically neutral hai.
        *   Kami (Limitation): Dusre experiments ke results explain nahi kar paya.
    *   **Rutherford ka Model (Nuclear Model)**
        *   Aadhar (Basis): Alpha (α)-particle scattering experiment (gold foil par).
        *   Observations: Zyada α-particles seedhe nikal gaye, kuch deflect hue, bahut kam (~1/12000) wapas aaye.
        *   Nishkarsh (Conclusions): Atom ka zyada hissa khali hai, positive charge aur mass ek chhote se center (Nucleus) mein concentrated hai.
        *   Model: Nucleus center mein, electrons uske charo taraf chakkar lagate hain.
        *   Kami (Drawback): Stable atom explain nahi kar paya (orbiting electron energy radiate karke nucleus mein gir jana chahiye).
    *   **Bohr ka Model**
        *   Sudhar (Improvement): Rutherford ke model ki kami door ki.
        *   Postulates: Electrons sirf kuch khaas orbits (discrete orbits ya energy levels/shells - K, L, M, N...) mein hi ghoom sakte hain bina energy radiate kiye.
5.  **Electron Vitran (Electron Distribution in Shells)**
    *   **Bohr-Bury Niyam (Rules):**
        *   Max electrons in a shell = 2n² (n = shell number). K(n=1)=2, L(n=2)=8, M(n=3)=18...
        *   Sabse bahari shell (outermost shell) mein max 8 electrons ho sakte hain (Octet rule).
        *   Electrons pehle andar ke shells ko bharte hain, phir bahar wale shells mein jaate hain (step-wise filling).
6.  **Sanyojakta (Valency)**
    *   Definition: Atom ki combining capacity.
    *   Aadhar (Basis): Outermost shell mein electrons (valence electrons) ko lose, gain ya share karke stable octet (8 electrons) achieve karna.
    *   Calculation: Lose karne wale electrons ki sankhya ya octet poora karne ke liye gain/share karne wale electrons ki sankhya.
7.  **Parmanu Sankhya aur Dravyaman Sankhya (Atomic Number and Mass Number)**
    *   **Parmanu Sankhya (Atomic Number, Z):** Nucleus mein protons ki sankhya. Yeh element ko define karti hai. (Z = number of protons). Neutral atom mein, Z = #protons = #electrons.
    *   **Dravyaman Sankhya (Mass Number, A):** Nucleus mein protons aur neutrons ka total sum (nucleons). (A = number of protons + number of neutrons). Notation: ᴬ<0xE1><0xB5><0xA_Z>X.
8.  **Samasthanik aur Sambharik (Isotopes and Isobars)**
    *   **Samasthanik (Isotopes):** Ek hi element ke atoms jinka atomic number (Z) same ho, lekin mass number (A) alag ho (kyunki neutron number alag hota hai). Chemical properties similar, physical properties different. Example: ¹H, ²H, ³H; ¹²C, ¹⁴C; ³⁵Cl, ³⁷Cl.
    *   **Sambharik (Isobars):** Alag elements ke atoms jinka atomic number (Z) alag ho, lekin mass number (A) same ho. Example: ⁴⁰Ar (Z=18) aur ⁴⁰Ca (Z=20).

## 📘 Key Learnings

Yahan hum har concept ko thoda detail mein samjhenge:

1.  **Atom ke Andar ke Kan (Particles inside Atom):**
    *   Shuru mein socha gaya ki atom indivisible hai (Dalton). Lekin static electricity jaise phenomena ne ishara kiya ki atoms mein charged particles hote hain.
    *   **Electron (e⁻):** J.J. Thomson ne discover kiya. Ye negatively charged hote hain aur inka mass bahut kam hota hai.
    *   **Proton (p⁺):** Goldstein ke canal rays experiment se positively charged radiations ka pata chala, jisse aage proton ki discovery hui. Ye positively charged hote hain aur inka mass electron se kafi zyada hota hai (lagbhag 1 atomic mass unit, u). Ye atom ke andar nucleus mein hote hain.
    *   **Neutron (n):** Chadwick ne 1932 mein discover kiya. In par koi charge nahi hota (neutral) aur inka mass lagbhag proton ke barabar hota hai. Ye bhi nucleus mein hote hain (Hydrogen ke protium isotope ko chhodkar).
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek simple diagram jo atom ke andar nucleus (protons + neutrons) aur bahar electrons dikhaye.

2.  **Atomic Models ka Vikas (Evolution of Atomic Models):**
    *   **Thomson Model (1904):** Ek positive "pudding" ya "tarbooz" jisme electrons "plums" ya "beej" ki tarah phase hue hain. Isne atom ki neutrality explain ki (total positive charge = total negative charge). Lekin ye model Rutherford ke experiment ko explain nahi kar saka.
        *   *Diagram:* Fig 4.1 jaisa (Watermelon/Pudding model).
    *   **Rutherford Model (1911):** Alpha particle scattering experiment par based.
        *   *Experiment:* Tez alpha particles ko patli gold foil par fire kiya gaya. (Dekho Fig 4.2)
        *   *Result:* Zyada particles seedhe nikal gaye (atom khali hai), kuch deviate hue (positive charge small space mein hai), bahut kam wapas aaye (sara positive charge aur mass 'nucleus' naam ke bahut chhote volume mein concentrated hai).
        *   *Model:* Chhota, dense, positive nucleus center mein aur electrons uske charo taraf orbits mein ghoom rahe hain.
        *   *Problem:* Classical physics ke hisab se, ghoomta hua electron energy radiate karega aur nucleus mein spiral kar jayega, jisse atom unstable ho jayega. Parantu atoms stable hote hain.
    *   **Bohr Model (1913):** Rutherford ki problem solve ki.
        *   *Idea:* Electrons sirf fixed energy wale 'discrete orbits' ya 'shells' (K, L, M, N...) mein hi ghoom sakte hain. In orbits mein ghoomte hue wo energy radiate nahi karte.
        *   *Diagram:* Fig 4.3 jaisa (Nucleus aur uske charo taraf K, L, M shells).

3.  **Electrons Ka Arrangement (Bohr-Bury Scheme):**
    *   Electrons alag-alag shells mein kaise distribute hote hain, iske rules hain:
        *   Shell ki capacity = 2n² (n=1 for K, n=2 for L, etc.). K=2, L=8, M=18...
        *   Outermost shell mein maximum 8 electrons aa sakte hain (Octet Rule). Isse atom stability pata chalti hai. Jinka outer shell poora bhara hota hai (like Neon, Argon), wo chemically inactive (inert) hote hain.
        *   Shells andar se bahar ki taraf fill hote hain.
    *   *Diagram:* Fig 4.4 mein pehle 18 elements ka structure dekhein.

4.  **Valency (Sanyojakta):**
    *   Ye atom ki dusre atoms ke saath combine karne ki power hai.
    *   Ye depend karta hai outermost shell ke electrons (valence electrons) par. Atoms apna outermost shell poora karne (octet complete karne) ke liye electrons lose, gain ya share karte hain.
    *   Example: Sodium (Na: 2,8,1) - 1 valence electron hai, ise lose karna aasan hai. Valency = 1.
    *   Example: Chlorine (Cl: 2,8,7) - 7 valence electrons hain, 1 electron gain karna aasan hai (octet ke liye). Valency = 1 (ya 8-7=1).
    *   Example: Oxygen (O: 2,6) - 6 valence electrons hain, 2 electron gain karna aasan hai. Valency = 2 (ya 8-6=2).
    *   Table 4.1 mein pehle 18 elements ki valency di gayi hai.

5.  **Atomic Number (Z) aur Mass Number (A):**
    *   **Atomic Number (Z):** Nucleus mein protons ki sankhya. Ye element ki identity hai. Jaise Carbon ka Z=6 hamesha.
    *   **Mass Number (A):** Nucleus mein protons aur neutrons ka total count (A = p + n). Atom ka mass lagbhag nucleus mein hi hota hai.
    *   *Notation:* Element ko ᴬ<0xE1><0xB5><0xA_Z>X likhte hain. Jaise Nitrogen ko ¹⁴₇N (A=14, Z=7). Iska matlab Nitrogen ke nucleus mein 7 protons aur (14-7)=7 neutrons hain.

6.  **Isotopes (Samasthanik) aur Isobars (Sambharik):**
    *   **Isotopes:** Same element (same Z), different mass number (A). Example: Chlorine ke do main isotopes hain ³⁵Cl aur ³⁷Cl. Dono ka Z=17 hai, lekin neutron number alag (18 aur 20). Nature mein Chlorine in dono ka mixture hota hai, isliye uska average atomic mass 35.5 u aata hai.
        *   *Uses:* Isotopes ke special uses hote hain. Uranium isotope nuclear reactors mein fuel ki tarah, Cobalt isotope cancer treatment mein, Iodine isotope goitre ke treatment mein use hota hai.
    *   **Isobars:** Different elements (different Z), same mass number (A). Example: Argon (⁴⁰₁₈Ar) aur Calcium (⁴⁰₂₀Ca). Dono ka mass number 40 hai, lekin atomic number alag (18 aur 20).

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

Atomic structure ke concepts universal hain, lekin inke applications aur related data ko hum Bharatiya paripekshya (Indian context) mein dekh sakte hain:

1.  **Nuclear Energy (Parmanu Urja):** India apne energy needs ke liye nuclear power par bhi depend karta hai. Hamare nuclear reactors mein **Uranium ke isotopes** (jaise U-235) ko fuel ki tarah use kiya jaata hai. Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) jaise sansthan is field mein research karte hain. *Data Point (Example):* India mein kayi Nuclear Power Plants hain, jaise Tarapur (Maharashtra), Kudankulam (Tamil Nadu), Rawatbhata (Rajasthan).
2.  **Medical Applications (Chikitsa mein Upyog):**
    *   **Cancer Treatment:** **Cobalt-60 isotope** ka use radiotherapy mein cancer cells ko destroy karne ke liye hota hai. Bharat mein Tata Memorial Hospital jaise bade cancer centers is technology ka istemal karte hain.
    *   **Goitre Treatment:** **Iodine-131 isotope** ka use thyroid disorders, jaise goitre (ghengha rog), ke diagnosis aur treatment mein hota hai. *Social Data (Example):* Bharat mein Iodine deficiency ek public health concern raha hai, khaas kar hilly areas mein. Isliye, **iodized salt (iodine yukt namak)** ka promotion kiya gaya hai taaki goitre jaisi bimariyon ko roka ja sake. Ye atomic structure ki samajh ka ek indirect societal application hai.
3.  **Agriculture (Krishi):** Kuch isotopes ka use agriculture research mein bhi hota hai, jaise ki fertilizers kitne efficiently absorb ho rahe hain, ya food preservation ke liye radiation techniques mein. Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI) jaise sansthan is par kaam karte hain.

In examples se pata chalta hai ki atoms aur isotopes ki fundamental understanding ka hamare desh ke vikas (development) aur logon ki zindagi par seedha asar padta hai.
