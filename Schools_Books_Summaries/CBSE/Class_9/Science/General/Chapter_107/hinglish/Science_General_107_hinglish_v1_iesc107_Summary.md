# [Class 9] General - Chapter 107 (Motion)

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter motion, yaani gati, ke basic ideas ko explore karta hai. Hum seekhenge ki objects kaise move karte hain aur unke motion ko kaise describe kiya jaata hai.

📊 **Concept Hierarchy:**

1.  **Motion (Gati):**
    *   Definition: Change in position with time (samay ke saath sthiti mein badlav).
    *   Rest (Viramaavastha): No change in position with time.
    *   Reference Point (Mool Bindu / Origin): Motion ko describe karne ke liye zaroori point. Example: School railway station se 2 km North mein hai (Yahan railway station reference point hai).
    *   Motion is Relative (Gati saapeksh hoti hai): Ek object ek observer ke liye moving aur doosre ke liye stationary ho sakta hai (Bus passenger example).

2.  **Describing Motion:**
    *   **Distance (Doori):** Total path length covered. Yeh ek scalar quantity hai (sirf magnitude hota hai, direction nahi). Unit: meter (m).
    *   **Displacement (Visthapan):** Shortest distance between initial and final position. Yeh ek vector quantity hai (magnitude aur direction dono hote hain). Unit: meter (m).
        *   Displacement zero ho sakta hai even if distance zero na ho (Example: Gol path par chalkar wapas starting point par aana).
        *   Magnitude of Displacement ≤ Distance.

3.  **Types of Motion:**
    *   **Uniform Motion (Ek Saman Gati):** Object covers equal distances in equal intervals of time.
    *   **Non-uniform Motion (Asaman Gati):** Object covers unequal distances in equal intervals of time (Example: Traffic mein car ka chalna).

4.  **Rate of Motion:**
    *   **Speed (Chaal):** Distance travelled per unit time. `Speed = Distance / Time`. Scalar quantity. SI unit: m/s.
    *   **Average Speed (Ausat Chaal):** `Total Distance / Total Time Taken`. Zyadatar non-uniform motion mein use hota hai.
    *   **Velocity (Veg):** Displacement per unit time OR Speed with direction. `Velocity = Displacement / Time`. Vector quantity. SI unit: m/s.
    *   **Average Velocity (Ausat Veg):** `(Initial Velocity + Final Velocity) / 2` (Only for uniform acceleration).

5.  **Rate of Change of Velocity:**
    *   **Acceleration (Tvaran):** Change in velocity per unit time. `Acceleration (a) = (Final Velocity (v) - Initial Velocity (u)) / Time (t)`. Vector quantity. SI unit: m/s².
        *   Positive Acceleration: Velocity increase hoti hai ya velocity ki direction mein hoti hai.
        *   Negative Acceleration (Retardation/Deceleration): Velocity decrease hoti hai ya velocity ki opposite direction mein hoti hai. (Example: Brake lagane par car ka slow hona).
        *   **Uniform Acceleration (Ek Saman Tvaran):** Velocity changes by equal amounts in equal time intervals (Example: Freely falling object).
        *   **Non-uniform Acceleration (Asaman Tvaran):** Velocity changes at a non-uniform rate.

6.  **Graphical Representation of Motion:**
    *   **Distance-Time Graphs:**
        *   Uniform Speed: Straight line. Slope = Speed.
        *   Non-uniform Speed: Curved line.
        *   At Rest: Straight line parallel to time axis.
    *   **Velocity-Time Graphs:**
        *   Uniform Velocity: Straight line parallel to time axis. Area under graph = Displacement.
        *   Uniform Acceleration: Straight line (sloping). Slope = Acceleration. Area under graph = Displacement.
        *   Non-uniform Acceleration: Curved line.

7.  **Equations of Motion (Uniform Acceleration):**
    *   `v = u + at` (Velocity-Time Relation)
    *   `s = ut + ½ at²` (Position-Time Relation)
    *   `2as = v² – u²` (Position-Velocity Relation)
    *   (yahan u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = uniform acceleration, t = time, s = distance/displacement)

8.  **Uniform Circular Motion (Ek Saman Vrittiye Gati):**
    *   Object moves in a circular path with constant *speed*.
    *   *Velocity* continuously changes due to change in direction.
    *   Isliye, yeh *accelerated motion* hai.
    *   Speed `v = 2πr / t` (jahan r = radius, t = time period).
    *   Direction of motion har point par tangential hoti hai (Pathar ko dhage se ghumaane wala example).

## 📘 Key Learnings

Yahan hum important concepts ko detail mein samjhenge.

1.  **Distance vs Displacement ka Antar:**
    *   Imagine aap O se A tak gaye (60 km) aur phir A se C tak wapas aaye (35 km) usi path par (Fig. 7.1).
        *   **Distance:** Total path = OA + AC = 60 km + 35 km = 95 km. Yeh hamesha positive hota hai.
        *   **Displacement:** Shortest path between initial (O) and final (C) position. O se C ka distance = 25 km. Direction O se C ki taraf.
    *   Agar aap O se A (60 km) jaakar wapas O par aa jate hain:
        *   Distance = OA + AO = 60 + 60 = 120 km.
        *   Displacement = 0 km (kyunki initial aur final position same hai).
    *   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.1 ko dekhein jo straight line path par object ki positions dikhata hai.

2.  **Speed aur Velocity mein Fark:**
    *   **Speed** sirf batati hai ki koi object kitna *tez* chal raha hai. Example: Car ki speed 50 km/h hai.
    *   **Velocity** batati hai ki object kitna tez *aur kis direction mein* chal raha hai. Example: Car 50 km/h ki speed se North direction mein ja rahi hai.
    *   Usha swimming pool example (Example 7.3): Usha 90m pool mein ek end se doosre end (A to B) jaati hai aur wapas (B to A) aati hai 1 minute mein. Total distance = 90m + 90m = 180m. Total time = 60s.
        *   Average Speed = 180m / 60s = 3 m/s.
        *   Displacement = 0m (kyunki woh wapas starting point par aa gayi).
        *   Average Velocity = 0m / 60s = 0 m/s.

3.  **Acceleration ko Samajhna:**
    *   Jab bhi velocity change hoti hai (speed badle, direction badle, ya dono), acceleration hota hai.
    *   Agar car rest se start hokar speed badhati hai, toh acceleration positive hai.
    *   Agar car par brake lagate hain, toh speed kam hoti hai, acceleration negative (retardation) hai (Example 7.7).
    *   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.6 (Velocity-Time graph for uniform acceleration) ek straight line hai jo origin se shuru hoti hai, dikhata hai ki velocity time ke saath uniformly badh rahi hai.

4.  **Graphs ki Kahani:**
    *   **Distance-Time Graph:**
        *   Slope (dhalan) speed batata hai. Steeper slope matlab zyada speed.
        *   Agar graph time axis ke parallel hai, matlab object rest par hai. (Fig 7.3 uniform speed, Fig 7.4 non-uniform speed).
    *   **Velocity-Time Graph:**
        *   Slope acceleration batata hai.
        *   Agar graph time axis ke parallel hai, matlab velocity constant (uniform velocity, zero acceleration). (Fig 7.5).
        *   Graph ke neeche ka area (area under the curve) displacement deta hai. (Fig 7.6 mein area ABCDE displacement dega).
        *   Fig 7.7 mein non-uniformly accelerated motion ke graphs dikhaye gaye hain (velocity decreasing or non-uniformly changing).

5.  **Equations of Motion ka Upyog:**
    *   Yeh equations tabhi use hoti hain jab acceleration *uniform* ho.
    *   Inse hum initial velocity (u), final velocity (v), acceleration (a), time (t), aur distance (s) ke beech relation nikal sakte hain.
    *   Example 7.5: Train rest (u=0) se start hokar 5 min (t=300s) mein 72 km/h (v=20 m/s) ki velocity paati hai.
        *   Acceleration `a = (v-u)/t = (20-0)/300 = 1/15 m/s²`.
        *   Distance `s` nikalne ke liye `2as = v² - u²` use kar sakte hain: `2 * (1/15) * s = 20² - 0²` => `s = 3000 m = 3 km`.

6.  **Uniform Circular Motion ki Pehchan:**
    *   Speed constant rehti hai, par direction har pal badalti hai.
    *   Isliye velocity badalti hai, aur motion accelerated hota hai.
    *   Example: Ghadi ki sui ki tip ka motion (agar speed constant hai), Earth ka Sun ke around revolution (approx.), satellite ka Earth ke around orbit.
    *   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.8 alag-alag shapes ke tracks dikhata hai. Jaise-jaise sides badhti hain, track circle jaisa ban jata hai, aur direction change zyada frequent ho jaati hai. Fig 7.9 mein pathar ko dhage se ghumaane par tangential direction mein jaane ka concept dikhaya gaya hai.
