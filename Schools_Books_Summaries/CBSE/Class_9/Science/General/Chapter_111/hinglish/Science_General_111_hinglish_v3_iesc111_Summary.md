# [Class 9] Dhwani (Sound) - Chapter 11

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Dhwani ka Utpadan (Sound Production)**
    *   Vibration (Kampan) se paida hoti hai (e.g., tuning fork, vocal cords, musical instruments).
2.  **Dhwani ka Sancharan (Sound Propagation)**
    *   Medium (Maadhyam) ki zaroorat: Solid (Thos), Liquid (Drav), Gas.
    *   Mechanical Wave (Yaantrik Tarang): Medium ke particles ka motion zaroori hai.
    *   Longitudinal Wave (Anudairghya Tarang): Particles ka oscillation wave ke direction ke parallel hota hai.
        *   Compressions (C) (Sampidan): High pressure/density region.
        *   Rarefactions (R) (Virlan): Low pressure/density region.
    *   Energy ka transfer hota hai, particles apni jagah se permanently move nahi karte.
3.  **Dhwani Tarang ke Gun (Characteristics of Sound Wave)**
    *   **Frequency (ν) (Aavritti):** Per unit time oscillations. Unit: Hertz (Hz). Determines **Pitch (Taaratva)** (High frequency = High pitch).
    *   **Amplitude (A) (Aayam):** Maximum disturbance from mean position. Determines **Loudness (Prabalta)** (High amplitude = Louder sound). Unit: Density/Pressure units.
    *   **Wavelength (λ) (Tarangdairghya):** Distance between two consecutive compressions or rarefactions. Unit: Metre (m).
    *   **Time Period (T) (Aavart Kaal):** Time for one complete oscillation. Unit: Second (s). Relation: ν = 1/T.
    *   **Speed (v) (Chaal):** Distance travelled per unit time. Unit: m/s. Relation: **v = λν**. Speed depends on medium properties and temperature. Generally, v(solid) > v(liquid) > v(gas).
    *   **Quality/Timber (Gunvata):** Helps distinguish sounds of same pitch and loudness (e.g., flute vs violin). Depends on the mix of frequencies (tone vs note).
4.  **Dhwani ka Paravartan (Reflection of Sound)**
    *   Sound waves solid/liquid surface se takrakar bounce back hoti hain.
    *   Laws of Reflection follow karti hain (Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection; incident, reflected, normal ek hi plane mein).
    *   **Echo (Pratidhwani):** Reflected sound jo original sound ke kuch time baad sunai de. Minimum time gap: 0.1s. Minimum distance (approx. at 22°C): 17.2m.
    *   **Reverberation (Anuranan):** Multiple reflections se sound ka persistence (goonjna) in a big hall. Kam karne ke liye sound-absorbent materials use hote hain.
5.  **Sunne ki Seema (Range of Hearing)**
    *   **Audible Range (Shravyata Parisar):** For humans: 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz (20 kHz).
    *   **Infrasound (Avashravya Dhwani):** Frequency < 20 Hz (e.g., Rhinoceros communication, Earthquakes).
    *   **Ultrasound (Parashravya Dhwani):** Frequency > 20 kHz (e.g., Bats, Dolphins, Medical uses).
6.  **Ultrasound ke Upyog (Applications of Ultrasound)**
    *   Cleaning (Safai): Hard-to-reach parts.
    *   Detecting Flaws (Dosh Pata Lagana): Metal blocks mein cracks.
    *   Medical (Chikitsa):
        *   Echocardiography (Heart imaging).
        *   Ultrasonography (Internal organs imaging - liver, kidney, uterus; foetus examination).
        *   Breaking Kidney Stones.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

1.  **Sound Kaise Produce Hoti Hai? (How is Sound Produced?)**
    *   Sound hamesha kisi object ke **vibrate (kampan)** karne se hi produce hoti hai. Jaise jab hum bolte hain, hamare **vocal cords** vibrate karte hain. Jab *tabla* bajate hain, uski membrane vibrate karti hai. Activity 11.1 mein **tuning fork** ko strike karne par woh vibrate karta hai aur sound produce karta hai. Bina vibration ke sound possible nahi hai. Hum energy use karke objects ko vibrate karate hain (e.g., clapping mein muscular energy).

2.  **Sound Travel Kaise Karti Hai? (How does Sound Travel?)**
    *   Sound ko travel karne ke liye ek **medium (maadhyam)** chahiye – solid, liquid, ya gas. Yeh vacuum mein travel nahi kar sakti (Isliye Chand par awaaz nahi sunai deti).
    *   Jab koi object vibrate karta hai, woh apne aas paas ke medium particles ko disturb karta hai. Yeh particle apne **equilibrium position (saamya sthiti)** se displace hota hai aur bagal wale particle par force lagata hai, jisse woh bhi displace hota hai. Phir pehla particle wapas apni position par aa jata hai.
    *   Yeh process chalta rehta hai aur disturbance (vikshobh) aage badhti hai, particles nahi. Ise **wave (tarang)** kehte hain.
    *   Sound waves **longitudinal waves (anudairghya tarangein)** hoti hain. Iska matlab medium ke particles wave ke chalne ki direction mein hi **aage-peeche (to and fro)** oscillate karte hain. [Refer Fig 11.5 - Slinky example].
    *   Wave mein **Compressions (C)** bante hain jahan particles paas-paas aa jate hain (high pressure/density) aur **Rarefactions (R)** bante hain jahan particles door-door ho jate hain (low pressure/density). Sound isi C aur R ki series ke form mein travel karti hai. [Refer Fig 11.4].

3.  **Sound Wave ki Characteristics (Gun)** 📊
    *   **Wavelength (λ):** Do lagatar C ya R ke beech ki doori. [Refer Fig 11.6(c)].
    *   **Frequency (ν):** Ek second mein kitne oscillations hue. High frequency matlab **high pitch** (awaaz patli/shrill), low frequency matlab **low pitch** (awaaz moti/grave). Jaise *machhar ki bhinbhinahat* (high pitch) vs *sher ki dahaad* (low pitch). [Refer Fig 11.7]. Unit: Hz.
    *   **Amplitude (A):** Mean position se maximum kitna displacement hua. Yeh sound ki **loudness (prabalta)** decide karta hai. Zyada amplitude matlab louder sound (zyada energy). Dheere se table par maaro toh soft sound (kam amplitude), zor se maaro toh loud sound (zyada amplitude). [Refer Fig 11.8].
    *   **Speed (v):** Wave kitni tezi se travel kar rahi hai (v = λν). Speed medium par depend karti hai. Solids mein sabse fast, gases mein sabse slow (at same temperature). Temperature badhne par speed badhti hai. Hawa mein approx. 344 m/s at 22°C.
    *   **Quality/Timber (Gunvata):** Same pitch aur loudness wali do alag sounds (jaise *sitar* aur *bansuri*) ko differentiate karne wali property. Yeh waveform par depend karti hai. Single frequency sound ko **tone** kehte hain, multiple frequencies ke mixture (pleasant) ko **note** kehte hain. Unpleasant sound ko **noise (shor)** kehte hain.

4.  **Sound ka Reflection (Paravartan)**
    *   Light ki tarah sound bhi surface se takrakar **reflect** hoti hai.
    *   **Laws of reflection** follow hote hain: angle i = angle r, aur teeno (incident ray, reflected ray, normal) same plane mein hote hain. [Refer Fig 11.9].
    *   **Echo (Pratidhwani):** Reflected sound ka sunai dena. Clear echo ke liye original aur reflected sound ke beech kam se kam **0.1 second** ka gap hona chahiye. Iske liye reflecting surface ki minimum doori lagbhag **17.2 meter** honi chahiye (at 22°C). Pahadon ya badi buildings ke paas echo sunai deti hai.
    *   **Reverberation (Anuranan):** Bade hall mein sound ka baar-baar reflect hokar der tak bane rehna. Auditoriums mein yeh undesirable hota hai, isliye walls/roofs par **sound-absorbing materials** (jaise compressed fibreboard, curtains) lagaye jaate hain.

5.  **Ultrasound ke Applications**
    *   Yeh high frequency (>20 kHz) sound waves hain jo obstacles ke bawajood well-defined paths mein travel kar sakti hain.
    *   **Cleaning:** Complex machines ke parts, spiral tubes ko clean karne mein. Solution mein object daalkar ultrasound bhejne se dirt particles detach ho jate hain.
    *   **Detecting Cracks:** Bade metal structures (bridges, machines in India) mein andar ke flaws/cracks detect karne ke liye. Ultrasound flaw se reflect hokar wapas aa jati hai. [Refer Fig 11.14].
    *   **Medical Uses (Bharat mein bhi common):**
        *   **Echocardiography:** Heart ki image banane mein.
        *   **Ultrasonography:** Pet ke andar ke organs (liver, kidney, gall bladder, uterus) ki images lena, gall bladder/kidney stones ya tumors detect karna, pregnancy mein foetus ki growth aur health monitor karna.
        *   **Kidney Stones:** Bade stones ko todkar chote grains mein badalna, jo urine ke saath nikal jaate hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh)

*   **Sangeet mein Dhwani:** Bharat mein sangeet ki ek rich tradition hai. Instruments jaise *Sitar, Veena, Tabla, Mridangam, Shehnai, Bansuri* alag-alag tarah se vibrations produce karke unique sound quality (timber) create karte hain. Inki banawat (design) aur material (lakdi, metal, khaal) inki sound ko influence karte hain. 🎶
*   **Medical Ultrasound:** Bharat ke hospitals aur diagnostic centers mein *ultrasound technology* ka bade paimane par istemal hota hai. Pregnancy ke dauran foetus ki monitoring, pet ki bimariyon ka pata lagana (like gallstones jo North India mein common hain), aur heart conditions (echocardiography) ke liye yeh ek important non-invasive tool hai. 🏥
*   **Architectural Acoustics:** Bharat mein bade *auditoriums*, modern *conference centers*, aur kuch bade *mandiron* ya *prayer halls* mein bhi acchi sound quality ke liye *acoustic design* ka dhyan rakha jaata hai. Walls par sound-absorbing panels, specific ceiling shapes (curved ceilings) ka use kiya jaata hai taaki awaaz saaf sunai de aur goonje nahi. 🏛️
*   **Prakriti mein Dhwani:** Bharat mein vividh prakar ke pashu-pakshi hain jo alag-alag frequency ki sound produce karte hain. Haathi (elephants) infrasound use karte hain communication ke liye, jo Bharat ke jungles mein paaye jaate hain. 🐘
