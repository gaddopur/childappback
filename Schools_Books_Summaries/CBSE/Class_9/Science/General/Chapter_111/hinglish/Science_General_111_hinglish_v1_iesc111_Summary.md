# [Class 9] General - Chapter 11: Sound (Dhwani)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Sound Production (Dhwani ka Utpadan)**
    *   Vibration (Kampan) hi sound ka source hai.
    *   Different methods: Plucking (kheenchna), striking (maarna), rubbing (ragadna), blowing (phoonk maarna), shaking (hilana).
    *   Examples: Tuning fork, vocal cords, musical instruments ke parts.
2.  **Sound Propagation (Dhwani ka Sancharan)**
    *   Sound ko travel karne ke liye medium (madhyam) chahiye (Solid, Liquid, Gas). Vacuum mein travel nahi karti.
    *   Sound waves mechanical waves hain.
    *   Propagation hota hai disturbance ke form mein, medium particles apni jagah oscillate karte hain.
    *   **Compressions (Sampidan - C):** High pressure/density regions.
    *   **Rarefactions (Virlan - R):** Low pressure/density regions.
    *   Sound waves **Longitudinal waves (Anudairghya tarangein)** hoti hain (particle oscillation wave propagation ke parallel hota hai).
3.  **Characteristics of Sound Wave (Dhwani Tarang ke Gun)**
    *   **Wavelength (Tarang dairghya - λ):** Do consecutive compressions ya rarefactions ke beech ki doori. Unit: metre (m).
    *   **Frequency (Aavritti - ν):** Number of oscillations per unit time. Unit: Hertz (Hz). Determines **Pitch (Taaratva)**. High frequency = High pitch.
    *   **Time Period (Aavart kaal - T):** Ek complete oscillation mein laga time. Unit: second (s). T = 1/ν.
    *   **Amplitude (Aayam - A):** Maximum disturbance medium mein mean position se. Determines **Loudness (Prabalta)**. High amplitude = Louder sound. Unit: Density ya Pressure ki unit.
    *   **Speed (Chaal - v):** Distance travelled by a point on the wave (like compression) per unit time. v = λν. Speed medium ki properties aur temperature par depend karti hai (Solid > Liquid > Gas).
    *   **Intensity (Tivrata):** Sound energy passing per second per unit area. Loudness intensity par depend karti hai, par subjective hai (ear response).
    *   **Quality/Timbre (Gunvata):** Woh characteristic jo same pitch aur loudness wali sounds ko distinguish karne mein help karti hai. Depends on waveform. Tone (single frequency) vs Note (multiple frequencies).
4.  **Reflection of Sound (Dhwani ka Paravartan)**
    *   Sound bhi light ki tarah reflect hoti hai.
    *   Laws of reflection follow hote hain (Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection; incident, reflected, normal same plane mein).
    *   **Echo (Pratidhwani):** Reflected sound jo original sound ke baad sunai deti hai. Distinct echo ke liye minimum time gap 0.1s chahiye. Minimum distance (at ~22°C): 17.2 m.
    *   **Reverberation (Anuranan):** Sound ka persistence due to multiple reflections in a large hall. Kam karne ke liye sound-absorbent materials use hote hain.
    *   **Uses of Multiple Reflection:** Megaphones, horns, stethoscope, curved ceilings/soundboards in halls.
5.  **Range of Hearing (Shravyata ka Parisar)**
    *   **Audible Range (Shravya Parisar):** Humans ke liye approx. 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz (20 kHz).
    *   **Infrasound (Avashravya Dhwani):** Frequency < 20 Hz. Example: Rhinoceros communication, earthquakes se pehle, pendulum vibrations.
    *   **Ultrasound (Parashravya Dhwani):** Frequency > 20 kHz. Example: Dolphins, bats, porpoises produce karte hain.
6.  **Applications of Ultrasound (Parashravya Dhwani ke Upyog)**
    *   Cleaning hard-to-reach parts.
    *   Detecting cracks/flaws in metal blocks.
    *   Medical uses: Echocardiography (heart imaging), Ultrasonography (internal organ imaging, foetus examination), breaking kidney stones.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Sound kaise produce hoti hai?** Jab bhi koi object vibrate (kampan) karta hai, jaise school ki ghanti bajne par, ya hamare bolne par vocal cords, toh sound produce hoti hai. Yeh vibration apne आसपास ke medium (jaise air) ke particles ko disturb karta hai. Bina vibration ke sound nahi ho sakti. Aap apni energy use karke hi sound produce kar sakte hain (e.g., clapping).
    *   *Diagram Reference:* Activity 11.1 mein tuning fork se table tennis ball ka hilna vibration dikhata hai. Fig 11.2 & 11.3 mein pani mein disturbance vibration ka effect hai.

*   **Sound travel kaise karti hai?** Sound ko ek jagah se doosri jagah jaane ke liye medium (solid, liquid, gas) ki zaroorat hoti hai. Yeh medium ke particles ke through travel karti hai. Jab vibrating object aage badhta hai, toh woh paas ki air ko push karke **Compression (C)** (high pressure area) banata hai. Jab object peeche jaata hai, toh **Rarefaction (R)** (low pressure area) banta hai. Yeh C aur R ki series medium mein travel karti hai, sound wave ke roop mein. Particles khud aage nahi badhte, sirf apni jagah oscillate karte hain aur disturbance aage badhta hai. Isliye sound waves **mechanical waves** hain.
    *   *Diagram Reference:* Fig 11.4 C aur R ka banna dikhata hai. Fig 11.5 mein Slinky se longitudinal wave ka demonstration hai, jisme coils paas (C) aur door (R) hote hain, bilkul sound ki tarah.

*   **Sound Wave ki Characteristics kya hain?**
    *   **Wavelength (λ):** Ek C se agle C tak ya ek R se agle R tak ki doori (Fig 11.6c). Lambi wavelength matlab particles door-door hain.
    *   **Frequency (ν):** Ek second mein kitni waves (ya oscillations) pass ho rahi hain. Isse sound ki **Pitch** decide hoti hai. High frequency = high pitch (shrill sound, like a whistle), Low frequency = low pitch (grave sound, like a drum) (Fig 11.7). Unit: Hertz (Hz).
    *   **Amplitude (A):** Particle apni mean position se kitna maximum displace hota hai (Fig 11.6c). Isse sound ki **Loudness** decide hoti hai. Zyada amplitude = louder sound (Fig 11.8).
    *   **Time Period (T):** Ek wave ko complete hone mein laga time. T = 1/ν.
    *   **Speed (v):** Wave kitni tezi se travel kar rahi hai. v = λ × ν. Speed medium par depend karti hai. Solids mein sabse fast, gases mein sabse slow (Table 11.1). Temperature badhne par speed badhti hai.

*   **Sound Reflect kaise hoti hai?** Jab sound kisi surface (solid/liquid) se takrati hai, toh bounce back hoti hai (reflection). Yeh light ke reflection laws follow karti hai.
    *   **Echo:** Reflected sound jo alag se sunai de. Minimum distance source aur reflector ke beech 17.2m honi chahiye (at 22°C) taaki original aur reflected sound mein kam se kam 0.1s ka gap ho. Pahadon mein echo sunai dena common hai.
    *   **Reverberation:** Bade hall mein multiple reflections se sound ka der tak rehna. Concert halls mein isse control karna zaroori hota hai using sound-absorbing materials.
    *   *Diagram Reference:* Fig 11.9 reflection experiment dikhata hai. Fig 11.10 (Megaphone), Fig 11.11 (Stethoscope), Fig 11.12 (Curved ceiling), Fig 11.13 (Soundboard) multiple reflection ke uses hain.

*   **Hearing Range aur Ultrasound:** Hum sirf 20 Hz se 20,000 Hz tak ki frequency sun sakte hain (Audible range). 20 Hz se kam ko **Infrasound** (elephants, whales use karte hain) aur 20 kHz se zyada ko **Ultrasound** (bats, dolphins use karte hain) kehte hain. Ultrasound ke medical (sonography, kidney stone removal) aur industrial (cleaning, crack detection) uses hain.
    *   *Diagram Reference:* Fig 11.14 ultrasound se metal block mein defect detection dikhata hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh) 📊

*   **Musical Instruments (Sangeet Vadya Yantra):** India mein vividh musical instruments hain jo vibration se sound produce karte hain. Jaise:
    *   **Sitar/Veena:** Stretched strings (taney hue taar) ko pluck karne se vibration hota hai. Pitch change karne ke liye string ki length/tension badalte hain.
    *   **Tabla/Dholak:** Stretched membrane (khinchi hui jhilli) par strike karne se vibration hota hai.
    *   **Bansuri (Flute):** Air column (hava ka stambh) mein phoonk maarne se vibration hota hai. Holes ko band/khol kar air column ki length change karke pitch badalte hain.
    *   **Shehnai:** Reed (patli patti) ke vibration aur air column se sound produce hoti hai. In sabhi instruments ki apni unique **Timbre (Gunvata)** hoti hai jisse hum unhe pehchan paate hain.

*   **Noise Pollution (Dhwani Pradushan):** Bharat ke shehron mein traffic, construction, loudspeakers (festivals, functions mein), aur firecrackers (patakhe) ke karan noise pollution ek badi samasya hai. CPCB (Central Pollution Control Board) noise levels monitor karta hai. Excessive noise se health problems jaise stress, hearing loss, sleep disturbance ho sakti hain. Is par control ke liye awareness aur regulations ka palan zaroori hai. (Yeh ek important *social issue* hai).

*   **Ultrasound in Healthcare:** India mein ultrasound technology ka medical diagnosis, khaas kar **Ultrasonography** ka, bahut widespread use hai. Pregnancy ke dauran foetus ki growth aur health monitor karne ke liye yeh ek important tool hai. Iske alawa, pet ke andar ke organs (liver, kidney, gall bladder) ki problems jaise stones ya tumours ka pata lagane mein bhi yeh bahut upyogi hai. Iski availability ne healthcare access ko improve kiya hai, lekin iske misuse ko rokna bhi ek challenge hai.
