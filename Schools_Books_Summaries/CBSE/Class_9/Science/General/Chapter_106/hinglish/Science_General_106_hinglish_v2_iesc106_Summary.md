# [Class 9] General - Chapter 6: Tissues (ऊतक)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ)

Yeh chapter tissues (ऊतक) ke baare mein hai, jo cells (कोशिकाएँ) ka group hota hai jinka structure (संरचना) aur function (कार्य) similar hota hai.

📊 **Concept Hierarchy:**

1.  **Tissue Definition:** Cells ka group jo ek specific function perform karta hai.
2.  **Plant Tissues (पादप ऊतक):**
    *   **Meristematic Tissue (विभज्योतक ऊतक):** Dividing cells, growth ke liye responsible.
        *   Apical (शीर्षस्थ): Tips of roots & stems (length badhata hai).
        *   Lateral (पार्श्व): Stem/root ki girth ( मोटाई) badhata hai (e.g., Cambium).
        *   Intercalary (अंतर्विष्ट): Nodes ke paas (kuch plants mein).
    *   **Permanent Tissue (स्थायी ऊतक):** Mature cells jo divide karne ki ability kho dete hain (differentiation ke baad).
        *   **Simple Permanent Tissue (सरल स्थायी ऊतक):** Ek hi type ke cells se bane hote hain.
            *   Parenchyma (पैरेन्काइमा): Food storage, photosynthesis (chlorenchyma), buoyancy (aerenchyma). Thin cell walls, intercellular spaces hote hain.
            *   Collenchyma (कॉलेन्काइमा): Flexibility aur mechanical support deta hai. Corners par thickened cell walls. Kam intercellular space.
            *   Sclerenchyma (स्क्लेरेन्काइमा): Hardness aur strength deta hai. Dead cells, thick walls (lignin ki wajah se). No internal space. (e.g., Coconut husk).
            *   Epidermis (एपिडर्मिस): Outer protective layer. Stomata (रंध्र) hote hain gas exchange aur transpiration ke liye. Root hairs paani absorb karte hain. Cuticle (क्यूटिकल) water loss rokta hai.
            *   Cork (कॉर्क): Older stems/roots mein protective tissue. Dead cells, suberin hota hai (impervious to gas/water).
        *   **Complex Permanent Tissue (जटिल स्थायी ऊतक):** Ek se zyada type ke cells se bane hote hain, coordinate karke function karte hain.
            *   Xylem (ज़ाइलम): Water aur minerals transport karta hai (Tracheids, Vessels, Xylem Parenchyma, Xylem Fibres). Mostly dead cells.
            *   Phloem (फ्लोएम): Food transport karta hai leaves se doosre parts tak (Sieve tubes, Companion cells, Phloem Parenchyma, Phloem Fibres). Mostly living cells (except fibres).
3.  **Animal Tissues (जंतु ऊतक):**
    *   **Epithelial Tissue (एपिथीलियमी ऊतक):** Covering/protective tissue. Tightly packed cells, continuous sheet banate hain.
        *   Squamous (शल्की): Thin, flat (e.g., lining of mouth, blood vessels, lung alveoli). Stratified squamous (स्तररित शल्की) skin mein hota hai wear & tear rokne ke liye.
        *   Cuboidal (घनाकार): Cube-shaped (e.g., kidney tubules, salivary gland ducts). Support deta hai.
        *   Columnar (स्तंभाकार): Pillar-like (e.g., intestine lining - absorption/secretion). Ciliated (पक्ष्माभी) columnar respiratory tract mein hota hai mucus move karne ke liye.
        *   Glandular (ग्रंथिल): Secretion ka kaam karta hai (glands banata hai).
    *   **Connective Tissue (संयोजी ऊतक):** Cells loosely spaced hote hain aur intercellular matrix mein embedded hote hain. Connect/support karta hai.
        *   Blood (रक्त): Fluid matrix (plasma) mein RBCs, WBCs, platelets hote hain. Transport ka kaam karta hai.
        *   Bone (अस्थि): Hard matrix (calcium/phosphorus), framework banata hai, support deta hai.
        *   Ligament (स्नायु): Bone ko bone se connect karta hai, elastic hota hai.
        *   Tendon (कंडरा): Muscle ko bone se connect karta hai, strong but less flexible.
        *   Cartilage (उपास्थि): Protein/sugar matrix, joints ko smooth karta hai (e.g., nose, ear, trachea). Flexible.
        *   Areolar (एरिओलर): Organs ke beech space fill karta hai, support/repair mein help karta hai.
        *   Adipose (वसामय): Fat store karta hai (insulation). Skin ke neeche, organs ke beech mein hota hai.
    *   **Muscular Tissue (पेशीय ऊतक):** Movement ke liye responsible. Elongated cells (muscle fibres) hote hain jinmein contractile proteins hote hain.
        *   Striated/Skeletal/Voluntary (रेखित/कंकाल/ऐच्छिक): Bones se attached, conscious control mein move karte hain. Long, cylindrical, unbranched, multinucleate, light/dark bands hote hain.
        *   Smooth/Unstriated/Involuntary (अरेखित/चिकनी/अनैच्छिक): Internal organs (e.g., alimentary canal, blood vessels) mein hote hain. Spindle-shaped, uninucleate, no bands. Involuntary control.
        *   Cardiac (कार्डियक/हृदयक): Sirf heart mein hote hain. Rhythmic contraction/relaxation karte hain life-long. Cylindrical, branched, uninucleate. Involuntary control.
    *   **Nervous Tissue (तंत्रिका ऊतक):** Stimulus ko receive aur transmit karta hai. Cells ko neurons (तंत्रिका कोशिका) kehte hain.
        *   Neuron Structure: Cell body (nucleus, cytoplasm), Dendrites (डेंड्राइट), Axon (एक्सॉन), Nerve ending (तंत्रिका का अंतिम सिरा). Brain, spinal cord, nerves banata hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख)

Is chapter mein humne seekha ki kaise multicellular organisms (बहुकोशिकीय जीव) mein different functions ke liye specialized cells hote hain jo tissues banate hain. Yeh "division of labour" (श्रम विभाजन) kehlata hai, jisse efficiency badhti hai.

📈 **Plant vs. Animal Tissues:**
*   **Plants:** Stationary hote hain, isliye supportive tissues (mostly dead) zyada hote hain. Growth specific regions (meristems) mein limited hoti hai.
*   **Animals:** Move karte hain, isliye living tissues zyada hote hain aur energy consumption bhi zyada hota hai. Growth more uniform hoti hai. Organ systems zyada specialized hote hain.

📈 **Plant Tissue Details:**
*   **Meristematic Tissues:** Yeh dividing tissues hain jo plant ki growth ke liye responsible hain. Apical meristem length badhata hai, lateral meristem girth ( मोटाई) badhata hai, aur intercalary meristem nodes ke paas paya jaata hai. Inke cells active hote hain, dense cytoplasm, prominent nuclei aur thin walls hote hain, par vacuoles nahi hote (kyunki inhe store karne ki zarurat kam hoti hai aur division ke liye space chahiye).
*   **Permanent Tissues:** Meristematic cells jab divide karna band kar dete hain aur ek specific role le lete hain (differentiation), toh permanent tissue banate hain.
    *   *Simple:* Parenchyma food store karta hai ya photosynthesis (chlorenchyma) karta hai, ya aquatic plants ko float karne mein help (aerenchyma) karta hai. Collenchyma plants ko bina toote bend hone deta hai (flexibility). Sclerenchyma plant ko hard banata hai (jaise nariyal ka husk). Epidermis protective layer hai jisme stomata gas exchange aur transpiration ke liye hote hain. Cork bhi ek protective layer hai jo older plants mein banti hai.
    *   *Complex:* Xylem paani aur minerals ko जड़ (root) se पत्तों (leaves) tak le jaata hai. Phloem पत्तों mein bane food ko plant ke doosre hisson tak pahunchata hai. Dono milkar vascular bundle banate hain.

📈 **Animal Tissue Details:**
*   **Epithelial Tissue:** Yeh body aur organs ki covering banata hai, protection deta hai aur substances ke exchange ko regulate karta hai. Alag-alag shapes (flat, cube, pillar-like) ke hisab se iske types hote hain - squamous, cuboidal, columnar. Skin mein layers hoti hain (stratified squamous). Kuch columnar cells mein cilia hote hain (respiratory tract). Kuch secrete karte hain (glandular).
*   **Connective Tissue:** Yeh tissues ko connect aur support karta hai. Iske cells matrix mein scattered hote hain. Matrix fluid (blood), hard (bone), jelly-like (cartilage) ya fibrous ho sakta hai. Blood transport karta hai, bone support deta hai, ligament bones ko jodta hai, tendon muscle ko bone se jodta hai, cartilage joints ko smooth karta hai, areolar tissue space fill karta hai, aur adipose tissue fat store karta hai.
*   **Muscular Tissue:** Body mein movement inhi tissues se hoti hai. Contractile proteins ki wajah se contract/relax karte hain. Voluntary muscles (striated) hum apni marzi se move kar sakte hain (limbs). Involuntary muscles (smooth, cardiac) apne aap kaam karte hain (stomach, heart). Cardiac muscles sirf heart mein hote hain aur life-long rhythmically contract karte hain.
*   **Nervous Tissue:** Yeh stimulus ko detect karke body mein rapidly signals (nerve impulses) bhejta hai. Neuron iska basic unit hai. Brain, spinal cord aur nerves isi tissue se bane hote hain. Yeh muscle tissue ke saath मिलकर movement ko control karta hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

*   **Plant Tissues & India:**
    *   **Coconut (नारियल):** Iska husk (जटा) sclerenchyma tissue ka classic example hai, jo India mein coir industry ka base hai (रस्सी, गद्दे banane mein use hota hai). 🥥
    *   **Jute (जूट):** Iske stem se milne wale fibres (mainly sclerenchyma and parenchyma) se bags (बोरे), ropes (रस्सी) banti hain. West Bengal aur Bihar jaise states mein iski kheti important hai. 🌾
    *   **Bamboo (बाँस):** Iski strength uske vascular bundles aur sclerenchyma ke kaaran hoti hai. India mein construction se lekar furniture aur handicrafts tak mein iska khoob istemal hota hai.
    *   **Agriculture (कृषि):** Xylem aur Phloem ki functioning samajhna Indian farmers ke liye crops ki health aur yield (उपज) improve karne mein madad karta hai, especially irrigation (सिंचाई) aur nutrient supply ke context mein. 💧🥕
*   **Animal Tissues & India:**
    *   **Blood Donation (रक्तदान):** Connective tissue 'Blood' ki importance India mein blood donation camps aur blood group awareness se judi hai. Har saal laakhon logon ko iski zarurat padti hai. ❤️
    *   **Traditional Medicine (पारंपरिक चिकित्सा):** Ayurveda jaisi paramparik chikitsa paddhatiyon mein bhi tissues (dhatus) ka concept hota hai, jo modern science ke tissue concept se thoda alag hai par health aur body functioning se related hai.
    *   **Textiles (वस्त्र उद्योग):** Cotton (कपास), jo ki plant seed hair (epidermal origin) hai, India ke textile industry ki रीढ़ hai. Ismein cellulose ki बनी cell walls important hain.
*   **Economic Relevance:** Tissues ki study ka direct impact India ki economy par hai - agriculture (फसल सुधार), medicine (रोग निदान, tissue engineering), textiles (फाइबर), forestry (लकड़ी उद्योग) jaise sectors mein. In tissues se milne wale products ka national aur international trade hota hai. 📊🇮🇳
