# [Class 9] General - Chapter 6: Tissues (Uttak - ऊतक)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein)

Ek detailed concept hierarchy 📊 jo tissues ke prakar aur unke functions ko samjhata hai:

1.  **Tissue (Uttak) ki Paribhasha**: Cells ka group jo structure mein similar ho aur ek saath specific function perform kare.
2.  **Multicellular Organisms mein Tissues ki Utility**: Division of labour (shram vibhajan) se efficiency badhti hai.
3.  **Plant Tissues (Paadap Uttak)**
    *   **Meristematic Tissue (Vibhajyotak Uttak)**: Dividing cells, growth ke liye responsible.
        *   Apical Meristem (Shirshasth Vibhajyotak): Tips (stems/roots) par, length badhata hai.
        *   Lateral Meristem (Parshva Vibhajyotak): Girth (motai) badhata hai (Cambium).
        *   Intercalary Meristem (Antarveshi Vibhajyotak): Nodes ke paas, kuch plants mein.
    *   **Permanent Tissue (Sthayi Uttak)**: Division ability kho chuke cells, specific function perform karte hain. (Process: Differentiation - Vibhedikaran)
        *   **Simple Permanent Tissue (Saral Sthayi Uttak)**: Ek hi type ke cells se bane hote hain.
            *   Parenchyma (Mridutak): Basic packing tissue, food storage, thin cell walls, living cells, intercellular spaces. (Variations: Chlorenchyma - photosynthesis, Aerenchyma - buoyancy)
            *   Collenchyma (Sthulkon Uttak): Flexibility aur mechanical support, living cells, corners par thickened walls, kam intercellular space.
            *   Sclerenchyma (Dridhotak): Strength aur hardness, dead cells, thick lignified walls, no internal space. (Example: Nariyal ka husk)
            *   Epidermis: Outer protective layer, stomata (gas exchange, transpiration) aur guard cells hote hain. Root hairs (water absorption). Cuticle (waxy layer).
            *   Cork (Chhaal): Older stems/roots mein protective tissue, dead cells, suberin deposition (impervious).
        *   **Complex Permanent Tissue (Jatil Sthayi Uttak)**: Ek se zyada type ke cells se bane hote hain, coordinate karke common function karte hain. (Vascular Tissues - Samvahan Uttak)
            *   Xylem (Daru): Water aur minerals ka transport (Tracheids, Vessels, Xylem Parenchyma, Xylem Fibres). Mostly dead cells.
            *   Phloem (Poshavah): Food ka transport (Sieve tubes, Companion cells, Phloem Parenchyma, Phloem Fibres). Mostly living cells.
4.  **Animal Tissues (Jantu Uttak)**
    *   **Epithelial Tissue (Epitheliumi Uttak)**: Covering/protective layer, tightly packed cells, barrier banata hai.
        *   Squamous (Shalki): Flat cells (Simple - blood vessels, alveoli; Stratified - skin).
        *   Cuboidal (Ghanakar): Cube-shaped cells (kidney tubules, salivary ducts).
        *   Columnar (Stambhakara): Pillar-like cells (intestine lining). Ciliated (Pakshmabhi) - respiratory tract.
        *   Glandular (Granthil): Secretion ke liye specialized.
    *   **Connective Tissue (Sanyoji Uttak)**: Cells loosely spaced, embedded in matrix (fluid, jelly-like, dense, rigid). Connects, supports, transports.
        *   Blood (Rakt): Fluid matrix (plasma), RBCs, WBCs, platelets. Transport ka kaam.
        *   Bone (Asthi): Hard matrix (Calcium, Phosphorus), framework, support.
        *   Ligament (Snayu): Connects bone to bone, elastic.
        *   Tendon (Kandra): Connects muscle to bone, strong, less flexible.
        *   Cartilage (Upasthi): Solid matrix (proteins, sugars), smoothens joints (nose, ear, trachea). Flexible.
        *   Areolar Tissue: Fills space, supports organs, repair mein help.
        *   Adipose Tissue (Vasamay Uttak): Fat storage, insulation.
    *   **Muscular Tissue (Peshiya Uttak)**: Elongated cells (muscle fibres), movement ke liye responsible, contractile proteins hote hain.
        *   Striated Muscle (Rekhit Peshi) / Skeletal / Voluntary: Attached to bones, conscious control, multinucleate, striations.
        *   Smooth Muscle (Arekhit Peshi) / Involuntary: Internal organs ki walls mein, no conscious control, uninucleate, spindle-shaped, no striations.
        *   Cardiac Muscle (Hridayak Peshi): Heart walls mein, involuntary, rhythmic contraction, branched, uninucleate, faint striations.
    *   **Nervous Tissue (Tantrika Uttak)**: Stimulus ko receive aur transmit karne ke liye specialized.
        *   Neuron (Tantrika Koshika): Basic unit (Cell body, Dendrites, Axon). Brain, spinal cord, nerves banata hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Siksha)

Is chapter mein humne seekha ki kaise cells organize hokar tissues banate hain aur multicellular organisms mein specific functions perform karte hain.

1.  **Tissue Kya Hai?**: Tissue cells ka ek group hai jo structure (sanrachna) aur function (karya) mein similar hote hain. Jaise blood (rakt), muscle (peshi), phloem sab tissues ke examples hain.
2.  **Division of Labour (Shram Vibhajan)**: Multicellular organisms (bahukoshikiya jeev) mein alag-alag kaam karne ke liye specialized cell groups (tissues) hote hain. Isse har kaam efficiently hota hai. Jaise humans mein muscle cells movement ke liye, nerve cells messages carry karne ke liye.
3.  **Plant vs Animal Tissues**:
    *   Plants stationary hote hain, isliye unmein supportive tissues zyada hote hain, jinmein kai dead cells hote hain. Growth kuch specific regions tak limited hoti hai (meristems).
    *   Animals move karte hain, isliye unhein zyada energy chahiye aur unke zyadaatar tissues living hote hain. Growth uniform hoti hai. Animal organ systems zyada specialized hote hain.
4.  **Plant Tissues ke Prakar**:
    *   **Meristematic Tissues**: Ye dividing tissues hain jo plant ke growing regions mein paye jaate hain (tips, girth, nodes). Inke cells active hote hain, dense cytoplasm, thin walls, prominent nuclei hote hain aur vacuoles nahi hote. Ye naye cells banate hain jo differentiate hokar permanent tissues banate hain.
    *   **Permanent Tissues**: Ye meristematic tissues se bante hain jab cells divide karne ki ability kho dete hain aur ek specific shape, size, aur function le lete hain (differentiation).
        *   *Simple Permanent*: Parenchyma (storage, photosynthesis - chlorenchyma, buoyancy - aerenchyma), Collenchyma (flexibility, support - jaise tendrils mein), Sclerenchyma (hardness, strength - jaise Nariyal ka chilka). Epidermis plant ki outer protective layer hai jisme stomata hote hain gas exchange aur transpiration ke liye. Cork purane pedon ki protective layer hai.
        *   *Complex Permanent*: Xylem (paani aur minerals ka transport जड़ se upar tak) aur Phloem (food ka transport पत्तों se doosre parts tak). Ye dono vascular bundle banate hain. Xylem mein tracheids, vessels, xylem parenchyma, xylem fibres hote hain. Phloem mein sieve tubes, companion cells, phloem parenchyma, phloem fibres hote hain. Diagram 6.7 mein inke components dikhaye gaye hain.
5.  **Animal Tissues ke Prakar**:
    *   **Epithelial Tissue**: Body aur organs ki covering/lining banata hai. Cells tightly packed hote hain. Ye protection aur materials ke exchange ko regulate karta hai. Types: Squamous (patli lining), Stratified Squamous (skin, protection), Cuboidal (support), Columnar (absorption/secretion), Ciliated (mucus movement), Glandular (secretion). Diagram 6.9 mein types dekhein.
    *   **Connective Tissue**: Cells matrix mein embedded hote hain. Ye tissues ko connect, support, ya separate karta hai. Types: Blood (transport), Bone (support, framework), Ligament (bone to bone connection), Tendon (muscle to bone connection), Cartilage (flexible support), Areolar (filling space, repair), Adipose (fat storage). Diagram 6.10 mein examples hain.
    *   **Muscular Tissue**: Movement ke liye responsible. Contractile proteins hote hain. Types: Striated (voluntary, skeletal), Smooth (involuntary, internal organs), Cardiac (involuntary, heart). Diagram 6.11 mein teeno types hain.
    *   **Nervous Tissue**: Stimuli ko detect karke rapidly transmit karta hai. Neuron iski basic unit hai (Diagram 6.12). Brain, spinal cord, aur nerves isi tissue se bane hote hain. Ye muscle tissues ke saath milkar movement ko control karta hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh)

1.  **Plant Examples**:
    *   **Nariyal (Coconut)** ka husk (resi) Sclerenchyma tissue ka ek common example hai, jo India mein widely paya jaata hai aur various purposes ke liye use hota hai.
    *   **Medicinal Plants**: Bharat mein paaye jaane wale medicinal plants jaise Neem, Tulsi, Ashwagandha mein specific chemical compounds hote hain jo unke tissues (like parenchyma, glandular epidermis) mein stored hote hain, jinki wajah se unki medicinal value hoti hai.
    *   **Aam (Mango) ya Bargad (Banyan)** jaise bade pedon mein strong supportive tissues (Sclerenchyma, Xylem) aur protective tissues (Cork) ka development dekhne ko milta hai.
2.  **Animal Tissue Relevance**:
    *   **Blood Donation**: India mein blood donation camps regularly organize hote hain, jo connective tissue (Blood) ki life-saving importance ko highlight karte hain. Blood groups ki diversity bhi ek important aspect hai.
    *   **Ayurveda**: Haalaanki scientific basis alag hai, par Ayurveda mein 'Sapta Dhatus' (saat tissues - Rasa, Rakta, Mamsa, Meda, Asthi, Majja, Shukra) ka concept hai, jo body ke different structural and functional components ko represent karta hai, kuch had tak modern concept of tissues se parallel draw kiya ja sakta hai (e.g., Rakta-Blood, Asthi-Bone, Mamsa-Muscle, Meda-Adipose).
    *   **Diet and Bone Health**: Bharat ke different regions mein traditional diets (like millets, dairy products) ka bone health (Bone - ek connective tissue) par impact study karna relevant hai. Calcium aur Phosphorus ki availability bone matrix ke liye crucial hai.
