# [Class 9] Vigyan - Adhyay 6: Ootak (Tissues)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter tissues (ootakon) ke baare mein hai, jo cells ka ek group hota hai jinka structure similar hota hai aur woh ek saath milkar ek specific function perform karte hain.

1.  **Ootak (Tissue) Kya Hai?**
    *   Definition: Cells ka samooh (group) jo structure aur function mein similar ho.
    *   Zarurat: Multicellular organisms mein 'Division of Labour' (shram vibhajan) ke liye important hai, jisse efficiency badhti hai.

2.  **Kya Plants aur Animals Ek Hi Tarah Ke Tissues Se Bane Hain?**
    *   **Mukhya Antar (Key Differences):**
        *   **Movement:** Plants stationary (sthir), Animals mobile (gatishil).
        *   **Support:** Plants ko zyada supportive tissue chahiye (mostly dead cells). Animals mein mostly living tissues hote hain.
        *   **Growth:** Plants mein growth specific regions (meristems) mein hoti hai. Animals mein growth more uniform hoti hai.
        *   **Energy Needs:** Animals consume more energy.
        *   **Complexity:** Animal organ systems generally more specialized hote hain.

3.  **Plant Tissues (Paadap Ootak):**
    *   **Meristematic Tissue (Vibhajyotak Ootak):** Dividing cells wale tissues, growth ke liye responsible.
        *   Apical Meristem: Tips of stems and roots (lambai badhata hai).
        *   Lateral Meristem (Cambium): Girth/motai badhata hai.
        *   Intercalary Meristem: Nodes ke paas (kuch plants mein).
    *   **Permanent Tissue (Sthayi Ootak):** Meristematic cells se bante hain jab unki divide karne ki ability khatm ho jati hai (Differentiation process).
        *   **Simple Permanent Tissue (Saral Sthayi Ootak):** Ek hi type ke cells se bane hote hain.
            *   Parenchyma: Living cells, thin walls, intercellular spaces. Function: Food storage, photosynthesis (Chlorenchyma), buoyancy (Aerenchyma in aquatic plants).
            *   Collenchyma: Living cells, corners par thickened walls. Function: Flexibility (jaise tendrils mein), mechanical support.
            *   Sclerenchyma: Dead cells, thick walls (lignin ki wajah se). Function: Hardness and strength (jaise Nariyal ka chhilka - husk of coconut).
            *   Epidermis: Outer protective layer. Stomata (pores for gas exchange and transpiration) hote hain, guarded by Guard Cells. Root hairs (water absorption) bhi epidermal cells hote hain. Cork (chhāl) older plants mein protective tissue hai (suberin ki presence).
        *   **Complex Permanent Tissue (Jatil Sthayi Ootak):** Ek se zyada type ke cells se bane hote hain, milkar kaam karte hain. Vascular tissues.
            *   Xylem: Pani (water) aur minerals ka transport. Components: Tracheids, Vessels, Xylem Parenchyma, Xylem Fibres. Mostly dead cells.
            *   Phloem: Food ka transport (leaves se doosre parts tak). Components: Sieve tubes, Companion cells, Phloem Parenchyma, Phloem Fibres. Mostly living cells (except fibres).

4.  **Animal Tissues (Jantu Ootak):**
    *   **Epithelial Tissue (Uparikala Ootak):** Covering ya protective tissues. Tightly packed cells, continuous sheet banate hain.
        *   Squamous (Shalki): Flat cells (e.g., lining of blood vessels, mouth, skin - stratified squamous).
        *   Cuboidal (Ghanakar): Cube-shaped cells (e.g., kidney tubules, salivary gland ducts).
        *   Columnar (Stambhaakar): Tall, pillar-like cells (e.g., inner lining of intestine - absorption). Ciliated Columnar (pakshmaabhi stambhaakar) respiratory tract mein hote hain (mucus movement).
        *   Glandular (Granthil): Secretion karne wale epithelial cells (glands banate hain).
    *   **Connective Tissue (Sanyoji Ootak):** Cells loosely spaced hote hain aur ek intercellular matrix mein embedded hote hain. Function: Connecting, supporting, transporting.
        *   Blood (Rakt): Fluid matrix (Plasma) mein RBCs, WBCs, Platelets hote hain. Transport ka kaam karta hai.
        *   Bone (Asthi): Hard matrix (Calcium & Phosphorus). Framework banata hai, support karta hai.
        *   Ligament (Snayu): Elastic, connects bone to bone.
        *   Tendon (Kandra): Fibrous, connects muscle to bone. Strong but less flexible.
        *   Cartilage (Upasthi): Solid matrix (proteins & sugars). Smoothens bone surfaces at joints (e.g., nose, ear, trachea mein). Flexible.
        *   Areolar Tissue: Fills space inside organs, supports internal organs, helps in tissue repair (e.g., between skin and muscles).
        *   Adipose Tissue (Vasaamay Ootak): Fat storage (fat globules). Acts as insulator. Found below skin, between internal organs.
    *   **Muscular Tissue (Peshiya Ootak):** Elongated cells (muscle fibres) se bana hota hai. Movement ke liye responsible (contractile proteins hote hain).
        *   Striated Muscle (Rekhit Peshi): Voluntary (ichhanusar) movement. Attached to bones (Skeletal muscle). Long, cylindrical, unbranched, multinucleate cells. Light/dark bands (striations) hote hain.
        *   Smooth Muscle (Arekhit/Chikni Peshi): Involuntary (anichhik) movement (e.g., alimentary canal, blood vessels). Spindle-shaped, uninucleate cells. No striations.
        *   Cardiac Muscle (Hridayak Peshi): Involuntary. Found only in heart. Rhythmic contraction/relaxation throughout life. Cylindrical, branched, uninucleate cells. Striated.
    *   **Nervous Tissue (Tantrika Ootak):** Highly specialized cells (Neurons) jo stimuli receive karke rapidly transmit karte hain.
        *   Neuron Structure: Cell body (nucleus & cytoplasm), Dendrites (short branches), Axon (single long part).
        *   Function: Brain, spinal cord, nerves banata hai. Coordination aur control mein help karta hai. Nerve impulse transmit karta hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

*   **Division of Labour (Shram Vibhajan):** Multicellular organisms mein alag-alag kaam karne ke liye specialized cell groups (tissues) hote hain. Jaise humans mein muscle cells movement ke liye, nerve cells messages ke liye, aur blood transport ke liye. Plants mein xylem pani transport karta hai aur phloem food. Isse kaam efficiently hota hai.
*   **Plant vs Animal Tissues:** Plants sthir hote hain, isliye unmein supportive tissues (mostly dead) zyada hote hain. Animals move karte hain, isliye unmein living tissues zyada hote hain aur energy consumption bhi zyada hota hai. Growth pattern bhi alag hota hai. (Refer Fig 6.2 for meristem location in plants).
*   **Plant Tissue Types:**
    *   **Meristematic:** Growth ke liye responsible. Apical (length), Lateral (girth), Intercalary (nodes ke paas). Inke cells active hote hain, dense cytoplasm, thin walls, prominent nuclei, aur vacuoles nahi hote. (Kyunki inhe store nahi karna, actively divide karna hai).
    *   **Permanent:** Divide nahi karte.
        *   **Simple:** Parenchyma (storage, support, photosynthesis), Collenchyma (flexibility, support), Sclerenchyma (hardness - e.g., *Nariyal ka chhilka*). Epidermis protection deti hai, ismein stomata (Fig 6.5) gas exchange aur transpiration ke liye hote hain. Cork bhi protective hai. (Refer Fig 6.4 for simple tissues).
        *   **Complex:** Xylem (pani/minerals transport - Fig 6.7 a,b,c) aur Phloem (food transport - Fig 6.7 d). Dono milkar vascular bundle banate hain.
*   **Animal Tissue Types:**
    *   **Epithelial:** Covering/protection. Types based on shape/function: Squamous, Cuboidal, Columnar, Ciliated, Glandular. (Refer Fig 6.9).
    *   **Connective:** Binding/supporting. Matrix important hai (fluid, dense, rigid). Examples: Blood, Bone, Cartilage, Ligament, Tendon, Areolar, Adipose. (Refer Fig 6.10). *Socho, kaan (ear) ka cartilage mod sakte hain, par haath ki haddi (bone) nahi. Kyun?* Cartilage flexible hai, bone non-flexible.
    *   **Muscular:** Movement ke liye. Contractile proteins hote hain. Types: Striated (voluntary, skeletal), Smooth (involuntary), Cardiac (involuntary, heart). (Refer Fig 6.11).
    *   **Nervous:** Stimulus response aur transmission. Main cell Neuron hai (Fig 6.12). Brain, spinal cord, nerves isi se bane hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh)

*   **Plant Examples:** Hum India mein aam taur par paaye jaane wale plants jaise *Neem* ya *Peepal* ke pedon ki moti chhaal (bark) mein protective cork tissue dekh sakte hain. *Nariyal* (coconut) ka example Sclerenchyma ko samajhne ke liye bahut common hai. Climbing plants jaise *lauki* ya *tori* ki bel mein Collenchyma ki flexibility dekhi ja sakti hai.
*   **Animal Examples:** Hamare desh mein paye jaane wale pashuon (animals) mein bhi yahi basic tissue types hote hain. Blood transport system sabhi vertebrates mein similar tarike se kaam karta hai, chahe woh insaan ho ya gaay (cow).
*   **Agriculture & Economy:** Plant tissues ka gyaan agriculture ke liye important hai. Xylem aur Phloem ki understanding se crops mein water aur nutrient supply ko behtar manage kiya ja sakta hai. Cotton fibres (jo Sclerenchyma se related hain, though different context) hamari textile industry ka base hain. Jute fibres bhi plant tissues hain jo economic importance rakhte hain.
