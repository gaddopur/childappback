# [Class 9] Vigyan - Adhyay 1: Hamare Aas-Paas Ke Padarth (Matter in Our Surroundings)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Padarth (Matter):**
    *   Definition: Koi bhi cheez jo jagah ghere (occupies space) aur jiska द्रव्यमान (mass) ho.
    *   Examples: Hawa, paani, pathar, taare, ped, jaanwar, etc.
    *   Ancient Indian View: *Panch Tatva* (Air, Earth, Fire, Sky, Water) se bana hai sab kuch.
    *   Modern Classification: Physical properties (भौतिक गुणधर्म) aur Chemical nature (रासायनिक प्रकृति) ke basis par. (Iss chapter mein physical properties par focus hai).

2.  **Padarth ka Swabhav (Nature of Matter):**
    *   **Particulate Nature (कणों से बना):** Matter chhote-chhote कणों (particles) se bana hota hai, continuous nahi hota (jaise lakdi ka block).
        *   *Evidence:* Paani mein namak/chini ka ghulna bina water level badhe (Activity 1.1). Potassium permanganate ke kuch crystals se bahut saara paani rangin hona (Activity 1.2).

3.  **Padarth ke Kano ke Gun (Characteristics of Particles):**
    *   **Particles ke beech jagah hoti hai (Have Space Between Them):** Namak/chini/Dettol/KMnO4 paani mein ghulkar particles ke beech ki jagah mein sama jaate hain. Chai, coffee, *nimbu paani* bante waqt bhi yahi hota hai.
    *   **Particles lagatar gatimaan hote hain (Continuously Moving):**
        *   Particles mein गतिज ऊर्जा (kinetic energy) hoti hai.
        *   Temperature badhne par kinetic energy badhti hai aur particles tez move karte hain.
        *   *Evidence:* Agarbatti ki smell door tak aana (Activity 1.3), paani mein ink/honey ka failna (Activity 1.4), garam paani mein KMnO4/Copper Sulphate ka tezi se ghulna (Activity 1.5).
        *   **Diffusion (विसरण):** Do alag tarah ke matter ke particles ka apne aap mil jaana. Garam karne par diffusion tez hota hai.
    *   **Particles ek doosre ko aakarshit karte hain (Attract Each Other):**
        *   Particles ke beech ek आकर्षण बल (force of attraction) kaam karta hai jo unhe saath rakhta hai.
        *   Yeh force alag-alag matter mein alag-alag strength ka hota hai.
        *   *Evidence:* Human chain activity (Activity 1.6), iron nail/chalk/rubber band ko todne ki koshish (Activity 1.7), paani ki surface ko kaatne ki koshish (Activity 1.8).

4.  **Padarth ki Avasthayein (States of Matter):** Particles ke characteristics mein variation ke kaaran matter 3 states mein exist karta hai:
    *   **Solid (ठोस):**
        *   Definite shape (निश्चित आकार), distinct boundaries (स्पष्ट सीमाएं), fixed volume (निश्चित आयतन).
        *   Negligible compressibility (नगण्य संपीड्यता).
        *   Rigid (दृढ़). Force lagane par shape maintain karte hain, zyada force par toot sakte hain.
        *   *Exceptions Explained:* Rubber band (force hatane par shape regain), Sugar/Salt (crystal shape fixed rehta hai), Sponge (holes mein air hoti hai, press karne par air nikalti hai).
        *   Particle arrangement: Closely packed, strong attraction, fixed positions (vibrate only).
    *   **Liquid (द्रव):**
        *   Fixed volume (निश्चित आयतन), but no fixed shape (जिस बर्तन में रखो उसी का shape).
        *   Flow karte hain (bah sakte hain), not rigid, hence called fluid (तरल).
        *   Low compressibility.
        *   Diffusion: Solids, liquids, gases can diffuse into liquids. (Aquatic animals dissolved oxygen use karte hain). Diffusion rate solids se zyada.
        *   Particle arrangement: Close but can move/slide past each other, moderate attraction, more space than solids.
    *   **Gas (गैस):**
        *   No fixed shape, no fixed volume. Container ko poora bhar deti hai.
        *   Highly compressible (अत्यधिक संपीड्यता). Examples: LPG, CNG, Oxygen cylinders.
        *   Particles move randomly at high speed, hit each other and container walls, causing pressure (दाब).
        *   High diffusion rate. (Garam khane ki smell door tak aana).
        *   Particle arrangement: Far apart, weak attraction, random high-speed motion, large spaces.

5.  **Avastha Parivartan (Change of State):**
    *   **Temperature ka Prabhav (Effect of Temperature):**
        *   **Melting (संगलन / Fusion):** Solid -> Liquid. Jis temperature par solid melt hota hai (atmospheric pressure par), use **Melting Point (गलनांक)** kehte hain. Ice ka melting point 0°C (273.15 K). Melting ke time temperature constant rehta hai.
        *   **Latent Heat of Fusion (संगलन की गुप्त ऊष्मा):** 1 kg solid ko uske melting point par liquid mein badalne ke liye zaroori heat energy (bina temperature badhe). Ice (0°C) se zyada energy water (0°C) mein hoti hai.
        *   **Boiling (क्वथन):** Liquid -> Gas. Jis temperature par liquid boil hota hai (atmospheric pressure par), use **Boiling Point (क्वथनांक)** kehte hain. Water ka boiling point 100°C (373 K). Boiling ek bulk phenomenon hai.
        *   **Latent Heat of Vaporization (वाष्पीकरण की गुप्त ऊष्मा):** 1 kg liquid ko uske boiling point par gas mein badalne ke liye zaroori heat energy. Steam (100°C) mein water (100°C) se zyada energy hoti hai (due to latent heat).
        *   **Sublimation (ऊर्ध्वपातन):** Solid -> Gas (directly). Example: Camphor (*kapur*), Naphthalene balls, Ammonium chloride.
        *   **Deposition (निक्षेपण):** Gas -> Solid (directly). Example: Frost formation.
        *   *Temperature Conversion:* K = °C + 273; °C = K - 273.
    *   **Pressure ka Prabhav (Effect of Pressure):**
        *   Pressure badhane aur temperature ghatane se gas ko liquefy kiya jaa sakta hai. Particles paas aa jaate hain.
        *   **Dry Ice (शुष्क बर्फ):** Solid Carbon Dioxide (CO2). High pressure par store hoti hai. Pressure kam karne par (1 atm tak) bina liquid bane seedhe gas mein badal jaati hai (sublimation).

6.  **Evaporation (वाष्पीकरण):**
    *   Liquid ka apne boiling point se kam temperature par vapours (वाष्प) mein badalna.
    *   Yeh ek surface phenomenon (सतही प्रक्रिया) hai. Surface ke particles zyada kinetic energy hone par attraction force todkar vapour ban jaate hain.
    *   **Factors Affecting Evaporation (वाष्पीकरण को प्रभावित करने वाले कारक):**
        *   Surface Area (सतह क्षेत्र): Badhane par rate badhta hai (kapde failakar sukhana).
        *   Temperature (तापमान): Badhane par rate badhta hai (zyada particles ko energy milti hai).
        *   Humidity (आर्द्रता): Hawa mein water vapour ki amount. Humidity zyada hone par rate ghatta hai (monsoon mein kapde der se sukhte hain).
        *   Wind Speed (वायु की गति): Badhne par rate badhta hai (water vapour particles hawa ke saath ud jaate hain).
    *   **Evaporation Causes Cooling (वाष्पीकरण से शीतलता):**
        *   Evaporation ke dauran liquid particles surroundings (ya body) se energy absorb karte hain (latent heat of vaporization ke barabar), jisse surroundings thandi ho jaati hain.
        *   Examples: Hatheli par acetone/petrol/perfume rakhne par thanda lagna, Garmi mein chhat par paani chhidakna, *Matka* (earthen pot) mein paani thanda hona, Garmi mein cotton kapde pehenna (paseena absorb karke evaporate karte hain, body cool rehti hai), Thande paani ke glass ke bahar water droplets ka dikhna (hawa ki water vapour thandi surface par condense ho jaati hai).

*   **Particle Characteristics in Action:**
    *   *Space:* Jab hum *nimbu paani* banate hain, toh chini, namak aur nimbu ka ras paani ke particles ke beech ki jagah mein sama jaate hain.
    *   *Motion & Diffusion:* Garam *agarbatti* ki khushboo door tak aati hai kyunki uske particles hawa ke particles ke saath mix hokar (diffusion) tezi se move karte hain (high kinetic energy). Thande paani ki tulna mein garam paani mein ink tezi se failti hai (Activity 1.4 & 1.5) kyunki temperature badhne se particles ki speed (kinetic energy) badh jaati hai.
    *   *Attraction:* Lohar jab lohe (iron) ko peet'ta hai, toh use kaafi force lagana padta hai kyunki iron particles ke beech strong attraction force hota hai. Chalk aasani se toot jaati hai (weaker force). Paani ko haath se kaatne par bhi surface jud jaati hai (Activity 1.8) kyunki water particles ke beech attraction force hota hai.

*   **States of Matter - A Particle View:** (Dekho Fig 1.5)
    *   *Solids (Thos):* Particles bahut paas, strong attraction, fixed position mein vibrate karte hain. Isliye shape/volume fixed aur rigid hote hain.
    *   *Liquids (Drav):* Particles paas hain par move kar sakte hain, attraction moderate hai. Isliye volume fixed par shape nahi, flow karte hain.
    *   *Gases:* Particles bahut door, negligible attraction, random high-speed motion. Isliye na shape fixed, na volume, highly compressible.

*   **Changing States - Energy ka Khel:**
    *   *Heating:* Heat energy dene par particles ki kinetic energy badhti hai. Melting mein energy attraction force todne mein use hoti hai (Latent Heat of Fusion). Boiling mein energy particles ko liquid se alag karke gas banane mein use hoti hai (Latent Heat of Vaporization). (Dekho Fig 1.6). Steam se jalna zyada severe hota hai boiling water se, kyunki steam mein latent heat ki extra energy hoti hai.

    *   *Sublimation:* Kuch solids (jaise *kapur*) heat karne par seedhe gas ban jaate hain. (Dekho Fig 1.7). Naphthalene balls kapdon mein rakhe-rakhe gayab ho jaati hain due to sublimation.

    *   *Pressure:* Pressure badhakar aur temperature kam karke gas particles ko paas lakar liquid banaya ja sakta hai (LPG/CNG cylinders). Dry Ice (Solid CO2) iska example hai jo pressure kam hone par seedha gas banti hai. (Dekho Fig 1.9 for interconversion).

*   **Evaporation - Cooling Effect:** Jab paseena (sweat) evaporate hota hai, woh body se heat lekar udta hai, jisse body thandi hoti hai. **Key Terms Summary:**

*   **Matter (पदार्थ):** Has mass and volume.
*   **Particles (कण):** Matter is made of tiny particles.
*   **Kinetic Energy (गतिज ऊर्जा):** Energy due to motion. Increases with temperature.
*   **Diffusion (विसरण):** Intermixing of particles.
*   **Force of Attraction (आकर्षण बल):** Holds particles together.
*   **States (अवस्थाएं):** Solid (ठोस), Liquid (द्रव), Gas (गैस).
*   **Melting Point (गलनांक):** Temp at which solid becomes liquid.
*   **Boiling Point (क्वथनांक):** Temp at which liquid becomes gas.
*   **Latent Heat (गुप्त ऊष्मा):** Hidden heat used for state change. (Fusion/Vaporization).
*   **Sublimation (ऊर्ध्वपातन):** Solid to Gas directly.
*   **Deposition (निक्षेपण):** Gas to Solid directly.
*   **Evaporation (वाष्पीकरण):** Liquid to Vapour below boiling point. Causes cooling.
*   **Compressibility (संपीड्यता):** Ability to be compressed. Highest in gases.
*   **Rigidity (दृढ़ता):** Tendency to maintain shape. Solids are rigid.
*   **Fluidity (तरलता):** Ability to flow. Liquids and gases are fluids.
*   **Density (घनत्व):** Mass per unit volume. Generally Solids > Liquids > Gases (Exception: Ice floats on water).
