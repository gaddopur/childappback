# [Class 9] Vigyan - Chapter 10: Karya tatha Urja (Work and Energy)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
Concept Hierarchy 📊

1.  **Karya (Work)**
    *   Scientific Definition (Vaigyanik Paribhasha) - Force & Displacement ki zaroorat.
    *   Conditions for Work Done (Kaam hone ki shartein)
    *   Work Done by Constant Force (Niyat Bal dwara kiya gaya Karya): `W = F × s`
    *   Unit of Work: Joule (J) [1 J = 1 N × 1 m]
    *   Positive Work (Dhanatmak Karya): Force aur displacement same direction mein.
    *   Negative Work (Rinatmak Karya): Force aur displacement opposite direction mein.
    *   Zero Work (Shunya Karya): Displacement zero ya force perpendicular ho displacement ke.

2.  **Urja (Energy)**
    *   Definition: Capacity to do work (Karya karne ki kshamta).
    *   Unit of Energy: Joule (J) (Same as Work).
    *   Forms of Energy (Urja ke Roop): Mechanical (Yaantrik), Heat (Ushma), Chemical (Rasayanik), Electrical (Vidyut), Light (Prakash), etc.
    *   **Mechanical Energy (Yaantrik Urja)**
        *   **Gatij Urja (Kinetic Energy - KE)**
            *   Definition: Energy due to motion (Gati ke kaaran urja).
            *   Formula: `KE = ½ mv²` (m = mass, v = velocity)
            *   Depends on mass and speed.
        *   **Sthitij Urja (Potential Energy - PE)**
            *   Definition: Energy due to position or configuration (Stithi ya aakaar ke kaaran urja).
            *   **Gravitational Potential Energy (Gurutviya Sthitij Urja)**
                *   Formula: `PE = mgh` (m = mass, g = acceleration due to gravity, h = height)
                *   Depends on height from a reference level.
            *   Elastic Potential Energy (Pratyasth Sthitij Urja) - Example: Stretched bow/rubber band.

3.  **Urja ka Roopantaran (Energy Transformation)**
    *   Energy can change from one form to another (Urja ek roop se doosre roop mein badal sakti hai).

4.  **Urja Sanrakshan ka Niyam (Law of Conservation of Energy)**
    *   Statement: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed. Total energy remains constant. (Urja na banayi ja sakti hai, na nasht ki ja sakti hai, sirf roop badalti hai. Kul urja hamesha sthir rehti hai).
    *   Total Mechanical Energy Conservation (Kul Yaantrik Urja Sanrakshan): In absence of friction/air resistance, `KE + PE = Constant`. Example: Freely falling body.

5.  **Shakti (Power)**
    *   Definition: Rate of doing work or rate of energy transfer (Karya karne ki dar ya urja sthanantaran ki dar).
    *   Formula: `P = W/t` (W = work, t = time) OR `P = E/t` (E = energy transferred, t = time).
    *   Unit of Power: Watt (W) [1 W = 1 J/s].
    *   Larger Unit: kilowatt (kW) [1 kW = 1000 W].
    *   Average Power (Ausat Shakti): Total Work Done / Total Time Taken.

6.  **Urja ka Vyavsayik Matrak (Commercial Unit of Energy)**
    *   Kilowatt-hour (kWh) - 'Unit' in electricity bills.
    *   `1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J`.

## 📘 Key Learnings
Detailed explanations with diagrams/examples 📈

**1. Scientific Conception of Work (Karya ki Vaigyanik Avdharna)**

*   **Definition:** Day-to-day life mein 'work' ka matlab kuch bhi useful physical ya mental labour ho sakta hai (like padhai karna). Lekin Science mein, Work tabhi hota hai jab do conditions poori hon:
    1.  Ek **force** object par act kare (Koi bal vastu par lage).
    2.  Object **displace** ho (Vastu mein visthapan ho).
*   Agar aap ek deewar ko dhakka de rahe hain (pushing a wall) aur woh move nahi karti, toh scientific view se koi work nahi hua, bhale hi aap thak gaye hon.
*   Agar koi kuli (porter) sar par bojh lekar khada hai (standing still with load), toh bhi work done on the load is zero kyunki displacement zero hai.
*   **Formula:** Jab constant force F, object ko 's' distance tak force ki direction mein displace karta hai (Fig 10.1):
    `Work Done (W) = Force (F) × Displacement (s)`
    `W = Fs`
*   **Unit:** SI unit Joule (J) hai. 1 Joule work tab hota hai jab 1 Newton ka force kisi object ko 1 meter displace kare force ki direction mein.
    ![Diagram Description: Fig 10.1 shows a block on a surface. A force F is applied horizontally, causing a displacement s in the same direction.](Diagram description: Fig 10.1 ek block ko surface par dikhata hai. Ek horizontal force F lagaya gaya hai, jisse usi direction mein s displacement hota hai.)

*   **Positive Work:** Jab force aur displacement same direction mein ho (e.g., baby pulling a toy car - Fig 10.4). `W = +Fs`.
*   **Negative Work:** Jab force displacement ke opposite direction mein ho (e.g., friction force acting on a moving car, ya gravity ka work jab object upar uthaya jaata hai). `W = -Fs`.
*   **Zero Work:** Jab displacement zero ho (e.g., pushing a wall) ya jab force displacement ke perpendicular (90°) ho (e.g., work done by gravity on a satellite moving around Earth, ya work done by a porter carrying load horizontally).

**Example 10.1:** Agar 5 N ka force ek object ko force ki direction mein 2 m displace karta hai, toh Work Done = 5 N × 2 m = 10 J.

**Example 10.2:** Ek porter 15 kg luggage ko zameen se 1.5 m upar uthata hai. Work done by porter = `mgh` = 15 kg × 10 m/s² × 1.5 m = 225 J (Yahan humne g ≈ 10 m/s² liya hai for simplicity, NCERT uses 9.8 m/s²). Yeh positive work hai (force upar, displacement upar). Gravity ne kitna work kiya? Gravity ka force neeche hai (mg), displacement upar (h), toh Work done by gravity = -mgh = -225 J (negative work).

**2. Energy (Urja)**

*   **Definition:** Kisi object ki work karne ki capacity (kshamta) ko energy kehte hain. Jis object mein energy hoti hai, woh doosre object par force laga kar work kar sakta hai.
*   **Unit:** Joule (J). 1 Joule energy 1 Joule work karne ke liye zaroori hai.
*   **Sources:** Sun (sabse bada natural source), nuclei of atoms (paramanu ke nabhik), interior of Earth (prithvi ka antarik bhaag), tides (jwar-bhata), etc.

**3. Kinetic Energy (Gatij Urja - KE)**

*   **Definition:** Energy possessed by an object due to its motion (kisi vastu mein uski gati ke kaaran nihit urja). Jitni tez object chalega, utni zyada KE hogi.
*   Examples: Chalti hui car, girta hua nariyal (falling coconut), behta hua pani (flowing water), hawa (blowing wind).
*   **Formula Derivation Idea:** Work done (W) to change velocity from u to v is `W = ½ m(v² - u²)`. Agar object rest (u=0) se start ho, toh `W = ½ mv²`. Yahi work done uski kinetic energy ban jaata hai.
*   **Formula:** `KE = ½ mv²`
    *   m = mass (dravyaman) in kg
    *   v = velocity (veg) in m/s
*   **Activity 10.7:** Trolley ko move karke wooden block se takraya jata hai. Block displace hota hai, matlab trolley ne work kiya. Agar trolley ka mass ya speed badhate hain, toh block zyada displace hota hai, indicating more KE.
    ![Diagram Description: Fig 10.5 shows a trolley on a table connected by a string over a pulley to a pan with weights. When weights are added, the trolley moves and hits a wooden block.](Diagram description: Fig 10.5 mein ek table par trolley hai jo pulley ke upar se jaati hui rassi se ek pan se judi hai jisme weights hain. Weight daalne par trolley chalti hai aur ek wooden block se takrati hai.)

**Example 10.3:** 15 kg mass ka object 4 m/s se move kar raha hai. KE = ½ × 15 kg × (4 m/s)² = 120 J.

**4. Potential Energy (Sthitij Urja - PE)**

*   **Definition:** Energy stored in an object due to its position or change in shape (kisi vastu mein uski sthiti ya aakaar mein parivartan ke kaaran sanchit urja).
*   **Gravitational Potential Energy (Gurutviya Sthitij Urja):**
    *   Jab object ko height 'h' tak uthaya jaata hai against gravity, toh us par work kiya jaata hai. Yeh work usmein PE ke form mein store ho jaata hai.
    *   **Formula Derivation Idea:** Work done to lift object of mass 'm' to height 'h' is `W = Force × displacement = (mg) × h = mgh`. Yahi work done Gravitational PE hai.
    *   **Formula:** `PE = mgh`
        *   m = mass (kg)
        *   g = acceleration due to gravity (approx 9.8 m/s² or 10 m/s²)
        *   h = height (m) above reference level (zameen se unchai)
    *   PE depends on the chosen zero level (reference point). Path par depend nahi karta, sirf vertical height difference par depend karta hai (Fig 10.8).
    ![Diagram Description: Fig 10.7 shows an object of mass m being lifted vertically to a height h from the ground.](Diagram description: Fig 10.7 mein m mass ke object ko zameen se h unchai tak seedha upar uthaya ja raha hai.)
*   **Elastic Potential Energy:** Stretched rubber band (Activity 10.8), compressed slinky (Activity 10.9), stretched bow string (Activity 10.12 - Fig 10.6) mein stored energy. Jab release karte hain, yeh PE, KE mein convert hoti hai.
    ![Diagram Description: Fig 10.6 shows a bow with a stretched string holding an arrow. The stored potential energy in the bow will convert to kinetic energy of the arrow when released.](Diagram description: Fig 10.6 mein ek dhanush (bow) dikhaya gaya hai jiski string kheenchi hui hai aur uspar teer (arrow) laga hai. Dhanush mein store hui potential energy release hone par teer ki kinetic energy mein badal jayegi.)

**Example 10.5:** 10 kg mass ko 6 m height par rakha hai (g=9.8 m/s²). PE = 10 kg × 9.8 m/s² × 6 m = 588 J.

**5. Law of Conservation of Energy (Urja Sanrakshan ka Niyam)**

*   **Statement:** Energy sirf ek form se doosre form mein transform ho sakti hai, total energy hamesha constant rehti hai.
*   **Example (Free Fall):** Jab object height 'h' se girta hai:
    *   At top: PE = mgh, KE = 0. Total Energy = mgh.
    *   During fall: Height kam hoti hai (PE decreases), speed badhti hai (KE increases).
    *   At any point: `PE + KE = Constant` (Total Mechanical Energy).
    *   Just before hitting ground (h≈0): PE ≈ 0, KE = max. Total Energy = max KE = initial mgh.
*   Yeh law har situation aur har transformation ke liye valid hai.

**6. Power (Shakti)**

*   **Definition:** Work karne ki rate (dar) ya energy transfer ki rate. Batata hai ki kaam kitni jaldi ya dheere ho raha hai.
*   **Formula:** `Power (P) = Work (W) / Time (t)` OR `P = Energy (E) / Time (t)`
*   **Unit:** SI unit Watt (W). 1 Watt power tab hoti hai jab 1 Joule work 1 second mein kiya jaaye (1 W = 1 J/s).
*   James Watt ke honour mein unit ka naam rakha gaya hai.
*   **Larger Unit:** kilowatt (kW). 1 kW = 1000 W = 1000 J/s.
*   **Average Power:** Agar power time ke saath change ho rahi hai, toh Average Power = Total Work Done / Total Time Taken.

**Example 10.7:** Do ladkiyan A aur B, dono ka weight 400 N hai, 8 m unchi rassi par chadhti hain. A ko 20 s lagte hain, B ko 50 s.
    *   Work done by both = `mgh` = Weight × height = 400 N × 8 m = 3200 J.
    *   Power of A = `W/t` = 3200 J / 20 s = 160 W.
    *   Power of B = `W/t` = 3200 J / 50 s = 64 W.
    *   Girl A zyada powerful hai kyunki usne same work kam time mein kiya.

**7. Commercial Unit of Energy (Urja ka Vyavsayik Matrak)**

*   Household aur industries mein use hone wali energy ko Joules mein measure karna inconvenient hai kyunki value bahut badi ho jaati hai.
*   Isliye **kilowatt-hour (kWh)** use karte hain, jise 'unit' bhi kehte hain.
*   **Definition:** 1 kWh woh energy hai jo 1 kW power wala device 1 hour tak use karta hai.
*   **Conversion:**
    `1 kWh = 1 kW × 1 h`
    `1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s`
    `1 kWh = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 × 10⁶ J`
