## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Karya (Work)**
    *   **Vaigyanik Paribhasha (Scientific Definition):** Jab kisi vastu par force lagaya jaye aur vastu force ki disha mein displace (visthapit) ho. (Force applied and object gets displaced in the direction of force).
    *   **Formula:** `W = F × s` (Work = Force × displacement)
    *   **Conditions for Work:** Force lagna chahiye (Force must act) AND Displacement hona chahiye (Displacement must occur).
    *   **Types:**
        *   **Positive Work:** Force aur displacement same direction mein. (e.g., Pushing a box forward)
        *   **Negative Work:** Force aur displacement opposite direction mein. (e.g., Work done by friction)
        *   **Zero Work:** Ya toh displacement zero ho, ya force zero ho, ya force aur displacement perpendicular (90°) ho. (e.g., Pushing a wall, holding a weight still, satellite orbiting Earth under gravity)
    *   **Unit:** Joule (J) (1 J = 1 Newton × 1 metre)

2.  **Urja (Energy)**
    *   **Paribhasha (Definition):** Karya karne ki kshamata (The capacity or ability to do work).
    *   **Unit:** Joule (J) (Same as work)
    *   **Urja ke Roop (Forms of Energy):**
        *   **Yantrik Urja (Mechanical Energy):** Kinetic Energy + Potential Energy
        *   **Gatij Urja (Kinetic Energy - KE):** Vastu ki gati (motion) ke karan urja. (Energy due to motion).
            *   **Formula:** `KE = ½ mv²` (m = mass, v = velocity)
        *   **Sthitij Urja (Potential Energy - PE):** Vastu ki position (sthiti) ya configuration (aakar) ke karan stored urja. (Stored energy due to position or configuration).
            *   **Gravitational PE:** Height ke karan. `PE = mgh` (m = mass, g = acceleration due to gravity, h = height)
            *   **Elastic PE:** Shape change ke karan (e.g., stretched rubber band, compressed spring).
        *   Other forms: Heat energy, Chemical energy, Electrical energy, Light energy.
    *   **Urja ka Rupantaran (Transformation of Energy):** Energy ek form se doosre form mein badal sakti hai. (Energy can change from one form to another).
    *   **Urja Sanrakshan ka Niyam (Law of Conservation of Energy):** Energy na toh banayi ja sakti hai, na hi nasht ki ja sakti hai; yeh sirf ek roop se doosre roop mein transform hoti hai. Kisi isolated system ki total energy hamesha constant rehti hai. (Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed. Total energy of an isolated system remains constant).

3.  **Shakti (Power)**
    *   **Paribhasha (Definition):** Karya karne ki dar ya urja transfer hone ki dar. (Rate of doing work or rate of energy transfer). Kitni jaldi work ho raha hai.
    *   **Formula:** `P = W / t` (Power = Work done / time taken) OR `P = E / t` (Power = Energy consumed / time taken)
    *   **Unit:** Watt (W) (1 W = 1 Joule / second)
    *   **Badi Units:** Kilowatt (kW) (1 kW = 1000 W)
    *   **Urja ki Commercial Unit:** Kilowatt-hour (kWh) - Ise 'unit' bhi kehte hain jo electricity bill mein use hoti hai. `1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J`.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

*   **Work ka Scientific Meaning:** Rozmarra ki life mein 'work' ka matlab alag hota hai science se. Agar aap ek bhaari pathhar (huge rock) ko dhakka de rahe ho par woh hil nahi raha, toh scientifically aapne koi work nahi kiya (W=0 kyunki s=0), bhale hi aap thak gaye ho aur energy kharch ki ho. Similarly, agar coolie sar par bojh (load) lekar khada hai, toh bhi work done on the load is zero. Lekin seedhiyan (stairs) chadhne mein work hota hai kyunki aap gravity ke against force lagakar displace ho rahe ho.
    *   *Diagram Concept (Fig 10.1):* Ek object par constant force F lag raha hai aur woh force ki direction mein 's' distance move karta hai. Toh Work Done `W = F × s`.

*   **Positive vs Negative Work:** Jab force object ki motion ki direction mein help karta hai (e.g., engine pulling a train), work done positive hota hai. Jab force motion ko oppose karta hai (e.g., brakes applying force opposite to car's motion), work done negative hota hai.
    *   *Diagram Concept (Fig 10.4):* Baby pulling a toy car parallel to ground - force and displacement in same direction, positive work.

*   **Kinetic Energy (Gatij Urja):** Har chalti hui cheez mein kinetic energy hoti hai. Jitni zyada speed (velocity) ya jitna zyada mass, utni zyada kinetic energy. Agar velocity double (2x) hogi, toh KE four times (4x) ho jayegi (kyunki KE ∝ v²).
    *   *Example:* Ek chalti hui car, ek flying bird, behta hua paani (flowing water) - sab mein KE hai.

*   **Potential Energy (Sthitij Urja):** Yeh stored energy hai.
    *   *Gravitational PE (Fig 10.7):* Kisi object ko height 'h' tak uthane mein jo work karna padta hai gravity ke against, woh usmein PE (`mgh`) ke form mein store ho jaata hai. Ground level par PE zero maani jaati hai. Height badhne par PE badhti hai. Path (raasta) par depend nahi karti, sirf vertical height difference par depend karti hai (Fig 10.8).
    *   *Elastic PE (Fig 10.6):* Kheenchi hui کمان (bow) ya rubber band mein stored energy. Jab release karte hain, toh yeh PE, arrow ya rubber band ki KE mein badal jaati hai.

*   **Law of Conservation of Energy:** Yeh ek fundamental law hai. Energy kabhi khatam nahi hoti, bas form badalti hai.
    *   *Example (Free Fall):* Jab ek object upar se girta hai, uski PE kam hoti jaati hai (height kam hone se) aur KE badhti jaati hai (velocity badhne se). Lekin har point par, Total Mechanical Energy (KE + PE) constant rehti hai (agar air resistance ko ignore karein). `mgh + ½ mv² = Constant`.
    *   *Example (Pendulum):* Pendulum jab oscillate karta hai, toh extreme positions par saari energy PE hoti hai (v=0), aur mean position par saari energy KE hoti hai (h=0, max velocity). Beech mein KE aur PE dono hoti hain, but total energy constant rehti hai (ideally).

*   **Power (Shakti):** Yeh batata hai ki energy kitni jaldi use ya transfer ho rahi hai. Ek powerful machine kam time mein zyada work kar sakti hai.
    *   *Example:* Agar do log same weight uthakar same height tak jaate hain, lekin ek kam time leta hai, toh uski power zyada hai (Example 10.7).

*   **Commercial Unit of Energy (kWh):** Humare gharon mein jo electricity use hoti hai, uska bill 'units' mein aata hai. 1 unit ka matlab hai 1 kilowatt power ko 1 ghante tak use karna. `1 Unit = 1 kWh = 1 kW × 1 h = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 × 10⁶ J`.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Context) 📊

*   **Energy Sources in India:** Bharat apni energy needs ke liye vibhinn sources par depend karta hai jaise Koyla (Coal - thermal power plants), Paani (Hydroelectric power - dams like Bhakra Nangal), Surya (Solar energy - becoming increasingly important), Hawa (Wind energy - windmills in coastal areas/plains), Nuclear energy, aur Biomass (gobar gas plants in villages). In sab mein energy transformation ke principles kaam karte hain.
*   **Traditional Work:** Aaj bhi kai Bharatiya gaon mein kheti ke liye bailon (bullocks) ka istemal hota hai hal (plough) kheenchne ke liye (Question 4 example). Coolies railway stations par bojh uthate hain. In sab mein work aur energy ke concepts apply hote hain.
*   **Household Energy Consumption & Cost:** Bharat mein gharon mein electricity ka consumption 'units' (kWh) mein measure hota hai. Electricity bill is consumption par based hota hai, jiska seedha asar ghar ke budget (economic data) par padta hai. Garmiyon mein AC/Cooler use karne se bill badh jaata hai, yeh power consumption ka practical example hai. Sarkaar energy conservation (urja bachane) par zor deti hai through campaigns and schemes like using LED bulbs.
*   **Renewable Energy Focus:** India is rapidly increasing its renewable energy capacity (solar, wind) to reduce dependence on fossil fuels like coal and petroleum, aligning with global efforts to combat climate change. Yeh energy transformation ka ek bada national-level example hai.
