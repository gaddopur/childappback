# [Class 9] General - Chapter 11: Work and Energy (कार्य तथा ऊर्जा)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein)

Ek detailed concept hierarchy 📊 jo iss chapter ke mukhya ideas ko jodta hai:

1.  **Work (Karya - कार्य)**
    *   **Scientific Definition (Vaigyanik Paribhasha):** Force (Bal - बल) × Displacement (Visthapan - विस्थापन) in the direction of force (Bal ki disha mein).
        *   Formula: `W = F × s`
    *   **Conditions for Work (Karya ke liye Shartein):**
        *   Force must act (Bal lagna chahiye).
        *   Object must be displaced (Vastu mein visthapan hona chahiye).
    *   **Types of Work (Karya ke Prakar):**
        *   **Positive Work (Sakaratmak Karya):** Force aur displacement same direction mein ho (e.g., pulling a trolley).
        *   **Negative Work (Nakaratmak Karya):** Force aur displacement opposite direction mein ho (e.g., work done by friction, work done by gravity on an object thrown upwards).
        *   **Zero Work (Shunya Karya):**
            *   No displacement (Visthapan nahi). (e.g., Pushing a wall - deewar ko dhakelna).
            *   Force is zero (Bal shunya hai).
            *   Force and displacement perpendicular hain (Bal aur visthapan lambvat hain). (e.g., Coolie carrying load on head and walking horizontally - Coolie ka sar par bojh lekar chalna).
    *   **Unit of Work (Karya ka Matrak):** Joule (J). 1 J = 1 Newton × 1 meter.

2.  **Energy (Urja - ऊर्जा)**
    *   **Definition:** The capacity to do work (Karya karne ki kshamta).
    *   **Unit of Energy (Urja ka Matrak):** Joule (J) (Same as work).
    *   **Forms of Energy (Urja ke Roop):**
        *   Mechanical Energy (Yantrik Urja): Kinetic + Potential
        *   Heat Energy (Ushmiya Urja)
        *   Chemical Energy (Rasayanik Urja)
        *   Electrical Energy (Vidyut Urja)
        *   Light Energy (Prakash Urja)
        *   Nuclear Energy (Naabhikiya Urja), etc.
    *   **Kinetic Energy (Gatij Urja - KE):** Energy due to motion (Gati ke karan urja).
        *   Formula: `KE = ½ mv²` (where m = mass, v = velocity).
        *   Depends on mass (dravyamann) and speed (chaal).
    *   **Potential Energy (Sthitij Urja - PE):** Energy due to position or configuration (Stithi ya aakriti mein parivartan ke karan urja).
        *   **Gravitational Potential Energy (Gurutviya Sthitij Urja):** Energy due to height above the ground (Zameen se unchai ke karan urja).
            *   Formula: `PE = mgh` (where m = mass, g = acceleration due to gravity, h = height).
        *   **Elastic Potential Energy (Pratyasth Sthitij Urja):** Energy stored in stretched or compressed objects (e.g., stretched rubber band - khinchi hui rubber band, compressed slinky, stretched bow - khincha hua dhanush).
    *   **Law of Conservation of Energy (Urja Sanrakshan ka Niyam):**
        *   Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed from one form to another (Urja na toh banayi ja sakti hai, na hi nasht ki ja sakti hai; sirf ek roop se doosre roop mein badalti hai).
        *   Total energy before and after transformation remains constant (Kul urja hamesha sthir rehti hai).
        *   Example: Free fall (mukt patan), Pendulum oscillation (lolak ka dolan).
    *   **Mechanical Energy (Yantrik Urja):** Sum of Kinetic and Potential Energy (`ME = KE + PE`). In many systems (like free fall without air resistance), total mechanical energy is conserved.

3.  **Power (Shakti - शक्ति)**
    *   **Definition:** Rate of doing work or rate of energy transfer (Karya karne ki dar ya urja sthanantaran ki dar). Measures how fast work is done (Kitni tezi se karya kiya ja raha hai).
    *   **Formula:** `P = W / t` (Work done / time taken) or `P = E / t` (Energy transferred / time taken).
    *   **Unit of Power (Shakti ka Matrak):** Watt (W).
        *   1 Watt = 1 Joule / second (1 W = 1 J/s).
    *   **Larger Unit:** Kilowatt (kW). 1 kW = 1000 W.
    *   **Average Power (Ausat Shakti):** Total work done / Total time taken.

4.  **Commercial Unit of Energy (Urja ka Vyavsayik Matrak)**
    *   Kilowatt-hour (kWh), commonly called a 'Unit'.
    *   Used for measuring electrical energy consumption in homes and industries (Gharon aur udyogon mein vidyut urja mapne ke liye).
    *   1 kWh = Energy consumed by a 1 kW device in 1 hour.
    *   Relation: `1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J`.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

Detailed explanations with examples and conceptual diagrams 📈:

1.  **Work: Scientific vs. Everyday Meaning (Karya: Vaigyanik banam Rozmarra ka Arth):**
    *   Rozmarra ki zindagi mein, hum kisi bhi physical ya mental mehnat ko 'work' (kaam) kehte hain, jaise padhai karna (studying), sochna (thinking).
    *   Lekin Science mein, 'Work' tabhi mana jata hai jab **force** lagane par **displacement** ho. Agar aap ek bhaari pathhar (heavy rock) ko dhakka dete hain par woh hilta nahi, toh scientific definition ke hisaab se aapne koi work nahi kiya, bhale hi aap thak gaye ho.
    *   **Diagram Concept:** Ek block par force (F) lag raha hai aur woh distance (s) move kar raha hai usi direction mein. Work = F × s.
    *   **Zero Work Example:** Ek coolie platform par sar par bojh (load) rakh kar khada hai. Force (gravity ke against) lag raha hai, par displacement zero hai. Isliye work done on the load zero hai. Agar woh horizontal chalta hai, toh force (upward) aur displacement (horizontal) perpendicular hain, isliye work done by him against gravity zero hai.

2.  **Calculating Work, KE, and PE:**
    *   **Work (W = Fs):** Agar 5 N ka force ek object ko 2 m displace karta hai force ki direction mein, toh Work = 5 N × 2 m = 10 J.
    *   **Kinetic Energy (KE = ½ mv²):** Ek 15 kg ka object 4 m/s se move kar raha hai. KE = ½ × 15 kg × (4 m/s)² = 120 J. Agar velocity double ho jaye (8 m/s), toh KE 4 guna ho jayegi (½ × 15 × 8² = 480 J). *Isse pata chalta hai ki KE velocity ke square par depend karti hai.*
    *   **Potential Energy (PE = mgh):** Ek 10 kg ka object zameen se 6 m upar hai (g = 9.8 m/s²). PE = 10 kg × 9.8 m/s² × 6 m = 588 J. *Yeh energy object mein uski position ke kaaran stored hai.*

3.  **Positive and Negative Work:**
    *   Jab aap ek object ko upar uthate hain, aapka applied force (upwards) aur displacement (upwards) same direction mein hain - **Positive work** by you.
    *   Usi samay, gravity ka force (downwards) displacement (upwards) ke opposite hai - **Negative work** by gravity.
    *   Jab ek car par brakes lagte hain, braking force (peeche ki taraf) displacement (aage ki taraf) ke opposite hota hai - **Negative work** by brakes.

4.  **Energy Transformation (Urja ka Roopantaran):**
    *   Energy ek form se doosre mein badalti hai.
    *   **Example 1 (Battery & Bulb):** Battery ki Chemical Energy -> Electrical Energy -> Bulb mein Light Energy + Heat Energy.
    *   **Example 2 (Riding Bicycle - Cycle Chalana):** Aapki body ki Chemical Energy (food se) -> Muscular Energy -> Kinetic Energy of bicycle + Heat Energy (friction ke karan).
    *   **Example 3 (Hydroelectric Dam - Jal Vidyut Baandh):** Stored water ki Potential Energy -> Flowing water ki Kinetic Energy -> Turbine ki Mechanical Energy -> Electrical Energy.
    *   **Diagram Concept (Free Fall):** Object at height h: Max PE, Zero KE. As it falls: PE decreases, KE increases. Just before hitting ground: Zero PE, Max KE. Har point par PE + KE = Constant (Total Mechanical Energy).

5.  **Law of Conservation of Energy:**
    *   Yeh ek fundamental law hai. Total energy hamesha conserved rehti hai.
    *   **Pendulum Example:** Jab pendulum bob ko ek side le jaakar chhodte hain: Extreme position par Max PE, Zero KE. Lowest position par Max KE, Min PE. Dusri extreme position par phir Max PE, Zero KE. Dheere-dheere air resistance (hava ka pratirodh) ke karan energy heat mein convert hoti hai aur bob ruk jata hai, lekin total energy (Mechanical + Heat) conserved rehti hai.

6.  **Power (Shakti):**
    *   Yeh batata hai ki kaam kitni jaldi ho raha hai.
    *   **Example:** Do ladkiyan (A aur B), dono ka weight 400 N, 8 m upar rassi se chadhti hain. A ko 20 s lagte hain, B ko 50 s.
        *   Work done dono ke liye same hai: W = mgh = 400 N × 8 m = 3200 J.
        *   Power of A (P_A) = W / t_A = 3200 J / 20 s = 160 W.
        *   Power of B (P_B) = W / t_B = 3200 J / 50 s = 64 W.
        *   Girl A zyada powerful hai kyunki woh same kaam kam time mein karti hai.

7.  **Commercial Unit - kWh ('Unit'):**
    *   Aapke ghar ka electricity meter 'Units' mein reading dikhata hai, jo kWh hai.
    *   Agar ek 100 W ka bulb 10 ghante chalta hai, toh energy consumed = Power × Time = 100 W × 10 h = 1000 Wh = 1 kWh = 1 Unit.
    *   `1 kWh = 1 kW × 1 h = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 × 10⁶ J`.

## 📝 Assessment Prep (Mulyankan ki Taiyari)

Case studies aur diagrams par focus karein 📝:

1.  **Case Study Problems:**
    *   **Porter Problem:** Ek coolie 15 kg ka luggage zameen se utha kar apne sar par 1.5 m upar rakhta hai. Usne luggage par kitna work kiya? (W = mgh = 15 kg × 10 m/s² × 1.5 m = 225 J). *Yahan g ≈ 10 m/s² le sakte hain calculation easy karne ke liye.*
    *   **Car Velocity Change:** Ek 1500 kg ki car ki velocity 30 km/h se 60 km/h karne ke liye kitna work karna padega? (Work Done = Change in KE = KE_final - KE_initial). Pehle velocities ko m/s mein convert karein! (30 km/h = 25/3 m/s, 60 km/h = 50/3 m/s). Work = ½ * 1500 * [(50/3)² - (25/3)²] = 156250 J. *Yeh Work-Energy Theorem ka application hai.*
    *   **Staircase Climbing:** Ek 50 kg ka ladka 45 steps (har step 15 cm = 0.15 m uncha) 9 seconds mein chadhta hai. Uski power calculate karo. (Total height h = 45 × 0.15 m = 6.75 m. Work W = mgh = 50 kg × 10 m/s² × 6.75 m = 3375 J. Power P = W/t = 3375 J / 9 s = 375 W).

2.  **Diagram Analysis:**
    *   **Work Diagrams:** Force aur displacement vectors ko samajhna. Agar angle 0° hai (same direction), work positive (F × s). Agar 180° hai (opposite direction), work negative (-F × s). Agar 90° hai (perpendicular), work zero. (Refer Exercise 18 diagrams).
    *   **Free Fall Diagram:** Ek diagram banayein jo dikhaye ki object girte samay different heights par PE aur KE kaise change hoti hai, lekin unka sum (Total Mechanical Energy) constant rehta hai (agar air resistance ignore karein).
    *   **Pendulum Diagram:** Ek oscillating pendulum ka diagram banayein aur extreme aur mean positions par PE aur KE ki relative values ko label karein.
