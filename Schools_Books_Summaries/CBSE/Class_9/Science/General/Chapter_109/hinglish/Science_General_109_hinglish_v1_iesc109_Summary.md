# [Class 9] General - Chapter 9: Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण)

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter gravitation ke fundamental ideas ko explore karta hai. Neeche diye gaye hierarchy mein core concepts samjhaye gaye hain:

📊 **Concept Hierarchy:**

1.  **Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण)**
    *   Introduction (परिचय): Har object dusre object ko attract karta hai. Example: Apple ka girna, Moon ka Earth ke around ghumna.
    *   **Universal Law of Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण का सार्वत्रिक नियम)**
        *   Statement: Universe mein har object dusre object ko ek force se attract karta hai jo unke masses ke product ke directly proportional aur unke beech ke distance ke square ke inversely proportional hota hai.
        *   Formula (सूत्र): `F = G * (M * m) / d²`
        *   G: Universal Gravitational Constant (सार्वत्रिक गुरुत्वाकर्षण स्थिरांक) - Value: 6.673 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻² (Henry Cavendish ne find kiya).
        *   Importance (महत्व): Earth se bandhe rehna, Moon ka motion, Planets ka motion, Tides (ज्वार-भाटा).
2.  **Free Fall (मुक्त पतन)**
    *   Concept: Jab objects sirf Earth ki gravitational force ke karan neeche girte hain.
    *   **Acceleration due to Gravity (गुरुत्वीय त्वरण 'g')**
        *   Definition: Free fall ke time object mein produce hua acceleration.
        *   Formula: `g = G * M / R²` (M = Earth ka mass, R = Earth ka radius)
        *   Value on Earth (पृथ्वी पर मान): Approx. 9.8 m/s².
        *   Variation (विभिन्नता): Poles par zyada, Equator par kam; altitude (ऊंचाई) ke saath decrease hota hai.
    *   Motion under Gravity (गुरुत्व के प्रभाव में गति): Equations of motion apply hote hain (a = g).
3.  **Mass and Weight (द्रव्यमान तथा भार)**
    *   Mass (द्रव्यमान): Measure of inertia, constant rehta hai har jagah. Unit: kg.
    *   Weight (भार): Force jisse Earth object ko attract karti hai (`W = m * g`). Unit: Newton (N). Weight location ke saath change hota hai kyunki 'g' change hota hai.
    *   Weight on Moon (चंद्रमा पर भार): Earth ke weight ka approx. 1/6th hota hai, kyunki Moon ka mass aur radius kam hai.
4.  **Thrust and Pressure (प्रणोद तथा दाब)**
    *   Thrust (प्रणोद): Perpendicular force acting on a surface. Unit: N.
    *   Pressure (दाब): Thrust per unit area (`Pressure = Thrust / Area`). Unit: Pascal (Pa) or N/m².
    *   Applications (अनुप्रयोग): Sharp knives (kam area, zyada pressure), wide straps of bags (zyada area, kam pressure), building foundations (zyada area, kam pressure).
5.  **Buoyancy (उत्प्लावकता)**
    *   Concept: Fluid (liquid/gas) dwara object par upar ki taraf lagaya gaya force (Upthrust or Buoyant Force). Example: Paani mein halka feel karna.
    *   **Archimedes' Principle (आर्किमिडीज़ का सिद्धांत)**
        *   Statement: Jab koi body fluid mein partially ya fully duboyi jaati hai, toh us par upar ki taraf ek force lagta hai jo us body dwara displace kiye gaye fluid ke weight ke barabar hota hai.
        *   Applications: Designing ships, submarines, Lactometers (milk purity), Hydrometers (liquid density).
    *   **Floating and Sinking (तैरना एवं डूबना)**
        *   Condition: Object ki density agar fluid ki density se kam hai toh float karega, agar zyada hai toh sink karega.
        *   Density (घनत्व): Mass per unit volume.

## 📘 Key Learnings

Is section mein hum important concepts ko detail mein samjhenge.

1.  **Universal Law of Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण का सार्वत्रिक नियम)**
    *   Newton ne realise kiya ki apple ko girane wala force aur moon ko Earth ke around ghumane wala force same hai - Gravitational Force.
    *   Yeh law kehta hai ki attraction force (F) dono objects ke masses (M aur m) ke product ke directly proportional hai (`F ∝ M * m`).
    *   Aur yeh force unke beech ki distance (d) ke square ke inversely proportional hai (`F ∝ 1/d²`).
    *   Combine karne par: `F ∝ (M * m) / d²` ya `F = G * (M * m) / d²`.
    *   `G` ek constant hai, jiski value har jagah same rehti hai.
    *   `[Diagram: Two masses M and m separated by distance d, showing attractive force F along the line joining their centers. Label F, M, m, d, and G in the formula.]`
    *   **Importance**: Yeh law samjhata hai ki hum Earth par kyun tike hain, planets Sun ke around kyun ghumte hain, aur samudra mein tides (ज्वार-भाटा) Moon aur Sun ki gravity ke karan kyun aate hain.

2.  **Free Fall and Acceleration due to Gravity ('g')**
    *   Jab koi object sirf gravity ke influence mein girta hai, use free fall kehte hain. Aisa girte waqt uski velocity badhti jaati hai, yaani acceleration hota hai.
    *   Is acceleration ko 'acceleration due to gravity' (g) kehte hain. `g = G * M / R²`.
    *   Earth par iski average value 9.8 m/s² hai.
    *   `[Diagram: Earth showing radius R and mass M. An object of mass m near the surface experiencing force mg downwards. Formula g = GM/R² highlighted.]`
    *   **Key Point**: 'g' object ke mass (m) par depend nahi karta! Isliye, vacuum mein, ek bhaari pathar aur ek halka pankh (feather) same rate se neeche girenge (Galileo's experiment). Hawa (air resistance) ke karan real life mein fark dikhta hai.
    *   Equations of motion free fall ke liye modify ho jaati hain:
        *   `v = u + gt`
        *   `s = ut + ½ gt²`
        *   `v² = u² + 2gs` (Note: Agar object upar ja raha hai toh g negative hoga).

3.  **Mass vs. Weight (द्रव्यमान बनाम भार)**
    *   **Mass (m)**: Kisi object mein matter ka amount hai. Yeh inertia ka measure hai aur kabhi change nahi hota, chahe aap Earth par hon, Moon par, ya space mein. Unit: kg.
    *   **Weight (W)**: Woh gravitational force hai jisse Earth (ya koi aur celestial body) object ko apni taraf kheenchti hai. `W = m * g`. Unit: N.
    *   Kyunki 'g' ki value jagah ke saath badalti hai (poles par zyada, equator par kam, Moon par kam), isliye weight bhi badalta hai.
    *   `[Diagram: A weighing scale showing different readings for the same mass on Earth and Moon. Earth: W = mg_earth. Moon: W = mg_moon. Highlight mg_moon ≈ (1/6)mg_earth.]`
    *   Example: Agar aapka mass 60 kg hai, toh Earth par weight hoga `60 * 9.8 ≈ 588 N`. Moon par 'g' Earth ka 1/6th hai, toh weight hoga `60 * (9.8/6) ≈ 98 N`. Mass dono jagah 60 kg hi rahega.

4.  **Thrust and Pressure (प्रणोद तथा दाब)**
    *   **Thrust**: Surface ke perpendicular lagne wala force. Jaise pin ko board par lagate waqt thumb se lagaya gaya force.
    *   **Pressure**: Unit area par lagne wala thrust. `Pressure = Thrust / Area`.
    *   `[Diagram: Comparing two scenarios: (a) A block resting on a large area, showing less pressure. (b) The same block resting on a smaller area, showing more pressure. Formula P = F/A highlighted.]`
    *   **Smaller Area = Larger Pressure** (for same force). Example: Nails ki pointed tip, knife ki sharp edge.
    *   **Larger Area = Smaller Pressure** (for same force). Example: School bag ke wide straps, building ki wide foundations, camel ke wide feet (sand par chalne ke liye).

5.  **Buoyancy and Archimedes' Principle (उत्प्लावकता और आर्किमिडीज़ का सिद्धांत)**
    *   Jab kisi object ko fluid (liquid ya gas) mein dala jaata hai, toh fluid us par upar ki taraf ek force lagata hai, jise **Buoyant Force** ya **Upthrust** kehte hain. Isiliye paani mein cheezein halki lagti hain.
    *   **Archimedes' Principle**: Yeh buoyant force us object dwara hataye gaye (displaced) fluid ke weight ke barabar hota hai.
    *   `[Diagram: (a) Stone weighed in air using spring balance. (b) Stone partially/fully submerged in water, showing reduced reading on spring balance due to buoyant force (Fb). Show displaced water. Highlight Fb = Weight of displaced fluid.]`
    *   **Floating/Sinking**:
        *   Agar object ki density < fluid ki density → Object floats ( तैरता है). (Example: Cork, plastic bottle)
        *   Agar object ki density > fluid ki density → Object sinks ( डूब जाता है). (Example: Iron nail, stone)
        *   Agar object ki density = fluid ki density → Object fluid ke andar float karta hai (suspended).
    *   Isi principle par ships (jo iron ke bane hote hain, but unka overall volume itna zyada hota hai ki average density paani se kam ho jaati hai) aur submarines kaam karte hain.
