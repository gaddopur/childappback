# [Class 9] General - Chapter 9: Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
Detailed concept hierarchy 📊

1.  **Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण)**
    *   Basic Idea: Har object doosre ko attract karta hai (Newton's Apple & Moon).
    *   Centripetal Force (अभिकेंद्र बल): Force jo circular motion maintain karta hai (center-seeking).
2.  **Universal Law of Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण का सार्वत्रिक नियम)**
    *   Statement: Force ∝ (Product of masses) / (Distance between them)²
    *   Formula: F = G (M × m) / d²
    *   G: Universal Gravitational Constant (सार्वत्रिक गुरुत्वाकर्षण स्थिरांक) - Value: 6.673 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻² (Given by Cavendish).
    *   Inverse-Square Law: Distance double hone par force one-fourth ho jaata hai.
    *   Importance: Explains Earth's gravity, Moon's orbit, Planets' orbits, Tides (ज्वार-भाटा).
3.  **Earth's Gravity (पृथ्वी का गुरुत्व)**
    *   Free Fall (मुक्त पतन): Jab koi object sirf gravity ke under girta hai.
    *   Acceleration due to Gravity (गुरुत्वीय त्वरण 'g'): Earth ke attraction se hone wala acceleration. Unit: m s⁻².
    *   Calculating 'g': g = G M / R² (M=Mass of Earth, R=Radius of Earth). Average value ≈ 9.8 m s⁻².
    *   Variation in 'g': Poles par zyada, Equator par kam (kyunki Earth perfect sphere nahi hai). Altitude ke saath bhi 'g' kam hota hai.
    *   Motion under Gravity: Equations of motion apply hote hain (a = g).
        *   v = u + gt
        *   s = ut + ½ gt²
        *   v² = u² + 2gs
4.  **Mass vs. Weight (द्रव्यमान vs भार)**
    *   Mass (m): Measure of inertia, constant rehta hai har jagah. Unit: kg.
    *   Weight (W): Force jisse Earth attract karti hai (W = m × g). Unit: Newton (N). Jagah ke saath badalta hai (kyunki 'g' badalta hai).
    *   Weight on Moon: Earth ke weight ka approx 1/6th hota hai (kyunki Moon ka mass aur radius kam hai).
5.  **Thrust and Pressure (प्रणोद तथा दाब)**
    *   Thrust: Perpendicular force acting on a surface. Unit: N.
    *   Pressure: Thrust per unit area (Pressure = Thrust / Area). Unit: Pascal (Pa) or N m⁻².
    *   Effect: Same force, smaller area par zyada pressure (e.g., nail tip, knife edge). Larger area par kam pressure (e.g., camel feet on sand, building foundations).
    *   Pressure in Fluids: Liquids aur gases bhi pressure exert karte hain (base and walls par). Pressure sab directions mein transmit hota hai.
6.  **Buoyancy (उत्प्लावकता)**
    *   Upthrust/Buoyant Force (उत्प्लावन बल): Fluid dwara object par upar ki taraf lagaya gaya force.
    *   Floating/Sinking:
        *   Agar object ki density fluid ki density se kam hai → Object float karega.
        *   Agar object ki density fluid ki density se zyada hai → Object sink karega.
7.  **Archimedes' Principle (आर्किमिडीज़ का सिद्धांत)**
    *   Statement: Jab koi body fluid mein partially ya fully duboyi jaati hai, toh uspar upar ki taraf ek force lagta hai jo us body dwara displace kiye gaye fluid ke weight ke barabar hota hai.
    *   Applications: Designing ships, submarines; Lactometers (milk purity); Hydrometers (liquid density).

## 📘 Key Learnings
Detailed explanations with diagrams 📈

*   **Gravitation kya hai?** Humne motion aur force ke baare mein padha. Newton ne samjha ki jo force apple ko Earth ki taraf girata hai, wahi force Moon ko Earth ke around aur planets ko Sun ke around ghumata hai. Yeh universal attraction force hi **Gravitational Force (गुरुत्वाकर्षण बल)** hai.

*   **Centripetal Force:** Jab hum ek pathar ko dhage se baandh kar ghumate hain (Activity 9.1, Fig 9.1), toh use circular path mein rakhne ke liye humein dhage ko andar ki taraf khechna padta hai. Yeh andar ki taraf lagne wala force **Centripetal Force (अभिकेंद्र बल)** kehlata hai. Moon ke case mein, Earth ka gravitational force hi centripetal force provide karta hai.
    *   *Imagine karo:* Dhaga chhod do toh pathar straight line (tangent) mein ud jaata hai. Aise hi agar gravity na ho toh Moon straight line mein chala jayega.

*   **Universal Law of Gravitation:** Universe mein har do objects ek doosre ko attract karte hain. Yeh force (F):
    *   Unke masses (M aur m) ke product ke directly proportional hota hai (F ∝ M × m).
    *   Unke beech ke distance (d) ke square ke inversely proportional hota hai (F ∝ 1/d²).
    *   Formula: **F = G (M × m) / d²** (Fig 9.2 shows force along the line joining centers).
    *   'G' ek constant hai, jiski value Henry Cavendish ne find ki thi.
    *   *Example 9.1:* Earth aur Moon ke beech ka force calculate kiya gaya hai using this formula.

*   **Importance of Universal Law:** Yeh law kayi cheezein explain karta hai: hum Earth se kyun bandhe hain, Moon ka motion, Planets ka motion, aur samudra mein tides (moon aur sun ki gravity se).

*   **Free Fall (मुक्त पतन):** Jab koi object sirf Earth ki gravitational force ke karan neeche girta hai, use free fall kehte hain. Girte waqt velocity badalti hai, yaani acceleration hota hai. Is acceleration ko **acceleration due to gravity (g)** kehte hain.
    *   F = mg (from Newton's 2nd Law)
    *   F = G Mm/d² (from Universal Law)
    *   Isliye, mg = G Mm/d² => **g = G M / d²**. Agar object Earth ki surface par ya paas hai, toh d ≈ R (Earth's radius), so **g = G M / R²**.
    *   'g' ki value object ke mass par depend nahi karti! Isliye, heavy aur light objects (vacuum mein) same rate se girte hain (Activity 9.3 - paper vs stone, air resistance ka effect). Galileo ne bhi Pisa ki leaning tower se objects gira kar yeh prove kiya tha.
    *   Earth ke surface par 'g' ki average value **9.8 m s⁻²** hai. Yeh poles par thodi zyada aur equator par thodi kam hoti hai.

*   **Motion under Gravity:** Free fall mein motion uniformly accelerated hota hai (agar 'g' constant maanein). Hum standard equations use kar sakte hain, bas 'a' ki jagah 'g' use karna hai.
    *   Upar jaate waqt 'g' negative, neeche aate waqt 'g' positive lete hain.
    *   *Examples 9.2 & 9.3:* Car ka girna aur object ko upar phenkne ke numericals in equations ka use dikhate hain.

*   **Mass vs Weight:**
    *   **Mass (m)**: Amount of matter, measure of inertia. Kahin nahi badalta (Earth, Moon, space). Unit: kg.
    *   **Weight (W)**: Gravitational force jis se Earth (ya koi planet/moon) object ko attract karti hai. **W = m × g**. Yeh location ke saath badalta hai kyunki 'g' badalta hai. Unit: Newton (N).
    *   **Weight on Moon:** Moon ka mass (Mm) aur radius (Rm) Earth se kam hai. Isliye Moon par 'g' kam hai (approx. 1/6th of Earth's 'g'). Toh Moon par weight bhi Earth ke weight ka **1/6th** hoga (W_m = G m Mm / Rm²). (Table 9.1 gives values).
    *   *Example 9.4 & 9.5:* Mass se weight nikalna aur Earth ke weight se Moon ka weight nikalna.

*   **Thrust and Pressure:**
    *   **Thrust:** Kisi surface par perpendicular lagne wala force.
    *   **Pressure:** Perpendicular force (Thrust) acting per unit area. **Pressure = Thrust / Area**. Unit: Pa (Pascal).
    *   *Real-life examples:*
        *   Loose sand par khade hone se pair zyada dhaste hain compared to letne par (same weight, different area).
        *   Nail ki tip pointed hoti hai (kam area, zyada pressure).
        *   Knife ki edge sharp hoti hai (kam area, zyada pressure).
        *   Truck/Bus ke tyres wide hote hain (zyada area, kam pressure).
        *   Buildings ki foundation wide hoti hai (zyada area, kam pressure).
    *   *Example 9.6:* Wooden block ko alag-alag sides par rakhne se tabletop par alag-alag pressure lagta hai.

*   **Pressure in Fluids:** Liquids aur gases (fluids) bhi weight ke karan pressure daalte hain container ke base aur walls par.

*   **Buoyancy (उत्प्लावकता):** Jab hum kisi cheez ko paani mein daalte hain, toh halka feel hota hai (e.g., well se bucket kheenchna, swimming). Paani (ya koi bhi fluid) object par upar ki taraf ek force lagata hai, jise **Buoyant Force** ya **Upthrust (उत्प्लावन बल)** kehte hain. (Activity 9.4 - plastic bottle). Yeh force fluid ki density par depend karta hai.

*   **Why Objects Float or Sink?** (Activity 9.5 & 9.6 - Iron nail vs Cork)
    *   Yeh object ki **density (घनत्व = Mass/Volume)** aur fluid ki density par depend karta hai.
    *   Agar object ki density < fluid ki density → Object **Float** karega (e.g., cork, plastic bottle). Kyunki buoyant force object ke weight se zyada hota hai.
    *   Agar object ki density > fluid ki density → Object **Sink** karega (e.g., iron nail, stone). Kyunki buoyant force object ke weight se kam hota hai.
    *   *Interesting point:* Iron nail sink karti hai, par iron se bana itna bada ship float karta hai! Kyunki ship ka design aisa hota hai ki uski overall average density paani se kam ho jaati hai (andar kaafi hollow space hota hai).

*   **Archimedes' Principle:** (Activity 9.7 - Stone ko spring balance se latka kar paani mein dubona, Fig 9.6)
    *   Jab stone ko paani mein dubote hain, spring balance ki reading kam ho jaati hai. Yeh isliye kyunki paani upar ki taraf buoyant force laga raha hai.
    *   Archimedes ne discover kiya: **Buoyant force ka magnitude = Object dwara displace kiye gaye fluid ke weight ke barabar.**
    *   *Applications:* Ships/Submarines design karna, Lactometer (doodh ki shuddhta napne wala yantra), Hydrometer (liquids ki density napne wala yantra).
