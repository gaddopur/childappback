# [Class 9] General - Chapter 10: Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण)

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter gravitation ke fundamental ideas ko explore karta hai. Yahan mukhya concepts ka hierarchy hai 📊:

1.  **Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण)**
    *   Introduction (परिचय): Har object doosre ko attract karta hai (Newton's apple story context). Earth ka attraction (objects fall down), Moon revolves around Earth, Planets revolve around Sun.
    *   **Universal Law of Gravitation (गुरुत्वाकर्षण का सार्वत्रिक नियम)**
        *   Statement: Universe mein har object har doosre object ko ek force se attract karta hai, jo unke masses ke product ke directly proportional aur unke beech ke distance ke square ke inversely proportional hota hai. (Force ∝ M × m / d²)
        *   Formula: `F = G * (M * m) / d²`
            *   F = Gravitational Force (गुरुत्वाकर्षण बल)
            *   M, m = Masses of two objects (दो वस्तुओं का द्रव्यमान)
            *   d = Distance between centers (केंद्रों के बीच की दूरी)
            *   G = Universal Gravitational Constant (सार्वत्रिक गुरुत्वाकर्षण स्थिरांक) - Value: 6.673 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻² (Discovered by Henry Cavendish). Yeh constant poore universe mein same rehta hai.
        *   Importance (महत्व): Explains force binding us to Earth, Moon's motion around Earth, Planets' motion around Sun, Tides (ज्वार-भाटा) due to Moon and Sun.
    *   **Centripetal Force (अभिकेंद्र बल)**: Force jo object ko circular path mein move karata hai, center ki taraf act karta hai (Example: Stone tied to thread, Moon's orbit). Gravitational force provides centripetal force for celestial bodies.

2.  **Earth's Gravity (पृथ्वी का गुरुत्व)**
    *   **Free Fall (मुक्त पतन)**: Jab objects sirf Earth ke gravitational force ke influence mein neeche girte hain.
    *   **Acceleration due to Gravity (गुरुत्वीय त्वरण - 'g')**
        *   Concept: Free fall ke dauran velocity mein change (acceleration) due to Earth's gravity. Unit: m s⁻².
        *   Relation with F: `F = m * g` (from Newton's Second Law)
        *   Derivation: `m * g = G * M * m / R²` => `g = G * M / R²`
            *   M = Mass of Earth (पृथ्वी का द्रव्यमान) ≈ 6 × 10²⁴ kg
            *   R = Radius of Earth (पृथ्वी की त्रिज्या) ≈ 6.4 × 10⁶ m
        *   Value on Earth's Surface: Approx. 9.8 m s⁻² (Calculated using G, M, R).
        *   Variation (g में भिन्नता): Earth perfect sphere nahi hai. 'g' poles par zyada hota hai (radius kam) aur equator par kam (radius zyada). Altitude ke saath bhi 'g' kam hota hai.
        *   Independence of Mass: Acceleration 'g' object ke mass par depend nahi karta (Galileo's experiment concept - Leaning Tower of Pisa). Sabhi objects (vacuum mein) same rate se girte hain. Air resistance practical scenarios mein affect karta hai.
    *   **Motion under Gravity (गुरुत्व के प्रभाव में गति)**: Uniformly accelerated motion ke equations apply hote hain, 'a' ko 'g' se replace karke.
        *   `v = u + gt`
        *   `s = ut + ½ gt²`
        *   `v² = u² + 2gs`
        *   Sign Convention: Downward motion ke liye 'g' positive, upward motion ke liye 'g' negative.

3.  **Mass vs. Weight (द्रव्यमान बनाम भार)**
    *   **Mass (द्रव्यमान - m)**: Measure of inertia (जड़त्व का माप). Fundamental property, constant rehta hai har jagah (Earth, Moon, space). SI unit: kg.
    *   **Weight (भार - W)**: Force jisse Earth kisi object ko attract karti hai. `W = m * g`. Yeh ek force hai, isliye SI unit Newton (N) hai. Weight location ke saath change hota hai kyunki 'g' change hota hai. Weight vertically downwards act karta hai (vector quantity).
    *   **Weight on Moon (चंद्रमा पर भार)**: Moon ka mass aur radius Earth se kam hai, isliye Moon par 'g' kam hai. Weight on Moon ≈ (1/6) * Weight on Earth.

4.  **Thrust and Pressure (प्रणोद तथा दाब)**
    *   **Thrust (प्रणोद)**: Force acting perpendicular (लंबवत) to a surface. Unit: Newton (N).
    *   **Pressure (दाब - P)**: Thrust per unit area. `Pressure = Thrust / Area`. SI unit: N/m² or Pascal (Pa).
    *   Effect: Same force agar small area par lage toh pressure zyada, large area par lage toh pressure kam.
    *   Applications: Nails have pointed tips, knives have sharp edges (small area, high pressure). Building foundations are wide, truck tyres are wide (large area, low pressure). Camel walks easily on sand (wide feet). Army tanks use continuous chains.
    *   **Pressure in Fluids (तरलों में दाब)**: Liquids aur gases (fluids) bhi weight ke kaaran pressure exert karte hain container ke base aur walls par. Pressure confined fluid mein sabhi directions mein equally transmit hota hai (Pascal's Law concept).

5.  **Buoyancy (उत्प्लावकता)**
    *   **Buoyant Force / Upthrust (उत्प्लावन बल)**: Jab koi object fluid (liquid/gas) mein immerse hota hai, toh fluid uspar ek upward force lagata hai. Isi ko buoyant force kehte hain. (Example: Feeling lighter in pool, bucket heavier outside water).
    *   **Floating and Sinking (तैरना और डूबना)**:
        *   Depends on density comparison between object and fluid. Density (घनत्व) = Mass / Volume.
        *   Object floats if its density < fluid's density (Upthrust > Weight). Example: Cork, plastic bottle.
        *   Object sinks if its density > fluid's density (Upthrust < Weight). Example: Iron nail.
    *   **Archimedes' Principle (आर्किमिडीज़ का सिद्धांत)**: Jab koi body fully ya partially fluid mein immerse hoti hai, toh uspar lagne wala upward buoyant force us body dwara displace kiye gaye fluid ke weight ke barabar hota hai.
        *   Applications: Designing ships and submarines (জাহাজ और पनडुब्बी). Lactometers (दूध की शुद्धता मापना), Hydrometers (द्रवों का घनत्व मापना).

## 📘 Key Learnings

Is chapter se humein gravitation aur uske effects ke baare mein detail mein samajh aata hai 📈:

1.  **Universal Law of Gravitation**: Yeh law batata hai ki ब्रह्मांड mein har do cheezein ek doosre ko attract karti hain. Force ka magnitude `F = G * (M * m) / d²` se diya jaata hai. Yeh force hamesha attractive hoti hai aur line joining the centers ke along act karti hai. (Imagine Fig 9.2: Do spheres M aur m, distance d apart, force F acting along the line joining them). Yeh law universal hai - celestial bodies se lekar chote objects tak sab par apply hota hai.

2.  **Acceleration due to Gravity ('g')**: Jab koi object free fall mein hota hai, toh Earth use accelerate karti hai. Is acceleration ko 'g' kehte hain, jiski value Earth ki surface par lagbhag 9.8 m/s² hoti hai. Iski value `g = G * M / R²` se calculate hoti hai aur yeh object ke mass par depend nahi karti, lekin location (poles vs equator, altitude) par depend karti hai.

3.  **Mass vs. Weight**: Mass (द्रव्यमान) object mein matter ka amount hai, jo constant rehta hai. Weight (भार) woh gravitational force hai jisse Earth object ko kheenchti hai (`W = m*g`). Weight jagah ke saath badalta hai kyunki 'g' badalta hai. Moon par weight Earth ke weight ka 1/6th hota hai.

4.  **Thrust and Pressure**: Thrust perpendicular force hai, jabki Pressure force per unit area (`P = F/A`) hai. Same force (thrust) agar kam area par lage toh pressure zyada hota hai (e.g., pin ki nok), aur zyada area par lage toh pressure kam hota hai (e.g., building ki neev, camel ke pair). Fluids bhi pressure exert karte hain.

5.  **Buoyancy and Archimedes' Principle**: Fluids mein immerse hone par objects par upar ki taraf ek force lagta hai (Buoyant Force). Koi object तैरेगा (float) ya डूबेगा (sink), yeh uski density aur fluid ki density par depend karta hai. Archimedes ne bataya ki buoyant force object dwara hataye gaye (displaced) fluid ke weight ke barabar hota hai. (Imagine Fig 9.6: Stone ko hawa mein spring balance se measure karna, phir paani mein immerse karke measure karna - reading kam ho jaati hai due to upthrust).

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

Gravitation ke principles ke kuch examples India ke context mein 📊:

1.  **ISRO's Achievements:** India ka space program (ISRO) successfully satellites aur space probes (Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan) launch karta hai. Yeh launches gravitational laws ki precise understanding aur application par based hain, jaise Earth ki gravity se escape karna aur planets/moon ke gravitational field mein enter karna.

4.  **Transportation:** Heavy vehicles jaise trucks aur buses ke liye wide tyres ka istemal Indian roads par common hai. Yeh vehicle ke weight ko larger area par distribute karke pressure kam karta hai, jisse roads par damage kam hota hai aur vehicle stability badhti hai.
5.  **Tides and Coastal Life:** India ki long coastline par tides (jwar-bhata) ka effect fishing communities aur coastal ecosystems ke liye important hai. Tides Moon aur Sun ke gravitational pull ke kaaran hote hain, jaisa Universal Law of Gravitation explain karta hai.
