# [Class 9] Science - Chapter 3: Atoms and Molecules (Parmanu evam Anu)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Matter ka Swabhav (Nature of Matter):**
    *   Ancient Ideas: Bharat aur Greece ke vichar (Maharishi Kanad ka 'Parmanu', Pakudha Katyayama, Democritus ke 'atoms').
    *   Padarth ka vibhajjan (Divisibility of matter).
2.  **Chemical Combination ke Niyam (Laws of Chemical Combination):**
    *   **Law of Conservation of Mass (Dravyaman Sanrakshan ka Niyam):** Mass na create hota hai, na destroy hota hai chemical reaction mein. (Lavoisier)
    *   **Law of Constant Proportions (Sthir Anupat ka Niyam):** Pure chemical compound mein elements hamesha mass ke fixed ratio mein hote hain. (Proust)
3.  **Dalton ki Atomic Theory:**
    *   Matter chote particles 'atoms' se bana hai.
    *   Atoms indivisible hain (baad mein galat saabit hua).
    *   Ek element ke sabhi atoms identical hote hain (mass, properties).
    *   Alag elements ke atoms alag hote hain.
    *   Atoms small whole number ratio mein combine hokar compounds banate hain.
    *   Compound mein relative number aur type of atoms constant rehte hain.
4.  **Atom (Parmanu):**
    *   Matter ka building block.
    *   Bahut chota size (measured in nanometers, nm).
    *   **Symbols (Prateek):** Elements ko represent karne ka tareeka (Dalton, Berzelius, IUPAC system - H, O, Na, Fe).
    *   **Atomic Mass (Parmanu Dravyamān):** Relative mass compared to Carbon-12 atom. Unit: 'u' (unified mass).
5.  **Molecule (Anu):**
    *   Do ya zyada atoms ka group jo chemically bonded ho.
    *   Smallest particle jo independently exist kar sakta hai aur substance ki properties show karta hai.
    *   **Elements ke Molecules:** Same type ke atoms (e.g., O₂, H₂, Ar). Atomicity (parmanukta) - molecule mein kitne atoms hain.
    *   **Compounds ke Molecules:** Different types ke atoms fixed ratio mein (e.g., H₂O, CO₂, NH₃).
6.  **Ion (Āyan):**
    *   Charged particles (atoms ya group of atoms jin par charge ho).
    *   **Cation:** Positively charged ion (e.g., Na⁺, Ca²⁺).
    *   **Anion:** Negatively charged ion (e.g., Cl⁻, O²⁻).
    *   **Polyatomic Ions (Bahu-parmānuk Āyan):** Group of atoms carrying a charge (e.g., NH₄⁺, SO₄²⁻, OH⁻).
7.  **Chemical Formulae Likhna (Writing Chemical Formulae):**
    *   Compound ki composition ka symbolic representation.
    *   **Valency (Sanyojakta):** Element ki combining capacity.
    *   Rules: Symbols, valencies, criss-cross method, balancing charges.
8.  **Molecular Mass (Ānvik Dravyamān) & Formula Unit Mass (Sutra Ikai Dravyamān):**
    *   **Molecular Mass:** Sum of atomic masses of all atoms in a molecule (for covalent compounds).
    *   **Formula Unit Mass:** Sum of atomic masses in a formula unit (for ionic compounds).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

**1. Laws of Chemical Combination (Rasayanik Sanyojan ke Niyam):**

*   **Conservation of Mass (Dravyaman Sanrakshan):** Imagine aapne 10g chini (sugar) ko 100g paani (water) mein milaya. Total mass = 110g. Agar yeh react karke kuch naya banate, toh reaction ke baad bhi total mass 110g hi rahega. Lavoisier ne experiments se prove kiya ki chemical reaction mein total mass constant rehta hai.
    *   *NCERT Activity 3.1 ka concept:* Jab hum conical flask mein Sodium Carbonate (Y) aur ignition tube mein Copper Sulphate (X) lekar weigh karte hain, aur phir mix karke react karake dobara weigh karte hain, toh mass same rehta hai (agar flask band hai toh). Cork lagana zaroori hai taaki koi gas bahar na nikle ya andar na aaye.
    ![Conceptual Diagram: Balance showing equal mass before and after reaction](https://www.example.com/conservation_of_mass_diagram.png) *(Note: Actual diagram needed)*

*   **Constant Proportions (Sthir Anupat):** Paani (H₂O) aap kahin se bhi lein - nadi, samudra, ya lab mein banayein - usmein Hydrogen aur Oxygen ka mass ratio hamesha 1:8 hi hoga. Matlab 9g paani ko decompose karne par hamesha 1g Hydrogen aur 8g Oxygen milegi. Aise hi Ammonia (NH₃) mein Nitrogen aur Hydrogen ka mass ratio hamesha 14:3 hota hai. Proust ne yeh law diya.

**2. Dalton's Atomic Theory:**

John Dalton ne in laws ko explain karne ke liye theory di:
*   Sab kuch 'atoms' se bana hai.
*   Atoms create ya destroy nahi ho sakte (yeh Law of Conservation of Mass ko explain karta hai).
*   Ek element ke atoms same hote hain, doosre element ke atoms se different.
*   Atoms simple whole number ratio mein judkar compounds banate hain (yeh Law of Constant Proportions ko explain karta hai). *Jaise H₂O mein H aur O atoms ka ratio 2:1 hai.*

**3. Atoms, Symbols aur Atomic Mass:**

*   Atoms bahut chhote hote hain, hum unhe naked eyes se nahi dekh sakte. Size 'nm' mein measure hota hai (1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m).
*   Har element ka ek unique symbol hota hai (IUPAC approved). Pehla letter Capital, doosra small (agar ho toh). Jaise Hydrogen (H), Aluminium (Al), Iron (Fe - Latin 'Ferrum' se), Sodium (Na - Latin 'Natrium' se). [Refer Table 3.1 in NCERT]
*   **Atomic Mass (u):** Ek atom ka mass directly measure karna mushkil tha. Toh relative mass nikala gaya. Standard liya gaya Carbon-12 isotope ko. Ek Carbon-12 atom ke mass ke 1/12th hisse ko 1 atomic mass unit (1 u) kehte hain. Baaki sab elements ke atomic mass iske comparison mein bataye jaate hain.
    *   *NCERT Fig 3.4 Analogy:* Jaise fruit seller bina standard weight ke tarbooz (watermelon) ko 12 unit maan le, uske 12 barabar tukde kare, aur phir har phal ko un tukdon ke comparison mein tole (relative fruit mass unit). Waise hi C-12 ke 1/12th part se compare karke atomic mass nikalte hain. [Refer Table 3.2 for examples like H=1u, O=16u, Na=23u]

**4. Molecules aur Ions:**

*   Atoms akele nahi reh paate (except noble gases like Argon, Helium). Woh judkar **Molecules** ya **Ions** banate hain.
*   **Molecule:** Atoms ka group jo chemically bonded ho (e.g., H₂O, O₂, N₂). Atomicity batati hai ek molecule mein kitne atoms hain (O₂ - diatomic, O₃ - triatomic, P₄ - tetra-atomic, S₈ - poly-atomic). [Refer Table 3.3]
*   **Ion:** Jab atom electron gain ya lose karta hai, toh woh charged ban jaata hai, use ion kehte hain.
    *   Metal atoms aksar electron lose karke **Cation** (+) banate hain (Na → Na⁺ + e⁻).
    *   Non-metal atoms aksar electron gain karke **Anion** (-) banate hain (Cl + e⁻ → Cl⁻).
    *   **Polyatomic ions** mein poore group par charge hota hai (e.g., Sulphate ion SO₄²⁻, Ammonium ion NH₄⁺). [Refer Table 3.6]

**5. Chemical Formula Likhna:**

*   Formula likhne ke liye elements ke symbols aur unki **Valency** (combining capacity) pata honi chahiye. [Refer Table 3.6 for common valencies]
*   **Rules:**
    1.  Pehle cation (metal/positive part) ka symbol, phir anion (non-metal/negative part) ka symbol likho.
    2.  Unke upar unki valencies (ya charges) likho.
    3.  Valencies ko **criss-cross** karke niche subscript mein likho (sign nahi lagana).
    4.  Agar subscript '1' hai, toh use nahi likhte.
    5.  Agar common factor hai toh simplify karo (e.g., Ca²⁺ O²⁻ → Ca₂O₂ → CaO).
    6.  Polyatomic ion agar ek se zyada ho, toh use bracket mein likh kar subscript lagao. Jaise Calcium Hydroxide: Ca²⁺ OH⁻ → Ca(OH)₂. Sodium Hydroxide: Na⁺ OH⁻ → NaOH (bracket nahi chahiye kyunki OH ek hi hai).
*   **Examples:**
    *   Hydrogen Chloride: H¹⁺ Cl¹⁻ → HCl
    *   Magnesium Chloride: Mg²⁺ Cl¹⁻ → MgCl₂
    *   Aluminium Oxide: Al³⁺ O²⁻ → Al₂O₃
    *   Sodium Carbonate: Na¹⁺ CO₃²⁻ → Na₂CO₃
    *   Ammonium Sulphate: NH₄¹⁺ SO₄²⁻ → (NH₄)₂SO₄

**6. Molecular Mass aur Formula Unit Mass:**

*   **Molecular Mass:** Molecule mein sabhi atoms ke atomic masses ka sum.
    *   H₂O = (2 × Atomic mass of H) + (1 × Atomic mass of O) = (2 × 1u) + (1 × 16u) = 18u.
    *   HNO₃ = (1 × H) + (1 × N) + (3 × O) = 1u + 14u + (3 × 16u) = 1 + 14 + 48 = 63u.
*   **Formula Unit Mass:** Ionic compounds ke liye use hota hai. Calculation ka tareeka same hai.
    *   NaCl = (1 × Na) + (1 × Cl) = 23u + 35.5u = 58.5u.
    *   CaCl₂ = (1 × Ca) + (2 × Cl) = 40u + (2 × 35.5u) = 40u + 71u = 111u.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh) 📊

*   **Ancient Indian Philosophy:** Lagbhag 500 BC mein, Bharatiya philosopher **Maharishi Kanad** ne 'Padarth' (matter) ke division ka vichar diya. Unhone postulate kiya ki agar hum padarth ko divide karte jayenge, toh अंत mein sabse chhote particles milenge jinhe aur divide nahi kiya ja sakta. Inhe unhone **'Parmanu'** naam diya.
*   Ek aur Bharatiya philosopher, **Pakudha Katyayama**, ne is vichar ko elaborate kiya aur kaha ki yeh 'Parmanu' aam taur par combined form (sanyukt roop) mein exist karte hain, jisse hamein alag-alag tarah ke matter milte hain.
*   Yeh vichar philosophical the aur experimental proof ke bina the, lekin yeh darshata hai ki matter ke fundamental nature ke baare mein soch Bharat mein bahut pehle se thi, jo baad mein scientific discoveries ka adhaar bani.
*   Aaj ke context mein, Bharat mein chemical industries (dawaaiyan, fertilizers, materials) kaafi developed hain, jo atoms aur molecules ki understanding par hi based hain. CSIR labs aur universities mein is field mein research jaari hai.
