# [Class 9] Vigyan - Chapter 3: Parmanu evam Anu (Atoms and Molecules)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein)

Yeh chapter matter ke fundamental building blocks - atoms aur molecules - ke baare mein hai. Concepts ka hierarchy kuch is tarah hai:

1.  **Matter (Padarth):** Har cheez jo jagah ghere aur jiska mass ho.
    *   **Divisibility Idea:** Kya matter ko divide kiya ja sakta hai?
        *   *Prachin Vichar:* Maharishi Kanad (Parmanu - smallest particle), Greek philosophers (Atoms - indivisible).
2.  **Chemical Combination ke Niyam (Laws of Chemical Combination):** Kaise elements combine hokar compounds banate hain.
    *   **Law of Conservation of Mass (Dravyaman Sanrakshan ka Niyam):** Chemical reaction mein mass na create hota hai, na destroy. Total mass constant rehta hai. (Lavoisier)
    *   **Law of Constant Proportions (Sthir Anupat ka Niyam):** Ek pure chemical compound mein elements hamesha mass ke ek definite (fixed) proportion mein hote hain. (Proust)
3.  **Dalton's Atomic Theory:** Pehli scientific theory jo matter ke nature ko explain karti hai.
    *   *Postulates:* Matter atoms se bana hai; atoms indivisible hain (later modified); ek element ke sabhi atoms identical hote hain; alag elements ke atoms different hote hain; atoms small whole number ratio mein combine hokar compounds banate hain; ek compound mein atoms ka relative number aur kind constant hota hai.
4.  **Atoms (Parmanu):** Element ka sabse chota particle jo chemical reaction mein participate karta hai.
    *   *Size:* Bahut chote (nanometers mein measure hote hain).
    *   *Symbols:* Elements ko represent karne ke liye short forms (e.g., H, O, Fe, Na). IUPAC approves symbols.
    *   *Atomic Mass (Parmanu Dravyamana):* Ek atom ka mass. Directly measure karna mushkil, isliye relative atomic mass use karte hain.
        *   *Atomic Mass Unit (u):* Standard unit, Carbon-12 atom ke mass ka 1/12th hissa.
5.  **Molecules (Anu):** Do ya zyada atoms ka group jo chemically bonded ho aur independently exist kar sake.
    *   *Molecules of Elements:* Same type ke atoms se bane (e.g., O2, H2, N2 - diatomic; He, Ar - monoatomic; O3 - triatomic; S8 - polyatomic).
        *   *Atomicity:* Ek molecule mein kitne atoms hain.
    *   *Molecules of Compounds:* Different elements ke atoms fixed ratio mein combine hote hain (e.g., H2O, CO2, NH3).
6.  **Ions:** Charged particles jo atom ya group of atoms ke electron gain ya lose karne se bante hain.
    *   *Cations:* Positively charged ions (e.g., Na+, Ca2+, Al3+). Metals generally cations banate hain.
    *   *Anions:* Negatively charged ions (e.g., Cl-, O2-, S2-). Non-metals generally anions banate hain.
    *   *Polyatomic Ions:* Group of atoms jin par net charge ho (e.g., NH4+ - ammonium, SO4^2- - sulphate, OH- - hydroxide).
7.  **Valency (Sanyojakta):** Ek element ki combining capacity. Atom kitne bond bana sakta hai.
8.  **Chemical Formulae (Rasayanik Sutra):** Kisi compound ke composition ka symbolic representation.
    *   *Writing Formulae:* Valency/charge ko balance karke (criss-cross method).
9.  **Molecular Mass (Aanvik Dravyamana):** Ek molecule mein sabhi atoms ke atomic masses ka sum.
10. **Formula Unit Mass:** Ionic compounds ke ek formula unit mein sabhi atoms ke atomic masses ka sum (calculation molecular mass jaisa hi).

📊 **Concept Hierarchy Visual:**
Matter -> Particles -> Ancient Ideas (Parmanu/Atom) -> Laws (Conservation of Mass, Constant Proportions) -> Dalton's Theory -> Atoms (Symbol, Mass) -> Molecules (Elements/Compounds, Atomicity) / Ions (Cation/Anion/Polyatomic) -> Valency -> Chemical Formula -> Molecular/Formula Unit Mass.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

**1. Laws of Chemical Combination (Rasayanik Sanyojan ke Niyam):**

*   **Dravyaman Sanrakshan ka Niyam (Law of Conservation of Mass):**
    *   *Statement:* "Kisi bhi chemical reaction mein, mass ko na toh banaya ja sakta hai, na hi mitaya ja sakta hai." (Mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction).
    *   *Matlab:* Reaction se pehle reactants ka total mass = Reaction ke baad products ka total mass.
    *   *Example Activity Concept:* Agar ek band flask (sealed conical flask) mein do solutions (jaise Barium Chloride aur Sodium Sulphate) ko mix karein, toh reaction ke baad bhi flask aur uske contents ka total weight same rahega. Cork lagana zaroori hai taaki koi gas bahar na nikle ya andar na aaye.
    *   📊 **Visual:** [Reactant A (Mass X) + Reactant B (Mass Y)] → [Product C (Mass P) + Product D (Mass Q)] ; Hamesha X + Y = P + Q hoga.

*   **Sthir Anupat ka Niyam (Law of Constant Proportions / Definite Proportions):**
    *   *Statement:* "Kisi bhi pure chemical compound mein, elements hamesha mass ke ek nishchit anupat (definite proportion) mein hi hote hain, chahe woh compound kahin se bhi liya gaya ho ya kaise bhi banaya gaya ho." (In a chemical substance, the elements are always present in definite proportions by mass).
    *   *Example:* Paani (Water, H2O) kahin se bhi lo (river, tap, sea) ya lab mein banao, usmein Hydrogen aur Oxygen ka mass ratio hamesha 1:8 hi hoga. Agar 9g water ko decompose karein toh 1g Hydrogen aur 8g Oxygen milega.
    *   *Example:* Ammonia (NH3) mein Nitrogen aur Hydrogen ka mass ratio hamesha 14:3 hota hai.
    *   📈 **Visual:** Pie chart for Water (H2O) showing mass %: Oxygen ≈ 89%, Hydrogen ≈ 11%. Ratio H:O = 1:8 by mass.

**2. Dalton ki Atomic Theory:**

John Dalton ne in laws ko explain karne ke liye ek theory di:
*   Sabhi padarth (matter) bahut chote particles 'atoms' se bane hain.
*   Atoms indivisible hain, jo chemical reaction mein na bante hain, na destroy hote hain (ye Law of Conservation of Mass ko explain karta hai).
*   Ek element ke sabhi atoms ka mass aur chemical properties same hoti hain.
*   Alag-alag elements ke atoms ka mass aur properties alag-alag hoti hain.
*   Atoms chhote whole numbers ke ratio mein combine hokar compounds banate hain.
*   Ek diye gaye compound mein atoms ka relative number aur type hamesha fixed rehta hai (ye Law of Constant Proportions ko explain karta hai).
*   *(Note: Baad mein pata chala ki atoms further divisible hain - into protons, neutrons, electrons - aur isotopes ke karan ek element ke atoms ka mass thoda different ho sakta hai. But Dalton ki theory ek major breakthrough thi.)*

**3. Atoms, Symbols, aur Atomic Mass:**

*   Atoms bahut hi zyada chote hote hain (radius ~ 10^-10 m). Hum unhe naked eye se nahi dekh sakte.
*   Har element ka ek unique symbol hota hai (IUPAC dwara approved). Pehla letter capital, dusra small (e.g., H, He, Na - Natrium se, Fe - Ferrum se).
*   **Atomic Mass Unit (u):** Relative atomic mass measure karne ki unit. 1 u = (1/12) * mass of one Carbon-12 atom.
    *   Example: Hydrogen ka atomic mass ≈ 1 u, Oxygen ≈ 16 u, Sodium ≈ 23 u. (Table 3.2 dekhein)

**4. Molecules aur Atomicity:**

*   **Molecule (Anu):** Do ya zyada atoms ka chemically bonded group jo independently exist kar sakta hai. Yeh substance ki sabhi properties show karta hai.
*   **Molecules of Elements:** Same atoms se bane.
    *   *Monoatomic:* He, Ne, Ar (Atomicity = 1)
    *   *Diatomic:* H2, O2, N2, Cl2 (Atomicity = 2)
    *   *Triatomic:* O3 (Ozone) (Atomicity = 3)
    *   *Polyatomic:* P4 (Tetra-atomic), S8 (Poly-atomic)
*   **Molecules of Compounds:** Different atoms fixed ratio mein jude hote hain.
    *   Example: H2O (Water) - 2 H atoms, 1 O atom. Ratio by number H:O = 2:1. Ratio by mass H:O = (2x1) : (1x16) = 2:16 = 1:8.
    *   Example: CO2 (Carbon Dioxide) - 1 C atom, 2 O atoms. Ratio by number C:O = 1:2. Ratio by mass C:O = (1x12) : (2x16) = 12:32 = 3:8.

**5. Ions (Aayan):**

*   Jab atom ya group of atoms electron gain (-) ya lose (+) karte hain, toh woh charged ho jate hain, inhe ions kehte hain.
*   **Cation:** Positive ion (electron lose karne se banta hai). Example: Na -> Na+ + e-
*   **Anion:** Negative ion (electron gain karne se banta hai). Example: Cl + e- -> Cl-
*   **Polyatomic Ions:** Group of atoms par overall charge. Example: SO4^2- (Sulphate), NH4+ (Ammonium).
*   Ionic Compounds (like NaCl, CaO, MgCl2) cations aur anions se bane hote hain. Inka overall charge neutral hota hai.

**6. Valency aur Chemical Formula Likhna:**

*   **Valency (Sanyojakta):** Element ki combining power. Ek atom kitne dusre atoms se jud sakta hai. Valency numbers (like 1, 2, 3) ya charge (like +1, -2) se represent karte hain. (Table 3.6 mein common ions ki valency di hai).
*   **Chemical Formula (Rasayanik Sutra):** Compound ko symbols se represent karna.
    *   *Rules:*
        1.  Symbols likho. Metal ya positive ion pehle, non-metal ya negative ion baad mein.
        2.  Unke upar valency/charge likho.
        3.  Valencies/charges ko **criss-cross** karke subscript (neeche chota number) banao. Signs (+) (-) hata do. Agar subscript '1' hai toh likhne ki zaroorat nahi. Agar common factor hai toh simplify karo (e.g., Ca2O2 -> CaO). Polyatomic ion agar ek se zyada ho toh bracket use karo.
    *   *Examples:*
        *   Hydrogen Chloride: H (+1), Cl (-1) -> H1Cl1 -> **HCl**
        *   Magnesium Chloride: Mg (+2), Cl (-1) -> Mg1Cl2 -> **MgCl2**
        *   Aluminium Oxide: Al (+3), O (-2) -> Al2O3 -> **Al2O3**
        *   Calcium Hydroxide: Ca (+2), OH (-1) -> Ca1(OH)2 -> **Ca(OH)2** (Bracket zaroori hai)
        *   Sodium Carbonate: Na (+1), CO3 (-2) -> Na2(CO3)1 -> **Na2CO3** (Bracket nahi chahiye kyunki CO3 ek hi hai)
        *   Ammonium Sulphate: NH4 (+1), SO4 (-2) -> (NH4)2(SO4)1 -> **(NH4)2SO4**

**7. Molecular Mass aur Formula Unit Mass:**

*   **Molecular Mass (Aanvik Dravyamana):** Molecule mein sabhi atoms ke atomic masses ka total sum (u mein).
    *   Example H2O: (2 × Atomic mass of H) + (1 × Atomic mass of O) = (2 × 1 u) + (1 × 16 u) = 18 u.
    *   Example HNO3: (1×H) + (1×N) + (3×O) = (1×1) + (1×14) + (3×16) = 1 + 14 + 48 = 63 u.
*   **Formula Unit Mass:** Ionic compounds ke liye use hota hai. Calculation ka tareeka same hai, bas 'molecule' ki jagah 'formula unit' bolte hain.
    *   Example NaCl: (1 × Atomic mass of Na) + (1 × Atomic mass of Cl) = (1 × 23 u) + (1 × 35.5 u) = 58.5 u.
    *   Example CaCl2: (1 × Atomic mass of Ca) + (2 × Atomic mass of Cl) = (1 × 40 u) + (2 × 35.5 u) = 40 + 71 = 111 u.

## 📝 Assessment Prep (Mulyankan ki Tayyari)

**Case Studies & Numericals:**

1.  **Conservation of Mass par Aadharit:**
    *   *Scenario:* Ek reaction mein 4g Sodium Carbonate ne 10g Hydrochloric Acid se react kiya. Products bane 2.5g Carbon Dioxide, 1.5g Water aur kuch Sodium Chloride. Kitna Sodium Chloride bana? Kaunsa law use hua?
    *   *Solution Approach:* Total Reactant Mass = 4g + 10g = 14g. Total Product Mass = 2.5g + 1.5g + Mass(NaCl). Law of Conservation of Mass ke according, 14g = 4g + Mass(NaCl). Isliye, Mass(NaCl) = 10g.

2.  **Constant Proportions par Aadharit:**
    *   *Scenario:* Ammonia (NH3) mein Nitrogen aur Hydrogen ka mass ratio 14:3 hai. Agar aapke paas 6g Hydrogen hai, toh usse completely react karne ke liye kitni Nitrogen chahiye hogi? Kitni Ammonia banegi?
    *   *Solution Approach:* Ratio N:H = 14:3. Agar H = 3g, toh N = 14g. Agar H = 6g (jo ki 3g ka double hai), toh N bhi double lagegi = 14g × 2 = 28g. Total Ammonia banegi = Mass(N) + Mass(H) = 28g + 6g = 34g.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh)

*   **Prachin Bharat mein Parmanu ki Avdharna 📜:** Aaj ki modern atomic theory se bahut pehle, lagbhag 500 BC mein, Bharatiya philosopher **Maharishi Kanad** ne 'Padarth' (matter) ke sukshmatam (smallest) कण ki baat ki thi. Unhone postulate kiya ki agar hum padarth ko divide karte jayein, toh ek stage aayegi jab sabse chota particle milega jise aur divide nahi kiya ja sakta. Is particle ko unhone **'Parmanu'** naam diya.
*   Ek aur Indian philosopher, **Pakudha Katyayama**, ne is vichar ko aage badhaya aur kaha ki ye 'Parmanu' aam taur par combined state (sanyukt roop) mein exist karte hain, jisse humein alag-alag tarah ke padarth milte hain (jo modern concept of molecules/compounds se milta-julta hai).
*   **Vicharon ka Mahatva:** Ye darshata hai ki matter ke fundamental nature ke baare mein gehri soch aur philosophical inquiry Bharat mein prachin kaal se hi maujood thi. Halanki ye ideas philosophical the aur experimental validation 18th century ke baad hua, inka historical significance hai. Ye Greek philosophers Democritus aur Leucippus ke 'atom' ke concept ke samay ke hi hain. 🇮🇳
