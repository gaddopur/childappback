## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter explore karta hai ki 'matter' (padarth) jo humare charo taraf hai, woh scientifically 'pure' (shuddh) hai ya nahi. Aam aadmi ke liye 'pure' ka matlab hota hai 'bina milavat ke', lekin science mein iska matlab alag hai.

*   **Pure Substance (Shuddh Padarth):** Ek hi tarah ke particles se bana hota hai, jinki chemical nature same hoti hai. Example: Iron (Loha), Oxygen, Water (Paani), Sugar (Cheeni). Inki composition hamesha fixed rehti hai.
*   **Mixture (Mishran):** Do ya zyada pure substances ko kisi bhi proportion mein milane se banta hai. Example: Sea water (Samudra ka paani), Soil (Mitti), Air (Hava), Milk (Doodh).

## 📘 Key Learnings

**1. Pure Substances vs. Mixtures (Shuddh Padarth vs Mishran):**
*   **Purity ka Scientific Matlab:** Scientist ke liye, milk pure nahi hai kyunki yeh water, fat, proteins ka mixture hai. Pure substance woh hai jiske sabhi कण (particles) chemically ek jaise hon. Jaise ki Sona (Gold) ek element hai, aur paani (H2O) ek compound hai - yeh dono pure hain.
*   **Mixtures ke Components:** Mixtures mein constituents apni properties retain karte hain aur unhe physical methods se alag kiya ja sakta hai. Jaise namak (Sodium Chloride) ko paani se evaporation (vaashpeekaran) dwara alag kar sakte hain.

**2. Types of Mixtures (Mishran ke Prakar):**

*   **Homogeneous Mixture (Samaangi Mishran):**
    *   Composition throughout uniform hoti hai. Particles alag se nahi dikhte.
    *   Inhe **Solutions (Vilayan)** bhi kehte hain.
    *   Examples: Namak ka paani mein ghol, Sugar solution, Air (Hava), Alloys (Mishra dhatu) jaise Brass (Peetal - Copper aur Zinc ka mixture).
    *   **Solution ke Components:**
        *   **Solvent (Vilayak):** Jo zyada मात्रा (quantity) mein hota hai aur doosre component ko dissolve karta hai (e.g., paani).
        *   **Solute (Vileya):** Jo kam मात्रा mein hota hai aur solvent mein dissolve hota hai (e.g., namak).
    *   **Properties of Solution:** Homogeneous, particles < 1 nm, light scatter nahi karte (Tyndall effect nahi dikhate), stable, filtration se separate nahi hote.
    *   **Concentration (Saandrata):** Solution mein solute kitna hai. Ise mass by mass %, mass by volume %, ya volume by volume % mein express kar sakte hain.
        *   `Mass by mass % = (Mass of solute / Mass of solution) * 100`
        *   `Mass by volume % = (Mass of solute / Volume of solution) * 100`
        *   `Volume by volume % = (Volume of solute / Volume of solution) * 100`
    *   **Saturated Solution (Santript Vilayan):** Ek given temperature par, jab solution mein aur solute dissolve na ho sake.
    *   **Unsaturated Solution (Asantript Vilayan):** Agar solution mein saturation level se kam solute ho.
    *   **Solubility (Vilayata):** Ek given temperature par saturated solution mein jitna solute present hai. Alag substances ki solubility alag hoti hai. Temperature badhane par generally solid ki solubility badhti hai.

*   **Heterogeneous Mixture (Vishamaangi Mishran):**
    *   Composition uniform nahi hoti. Components physically distinct hote hain aur aksar dekhe ja sakte hain.
    *   Examples: Mitti (Soil), Tel aur Paani (Oil and water), Chalk powder in water, Iron filings aur Sulphur ka mixture.
    *   **Types:**
        *   **Suspension (Nilamban):** Solid particles liquid mein dispersed rehte hain, dissolve nahi hote. Particles aankhon se dikh jaate hain.
            *   Properties: Heterogeneous, particles visible, light scatter karte hain (Tyndall effect dikhate hain jab tak particles settle na ho), unstable (particles settle ho jaate hain), filtration se separate ho sakte hain. Example: Muddy water (Ganda paani), Chalk powder in water.
        *   **Colloid (Kolloid):** Mixture homogeneous lagta hai, par actually heterogeneous hota hai. Particle size solution se bada, suspension se chhota hota hai (1 nm - 1000 nm).
            *   Properties: Heterogeneous (microscopic level par), particles aankhon se nahi dikhte par light scatter karte hain (**Tyndall Effect** - jaise cinema hall mein projector ki light ya ghane jungle mein sunlight ka path), stable (particles settle nahi hote), filtration se separate nahi hote (par centrifugation se ho sakte hain).
            *   **Components:**
                *   **Dispersed Phase:** Solute jaisa component (kam मात्रा mein).
                *   **Dispersing Medium:** Solvent jaisa component (zyada मात्रा mein).
            *   Examples: Milk (Doodh - fat dispersed in water), Fog (Kohra - water droplets in air), Smoke (Dhuan - solid particles in air), Jelly, Cheese (Paneer), Ink. (Refer Table 2.1 for more examples like Aerosol, Emulsion, Sol, Gel, Foam).

**3. Physical vs. Chemical Changes (Bhautik vs Rasayanik Parivartan):**

*   **Physical Change:** Substance ki physical properties (colour, hardness, state) badalti hain, par chemical composition nahi. Koi naya substance nahi banta. Reversible ho sakta hai.
    *   Examples: Melting of ice (Baraf ka pighalna), Boiling of water (Paani ka ubalna), Cutting trees, Melting butter.
*   **Chemical Change:** Substance ki chemical composition badal jaati hai. Naya substance banta hai jiski properties alag hoti hain. Generally irreversible hota hai. Ise **Chemical Reaction** bhi kehte hain.
    *   Examples: Burning of wood/paper (Lakdi/Kagaz ka jalna), Rusting of iron (Lohe mein jung lagna), Cooking food (Khana pakana), Digestion of food (Bhojan ka pachna).
    *   *Candle ka jalna dono hai:* Wax ka pighalna (Physical), Wax ka jalna (Chemical).

**4. Types of Pure Substances (Shuddh Padarth ke Prakar):**

*   **Elements (Tatva):** Matter ka basic form, jise chemical reactions se aur simple substances mein toda nahi ja sakta. (Defined by Lavoisier).
    *   **Metals (Dhatu):** Lustrous (chamkeele), Malleable (chaadar ban sakti hai), Ductile (taar ban sakti hai), Good conductors of heat & electricity, Sonorous (aawaz karte hain). Examples: Iron, Gold, Silver, Copper. Mercury (Para) room temp par liquid hai.
    *   **Non-metals (Adhatu):** Variety of colours, Poor conductors, Not lustrous, malleable, ductile, or sonorous. Examples: Hydrogen, Oxygen, Carbon, Sulphur, Bromine.
    *   **Metalloids (Updhatu):** Metals aur non-metals ke beech ki properties show karte hain. Examples: Boron, Silicon, Germanium.

*   **Compounds (Yaugik):** Do ya zyada elements chemically combined hote hain, ek fixed proportion mein.
    *   Properties: Naye compound ki properties uske constituent elements se bilkul alag hoti hain. Composition hamesha fixed rehti hai. Constituents ko sirf chemical ya electrochemical reactions se hi alag kiya ja sakta hai.
    *   Example: Water (H₂O) - Hydrogen (gas, combustible) aur Oxygen (gas, supports combustion) se banta hai, par Paani (liquid, extinguishes fire) ki properties bilkul alag hain. Iron Sulphide (FeS) - Activity 2.4 mein Group II ne banaya, iski properties Iron aur Sulphur se alag hain.

📊 **Mixture vs. Compound (Tulna):**

| Feature             | Mixture (Mishran)                                  | Compound (Yaugik)                                       |
| :------------------ | :------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Formation**       | Just mix, no new substance                         | Elements react, new substance forms                     |
| **Composition**     | Variable (Badal sakti hai)                         | Fixed (Hamesha nishchit)                                |
| **Properties**      | Shows properties of constituents                   | Totally different properties from constituents          |
| **Separation**      | Easy by physical methods (Filtration, Evaporation) | Only by chemical/electrochemical reactions              |
| **Example (Fe + S)**| Iron filings + Sulphur powder (Activity Group I)   | Iron Sulphide (FeS) after heating (Activity Group II) |

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

*   **Daily Life Examples:** Hum rozmarra ki zindagi mein dher saare mixtures use karte hain - Doodh (Milk - emulsion), Chai (Tea - solution + suspension), Lassi/Curd (Colloid/Suspension), Sharbat (Solution), Ganga jal (Suspension ho sakta hai agar mitti ho), Hawa (Air - gaseous solution). Packaged items jaise Namak (Salt), Masale (Spices), Ghee, Butter par 'Pure' likha hona common hai, par scientific view alag hai.
*   **Traditional Practices:** Ghar par 'Mutka' (earthen pot) filtration system banana (Group Activity) ek traditional water purification technique hai jo suspension ke principles par based hai. Dahi se Makhan (Butter) nikalna centrifugation ka ek desi roop hai.
*   **Economic/Social Data Link 📊:** Bharat mein food adulteration (khadya padarthon mein milavat) ek serious issue hai. Scientific knowledge humein yeh samajhne mein madad karti hai ki 'shuddhata' ka asli matlab kya hai aur hum kaise better consumer ban sakte hain. FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) jaise organizations standards set karti hain, jo health aur economy dono ke liye important hai. Yeh chapter humein in standards ko critically evaluate karne ki samajh deta hai. For example, knowing that milk is a colloid helps understand why its fat content (related to quality and price) is measured and standardized.
