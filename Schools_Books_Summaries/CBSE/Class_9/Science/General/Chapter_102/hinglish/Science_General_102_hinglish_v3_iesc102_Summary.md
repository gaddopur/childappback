## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ)

Yeh chapter matter ke classification par focus karta hai, based on its chemical composition. Hum dekhenge ki 'pure' ka matlab scientific context mein kya hota hai aur mixtures kaise bante hain.

**Hierarchy Breakdown:**

1.  **Matter (पदार्थ):** Anything that has mass and occupies space.
2.  **Pure Substances (शुद्ध पदार्थ):** Consist of only one type of particle (atoms or molecules). Inka chemical nature same hota hai.
    *   **Elements (तत्व):** Basic form of matter, cannot be broken down chemically (e.g., Iron (लोहा), Oxygen (ऑक्सीजन), Gold (सोना)).
        *   Metals (धातु): Lustrous, conduct heat/electricity, malleable, ductile (e.g., Copper (तांबा), Silver (चांदी)).
        *   Non-metals (अधातु): Poor conductors, not lustrous (e.g., Hydrogen (हाइड्रोजन), Carbon (कार्बन)).
        *   Metalloids (उपधातु): Intermediate properties (e.g., Silicon (सिलिकॉन), Boron (बोरॉन)).
    *   **Compounds (यौगिक):** Formed when two or more elements combine chemically in a fixed proportion (e.g., Water (पानी - H₂O), Salt (नमक - NaCl)). Inki properties constituent elements se bilkul different hoti hain.
3.  **Mixtures (मिश्रण):** Contain more than one type of pure substance mixed in any proportion. Constituents apni properties retain karte hain.
    *   **Homogeneous Mixtures (समांगी मिश्रण):** Uniform composition throughout (e.g., Salt water (नमक का पानी), Sugar solution (चीनी का घोल), Air (हवा), Alloys (मिश्र धातु)). Inhe **Solutions (विलयन)** bhi kehte hain.
    *   **Heterogeneous Mixtures (विषमांगी मिश्रण):** Non-uniform composition, physically distinct parts (e.g., Soil (मिट्टी), Oil and water (तेल और पानी), Mixture of sand and salt (रेत और नमक का मिश्रण)).
        *   **Suspensions (निलंबन):** Insoluble solid particles dispersed in a liquid, particles visible and settle down (e.g., Muddy water (गंदला पानी), Chalk powder in water).
        *   **Colloids (कोलाइड):** Particle size intermediate between solutions and suspensions. Appears homogeneous but is heterogeneous. Particles don't settle down (e.g., Milk (दूध), Ink (स्याही), Fog (कोहरा)).

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य बातें)

**1. Purity ka Matlab (शुद्धता का अर्थ):**
*   **Common Person:** No adulteration (मिलावट नहीं). Jaise market mein packet par likha 'Pure Ghee' ya 'Pure Milk'.
*   **Scientist:** Substance made of only *one type* of particle with the same chemical nature. Is hisaab se, milk ek mixture hai (water, fat, proteins ka). Pure substance jaise ki Sodium Chloride (NaCl) ya Sugar (चीनी).

**2. Mixtures aur Unke Types (मिश्रण और उनके प्रकार):**
*   **Mixture (मिश्रण):** Jab do ya zyada pure substances kisi bhi ratio mein milaye jaate hain, bina kisi chemical reaction ke. Example: हवा (Air) - Oxygen, Nitrogen, etc. ka mixture; समुद्री पानी (Sea water).
*   **Homogeneous (समांगी):** Composition har jagah same hota hai. Particles itne chhote ki dikhai nahi dete. Example: Namak ka paani. Inhe **Solutions (विलयन)** kehte hain.
    *   ![Homogeneous Mixture](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c5/SaltSolution.png/200px-SaltSolution.png) (Diagram showing salt dissolved uniformly in water)
*   **Heterogeneous (विषमांगी):** Composition uniform nahi hota. Components ko alag-alag dekha ja sakta hai. Example: Tel aur paani ka mixture, daal mein kankad.
    *   ![Heterogeneous Mixture](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Oil_and_Water_Heterogeneous_Mixture.jpg/200px-Oil_and_Water_Heterogeneous_Mixture.jpg) (Diagram showing oil floating on water)

**3. Solutions (विलयन):**
*   Yeh homogeneous mixtures hain.
*   Components:
    *   **Solvent (विलायक):** Jo zyada quantity mein hota hai aur jismein dusra component dissolve hota hai (e.g., Paani in salt water).
    *   **Solute (विलेय):** Jo kam quantity mein hota hai aur solvent mein dissolve hota hai (e.g., Namak in salt water).
*   **Properties:**
    *   Particle size < 1 nm (bahut chhota).
    *   Particles aankhon se nahi dikhte.
    *   Light ko scatter nahi karte (No Tyndall effect).
    *   Stable hote hain (particles settle nahi hote).
    *   Filtration se separate nahi kiye ja sakte.
*   **Examples:** Sugar solution, Tincture of Iodine (Iodine in alcohol), Aerated drinks (CO₂ in water), Air (Gas in gas), Alloys (मिश्र धातु) jaise Brass (पीतल - Zinc + Copper).

**4. Concentration of Solution (विलयन की सांद्रता):**
*   Kitna solute, kitne amount of solution ya solvent mein present hai.
*   **Types:**
    *   **Unsaturated (असंतृप्त):** Aur solute dissolve kiya ja sakta hai.
    *   **Saturated (संतृप्त):** Maximum possible solute dissolved hai at a given temperature. Aur dissolve nahi hoga.
    *   **Solubility (विलेयता):** Amount of solute present in a saturated solution at a specific temperature.
*   **Express karne ke طریقے (Methods):**
    *   Mass by mass percentage = `(Mass of solute / Mass of solution) * 100`
    *   Mass by volume percentage = `(Mass of solute / Volume of solution) * 100`
    *   Volume by volume percentage = `(Volume of solute / Volume of solution) * 100`
*   **Example Calculation:** Agar 40g salt ko 320g water mein milaya, toh solution ka mass = 40g + 320g = 360g. Concentration (mass %) = (40g / 360g) * 100 = 11.1%.

**5. Suspensions (निलंबन):**
*   Yeh heterogeneous mixtures hain.
*   Insoluble solid particles liquid mein suspended rehte hain.
*   **Properties:**
    *   Particles aankhon se dikh jaate hain.
    *   Light ko scatter karte hain (Tyndall effect dikhate hain jab particles suspended ho).
    *   Unstable hote hain (particles settle down ho jaate hain).
    *   Filtration se separate kiye ja sakte hain.
*   **Examples:** Chalk powder in water, Muddy water (गंदला पानी), Wheat flour (आटा) in water.
    *   ![Suspension Settling](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3e/Suspension_diagram.svg/200px-Suspension_diagram.svg.png) (Diagram showing particles settling down)

**6. Colloids (कोलाइड):**
*   Yeh bhi heterogeneous mixtures hain, par homogeneous jaise dikhte hain.
*   Particle size solution se bada, suspension se chhota hota hai (1 nm - 1000 nm approx).
*   **Components:**
    *   **Dispersed Phase (परिक्षिप्त प्रावस्था):** Solute jaisa component (kam मात्रा mein).
    *   **Dispersing Medium (परिक्षेपण माध्यम):** Solvent jaisa component (zyada मात्रा mein).
*   **Properties:**
    *   Particles aankhon se nahi dikhte, par light ko scatter karte hain (**Tyndall Effect**).
    *   Stable hote hain (particles settle nahi hote).
    *   Filtration se easily separate nahi hote, par **Centrifugation** (अपकेंद्रण) technique use hoti hai.
*   **Examples:** Milk (Fat dispersed in water), Fog (Water droplets in air), Smoke (Solid particles in air), Jelly (Liquid in solid), Blood.
    *   **Tyndall Effect:** Scattering of light by colloidal particles. Jaise cinema hall mein projector ki beam ya ghane jungle (dense forest) mein sunlight ka path dikhna.
    *   ![Tyndall Effect](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b3/Tyndalleffect.jpg/250px-Tyndalleffect.jpg) (Diagram showing light beam path visible in colloid)
*   **Common Colloids Table (Table 2.1):** Aerosol (Fog, Smoke), Foam (Shaving cream), Emulsion (Milk), Sol (Mud), Gel (Jelly).

**7. Physical vs. Chemical Changes (भौतिक बनाम रासायनिक परिवर्तन):**
*   **Physical Change (भौतिक परिवर्तन):** Substance ki physical properties (colour, state, density) change hoti hain, par chemical composition same rehta hai. No new substance is formed. Reversible ho sakta hai.
    *   Examples: Melting of ice (बर्फ का पिघलना), Boiling of water (पानी का उबलना), Cutting trees (पेड़ काटना), Dissolving salt in water (नमक का पानी में घुलना).
*   **Chemical Change (रासायनिक परिवर्तन):** Substance ka chemical composition change ho jaata hai. New substance(s) bante hain with different properties. Generally irreversible. Isse **Chemical Reaction (रासायनिक अभिक्रिया)** bhi kehte hain.
    *   Examples: Burning of wood/paper (लकड़ी/कागज का जलना), Rusting of iron (लोहे में जंग लगना), Cooking food (खाना पकाना), Digestion of food (भोजन का पाचन).
    *   Burning of candle mein dono hote hain: Wax ka pighalna (Physical), Wax ka jalna (Chemical).

**8. Elements (तत्व):**
*   Basic form of matter, chemical reaction se simpler substances mein break nahi ho sakte. (Lavoisier ki definition).
*   Types:
    *   **Metals (धातु):** Lustre (चमक), Good conductors, Malleable (पीटकर sheet बनाना), Ductile (खींचकर तार बनाना), Sonorous (आवाज़ करना). E.g., Iron, Gold, Copper. Mercury (पारा) room temperature par liquid metal hai.
    *   **Non-metals (अधातु):** No lustre (except Iodine), Poor conductors, Not malleable/ductile. E.g., Oxygen, Hydrogen, Carbon, Bromine (room temp par liquid non-metal).
    *   **Metalloids (उपधातु):** Properties metals aur non-metals ke beech ki hoti hain. E.g., Boron, Silicon, Germanium.

**9. Compounds (यौगिक):**
*   Do ya zyada elements chemically combined hote hain, ek **fixed proportion** mein by mass.
*   Properties apne constituent elements se bilkul alag hoti hain. Example: Hydrogen (H₂) aur Oxygen (O₂) gases hain, par unse bana compound Water (H₂O) liquid hai.
*   Components ko sirf chemical ya electrochemical methods se hi separate kiya ja sakta hai.
*   **Activity 2.4 Example:**
    *   Group I: Iron filings + Sulphur powder ko mix kiya (Physical change) -> Mixture bana. Iron magnet se attract hoga, Sulphur Carbon disulphide mein ghulega.
    *   Group II: Iron filings + Sulphur powder ko heat kiya (Chemical change) -> Compound (Iron Sulphide - FeS) bana. Ab yeh magnet se attract nahi hoga, iski properties Iron aur Sulphur se alag hongi.

**Mixtures vs. Compounds (मिश्रण बनाम यौगिक):**

| Feature          | Mixture (मिश्रण)                               | Compound (यौगिक)                                      |
| :--------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| Formation        | Just mix, no new substance (सिर्फ मिलते हैं)    | Elements react, new substance forms (अभिक्रिया होती है) |
| Composition      | Variable (बदल सकता है)                         | Fixed (निश्चित होता है)                               |
| Properties       | Shows properties of constituents (घटकों के गुण) | Totally different properties (बिलकुल नए गुण)           |
| Separation       | Easy by physical methods (भौतिक तरीकों से)     | Only by chemical methods (रासायनिक तरीकों से)         |

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

*   **Purity Concerns:** Bharat mein festivals ya daily use ke liye ghee, milk, honey, spices khareedte waqt 'purity' ek bada concern rehta hai due to common adulteration (मिलावट). Scientific definition of purity helps understand that even unadulterated milk is a mixture. FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) sets standards for food items.
*   **Traditional Separation:** Techniques like winnowing ( बरसाना / उड़ाना - separating husk from grains) or hand-picking (stones from rice/dal) are traditional physical methods of separation used in Indian households, demonstrating separation of heterogeneous mixtures.
*   **Everyday Examples:**
    *   **Tea (चाय) Making:** Is process mein solution (sugar in water), solvent (water), solute (sugar, tea essence), dissolving, soluble (sugar), insoluble (tea leaves), filtrate (chai), and residue (used tea leaves) concepts apply hote hain.
    *   **Alloys:** Use of Brass (पीतल) and Bronze (कांसा) for utensils and statues, Stainless Steel (इस्पात) in kitchens, and Gold alloys in jewellery are common examples of solid solutions in India.
    *   **Water Sources:** River water (नदी का पानी) is often a suspension, while well water (कुएं का पानी) might contain dissolved salts (solution). Cleaning these involves understanding mixture types. The 'mutka' filtration is a simple, indigenous technique.
*   **Air Quality Data:** Cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata often report Air Quality Index (AQI) data, which reflects the concentration of various pollutants (solutes/dispersed phase) in the air (solvent/dispersing medium). This data highlights air as a complex mixture whose composition varies.
