## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख)

Har game ke specific rules (नियम), playing area (खेल क्षेत्र), aur skills (कौशल) hote hain. Teamwork (टीम वर्क) aur coordination (समन्वय) sabhi games mein jeetne ke liye zaroori hain.

**1. Basketball:**
*   **Aim:** Ball ko basket mein daal kar points score karna.
*   **Court:** 28m x 15m आयताकार court, dono ends par basket.
*   **Gameplay:** 5 players per team court par. Ball ko dribble ya pass karke move karna. 4 quarters (प्रत्येक 10 मिनट).
*   **Skills:** Dribbling (बॉल को लगातार बाउंस करना), Passing (साथी खिलाड़ी को बॉल देना - Chest Pass, Bounce Pass), Shooting (बास्केट में बॉल डालना - Set shot, Lay-up shot), Rebounding (missed shot के बाद बॉल हासिल करना).
*   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.1 Basketball Court ka layout dikhata hai jismein centre circle, free-throw line, three-point line shamil hain. Fig 7.3 Player Stance (खिलाड़ी की मुद्रा) dikhata hai - ghutne mude hue, ball dono haathon mein.

**2. Cricket:**
*   **Aim:** Batting team ka aim runs banana, fielding team ka aim batsmen ko out karna aur runs rokna.
*   **Field:** Circular/Oval ground jiske centre mein 20.12m lambi pitch hoti hai. Dono ends par wickets.
*   **Gameplay:** 11 players per team. Ek team bat karti hai, doosri field. Bowler ball pitch par deliver karta hai. Batsman ball hit karke runs leta hai. Innings ke baad roles switch hote hain.
*   **Skills:** Batting (बल्लेबाजी - drives, cuts, defence), Bowling (गेंदबाजी - fast, spin), Fielding (क्षेत्ररक्षण - catching, throwing).
*   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.10 Cricket field positions (जैसे slips, mid-on, cover) dikhata hai. Fig 7.11 Cricket Pitch ki dimensions (लंबाई, चौड़ाई, crease markings) dikhata hai.

**3. Football:**
*   **Aim:** Ball ko kick karke opponent ke goal post mein daalna. Haathon ka istemal (goalkeeper ke alawa) mana hai.
*   **Field:** Rectangular field (आयताकार मैदान), dono ends par goal posts. (Dimensions Fig 7.12 mein).
*   **Gameplay:** 11 players per team. 90 minutes ka game (2 halves of 45 mins). Ball ko dribble, pass, ya kick karke move karna.
*   **Skills:** Dribbling (पैरों से बॉल कंट्रोल करते हुए दौड़ना), Passing (साथी को बॉल देना), Shooting (गोल पर मारना), Tackling (विरोधी से बॉल छीनना), Heading (सिर से बॉल मारना).
*   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.12 Football field ki dimensions aur markings (जैसे penalty area, centre circle, goal line) dikhata hai.

**4. Hockey:**
*   **Aim:** Stick se ball ko hit karke opponent ke goal mein daalna.
*   **Field:** Rectangular field (आयताकार मैदान), usually artificial turf. Dono ends par goal. Striking circle (शूटिंग सर्कल) se hi goal score ho sakta hai. (Dimensions Fig 7.19 mein).
*   **Gameplay:** 11 players per team. Stick ke flat side se hi ball khelna hai. 2 halves (प्रत्येक 35 मिनट).
*   **Skills:** Dribbling (स्टिक से बॉल कंट्रोल करना), Hitting (जोर से मारना), Pushing (धकेलना), Stopping (बॉल रोकना), Tackling.
*   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.19 Hockey field ki dimensions, shooting circle, 23m line, centre line dikhata hai.

**5. Kabaddi:**
*   **Aim:** Raider (रेडर) opponent ke court mein jaakar 'Kabaddi' bolte hue defenders (बचाव करने वाले) ko touch karke apne court mein safe lautna. Defenders ka aim raider ko rokna.
*   **Court:** 13m x 10m (Men), divided into two halves by a midline, with baulk lines and bonus lines.
*   **Gameplay:** 7 players per team court par. Teams alternate between raiding and defending. Points touch karne ya raider ko pakadne se milte hain.
*   **Skills:** Raiding (Hand touch, Foot touch, Kicking, Dodging), Defending (Ankle hold, Waist hold, Chain tackle, Blocking).
*   📈 **Diagram:** Kabaddi court ka layout (Fig 7.23 jaisa action dikhata hai, court layout text mein describe kiya gaya hai).

**6. Kho-Kho:**
*   **Aim:** Chasing team (पीछा करने वाली टीम) ke members runners (दौड़ने वाले) ko kam se kam time mein touch karke out karna.
*   **Court:** 29m x 16m rectangular court, centre mein 8 squares, dono ends par poles. (Layout Fig 7.26 mein).
*   **Gameplay:** 9 chasers field par (8 बैठे hue, 1 active), 3 runners ek batch mein. Chaser 'Kho' dekar teammate ko active karta hai. Specific rules for changing direction and crossing central lane.
*   **Skills:** Chasing (Giving Kho, Diving, Trapping, Pole dive), Running (Dodging, Ring play, Chain play).
*   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.26 Kho-Kho playfield ka detailed layout (dimensions, squares, poles, free zone) dikhata hai.

**7. Volleyball:**
*   **Aim:** Ball ko net ke upar se मारकर opponent ke court mein ground karna.
*   **Court:** 18m x 9m court, beech mein net (Men: 2.43m high, Women: 2.24m high). Attack line 3m par. (Layout Fig 7.30 mein).
*   **Gameplay:** 6 players per team court par. Ball ko serve karke shuru karte hain. Team ball ko max 3 baar touch kar sakti hai (block ko chhodkar). Points rally jeetne par milte hain. Players clockwise rotate karte hain jab serve milta hai.
*   **Skills:** Service (Underhand, Overhead), Passing (Underhand/Bump, Overhead/Set), Spiking (जोर से मारना), Blocking (नेट पर रोकना).
*   📈 **Diagram:** Fig 7.30 Volleyball court ka layout (dimensions, zones - front/back, service zone, net height) dikhata hai. Fig 7.31 Underhand Serve ke steps dikhata hai. Fig 7.32 Spiking action dikhata hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

Team games India ke culture ka important hissa hain. Kuch games jaise Kabaddi aur Kho-Kho ka origin India mein hua hai.

*   **Kabaddi & Kho-Kho:** Ye indigenous games (स्वदेशी खेल) hain jo ab international level par bhi khele jaate hain. Kabaddi Asian Games ka hissa hai aur Pro Kabaddi League ne ise bahut popular banaya hai. AKFI (Amateur Kabaddi Federation of India) iski governing body hai.
*   **Cricket:** British dwara laya gaya yeh khel India mein sabse popular hai. India ne 1932 mein pehla Test match khela. Ranji Trophy (Prince Ranjit Singh ke naam par) hamara premier domestic tournament hai. India ne Cricket World Cups (1983, 2011 - ODI; 2007 - T20) jeete hain. IPL (Indian Premier League) duniya ki sabse badi T20 league hai. BCCI (Board of Control for Cricket in India) yahan cricket ko govern karti hai.
*   **Hockey:** Ek samay mein Hockey India ka national game mana jata tha. India ne Olympics mein 8 Gold Medals jeete hain (Golden Era: 1928-1956). Major Dhyan Chand jaise legends isi khel se hain. Hockey India desh mein game ko promote karti hai.
*   **Football:** Football bhi India mein kafi popular hai, khaas kar West Bengal, Goa, Kerala aur North-East mein. AIFF (All India Football Federation) iski governing body hai. India ne Asian Games (1951, 1962) mein Gold jeeta tha. ISL (Indian Super League) ne game ko naya boost diya hai.
*   **Basketball:** BFI (Basketball Federation of India) 1950 mein bani. Schools aur colleges mein yeh game popular ho raha hai. National championships regularly hoti hain.
*   **Economic/Social Data (आर्थिक/सामाजिक डेटा) 📊:** Sports leagues jaise IPL aur PKL ne sports economy ko badhaya hai, players ke liye income opportunities create ki hain, aur viewership badhai hai. Yeh games social cohesion (सामाजिक एकजुटता) bhi badhate hain jab log apni favourite teams ko support karte hain. Khelo India jaise sarkari initiatives bhi grassroots talent ko promote kar rahe hain.
