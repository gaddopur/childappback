## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter team games ke baare mein hai, jahan players ek saath milkar ek common goal ke liye kaam karte hain. Team games humein cooperation, respect, self-esteem aur team cohesion sikhate hain. Is chapter mein hum kuch popular team games ke baare mein jaanenge.

**Concept Hierarchy 📊:**

## 📘 Key Learnings

Har game ke specific rules, skills, aur history ko samajhna important hai.

**1. Basketball 🏀**

*   **Origin:** Dr. James Naismith ne 1891 mein Springfield College, USA mein invent kiya. India mein YMCA Kolkata ne introduce kiya. BFI (Basketball Federation of India) 1950 mein bani.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Court: 28m x 15m, indoor ya outdoor. Beech mein mid-court line.
    *   Teams: 2 teams, har team mein 5 players court par (total 12 members). Unlimited substitutions jab play stopped ho.
    *   Duration: 4 quarters of 10 minutes each. Rest: 2 min (Q1-Q2, Q3-Q4), 15 min (Q2-Q3). Extra time: 5 min.
    *   Goal: Ball ko horizontally lage basket mein daalna.
    *   Start: Jumpball se center circle mein.
    *   Movement: Dribbling (ball ko bounce karna) aur passing se ball move hoti hai.
    *   Fouls: Illegal physical contact. 5 fouls ke baad player game se bahar. Free throws mil sakte hain.
*   **Equipment:** Ball, Clocks, Score sheets, Scoreboard, Whistle, etc. Uniform: Jersey, shorts, high-top sneakers.
*   **Skills:** Player Stance, Ball Handling, Dribbling (stationary, cross-over, reverse, behind-the-back), Passing (chest, overhead, bounce, etc.), Shooting (set shot, lay-up, jump shot), Rebounding, Individual Defence.
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.1 (Court), Fig 7.2 (Ring, Net, Ball), Fig 7.3 (Stance), Fig 7.4 (Passing), Fig 7.5 (Shooting), Fig 7.6 (Rebounding), Fig 7.7 (Defence).

**2. Cricket 🏏**

*   **Origin:** England mein develop hua. MCC (Marylebone Cricket Club) important hai. ICC (International Cricket Council) governing body hai. India mein British laaye. BCCI (Board of Control for Cricket in India) 1928 mein bani. Ranji Trophy important national championship hai.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Teams: 2 teams, 11 players each. Ek team bat karti hai, dusri field.
    *   Pitch: Center mein rectangular area (20.12m long).
    *   Goal: Batting team runs banati hai, fielding team batsmen ko out karti hai.
    *   Runs: Batsman ball hit karke pitch ke dusre end tak daud kar score karta hai. Boundary par 4 ya 6 runs milte hain.
    *   Out: Bowled (ball wicket par lagna), Caught (ball hawa mein pakadna), etc. 10 batsmen out hone par inning khatam.
    *   Overs: Bowler 6 balls ka set (over) dalta hai.
*   **Field & Equipment:** Pitch, Leather Ball (155.9-163g), Wooden Bat (max 10.8cm wide, 96.5cm long), Wickets (3 stumps, 2 bails).
*   **Officials:** 2 Umpires on-field, 1 Third Umpire.
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.8 (Cricket Action), Fig 7.9 (Out), Fig 7.10 (Field Positions), Fig 7.11 (Pitch).

**3. Football ⚽**

*   **Origin:** Ancient China mein evidence. Modern game FIFA (Federation Internationale de Football Association, 1904) govern karti hai. India mein British laaye. AIFF (All India Football Federation) governing body hai. 1951-1962 Indian football ka 'Golden Era' tha.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Field: Rectangular (dimensions in Fig 7.12).
    *   Teams: 2 teams, 11 players each (including goalkeeper). Min 7 players to start.
    *   Duration: 2 halves of 45 minutes each. Interval 10-15 min.
    *   Goal: Ball ko opponent ke goal post ke andar daalna.
    *   Start: Coin toss ke baad Kick-off se center line se.
    *   Fouls: Kicking, tripping opponent etc. Direct/Indirect Free Kick ya Penalty Kick milta hai.
    *   Other Rules: Throw-in (ball sideline se bahar), Corner Kick (defending team se ball goal line ke bahar).
    *   Cards: Yellow (caution), Red (sending off).
*   **Equipment:** Leather Ball (circumference 68-70cm, weight 410-450g).
*   **Skills:** Dribbling, Heading, Kicking, Tackling (ball jeetna), Passing.
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.12 (Field), Fig 7.13 (Dribbling), Fig 7.14 (Heading), Fig 7.15 (Kicking), Fig 7.16 (Tackling), Fig 7.17 (Passing).

**4. Hockey 🏑**

*   **Origin:** British servicemen India laaye. First club Kolkata (1885). FIH (International Hockey Federation, 1924) global body hai. India ne Olympics mein kai gold medals jeete (1928-1956, 1964, 1980). Artificial turf 1976 mein introduce hua.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Field: Rectangular (dimensions in Fig 7.19). Shooting circle important hai.
    *   Teams: 2 teams, 11 players each (including goalkeeper).
    *   Duration: 2 halves of 35 minutes each. Rest 10 min. Tie hone par extra time (Golden Goal rule) ya tie-breaker.
    *   Goal: Ball ko stick ke flat side se striking circle ke andar se goal mein daalna.
    *   Fouls: Obstruction, playing with rounded side of stick, dangerous play. Penalty Corner ya Penalty Stroke mil sakta hai.
    *   Other Rules: 16-yard hit, Push-in/Hit-in (ball sideline se bahar).
*   **Equipment:** Hockey Stick (wood/composite), Ball (156-163g), Goalkeeper protective gear (helmet, pads, etc.).
*   **Skills:** Hitting, Stopping, Dribbling, Scoop (aerial pass), Overhead Flick, Push.
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.18 (Stick & Ball), Fig 7.19 (Field), Fig 7.20 (Playing Hockey), Fig 7.21 (Dribbling), Fig 7.22 (Pushing).

**5. Kabaddi 🏃‍♂️**

*   **Origin:** Indigenous Indian game. Rules Maharashtra mein 1921 mein frame hue. AKFI (Amateur Kabaddi Federation of India, 1972) governing body hai. Asian Games mein 1990 se shamil hai.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Court: Rectangular, soft surface (13m x 10m for men). Bonus line, Baulk line important hain.
    *   Teams: 2 teams, 7 players play (total 12).
    *   Duration: 2 halves of 20 minutes each. Interval 5 min.
    *   Gameplay: Ek team ka player (Raider) dusri team ke court mein "Kabaddi Kabaddi" bolte hue (Cant) jaata hai aur opponents ko touch karke apne court mein safe lautne ki koshish karta hai. Defenders (Antis) raider ko pakadne ki koshish karte hain.
    *   Scoring: Touch karke out karna, raider ko pakadna, boundary se bahar jaana, Lona (poori team out hone par extra points).
    *   Time Out: Har team ko har half mein 2 time-outs (30 sec each).
*   **Skills:**
    *   Raiding: Feint, Leg raid, Hand touch, Toe touch, Foot touch, Kicking, Safe raid tactics (jumping, diving).
    *   Defensive: Catch/Hold (wrist, ankle, trunk), Blocking (chain formation).
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.23 (Catch), Fig 7.24 (Hand Touch), Fig 7.25 (Foot Touch).

**6. Kho-Kho 🏃‍♀️**

*   **Origin:** Indigenous Indian game, modified 'Run and Chase'. Maharashtra mein 'Rathera' ke naam se jaana jaata tha. Rules Pune Gymkhana (1914) ne frame kiye.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Field: Rectangular (29m x 16m). Central lane, squares, posts hote hain.
    *   Teams: 2 teams, 9 players play (total 12). Ek team chase karti hai (Chasers), dusri run (Runners).
    *   Duration: 2 innings, har inning mein 2 turns (9 min each). Interval 5 min.
    *   Gameplay: 8 chasers squares mein alternate directions mein face karke baithte hain. 9th chaser (Attacker) run karta hai. Attacker baithhe hue chaser ko peeth par touch karke "Kho" bolta hai, jo fir active chaser ban jaata hai. Chaser ko runners ko touch karke out karna hota hai. Runners batches mein (3 players) aate hain.
    *   Fouls: Central lane cross karna, galat direction mein mudna, bina Kho liye square chhodna.
*   **Skills:**
    *   Chasing: Giving Kho, Pole Dive, Trapping, Diving (various types), Getting up techniques (Monkey crawl, Standing), Sitting techniques (Toe method, Parallel toe method).
    *   Running: Taking positions, Chain running (single, double).
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.26 (Field), Fig 7.27 (Pole Tackle), Fig 7.28 (Kho Giving), Fig 7.29 (Kho Chase).

**7. Volleyball 🏐**

*   **Origin:** William G. Morgan ne 1895, USA mein invent kiya. Recreational game tha pehle. 1964 Tokyo Olympics mein shamil hua.
*   **Kaise Khele:**
    *   Court: Rectangular (18m x 9m). Net height: 2.43m (Men), 2.24m (Women). Attack line (3m from center).
    *   Teams: 2 teams, 6 players play (total 12). Libero ek special defensive player hota hai.
    *   Gameplay: Toss ke baad ek team serve karti hai. Ball ko net ke upar se opponent ke court mein ground karna hota hai. Team ball ko max 3 baar touch kar sakti hai (block count nahi hota).
    *   Scoring (Rally Point System): Har rally (serve se ball dead hone tak) par point milta hai. Point jeetne wali team serve karti hai (agar pehle receiving team thi).
    *   Set: Pehle 25 points (min 2 points lead) jeetne wali team set jeet ti hai. Match: Best of 5 sets (5th set 15 points tak).
    *   Rotation: Serve jeetne par players clockwise rotate karte hain.
*   **Equipment:** Ball (Leather/Synthetic, 65-67cm, 260-280g), Net, Posts, Antenna (flexible rod on net).
*   **Skills:** Service (Underhand, Overhead), Passing (Underhand/Bump, Overhead/Set), Spike/Smash (Attack), Block.
*   **Diagram Reference:** Fig 7.30 (Court), Fig 7.31 (Underhand Serve), Fig 7.32 (Spiking).

## 🧩 Active Learning

*   **Activity ✍️:** Apne school ke ground mein Kho-Kho ya Kabaddi court ke dimensions ko measure karke chalk se mark karo. Rules follow karte hue ek friendly match khelo. (Applying/Creating)

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

India ka team games mein rich history aur contribution hai.

*   **Indigenous Games:** Kabaddi aur Kho-Kho Bharat ke apne khel hain jinki shuruat yahin hui. Maharashtra ne inke development mein important role play kiya hai. Aaj ye games national aur international level par khele jaate hain. (e.g., Pro Kabaddi League ne Kabaddi ko bahut popular banaya hai).
*   **Cricket:** Cricket India mein ek religion ki tarah hai. British Raj se shuru hokar aaj BCCI duniya ka sabse rich cricket board hai. Ranji Trophy jaise domestic tournaments ne kai international stars diye hain. India ne Cricket World Cups (1983, 2011 - Men's ODI; 2007 - Men's T20) jeete hain. IPL (Indian Premier League) ek global phenomenon ban gaya hai.
*   **Hockey:** Ek samay tha jab Indian Hockey team ka Olympics mein दबदबा (dominance) tha, humne 8 Gold medals jeete hain. Major Dhyan Chand jaise legends isi khel se hain. Aaj bhi Hockey India mein popular hai.
*   **Football:** India mein football ki popularity badh rahi hai, khaas kar West Bengal, Kerala, Goa aur North-East states mein. Indian Super League (ISL) ne game ko boost diya hai. AIFF game ko promote karne ke liye kaam kar rahi hai. India ne 1951 aur 1962 Asian Games mein Gold medal jeeta tha.
*   **Basketball & Volleyball:** Ye games schools aur colleges mein kafi popular hain. BFI aur VFI national level par tournaments organize karte hain. Indian teams international level par compete kar rahi hain. YMCA ne in games ko India mein popularize karne mein help ki.

Yeh sabhi games na sirf physical fitness dete hain balki national pride aur unity ki भावना (feeling) bhi badhate hain.
