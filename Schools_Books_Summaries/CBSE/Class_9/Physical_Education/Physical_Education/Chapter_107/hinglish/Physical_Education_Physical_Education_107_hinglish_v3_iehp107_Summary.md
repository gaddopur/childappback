# [Class 9] Physical Education - Chapter 107 (Team Games)

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter team games ke baare mein hai, jahan khiladi ek saath milkar ek common goal ke liye kaam karte hain. Isse cooperation aur respect badhta hai. Hum alag-alag team games ke baare mein jaanenge.

**Concept Hierarchy 📊:**

1.  **Team Games ka Introduction**
    *   Teamwork ka mahatva (importance)
    *   Self-esteem aur team cohesion building
2.  **Basketball 🏀**
    *   Itihaas (History): Dr. James Naismith, India mein YMCA ka role, BFI.
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Court dimensions, Teams (5 players on court), Duration (Quarters), Jumpball, Offense/Defense, Scoring, Fouls, Officials.
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Court (Indoor/Outdoor), Ball, Ring, Net.
    *   Buniyadi Kaushal (Fundamental Skills): Player Stance, Ball Handling, Dribbling (Stationary, Cross-over, Reverse, Behind-the-back), Passing (Chest, Overhead, Bounce etc.), Shooting (Set shot, Lay-up, Jump shot), Rebounding, Individual Defence.
3.  **Cricket 🏏**
    *   Itihaas (History): Hambeldon Club, MCC, ICC, British India mein shuruat, BCCI, Ranji Trophy, ODI, T20/IPL.
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Teams (11 players), Innings, Batting/Fielding, Wicket, Runs (scoring), Boundaries (4s, 6s), Dismissal (Out hona), Overs.
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Pitch, Ball (Leather), Bat (Wooden), Wickets (Stumps, Bails), Crease, Fielding positions, Umpires.
4.  **Football ⚽**
    *   Itihaas (History): FIFA, AIFF, Ancient China roots, British India mein popularity, Golden Era (1951-62), Olympic/Asian Games mein India.
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Duration (Halves), Teams (11 players), Toss, Kick-off, Goal scoring, Fouls (Direct/Indirect free kick, Penalty kick), Throw-in, Corner kick, Card system (Yellow/Red).
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Field dimensions, Ball (Leather).
    *   Buniyadi Kaushal (Fundamental Skills): Dribbling, Heading, Kicking, Tackling, Passing.
5.  **Hockey 🏑**
    *   Itihaas (History): British India mein shuruat, Kolkata Club, FIH, India ka Olympic dominance, Artificial turf ka impact.
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Teams (11 players), Duration (Halves), Stick use (flat side only), Goal scoring, Tie-breaker (Golden Goal, Penalty shootout), Fouls, Penalty Corner, Penalty Stroke, 16-yard hit, Push-in.
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Stick (Wood/Composite), Ball, Goalkeeper's protective gear, Goal post dimensions, Field lines (Shooting circle, 23m line).
    *   Buniyadi Kaushal (Fundamental Skills): Hitting, Stopping, Dribbling, Scoop (Aerial pass), Overhead flick, Push.
6.  **Kabaddi 🏃‍♂️**
    *   Itihaas (History): Maharashtra mein rules (1921), AKFI (1972), Asian Games mein shamil hona.
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Teams (7 players on court), Toss, Duration (Halves), Raider, Cant (Kabaddi bolna), Out rules (Touch, Hold), Boundary rules, Time-outs, Substitution, Scoring, Lona, Bauck line.
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Court dimensions (soft surface).
    *   Buniyadi Kaushal (Fundamental Skills):
        *   Raiding Skills: Feint, Leg raid, Shuffling, Reverse step, Hand touch/swings, Toe/Foot touch, Kicking, Safe raid tactics.
        *   Defensive Skills: Catch/Hold (Wrist, Ankle, Trunk), Blocking (Chain formation).
7.  **Kho-Kho 🏃‍♀️**
    *   Itihaas (History): Modified 'Run and Chase', Rathera, Pune Gymkhana rules (1914).
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Teams (9 chasers, 3 runners initially), Toss (Chasing/Running choice), Chaser positions (Squares), Runner batches, Getting out, Giving 'Kho', Central lane rule, Direction rule, Foul rules, Innings/Turns duration.
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Rectangular court dimensions, Posts.
    *   Buniyadi Kaushal (Fundamental Skills):
        *   Chasing Skills: Giving Kho, First step after Kho, Pole dive, Trapping, Diving (various types), Getting up (Monkey crawl, Standing), Sitting techniques (Toe method, Parallel toe method).
        *   Running Skills: Batch running, Chain/Double Chain (zig-zag running).
8.  **Volleyball 🏐**
    *   Itihaas (History): William G. Morgan (1895), Recreational game se popular sport, Olympic inclusion (1964).
    *   Kaise Khele (How to Play): Teams (6 players on court), Toss, Libero player, Scoring (Point, Set, Match), Faults (e.g., >3 touches, double touch), Rally, Rotation order/fault, Default.
    *   Maidan aur Upkaran (Field and Equipment): Court dimensions, Net height (Men/Women), Antenna, Posts, Ball (Leather/Synthetic).
    *   Buniyadi Kaushal (Fundamental Skills): Service (Underhand, Overhead), Passing (Underhand/Bump, Overhead/Set), Spike/Smash, Blocking (mentioned implicitly under faults).

## 📘 Key Learnings

Har team game ke apne unique niyam (rules), itihaas (history), aur kaushal (skills) hote hain.

1.  **Basketball:** Ek fast-paced game jisme ball ko basket mein daalna hota hai. Neuromuscular coordination zaroori hai. Skills jaise dribbling, passing, shooting, aur rebounding important hain. Court ke specific dimensions aur rules hote hain jaise fouls aur free throws. 📈 Diagram: Basketball Court (Fig 7.1), Player Stance (Fig 7.3), Passing (Fig 7.4), Shooting (Fig 7.5).
2.  **Cricket:** Bat aur ball ka game jisme 2 teams khelti hain. Ek team batting karke runs banati hai, doosri fielding karke batsmen ko out karti hai. Alag-alag formats hain (Test, ODI, T20). Skills mein batting, bowling, fielding, wicket-keeping shamil hain. Pitch, bat, ball, wickets ke specific measurements hote hain. 📈 Diagram: Cricket Field Positions (Fig 7.10), Cricket Pitch (Fig 7.11).
3.  **Football:** Duniya ka sabse popular game. Ball ko pairon se control karke opponent ke goal post mein daalna hota hai. Haath use karna allowed nahi hai (goalkeeper ke alawa penalty area mein). Skills jaise dribbling, passing, tackling, shooting, heading important hain. Rules mein offside, fouls, free kicks, penalty kicks, cards shamil hain. 📈 Diagram: Football Field (Fig 7.12), Dribbling (Fig 7.13), Heading (Fig 7.14), Kicking (Fig 7.15).
4.  **Hockey:** Stick aur ball ka game. Ball ko stick ke flat side se khelkar opponent ke goal mein daalna hota hai (shooting circle ke andar se). Skills mein dribbling, hitting, stopping, pushing, scooping shamil hain. Goalkeeper ke liye special protective gear hota hai. Penalty corner aur penalty stroke specific situations mein diye jaate hain. 📈 Diagram: Hockey Field (Fig 7.19), Stick and Ball (Fig 7.18).
5.  **Kabaddi:** Ek traditional Indian game jisme agility, power, aur breath control chahiye. Raider opponent ke court mein 'Kabaddi' bolte hue jaata hai aur defenders ko touch karke wapas aane ki koshish karta hai. Defenders raider ko rokne ki koshish karte hain. Skills mein raiding (hand touch, foot touch, kicks) aur defending (holds, blocks) shamil hain. 📈 Diagram: Kabaddi Catch (Fig 7.23), Hand Touch (Fig 7.24), Foot Touch (Fig 7.25).
6.  **Kho-Kho:** Ek aur popular Indian game. Chasing team ke members squares mein baithte hain, ek active chaser runners ko pakadne ki koshish karta hai. 'Kho' dekar chase transfer hota hai. Skills mein chasing (giving kho, pole dive, trapping) aur running (dodging, chain running) shamil hain. 📈 Diagram: Kho-Kho Play Field (Fig 7.26), Kho Giving/Chase (Fig 7.28, 7.29).
7.  **Volleyball:** Net ke upar ball ko haathon se khelkar opponent ke court mein ground karna hota hai. Team ko ball ko 3 touch mein hi wapas bhejna hota hai (block ko touch nahi gina jaata). Skills mein serving, passing (bump/set), spiking, blocking shamil hain. Rotation ka specific pattern follow karna hota hai. 📈 Diagram: Volleyball Court (Fig 7.30), Underhand Serve (Fig 7.31), Spiking (Fig 7.32).

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

Team games ka India mein ek rich itihaas aur significant presence hai.

*   **Indigenous Games:** Kabaddi aur Kho-Kho Bharat ke apne traditional games hain jo ab international level par bhi khele jaate hain. Amateur Kabaddi Federation of India (AKFI) Kabaddi ko promote karti hai. India ne Asian Games mein Kabaddi mein consistently dominate kiya hai. Kho-Kho Federation of India (KKFI) is game ko govern karti hai.
*   **Cricket Craze:** Cricket, British dwara laya gaya, India ka sabse popular sport ban gaya hai. Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) duniya ke sabse rich sports bodies mein se ek hai. Ranji Trophy national championship hai. IPL ne game ko aur popular banaya hai. India ne Cricket World Cups (ODI & T20) jeete hain. 🏏🇮🇳
*   **Hockey Heritage:** Hockey mein India ka golden past raha hai, multiple Olympic gold medals (1928-1956, 1964, 1980) jeete hain. Dhyan Chand jaise legends isi game se hain. Hockey India ab national governing body hai. 🏑🥇
*   **Football Growth:** Football bhi India mein popular hai, khaas kar West Bengal, Kerala, Goa, aur North-East mein. All India Football Federation (AIFF) isko govern karti hai. India ne 1951 aur 1962 Asian Games mein gold medal jeeta tha. Indian Super League (ISL) ne game ko boost diya hai. ⚽🏆
*   **Basketball & Volleyball:** Yeh games bhi schools aur colleges mein popular ho rahe hain. Basketball Federation of India (BFI) aur Volleyball Federation of India (VFI) inke development ke liye kaam kar rahi hain. YMCA ne Basketball ko India mein introduce karne mein important role nibhaya. 🏀🏐
*   **National Data:** Sports Authority of India (SAI) aur Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports desh mein sports ko promote karne ke liye various schemes chalate hain. Khelo India Games jaise initiatives grassroots level par talent ko identify karne mein madad karte hain. 📊🇮🇳 Team games national integration aur pride ka bhi source hain.
