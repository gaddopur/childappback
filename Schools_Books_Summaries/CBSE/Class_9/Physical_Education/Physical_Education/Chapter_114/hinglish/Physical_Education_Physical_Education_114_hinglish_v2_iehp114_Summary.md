# [Class 9] Physical Education - Chapter 14: Adolescent Friendly Health Services (AFHS)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
**Adolescent Health Needs aur AFHS ka Framework 📊**

1.  **Adolescence (Kishoravastha):**
    *   Age Group: 10-19 years.
    *   India ki Population ka Bada Hissa: ~243 million (Census 2011 ke anusaar 1.21 billion total population ka 1/5th hissa). Desh ka future resource.
    *   Unique Health Needs: Is stage par physical, mental, aur social changes hote hain jinke liye special attention chahiye.

2.  **Major Health Needs of Adolescents (Kishoron ki Mukhya Swasthya Zarooratein):**
    *   **Nutritional Needs (Poshan Sambandhi Zarooratein):**
        *   Undernutrition & Anaemia (Khoon ki kami): Especially girls mein common (NFHS-3: 15-24 age group mein 56% females anaemic). Affects growth & performance.
        *   Healthy Eating Habits & Exercise: Future health ki neev (foundation).
    *   **Reproductive and Sexual Health Needs (Prajnan aur Yaun Swasthya):**
        *   Information Gap: Taboo ke karan sahi jaankari nahi milti.
        *   Risks: HIV/STIs (NACO: ~50% new HIV cases 15-24 yrs mein), early marriage & pregnancy (NFHS-3: 12% married girls 15-19 yrs already mothers).
        *   Safety Concerns: Sexual harassment, bullying, ragging.
    *   **Mental Health Concerns (Mansik Swasthya Chintayein):**
        *   Common Issues: Depression, mood disturbances, stress (~20% youth affected).
        *   Serious Risks: Suicide (2nd leading cause of death in 15-19 yrs), violence.
    *   **Substance Abuse (Nashe ki Lat):**
        *   Prevalence: Tobacco (NFHS-3: 28.6% males 15-19 yrs), alcohol (NFHS-3: 11% males 15-19 yrs), drugs (UNODC: ~3 million drug abusers in India, mostly 16-35 yrs). Early initiation common.
    *   **Accidental Injuries (Aakasmik Chot):**
        *   Leading Causes: Road traffic accidents, drowning, burns. High rates in adolescents.

3.  **Adolescent Friendly Health Services (AFHS):**
    *   Definition: Specialised health services (preventive, promotive, curative) designed for adolescents.
    *   Goal: To provide care in a friendly, non-threatening, confidential environment.

4.  **Need for AFHS (AFHS ki Zaroorat):**
    *   Hesitation: Adolescents feel shy/embarrassed discussing personal issues.
    *   Lack of Understanding: Apni needs ko lekar confusion.
    *   Peer Influence: Often rely on peers instead of authentic sources.

5.  **Barriers to Using Health Services (Swasthya Sevaon ke Upyog mein Badhayein):**
    *   Myths & Misconceptions.
    *   Lack of Awareness (Location/Services).
    *   Timing Conflicts (School/College).
    *   Privacy & Confidentiality Concerns.
    *   Long Waiting Times.
    *   Cost (Private clinics).
    *   Transport Issues (Rural areas).
    *   Staff Attitude (Judgmental).
    *   Requirement of Parental Consent.

6.  **Characteristics & Services of AFHS (AFHS ki Visheshtayein aur Sevaayein):**
    *   Staff Role: Respectful, non-judgmental, patient, ensures privacy & confidentiality.
    *   Services: Growth monitoring, nutrition counselling, immunization, injury prevention, substance abuse counselling, sexual/reproductive health guidance, life skills, disease prevention (STIs, AIDS, lifestyle diseases).
    *   Location Models: Fixed sites (Hospitals, PHCs under NRHM), Outreach (School clinics, mobile vans, NYK/NSS links), Helplines, Websites.

7.  **Reaching High-Risk Adolescents:** Focus on out-of-school youth (street children, working adolescents, etc.).

## 📘 Key Learnings

**1. Adolescents: Ek Important Population Group**
India mein kishor (10-19 saal ke log) population ka ek bahut bada hissa (~243 million) hain. Yeh hamare desh ka bhavishya (future) hain. Isliye unki health needs ko samajhna aur poora karna zaroori hai. Lekin aksar existing health services unki specific zarooraton ko poora nahi kar paati hain.

**2. Kishoron ki Alag Health Needs 📈**
Adolescence mein tezi se physical aur emotional changes hote hain, jisse kuch khaas health needs paida hoti hain:
*   **Poshan (Nutrition):** Bahut se kishor, khaaskar ladkiyan, anaemia (khoon ki kami) se pareshan hain. NFHS-3 ke data ke anusaar, 15-24 saal ki 56% ladkiyan anaemic hain. Sahi poshan na milne se growth ruk sakti hai aur padhai mein bhi dikkat aa sakti hai.
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek simple pie chart jo dikhaye ki kitne percent young females anaemic hain.
*   **Yaun aur Prajnan Swasthya (Sexual & Reproductive Health):** Is vishay par baat karna समाज (society) mein aasan nahi hai. Isliye kishoron ko sahi jaankari nahi mil paati aur woh galat sources ya myths par depend karte hain. Isse HIV/AIDS aur dusre Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) ka khatra badh jaata hai. NACO ke anusaar, lagbhag 50% naye HIV infections 15-24 saal ke logon mein ho rahe hain. Kam umar mein shaadi (early marriage) aur pregnancy bhi ek badi samasya hai, jisse maa aur bachche dono ki health par bura asar padta hai.
*   **Mansik Swasthya (Mental Health):** Padhai ka pressure, relationships, body changes ke karan stress, depression, aur mood swings ho sakte hain. Lagbhag 20% yuva kisi na kisi mental health problem se guzarte hain. Suicide is age group mein maut ka doosra bada karan hai.
*   **Nashe ki Lat (Substance Abuse):** Dosto ke pressure ya curiosity mein aakar kuch kishor tobacco, alcohol ya drugs try kar sakte hain. NFHS-3 data batata hai ki 15-19 saal ke 28.6% ladke tobacco aur 11% ladke alcohol use karte hain. Yeh aadat health ke liye bahut harmful hai.
*   **Durghatnayein (Accidents):** Road accidents, khelte waqt chot lagna, jalna ya doobna is age group mein common hai aur disability ya death ka karan ban sakta hai.

**3. AFHS Kya Hain?**
Adolescent Friendly Health Services (AFHS) aisi special health facilities hain jo kishoron ki zarooraton ko dhyaan mein rakhkar banayi gayi hain. Yahan unhe health advice, treatment, aur counselling ek friendly mahol mein milti hai. Yeh services aksar sarkari (government) hoti hain aur free ya bahut kam charge par available hoti hain.

**4. AFHS Kyun Zaroori Hain?**
Kishor apni personal problems, khaaskar sexual health se judi baatein, parents ya badon se share karne mein hichkichate (hesitate) hain. AFHS unhe ek aisi jagah deta hai jahan woh bina dare ya sharmaye apni baat keh sakte hain aur sahi scientific jaankari paa sakte hain.

**5. Health Services Use Karne Mein Rukavatein (Barriers) 🚧**
Kayi reasons se kishor health services tak nahi pahunch paate:
*   Privacy na milne ka darr.
*   Confidentiality (baat gupt rakhne) par doubt.
*   Clinic ke timings school/college se clash karna.
*   Fees zyada hona (private clinics mein).
*   Staff ka behaviour judgmental ya unfriendly hona.
*   Jaankari ki kami (kahan jayein, kya services milengi).
*   Transport ki suvidha na hona, especially rural areas mein.
*   Parental consent ki zaroorat.

**6. Ek Ideal AFHS Kaisa Hona Chahiye?**
*   **Staff:** Trained, respectful, non-judgmental, patient ho. Privacy aur confidentiality maintain kare.
*   **Environment:** Welcoming aur comfortable ho.
*   **Timings:** Convenient ho.
*   **Location:** Accessible ho (Fixed clinics jaise PHC/Hospital mein ya outreach models jaise school clinics, mobile vans).
*   **Services:** Comprehensive ho - nutrition, mental health, reproductive health, substance abuse, immunisation sab cover kare.

**7. AFHS Mein Milne Wali Services:**
AFHS mein kishoron ko kai tarah ki madad milti hai:
*   Growth aur development check karna.
*   Healthy diet par salah.
*   Missed vaccinations lagwana.
*   Chot se bachne ke tareeke batana.
*   Nashe (substance abuse) ke nuksan batana aur bachne mein madad karna.
*   Sexual aur reproductive health par counselling aur sahi jaankari dena.
*   Life skills sikhana.
*   Common bimariyon (STIs, AIDS, diabetes, heart disease) se bachav ki jaankari dena.

**8. Sabhi Kishoron Tak Pahunchna:**
Yeh zaroori hai ki AFHS ki jaankari sirf school jaane wale bachchon tak hi nahi, balki un kishoron tak bhi pahunche jo school nahi jaate (jaise street children, working adolescents, domestic help). Unhe in services ke baare mein batana aur unhe access karne mein help karna hum sabki zimmedari hai.

## 🧩 Active Learning

*   **Activity 1 (Research & Analysis) 🔍:** Apne classmates se baat karke pata lagao ki woh AFHS ke baare mein kitna jaante hain. Kya woh kabhi gaye hain? Unka experience kaisa raha? Agar nahi gaye toh kyun? Ek choti report/case study banao.
*   **Activity 2 (Field Visit & Discussion) 🌍:** Nazdeeki AFHS center visit karo (teacher ke saath). Wahan ke doctor/nurse se kishoron ke common problems aur myths par discuss karo. Pata lagao ki privacy kaise maintain ki jaati hai. Wahan aaye kuch youth se baat karke services ki usefulness jaano.
*   **Activity 3 (Resource Mapping):** Apne area ke AFHS centers ki list banao (address, contact number). Is list ko school notice board par lagao taaki sabko fayda ho.
*   **Activity 4 (Problem Solving/Counselling):** Agar tumhara koi dost depression mein hai aur AFHS jaane se mana kar raha hai, toh use kaise convince karoge? Teen convincing points likho.
*   **Discussion:** Real world mein AFHS kitne effective hain? Unke saamne kya challenges hain? Is par class mein critically analyze karo.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context

*   **Demographic Significance:** Bharat mein duniya ki sabse badi adolescent population mein se ek hai (~243 million), jo desh ke development ke liye crucial hai. 🇮🇳
*   **National Health Data 📊:**
    *   **NFHS-3 (National Family Health Survey):** Yeh survey important data deta hai jaise:
        *   Anaemia: 15-24 yrs ki 56% females aur 25% males anaemic hain.
        *   Early Motherhood: 15-19 yrs ki 12% married females maa ban chuki hain.
        *   Substance Use (15-19 yrs): Tobacco - 3.5% females, 28.6% males; Alcohol - 1% females, 11% males.
    *   **NACO (National AIDS Control Organisation):** Lagbhag 50% naye HIV infections 15-24 saal ke age group mein ho rahe hain.
    *   **UNODC Data:** India mein lagbhag 3 million drug abusers hain, mostly 16-35 saal ke. Cannabis use bhi common hai.
*   **Government Initiatives:**
    *   **NRHM (National Rural Health Mission) / NHM (National Health Mission):** Iske तहत Primary Health Centres (PHCs) aur dusre health centers par AFHS ko promote kiya ja raha hai.
    *   **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW):** AFHS staff ki training aur guidelines develop karne mein active hai.
    *   **Partnerships:** Services ko youth tak pahunchane ke liye NACO, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports (Nehru Yuva Kendra - NYK), National Service Scheme (NSS) jaise organisations ke saath milkar kaam kiya ja raha hai.
*   **Social Context:** Yaun swasthya (sexual health) jaise mudde abhi bhi 'taboo' hain, jisse sahi jaankari milne mein mushkil hoti hai aur AFHS ki zaroorat badh jaati hai. Early marriage bhi kai jagahon par ek social issue hai.
