## 📘 Key Learnings

Har individual sport ki apni unique history, rules, aur skills hote hain. Inhe samajhna khel ko correctly khelne aur appreciate karne ke liye zaroori hai.

**1. Badminton:**
*   **History:** India mein Pune mein iske pehle rules bane (1901). IBF (ab WBF) 1934 mein bani. Badminton Association of India (BAI) bhi 1934 mein establish hui.
*   **Court & Equipment:** Doubles court: 13.40m x 6.10m. Net height: 1.55m (sides), 1.52m (centre). Racket aur shuttlecock chahiye.
    ```
    <------------------ 13.40 m ------------------>
    ^                                               ^
    |                                               | 6.10 m
    | <---- Net (1.55m / 1.52m high) ---->          |
    v                                               v
    ```
*   **Gameplay:** Toss se start hota hai. Service diagonally opposite court mein, waist ke neeche se karni hoti hai. Rally winner ko 1 point milta hai. Game 21 points ka hota hai (2 points lead zaroori). 'Let' ka matlab rally stop without point. Yellow, Red, Black cards misconduct ke liye use hote hain.
*   **Skills:**
    *   Grip: Forehand (shake hand), Backhand.
    *   Footwork: Court mein efficiently move karna.
    *   Strokes: Forehand, Backhand, Smash (forceful downward shot), Drop (gentle shot near net), Drives (flat shots), Toss/Clear (high deep shot).
    *   Service: Long/High, Short/Low.

**2. Gymnastics:**
*   **History:** Greek word 'Gymnos' (naked art) se aaya. Friedrich Ludwig Jahn ko 'Father of Gymnastics' maana jaata hai. FIG 1881 mein bani. Olympics mein 1896 (Men) aur 1928 (Women) se shamil hai.
*   **Apparatus:**
    *   Men: Floor Exercise, Pommel Horse, Roman Rings, Vaulting Table, Parallel Bars, Horizontal Bar.
    *   Women: Vaulting Table, Uneven Bars, Balancing Beam, Floor Exercises.
*   **Basic Skills (Examples):**
    *   Floor: Handstand, Cartwheel, Front Handspring, Round-off. (Mat: 12m x 12m)
    *   Pommel Horse: Leg Swings, One Leg Circle.
    *   Roman Rings: Inlocation, Dislocation.
    *   Vaulting Table: Squat Vault, Split Vault.
    *   Parallel Bars: Upper arm support swing, L-hold.
    *   Horizontal Bar: Front/Back hip circle.
    *   Balancing Beam: 'T' Balance, Walking.
    *   Uneven Bars: Front/Back hip circle.
*   **Evaluation:** Har element ke points hote hain (e.g., 5 points per element). Total score apparatus ke basis par calculate hota hai.

**3. Judo:**
*   **History:** Japanese art 'Ju-jitsu' se originate hua. Prof. Jigoro Kano ne 1882 mein pehla school shuru kiya. Olympics mein 1964 (Tokyo) se shamil.
*   **Grading:** Kyu (pupil) aur Dan (master) grades hote hain, jo belt ke colour se pata chalte hain (White -> Yellow -> Orange -> Green -> Blue -> Brown -> Black -> Red/White -> Red).
*   **Area & Categories:** Platform: 18m x 18m. Contest Area: 10m x 10m (incl. 1m danger zone). Safety Area: 3m. India mein Sub-junior, Junior, Senior (Men/Women) ke liye alag weight categories hain (ye change hoti rehti hain).
*   **Scoring:**
    *   Ippon (Full point): Perfect throw ya 20 sec hold.
    *   Waza-ari (Half point): Almost perfect throw ya 15-19 sec hold. (Do Waza-ari = Ippon).
    *   Yuko: Throw mein 2 components missing ya 10-14 sec hold.
*   **Skills:** Rei (Salutation), Kumikata (Grip), Shintai (Walking), Shizentai (Posture), Ukemi (Breakfall), Tai-sabaki (Turning), Kuzushi (Off-balancing), Tsukuri (Fitting), Kake (Execution).

**4. Swimming:**
*   **Basics:** Body ko paani mein propel karna using arms (strokes) and legs (kick). Non-weight bearing exercise, good for rehab.
*   **History:** Olympics mein 1896 se shamil. FINA 1908 mein bani.
*   **Pool & Equipment:** Standard Pool: 50m x 25m, Depth 1.80m. 8 lanes (2.5m wide). Starting platform: 50-75cm high. Water temp: 24°C. Costume, cap, goggles chahiye.
*   **Strokes:**
    *   Freestyle (Crawl): Alternate over-arm strokes, flutter kick. Breathing side mein.
    *   Backstroke: Supine position, alternate arm action, flutter kick. Face upar rehta hai.
    *   Breaststroke: Simultaneous 'heart-shaped' arm pull, frog-like kick. Touch wall with both hands.
    *   Butterfly: Simultaneous over-arm recovery, dolphin kick. Touch wall with both hands.
*   **Care:** Nahana (before/after), warm-up, stretching, lifeguard ki presence mein swim karna.

**5. Table Tennis (TT):**
*   **History:** ITTF 1926 mein bani. Olympic sport since 1988. Pehle 'Ping-Pong' bhi kehte the.
*   **Table & Equipment:** Table: 274cm x 152.5cm, Height 76cm. Dark matt surface, white lines. Net: 15.25cm high. Ball: 40mm diameter, 2.7g weight, orange/white. Bat/Racket: Wooden blade, rubber surfaces (red/black).
    ```
      <---------- 274 cm ---------->
      ^                             ^
      |                             | 152.5 cm
      | <--- Net (15.25cm high) --->|
      v                             v
      (Table Height: 76 cm)
    ```
*   **Gameplay:** Toss winner chooses serve/receive/side. Service: Ball ko palm se toss karke hit karna, pehle server ke half mein bounce, phir net ke upar se opponent ke half mein. Return: Ball ko opponent ke half mein hit karna. Doubles mein alternate players hit karte hain. Let: Service net ko touch karke sahi court mein jaaye ya receiver ready na ho. Game 11 points ka (2 points lead zaroori).
*   **Skills:**
    *   Grip: Shake hand, Pen hold.
    *   Spin: Top spin (bat angled down), Back spin (bat angled up), Side spin (often in service).
    *   Strokes: Offensive/Defensive pushes (Forehand/Backhand).

**6. Wrestling:**
*   **Styles:** Freestyle (legs use kar sakte hain), Greco-Roman (legs ka use mana hai). Indian Style: Pehlwani/Kushti (mitti ke akhade mein).
*   **History:** Freestyle Olympics mein 1904 se, Women's Freestyle 2004 se.
*   **Mat & Area:** Indoor sport. Mat: 12m x 12m, Thickness 5-7cm. Bout yellow area (7m diameter) mein hota hai. Centre circle 1m diameter.
*   **Gameplay:** Weight categories hote hain. Red/Blue uniform. 3 rounds (2 min each). Fall se jeet sakte hain. Points: Takedown (1), Exposure (2), Push-out (1), Throw (max 5). Par terre: Mat par wrestling. Tie-breaking rules hote hain. Clinch rule: Scoreless period mein action force karne ke liye.
*   **Skills:** Stance (Square/Staggered), Motion (Circling, Attack, Defence), Level Change (Lowering hips), Penetration (Deep step for takedown), Lifting (Using legs/hips), Back step, Back arch.
