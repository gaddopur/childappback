## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Seekh) 📈

Har individual sport mein maharat haasil karne ke liye uske specific rules aur techniques samajhna zaroori hai.

**1. Badminton:**
*   **Court:** Doubles court 13.40m x 6.10m. Net height center mein 1.52m, sides pe 1.55m. (Diagram Fig 6.1 dekhein).
*   **Scoring:** Rally point system. Har rally jeetne par 1 point. Game 21 points ka hota hai, 2 points ki lead zaroori hai (e.g., 21-19, ya 22-20).
*   **Service:** Diagonally opposite court mein serve karna hota hai. Shuttle ko waist se neeche hit karna hai. Racket ka shaft neeche ki taraf point karna chahiye. Dono pair zameen par hone chahiye jab tak service deliver na ho.
*   **Faults:** Service galat ho, shuttle court ke bahar gire, net ke neeche se jaye, ya player net ko touch kare.
*   **Skills:**
    *   *Grip:* Shakehand grip (Fig 6.3) aur Backhand grip.
    *   *Strokes:* Smash (forceful downward shot), Drop (gentle shot near net), Clear (high shot to baseline).

**2. Gymnastics:**
*   **Apparatus:** Men aur Women ke liye alag-alag apparatus hote hain. Jaise Men ke liye Pommel Horse, Rings; Women ke liye Balancing Beam, Uneven Bars.
*   **Floor Exercise:** 12m x 12m mat par perform kiya jata hai (Fig 6.5). Skills jaise Handstand (Fig 6.5.1), Cartwheel (Fig 6.6), Round-off (Fig 6.7.1) shamil hain.
*   **Evaluation:** Har skill/element ke points hote hain (e.g., 5 points per element). Total score sabhi apparatus ke performance par depend karta hai.

**3. Judo:**
*   **Area:** Contest area 10m x 10m (including danger zone). Safety area 3m wide. (Diagram Fig 6.15 jaisa layout).
*   **Scoring:**
    *   *Ippon (Full Point):* Opponent ko force, speed se control ke saath back par girana ya 20 sec hold karna. Match khatam.
    *   *Waza-ari (Half Point):* Throw mein koi ek component (force, speed, control, back landing) thoda kam ho ya 15-19 sec hold. Do Waza-ari milakar Ippon banta hai.
    *   *Yuko:* Throw mein do components kam ho ya 10-14 sec hold.
*   **Skills:** *Ukemi* (Breakfall/Girne ka tarika) bahut important hai chot se bachne ke liye. *Kuzushi* (balance bigadna) attack ke liye zaroori hai.

**4. Swimming:**
*   **Pool:** Standard pool 50m lamba, 25m chauda, 1.8m gehra. 8 lanes (2.5m wide). Water temp 24°C.
*   **Strokes:**
    *   *Freestyle (Crawl):* Alternate arm action over water, flutter kick. Breathing side mein. (Fig 6.16)
    *   *Backstroke:* Peeth ke bal, alternate arm action, flutter kick. Face upar rehta hai. (Fig 6.17)
    *   *Breaststroke:* Simultaneous arm movement (heart shape), frog kick. Dono haath se wall touch karna zaroori hai turn/finish par. (Fig 6.18)
    *   *Butterfly:* Simultaneous arm movement over water, dolphin kick (legs together). Dono haath se wall touch karna zaroori hai. (Fig 6.19)

**5. Table Tennis:**
*   **Table:** 274cm x 152.5cm. Height 76cm. (Fig 6.21)
*   **Equipment:** Net (15.25cm high), Ball (40mm diameter, 2.7g), Bat/Racket (Fig 6.20).
*   **Service:** Ball ko haath se uchhal kar (toss) hit karna hai. Ball pehle server ke side table par touch kare, phir net ke upar se opponent ke side.
*   **Scoring:** 11 points ka game. 10-10 score par 2 points ki lead chahiye jeetne ke liye.
*   **Skills:** *Grips* (Shakehand - Fig 6.22, Penhold - Fig 6.23), *Spin* (Topspin, Backspin), *Strokes*.

**6. Wrestling:**
*   **Mat:** 12m x 12m area. Bout 7m diameter yellow circle mein hota hai. Center circle 1m diameter. (Fig 6.24)
*   **Gameplay:** 3 rounds, 2 minutes each. Fall se bhi jeet sakte hain. Points takedown, exposure (opponent ki peeth mat par dikhana), push-out, par terre (ground wrestling) se milte hain.
*   **Skills:** *Stance* (Square - Fig 6.25, Staggered - Fig 6.26), *Level Change* (hips neeche karna), *Penetration* (takedown ke liye deep step), *Lifting*.
*   **Indian Context:** *Pehlwani/Kushti* mitti ke Akhade mein kheli jaati hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Sandarbh)

*   **Historical Roots:** Kai khelon ki jadein Bharat se judi hain. Badminton ke pehle niyam **Pune** mein bane the. Wrestling ka traditional form **Pehlwani/Kushti** aaj bhi **Akhado** mein practice kiya jaata hai, jo ek unique cultural aspect hai.
*   **Popularity & Participation:** Badminton, Table Tennis, aur Wrestling Bharat mein kaafi popular hain, school level se lekar professional level tak. Swimming aur Gymnastics mein bhi participation badh raha hai. Judo bhi self-defense aur sport ke roop mein schools mein sikhaya ja raha hai.
*   **National Pride:** Indian athletes ne in sabhi individual sports mein international level par desh ka naam roshan kiya hai (e.g., PV Sindhu, Saina Nehwal in Badminton; Dipa Karmakar in Gymnastics; Sushil Kumar, Sakshi Malik in Wrestling). Unki success ne yuvaon ko inspire kiya hai.
*   **Health & Fitness:** Bharat mein non-communicable diseases (jaise diabetes, hypertension) badh rahe hain. Regular sports activity, jaise ki is chapter mein discuss kiye gaye khel, physical fitness maintain karne aur in bimariyon se bachne mein madad karte hain (Box 6.1). Government initiatives jaise **Khelo India** bhi sports participation ko badhawa de rahe hain taaki ek swasth (healthy) nation banaya ja sake.
*   **Social Impact:** Sports discipline, teamwork (doubles events mein), respect for opponents (jaise Judo mein Rei), aur resilience jaise values sikhaate hain, jo ek strong character build karne mein madad karte hain. Judo jaisi activities self-confidence aur self-defense skills bhi deti hain, especially ladkiyon ke liye.
