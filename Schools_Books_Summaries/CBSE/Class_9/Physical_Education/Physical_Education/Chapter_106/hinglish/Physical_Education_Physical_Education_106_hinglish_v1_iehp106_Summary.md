# [Class 9] Physical Education - Chapter 106 (Individual Sports)

## 🌟 Core Concepts
**Vyaktigat Khelon ka Vistarit Overview 📊**

Yeh chapter alag-alag individual sports (vyaktigat khel) par focus karta hai. Har khel ka apna itihas (history), niyam (rules), zaroori upkaran (equipment), aur buniyadi kaushal (fundamental skills) hote hain.

1.  **Introduction (परिचय)**
    *   Games aur sports human life ka hissa hain, survival ya pleasure ke liye.
    *   Dheere-dheere community events bane.
    *   Specific skills ki zaroorat mahsus hui.
    *   Health benefits: Dil मजबूत, hypertension kam, weight control, haddiyon ki sehat. (Box 6.1)

2.  **Badminton (बैडमिंटन)**
    *   Origin: Pune, India (1901 mein pehle rules). International Badminton Federation (IBF) 1934 mein bani. Ab World Badminton Federation (WBF) regulate karta hai. Badminton Association of India (BAI) 1934 mein bani.
    *   Events: Singles (Boys/Girls), Doubles (Boys/Girls), Mixed Doubles.
    *   Upkaran (Equipment): Racket, Net, Shuttlecock.
    *   Court: Doubles - 13.40m x 6.10m. Net height - 1.55m (sides), 1.52m (centre).
    *   Khelna (How to Play): Toss, Service rules, Scoring (21 points, 2-point lead), Let situations, Faults, Cards (Yellow, Red, Black).
    *   Skills (कौशल): Grips (Forehand, Backhand), Footwork, Strokes (Forehand, Backhand, Smash, Drop, Drive, Toss/Clear), Service (Long/High, Short/Low).

3.  **Gymnastics (जिमनास्टिक्स)**
    *   Origin: Greek word 'Gymnos' (naked art). Father of Gymnastics - Friedrich Ludwig Jahn. Federation International de Gymnastique (FIG) 1881 mein bani. Olympics mein 1896 (Men), 1928 (Women) mein shamil hua.
    *   Types: Basic, Sportive (Artistic, Rhythmic, Aerobics, Acrobatics, Trampoline), Auxiliary. Chapter focuses on Artistic Gymnastics.
    *   Apparatus:
        *   Men: Floor Exercise, Pommel Horse, Roman Rings, Vaulting Table, Parallel Bars, Horizontal Bar.
        *   Women: Vaulting Table, Uneven Bars, Balancing Beam, Floor Exercise.
    *   Basic Skills (कौशल):
        *   Floor: Handstand, Cartwheel, Front Handspring, Round-off.
        *   Pommel Horse: Leg Swings, One Leg Circle.
        *   Roman Rings: Inlocation, Dislocation.
        *   Vaulting Table: Squat Vault, Split Vault.
        *   Parallel Bars: Upper arm support swing, L-hold position.
        *   Horizontal Bar: Front hip circle, Back hip circle.
        *   Balancing Beam: Balance on one leg (T-Balance), Walking.
        *   Uneven Bars: Front hip circle, Back hip circle.
    *   Evaluation: Har element ke 5 points. Total points vary for boys (70) and girls (50).

4.  **Judo (जूडो)**
    *   Origin: Japanese art 'Ju-jitsu'. Started by Professor Jigoro Kano (1882). Olympics mein 1964 (Tokyo) mein shamil hua.
    *   Competition Area: 14m x 14m contest area, 1m danger zone, 3m safety area.
    *   Duration: Boys 5 min, Girls 4 min. Weight categories hote hain (India mein Sub-junior, Junior, Senior levels).
    *   Grades (Belts): Kyu (pupil - White to Brown), Dan (master - Black, Red/White, Red).
    *   Scoring: Ippon (perfect throw/hold 20s), Waza-ari (near Ippon/hold 15-19s), Yuko (less perfect throw/hold 10-14s). Two Waza-ari = Ippon.
    *   End of Contest: Ippon, Waza-Ari Awasete Ippon, Sogo-Gachi, Fusen-Gachi, Hansoku-Make, Injury, Time expiry.
    *   Fundamental Skills (कौशल): Rei (Salutation - Ritsu-Rei, Za-Rei), Kumikata (Grip), Shintai (Walking), Shizentai (Posture), Ukemi (Breakfall - Ushiro, Yoko, Mae, Mae Mawari), Tai-sabaki (Turning), Kuzushi (Off-balancing), Tsukuri (Fitting), Kake (Execution).

5.  **Swimming (तैराकी)**
    *   Introduction: Body ko paani mein propel karna using arms (strokes) and legs (kick). Rehabilitation aur recreation ke liye accha hai. Olympics mein 1896 (Athens) se shamil. FINA (Federation Internationale de Natations Aquatics) 1908 mein bani.
    *   Facilities/Equipment: Pool (50m x 25m, 1.8m deep), 8 lanes (2.5m wide), Starting platform (50-75cm high), Water temp (24°C), Costume (Nylon/Lycra), Cap, Goggles.
    *   Strokes (प्रकार): Front Stroke/Freestyle (Crawl), Back Stroke, Breast Stroke, Butterfly Stroke. Har stroke ki specific technique (arm pull, leg kick, breathing, turns).
    *   Care: Nahana (before/after), Towel use, Lifeguard presence, Warm-up/Stretching.

6.  **Table Tennis (टेबल टेनिस)**
    *   History: Also known as Ping-Pong. International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF) 1926 mein bani. Olympic sport since 1988.
    *   Facilities/Equipment: Table (274cm x 152.5cm, 76cm high), Net Assembly (15.25cm high), Ball (40mm diameter, 2.7g, Orange/White), Bat/Racket (Wooden blade, Red/Black rubber), Scoreboard, Net Gauge.
    *   Events: Singles, Doubles, Mixed Doubles.
    *   How to Play: Toss, Service rules (toss, bounce on own side first), Return rules, Let situations, Scoring (11 points, 2-point lead). Doubles mein alternate hits.
    *   Basic Skills (कौशल): Grip (Shake hand, Pen hold), Spin (Topspin, Backspin, Sidespin), Strokes (Offensive/Defensive - Backhand push, Forehand push).

7.  **Wrestling (कुश्ती)**
    *   Styles: Olympic styles - Freestyle (arms & legs use allowed), Greco-Roman (only upper body attacks/defense, no leg use). Freestyle Olympics mein 1904 (Men), 2004 (Women) mein shamil hua. Indian Style - Pehlwani/Kushti (clay/mud pit).
    *   Equipment/Facilities: Indoor mat (12m x 12m square, 5-7cm thick), Playing area (Yellow circle, 7m diameter), Centre circle (1m diameter, Red/White).
    *   How to Wrestle: Weight categories (minimize injury, ensure fairness). Uniform (Red/Blue). Bout: 3 rounds of 2 mins each. Scoring (Takedown - 1 pt, Takedown with back exposure - 2+ pts, Throw - max 5 pts, Push out/Escape - 1 pt). Par terre (mat wrestling - turning opponent - 2 pts, hold 5s+ - extra 1 pt). Tie-breaking rules. Clinch rule (ordered position).
    *   Basic Skills (कौशल): Stance (Square, Staggered), Motion (Circling, Attack, Snap, Sprawl), Level Change (Lowering hips), Penetration (Shooting for takedown), Lifting, Back Step, Back Arch.

**Mahatvapurna Concepts aur Techniques 📈**

*   **Badminton Scoring & Service:** Game 21 points ka hota hai, jeetne ke liye 2 points ki lead chahiye. Service diagonally opposite court mein, kamar ke neeche se, bina pair uthaye karni hoti hai. *Diagram Fig 6.1 (Court) aur Fig 6.4 (Service) important hain.*
*   **Gymnastics Apparatus & Basic Moves:** Men aur Women ke liye alag apparatus hote hain. Floor exercises mein Handstand (Fig 6.5.1) aur Cartwheel (Fig 6.6) buniyadi skills hain. Vaulting mein Squat Vault (Fig 6.10) aur Split Vault (Fig 6.10.1) alag techniques hain. *Diagrams skills ko samajhne mein madad karte hain.*
*   **Judo Scoring System:** Ippon (एक अंक) se turant jeet milti hai (perfect throw ya 20 sec hold). Waza-ari (आधा अंक) do baar milne par Ippon ke barabar hota hai. Yuko (छोटा अंक) kam points deta hai. *Yeh scoring samajhna match follow karne ke liye zaroori hai.*
*   **Swimming Strokes Technique:**
    *   *Freestyle (Fig 6.16):* Alternate arm pull over water, flutter kick, side breathing. Sabse tez stroke.
    *   *Backstroke (Fig 6.17):* Peeth ke bal, alternate arm pull, flutter kick. Chehra paani se bahar rehta hai.
    *   *Breaststroke (Fig 6.18):* Dono arms ek saath heart shape mein move karte hain, frog kick. Dono haath se wall touch karna zaroori hai turn/finish par.
    *   *Butterfly (Fig 6.19):* Dono arms ek saath move karte hain, dolphin kick (legs together). Dono haath se wall touch karna zaroori hai.
*   **Table Tennis Grip & Spin:** Shake hand grip (Fig 6.22) zyada common hai. Pen hold grip (Fig 6.23) bhi use hota hai. Spin (Topspin, Backspin, Sidespin) ball ke trajectory ko control karne mein madad karta hai.
*   **Wrestling Styles & Basic Skills:** Freestyle mein legs use kar sakte hain, Greco-Roman mein nahi. Stance (Fig 6.25, 6.26) balance aur attack/defense ke liye important hai. Level Change (Fig 6.28) takedown ke liye zaroori hai. *Indian style Kushti ek alag parampara hai.*

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context
**Rashtriya Khel Paridrishya aur Data 📊**

*   **Historical Roots (Itihaasik Jaden):** Badminton ke modern rules ka vikas Pune, Maharashtra mein hua, jo is khel se Bharat ka gehra sambandh darshata hai.
*   **Traditional Sports (Paramparik Khel):** Wrestling mein, Bharat ki apni paramparik shaili 'Kushti' ya 'Pehlwani' hai, jo aaj bhi 'Akhadas' mein practice ki jaati hai. Yeh sirf ek khel nahi, balki ek jeevan shaili (lifestyle) hai. Yeh Bharat ki rich sports heritage ka hissa hai.
*   **Governing Bodies (Niyantrak Nikay):** Har khel ke liye national federations hain, jaise Badminton Association of India (BAI) (1934 se active), jo state associations se judi hain aur desh mein khel ko regulate aur promote karti hain. Judo Federation of India (JFI) bhi desh mein Judo ko govern karti hai (website reference text mein hai).
*   **National Pride & Social Impact:** Indian athletes ka international level par in individual sports mein safal hona (jaise PV Sindhu, Saina Nehwal - Badminton; Dipa Karmakar - Gymnastics; Sushil Kumar, Sakshi Malik, Bajrang Punia - Wrestling; Sharath Kamal - Table Tennis) desh ke liye garv ka vishay hai aur yuvaon ko in khelon mein bhaag lene ke liye prerit karta hai.
*   **Accessibility & Development:** Sarkaar aur sports bodies infrastructure (jaise SAI centres) aur coaching facilities provide karke in khelon ko grassroots level par promote karne ki koshish kar rahi hain, taaki har talent ko mauka mil sake. Khelo India jaisi schemes is disha mein ek kadam hain.
