## 📘 Key Learnings

This chapter introduces several individual sports, detailing their history, governing bodies, required facilities and equipment, basic rules of play, scoring systems, and fundamental skills.

**1. Badminton:** 🏸
*   **Origin & Governance:** Believed to have originated in Pune, India (1901 rules). Governed internationally by WBF and nationally by BAI (f. 1934).
*   **Playing Area:** A rectangular court (13.40m x 6.10m for doubles) divided by a net (1.55m high at sides, 1.52m at centre). 📈
*   **Gameplay:** Starts with a toss. Service must be diagonal, underhand, below the waist. Points scored on each rally (rally point system). A game is typically 21 points, requiring a 2-point lead. 'Let' is a replay without a point.
*   **Key Skills:** Proper grips (Forehand 'shake hand', Backhand), agile footwork, various strokes (Smash for attack, Drop for finesse, Clear for defense), and effective serves (High/Long, Low/Short).

**2. Gymnastics:** 🤸
*   **Origin & Governance:** Ancient Greek roots ('Gymnos'). Modern form shaped by pioneers like Jahn. Governed by FIG (f. 1881).
*   **Disciplines:** Focuses on Artistic Gymnastics. Men compete on 6 apparatus (Floor, Pommel Horse, Rings, Vault, Parallel Bars, Horizontal Bar), Women on 4 (Vault, Uneven Bars, Beam, Floor).
*   **Basic Skills:** Each apparatus requires specific skills like Handstands/Cartwheels (Floor), Swings/Circles (Pommel Horse, Bars, Rings), Vaulting techniques (Squat, Split), and Balance (Beam). Diagrams illustrate skills like Handstand (Fig 6.5.1), Cartwheel (Fig 6.6), Leg Swings (Fig 6.8.1).
*   **Evaluation:** Performances are scored based on the execution and difficulty of predefined elements.

**3. Judo:** 🥋
*   **Origin & Governance:** Developed from Japanese Ju-jitsu by Jigoro Kano (1882). Olympic sport since 1964.
*   **Playing Area:** A square platform with a 14m x 14m competition area, including a 1m danger zone and 3m safety area. 📈
*   **Progression & Scoring:** Skill level indicated by belt colour (Kyu/Dan grades). Points (Ippon, Waza-ari, Yuko) awarded for throws based on force, control, and landing, or for holding opponents for specific durations (20s for Ippon, 15-19s for Waza-ari, 10-14s for Yuko).
*   **Key Skills:** Respect (Rei), Gripping (Kumikata), Movement (Shintai), Posture (Shizentai), Falling Safely (Ukemi), Body Management (Tai-sabaki), and the sequence of a throw: Off-balancing (Kuzushi), Positioning (Tsukuri), Execution (Kake).

**4. Swimming:** 🏊‍♀️
*   **Benefits & Governance:** Excellent for fitness, recreation, and rehabilitation. Governed by FINA (f. 1908).
*   **Pool Standards:** Standard competition pool is 50m long, 25m wide, 1.8m deep, with 8-10 lanes (2.5m wide each). Water temperature ideally 24°C. 📈
*   **Competitive Strokes:**
    *   *Freestyle:* Fastest stroke, usually crawl (alternate arms, flutter kick).
    *   *Backstroke:* Performed on the back (alternate arms, flutter kick).
    *   *Breaststroke:* Simultaneous arm pull and 'frog' kick.
    *   *Butterfly:* Simultaneous arm recovery over water and 'dolphin' kick.
    Each stroke has specific rules for turns and finishes (e.g., two-hand touch for Breaststroke and Butterfly).
*   **Safety:** Emphasizes warming up, showering, and swimming under supervision.

**5. Table Tennis:** 🏓
*   **Origin & Governance:** Evolved from parlour games. Governed by ITTF (f. 1926). Olympic sport since 1988.
*   **Equipment:** Played on a rectangular table (274cm x 152.5cm, 76cm high) divided by a net (15.25cm high). Uses a small, light ball (40mm, 2.7g) and a racket/bat. 📈
*   **Gameplay:** Service involves tossing the ball from an open palm and striking it to bounce once on the server's side, then over the net to the receiver's side. Play continues with players returning the ball to bounce on the opponent's side. Games are typically 11 points (2-point lead needed if tied 10-10).
*   **Key Skills:** Grips (Shake hand, Pen hold), applying Spin (Topspin, Backspin, Sidespin), and executing various Strokes (Push, Drive, Block).

**6. Wrestling:** 🤼
*   **Styles & Governance:** Olympic styles are Freestyle (using arms and legs) and Greco-Roman (upper body only). Traditional Indian style is Kushti/Pehlwani.
*   **Playing Area:** Performed on a circular area (7m diameter) within a larger safety mat (12m x 12m). 📈
*   **Gameplay:** Bouts consist of rounds (e.g., 3 x 2 min). Points awarded for takedowns, exposing opponent's back (risk), forcing opponent out of bounds, and controlling from 'Par terre' (mat wrestling) position. Specific rules for tie-breaking and scoreless periods (Clinch rule).
*   **Key Skills:** Maintaining a stable Stance, effective Motion, changing Levels quickly, deep Penetration for takedowns, Lifting opponents, and defensive maneuvers like Back Step and Back Arch.

**General Health Benefits (Box 6.1):** Regular participation (30 mins/day) strengthens the heart, reduces hypertension, helps maintain optimal weight, prevents bone disorders, reduces stress, boosts self-esteem, and improves sleep.
