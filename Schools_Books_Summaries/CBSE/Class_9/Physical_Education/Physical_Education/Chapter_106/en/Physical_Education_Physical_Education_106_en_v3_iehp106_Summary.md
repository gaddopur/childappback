## 📘 Key Learnings

This chapter introduces several individual sports, detailing their history, rules, required facilities and equipment, fundamental skills, and scoring methods. Understanding these aspects is crucial for participation and appreciation of the sports.

**General Health Benefits (Box 6.1):**
Regular participation (at least 30 mins/day) in sports like these:
*   Strengthens heart muscles.
*   Reduces hypertension (high blood pressure).
*   Aids in maintaining optimal body weight relative to height.
*   Helps prevent bone-related disorders.
*   Promotes feelings of strength, motivation, stress reduction, high self-esteem, and better sleep.

**1. Badminton:**
*   **Origin:** Believed to have originated in India (Pune, 1901 rules). Became international after All England Championship. Governed by BWF (formerly IBF).
*   **Court & Equipment:** Doubles court: 13.40m x 6.10m. Net height: 1.55m (sides), 1.52m (centre). Requires racket, shuttlecock, net.
    *(Conceptual Diagram: Badminton court layout showing dimensions, service courts, net position)*
*   **Gameplay:** Singles (1v1), Doubles (2v2), Mixed Doubles. Starts with a toss. Serve diagonally below waist height. Rally scoring system; game is typically 21 points (win by 2). 'Let' is a replay without a point. Faults lead to loss of rally.
*   **Skills:**
    *   **Grips:** Forehand (shake hand), Backhand (thumb flat on back edge).
    *   **Footwork:** Essential for court coverage.
    *   **Strokes:** Forehand, Backhand, Smash (forceful downward shot), Drop (gentle shot near net), Drives (flat side shots), Toss/Clear (high, deep shot).
    *   **Service:** Long/High, Short/Low.

**2. Gymnastics:**
*   **Origin:** Ancient Greek 'Gymnos' (naked art). Modern pioneers: Guts Muths, Friedrich Ludwig Jahn. Governed by FIG (1881).
*   **Types:** Artistic (Men: Floor, Pommel Horse, Rings, Vault, Parallel Bars, Horizontal Bar; Women: Vault, Uneven Bars, Beam, Floor), Rhythmic, Aerobics, Acrobatics, Trampoline.
*   **Basic Artistic Skills (Examples):**
    *   **Floor (12m x 12m mat):** Handstand, Cartwheel, Front Handspring, Round-off.
        *(Conceptual Diagram: Sequence of a Cartwheel or Front Handspring)*
    *   **Pommel Horse:** Leg Swings, One Leg Circle.
    *   **Roman Rings:** Inlocation, Dislocation.
    *   **Vaulting Table:** Squat Vault, Split Vault.
    *   **Parallel Bars:** Upper Arm Support Swing, L-Hold.
    *   **Horizontal Bar:** Front Hip Circle, Back Hip Circle.
    *   **Balancing Beam (Women):** 'T' Balance, Walking variations.
    *   **Uneven Bars (Women):** Front Hip Circle, Back Hip Circle.
*   **Evaluation:** Based on performing specific elements on each apparatus, with points awarded for each element (e.g., 5.00 points per element).

**3. Judo:**
*   **Origin:** Japanese art of Ju-jitsu. Founded by Prof. Jigoro Kano (1882). Olympic sport since 1964.
*   **Area:** 14m x 14m competition area on a raised platform, including a 1m danger zone and 3m safety area.
    *(Conceptual Diagram: Judo competition area showing contest area, danger zone, safety area)*
*   **Gameplay:** Bouts based on weight categories (vary by age/level). Duration: 5 min (boys), 4 min (girls). Objective is to score points through throws or holds.
*   **Grading:** Kyu (pupil) and Dan (master) ranks indicated by belt colour (White -> Yellow -> Orange -> Green -> Blue -> Brown -> Black -> Red/White -> Red).
*   **Scoring:**
    *   **Ippon (Full point - ends match):** Perfect throw OR Hold for 20 sec.
    *   **Waza-ari (Half point - 2 Waza-ari = Ippon):** Throw lacking one element of Ippon OR Hold for 15-19 sec.
    *   **Yuko (Lower score):** Throw lacking two elements of Ippon OR Hold for 10-14 sec.
*   **Skills:** Rei (Salutation), Kumikata (Grip), Shintai (Walking), Shizentai (Posture), Ukemi (Breakfalls - essential for safety), Tai-sabaki (Body turning), Kuzushi (Off-balancing), Tsukuri (Fitting in), Kake (Execution).

**4. Swimming:**
*   **Basics:** Propelling body through water using strokes (arms) and kicks (legs). Non-weight bearing exercise, good for rehab and recreation. Governed by FINA (1908).
*   **Pool & Equipment:** Standard pool: 50m length, 25m width, 1.80m depth, 8-10 lanes (2.5m width each). Starting platform: 50-75cm height. Water temp: ~24°C. Requires costume, cap, goggles.
    *(Conceptual Diagram: Overhead view of a standard swimming pool showing lanes and dimensions)*
*   **Strokes:**
    *   **Freestyle (Crawl):** Prone position, alternate over-arm recovery, flutter kick, side breathing.
        *(Conceptual Diagram: Side view sequence showing arm pull/recovery and kick in Freestyle)*
    *   **Backstroke:** Supine position, alternate straight-arm recovery, flutter kick, breathing as needed.
    *   **Breaststroke:** Prone position, simultaneous 'heart-shaped' arm pull, frog kick, breathing during arm in-sweep.
    *   **Butterfly:** Prone position, simultaneous over-arm recovery ('keyhole' pull), dolphin kick (legs together), breathing during arm press down.
*   **Safety:** Bathe before/after, use clean towel, swim with lifeguard supervision, warm-up/stretch.

**5. Table Tennis:**
*   **History:** Known as ping-pong, whiff-whaff. Governed by ITTF (1926). Olympic sport since 1988.
*   **Table & Equipment:** Table: 274cm x 152.5cm, 76cm high. Dark, matt surface with white lines. Net: 15.25cm high. Ball: 40mm diameter, 2.7g, orange/white. Bat (Racket/Paddle): Wooden blade, specific rubber colours (black/red).
    *(Conceptual Diagram: Table Tennis table showing dimensions and net position)*
*   **Gameplay:** Singles, Doubles, Mixed. Toss determines serve/receive/side. Serve: Ball tossed from open palm, hit to bounce once on server's side, then opponent's side. Rally scoring; game is 11 points (win by 2). Let service if net is touched but ball lands correctly.
*   **Skills:**
    *   **Grip:** Shake hand (common), Pen hold.
        *(Conceptual Diagram: Illustration of Shake hand and Pen hold grips)*
    *   **Spin:** Topspin (bat angled down), Backspin (bat angled up), Sidespin (often used in serves).
    *   **Strokes:** Offensive and Defensive pushes/drives (Forehand/Backhand).

**6. Wrestling:**
*   **Styles:** Olympic: Freestyle (arms & legs used), Greco-Roman (upper body only). Indian: Pehlwani/Kushti (often in clay pit). Governed internationally by FILA (now UWW).
*   **Mat & Facilities:** Indoor mat, 12m x 12m overall. Competition area: 7m diameter yellow circle. Centre circle: 1m diameter. Mat thickness: 5-7cm.
    *(Conceptual Diagram: Wrestling mat showing competition circle, passivity zone, centre circle)*
*   **Gameplay:** Bouts based on weight categories. Red/Blue uniforms. 3 rounds of 2 minutes. Win by fall, point superiority, or opponent disqualification.
*   **Scoring:** Takedown (1-5 points depending on control/exposure), Push-out (1 point), Reversal, Exposure points in 'Par terre' (mat wrestling). Tie-breaking rules prioritize cautions, highest scoring move, last point scored. Clinch rule forces action in scoreless periods.
*   **Skills:** Stance (Square, Staggered), Motion (Circling, Attacking, Defending), Level Change (Lowering hips), Penetration (Shooting for takedowns), Lifting (Using legs/hips), Back Step, Back Arch (Defensive/Offensive maneuvers).
