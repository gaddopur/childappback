# [Class 9] General - Chapter 103: Coordinate Geometry (निर्देशांक ज्यामिति)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ) 📊

1.  **Introduction (परिचय):**
    *   Kisi point ya object ki position ko ek plane (समतल) par locate karne ki zaroorat.
    *   Single reference point (jaise number line par) kaafi nahi hota hai plane mein position batane ke liye.
    *   Example: Street aur House number dono chahiye address locate karne ke liye (Fig 3.1). Dot ki position paper par batane ke liye do edges se distance chahiye (Fig 3.2).

2.  **Need for Coordinate System (निर्देशांक प्रणाली की आवश्यकता):**
    *   Plane mein kisi bhi point ki exact position batane ke liye do independent information chahiye.
    *   Yeh idea Coordinate Geometry ka base hai.
    *   Developed by René Déscartes (रेने डेकार्टेस).

3.  **Cartesian System (कार्तीय प्रणाली):**
    *   Do perpendicular lines (लंबवत रेखाएँ) ka use hota hai.
    *   **Coordinate Axes (निर्देशांक अक्ष):**
        *   Horizontal line (क्षैतिज रेखा): **x-axis** (X'X)
        *   Vertical line (ऊर्ध्वाधर रेखा): **y-axis** (Y'Y)
    *   **Origin (मूलबिंदु):** Point jahan dono axes intersect karte hain (O). Coordinates hote hain (0, 0).
    *   **Directions (दिशाएँ):**
        *   Positive Directions: OX (positive x-axis), OY (positive y-axis)
        *   Negative Directions: OX' (negative x-axis), OY' (negative y-axis)
    *   **Quadrants (चतुर्थांश):** Axes plane ko chaar parts mein divide karte hain:
        *   Quadrant I (+, +)
        *   Quadrant II (–, +)
        *   Quadrant III (–, –)
        *   Quadrant IV (+, –)
        (Anticlockwise direction mein numbering hoti hai OX se start karke).
    *   **Cartesian Plane / Coordinate Plane / xy-plane:** Woh plane jisme axes aur quadrants hote hain.

4.  **Coordinates of a Point (एक बिंदु के निर्देशांक):**
    *   Kisi point ki position ko represent karne wale numbers.
    *   **x-coordinate (x-निर्देशांक) or Abscissa (भुज):** Point ka perpendicular distance y-axis se, measured along the x-axis. Positive on OX side, negative on OX' side.
    *   **y-coordinate (y-निर्देशांक) or Ordinate (कोटि):** Point ka perpendicular distance x-axis se, measured along the y-axis. Positive on OY side, negative on OY' side.
    *   **Notation (संकेतन):** Coordinates ko bracket mein likhte hain (x, y). Pehle x-coordinate, phir y-coordinate. Example: P(4, 3), Q(–6, –2).
    *   **Uniqueness:** (x, y) point ki unique position batata hai. (3, 4) aur (4, 3) alag points hain (unless x=y).

5.  **Points on Axes (अक्षों पर बिंदु):**
    *   **On x-axis:** y-coordinate hamesha 0 hota hai. Coordinates hote hain (x, 0). Example: A(4, 0), C(–5, 0).
    *   **On y-axis:** x-coordinate hamesha 0 hota hai. Coordinates hote hain (0, y). Example: B(0, 3), D(0, –4).

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख) 📈

*   **Locating a Point:** Kisi bhi point ko plane mein locate karne ke liye, humein do perpendicular lines (axes) ke reference mein uski position batani hoti hai. Jaise ek sheher mein kisi jagah ko dhundhne ke liye hum East-West road aur North-South street ka reference lete hain. (Fig 3.1)

    ![Street Plan Example](https://www.learncbse.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/NCERT-Solutions-for-Class-9-Maths-Chapter-3-Coordinate-Geometry-Exercise-3.1-1.png) *(Image similar to Fig 3.1 for illustration)*

*   **Cartesian Plane ka Structure:** Do number lines (x-axis aur y-axis) ko unke zero (origin) par perpendicular intersect karake Cartesian plane banta hai. Yeh plane ko 4 quadrants mein divide karta hai.

    ![Cartesian Plane with Quadrants](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Cartesian_coordinate_system_with_points.svg/300px-Cartesian_coordinate_system_with_points.svg.png) *(Image similar to Fig 3.9/3.13 for illustration)*

*   **Understanding Coordinates:**
    *   Point P(4, 3) ka matlab hai:
        *   y-axis se perpendicular distance (along positive x-axis) = 4 units (Abscissa).
        *   x-axis se perpendicular distance (along positive y-axis) = 3 units (Ordinate).
    *   Point Q(–6, –2) ka matlab hai:
        *   y-axis se perpendicular distance (along negative x-axis) = 6 units (Abscissa = -6).
        *   x-axis se perpendicular distance (along negative y-axis) = 2 units (Ordinate = -2).

    ![Finding Coordinates](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/N4nrdf0yYfM/maxresdefault.jpg) *(Image similar to Fig 3.10/3.11 for illustration)*

*   **Signs in Quadrants:** Point ke coordinates ke sign (+/-) se pata chalta hai ki woh kis quadrant mein hai:
    *   I: (+, +) - Jaise (4, 3)
    *   II: (–, +) - Jaise (–3, 4)
    *   III: (–, –) - Jaise (–5, –4)
    *   IV: (+, –) - Jaise (3, –4)

*   **Points on Axes:**
    *   Agar koi point x-axis par hai, toh uska y-coordinate 0 hoga, jaise (4, 0).
    *   Agar koi point y-axis par hai, toh uska x-coordinate 0 hoga, jaise (0, 3).
    *   Origin ke coordinates (0, 0) hote hain.

    ![Points on Axes](https://cdn1.byjus.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/coordinate-geometry-class-9-points-on-axes.png) *(Image similar to Fig 3.12 for illustration)*
