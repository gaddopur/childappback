# [Class 9] Maths - Chapter 3: Coordinate Geometry (निर्देशांक ज्यामिति)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ) 📊

1.  **Introduction (परिचय):**
    *   Kisi bhi point ya object ki position ek plane (समतल) mein batane ke liye do references ki zaroorat hoti hai. (Need for two references to locate a point in a plane).
    *   Example: Street number aur house number; ya paper par dot ki left edge aur bottom edge se doori.

2.  **Cartesian System (कार्तीय पद्धति):**
    *   Developed by René Déscartes.
    *   Do perpendicular lines (दो लंबवत रेखाएँ) use hoti hain:
        *   **Horizontal Line (क्षैतिज रेखा):** x-axis (X'OX)
        *   **Vertical Line (ऊर्ध्वाधर रेखा):** y-axis (Y'OY)
    *   **Origin (मूलबिंदु):** Jahaan dono axes intersect karte hain (O). Yeh (0, 0) hota hai.
    *   **Coordinate Axes (निर्देशांक अक्ष):** x-axis aur y-axis ko milakar kehte hain.
    *   **Coordinate Plane / Cartesian Plane / xy-plane (निर्देशांक समतल / कार्तीय तल / xy- तल):** Woh plane jismein coordinate axes hote hain.

3.  **Quadrants (चतुर्थांश):**
    *   Axes plane ko chaar parts mein divide karte hain: Quadrants.
    *   Numbering anticlockwise hoti hai OX se shuru karke:
        *   **Quadrant I:** (+, +) (Positive x-axis aur positive y-axis ke beech ka area)
        *   **Quadrant II:** (–, +) (Negative x-axis aur positive y-axis ke beech ka area)
        *   **Quadrant III:** (–, –) (Negative x-axis aur negative y-axis ke beech ka area)
        *   **Quadrant IV:** (+, –) (Positive x-axis aur negative y-axis ke beech ka area)

4.  **Coordinates of a Point (बिंदु के निर्देशांक):**
    *   Kisi bhi point P ke liye plane mein:
        *   **x-coordinate (x-निर्देशांक) or Abscissa (भुज):** Point ki y-axis se perpendicular distance (लंबवत दूरी), measured along the x-axis. Positive on OX side, negative on OX' side.
        *   **y-coordinate (y-निर्देशांक) or Ordinate (कोटि):** Point ki x-axis se perpendicular distance (लंबवत दूरी), measured along the y-axis. Positive on OY side, negative on OY' side.
    *   Coordinates ko hamesha (x, y) ke form mein likhte hain, bracket mein. Pehle x-coordinate, phir y-coordinate.
    *   Example: Point P(4, 3) ka matlab hai x-coordinate = 4 aur y-coordinate = 3. Point Q(-6, -2) ka matlab hai x-coordinate = -6 aur y-coordinate = -2.
    *   **Important:** (4, 3) aur (3, 4) alag-alag points hain, unless x=y.

5.  **Points on Axes (अक्षों पर बिंदु):**
    *   **On x-axis:** Koi bhi point x-axis par hoga toh uska y-coordinate hamesha 0 hoga. Form: (x, 0). Example: (4, 0), (-5, 0).
    *   **On y-axis:** Koi bhi point y-axis par hoga toh uska x-coordinate hamesha 0 hoga. Form: (0, y). Example: (0, 3), (0, -4).
    *   **Origin (मूलबिंदु):** Coordinates (0, 0) hote hain.

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य बातें) 📈

*   **Locating a Point (बिंदु का स्थान निर्धारण):** Kisi plane mein ek point ko uniquely locate karne ke liye, humein do perpendicular lines (axes) ke reference mein uski position batani hoti hai. Jaise hum kisi sheher mein address batane ke liye gali number aur makan number batate hain (Fig 3.1). Ya paper par ek dot ki position batane ke liye left edge se doori aur bottom edge se doori batate hain (Fig 3.2).

*   **The Cartesian Plane (कार्तीय तल):** Yeh system René Déscartes ne develop kiya tha. Ismein do number lines (x-axis aur y-axis) ek doosre ko unke zero (origin 'O') par perpendicularly intersect karti hain (Fig 3.8).
    *   X'OX horizontal line hai (x-axis). OX positive direction hai, OX' negative direction hai.
    *   Y'OY vertical line hai (y-axis). OY positive direction hai, OY' negative direction hai.

*   **Understanding Coordinates (निर्देशांक समझना):**
    *   Kisi point P(x, y) ke liye:
        *   `x` (Abscissa/भुज): y-axis se perpendicular doori. Agar point y-axis ke right mein hai toh positive `x`, left mein hai toh negative `x`.
        *   `y` (Ordinate/कोटि): x-axis se perpendicular doori. Agar point x-axis ke upar hai toh positive `y`, neeche hai toh negative `y`.
    *   Example (Fig 3.10): Point P(4, 3) y-axis se 4 units right mein hai aur x-axis se 3 units upar hai. Point Q(-6, -2) y-axis se 6 units left mein hai aur x-axis se 2 units neeche hai.

*   **Quadrants and Signs (चतुर्थांश और चिह्न):** Point ki location (quadrant) uske coordinates ke signs se pata chalti hai (Fig 3.13):
    *   Quadrant I: (+, +) - Dono coordinates positive.
    *   Quadrant II: (–, +) - x negative, y positive.
    *   Quadrant III: (–, –) - Dono coordinates negative.
    *   Quadrant IV: (+, –) - x positive, y negative.

    ![Quadrants and Sign Conventions](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c6/Cartesian_coordinates_2D.svg/300px-Cartesian_coordinates_2D.svg.png)
    *(Diagram showing the four quadrants and the signs of coordinates in each)*

*   **Coordinates on Axes (अक्षों पर निर्देशांक):**
    *   Agar koi point x-axis par hai, toh uska y-coordinate 0 hoga. Jaise A(4, 0), C(-5, 0) in Fig 3.12.
    *   Agar koi point y-axis par hai, toh uska x-coordinate 0 hoga. Jaise B(0, 3), D(0, -4) in Fig 3.12.
    *   Origin O ke coordinates (0, 0) hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (भारतीय संदर्भ)

1.  **Mapping India (भारत का मानचित्रण):** Jaise world maps par Latitude (अक्षांश) aur Longitude (देशांतर) ka grid hota hai, woh bhi ek tarah ka coordinate system hai. Bharat mein kisi bhi jagah ki exact location batane ke liye inhi coordinates ka istemal hota hai. For example, Delhi lagbhag 28.6° N Latitude aur 77.2° E Longitude par hai. Yeh large scale coordinate geometry hai!

2.  **Urban Planning (शहरी नियोजन):** Bharat ke planned cities jaise Chandigarh mein grid pattern ka use hua hai. Sectors ko numbers se identify kiya jaata hai, jo ek tarah se coordinate system jaisa hai, jisse navigation aasan ho jaati hai. Naye develop ho rahe smart cities mein bhi precise location ke liye advanced coordinate systems (GIS - Geographic Information Systems) ka use hota hai.

3.  **Economic/Social Data Representation (आर्थिक/सामाजिक डेटा प्रस्तुति):** Bharat Sarkar aur various organizations desh ke economic aur social parameters (jaise gareebi rekha se neeche log, literacy rate, population growth) ko represent karne ke liye graphs ka istemal karte hain. In graphs mein usually time ko x-axis par aur data value ko y-axis par plot kiya jaata hai. For example, Census of India data ko state-wise compare karne ke liye bar graphs ya line graphs banaye jaate hain, jo coordinate plane par hi bante hain. Hum isse trends (रुझान) aur comparisons ko aasani se samajh sakte hain. Example: Plotting state-wise Per Capita Income (प्रति व्यक्ति आय) ya Infant Mortality Rate (शिशु मृत्यु दर) over the years.
