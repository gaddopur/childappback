# [Class 9] Maths - Chapter 3: Coordinate Geometry ( निर्देशांक ज्यामिति)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Introduction (Parichay):**
    *   Kisi plane (satah) par ek point (bindu) ki position ko describe karne ki zaroorat. (Need to describe the position of a point on a plane).
    *   Ek single reference (jaise number line par) kaafi nahi hai plane mein position batane ke liye. (A single reference isn't enough to describe position in a plane).
    *   Example: Street address (Street no. and House no.), Seating plan (Row and Column).

2.  **Cartesian System (Karteesiya Paddhati):**
    *   Developed by René Descartes. Isliye ise Cartesian System kehte hain.
    *   **Coordinate Axes (Nirdeshank Aksh):**
        *   Do perpendicular lines (do lambvat rekhaayein).
        *   **x-axis:** Horizontal line (Kshaitij rekha - X'OX).
        *   **y-axis:** Vertical line (Urdhvadhar rekha - Y'OY).
    *   **Origin (Moolbindu):**
        *   Point jahan dono axes intersect karte hain (katate hain). Denoted by O.
        *   Origin ke coordinates (0, 0) hote hain.
    *   **Quadrants (Chaturthansh):**
        *   Axes plane ko chaar parts mein divide karte hain.
        *   Quadrant I, II, III, IV (anticlockwise direction mein, OX se shuru karte hue).
    *   **Cartesian Plane (Karteesiya Tal):**
        *   The plane containing the axes and quadrants. Ise coordinate plane ya xy-plane bhi kehte hain.

3.  **Coordinates of a Point (Ek Bindu ke Nirdeshank):**
    *   Kisi point ki position ko uniquely describe karne ke liye numbers ka pair.
    *   **x-coordinate (Abscissa / Bhuj):**
        *   Point ka perpendicular distance y-axis se, measured along the x-axis.
        *   Positive on OX side, negative on OX' side.
    *   **y-coordinate (Ordinate / Koti):**
        *   Point ka perpendicular distance x-axis se, measured along the y-axis.
        *   Positive on OY side, negative on OY' side.
    *   **Convention (Parampara):**
        *   Coordinates ko hamesha (x, y) ke form mein likhte hain (x-coordinate pehle, y-coordinate baad mein).
        *   Example: Point P(4, 3) ka matlab hai x=4, y=3. Yeh point (3, 4) se alag hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

1.  **Locating a Point (Bindu ka Sthaan Nirdhaarit Karna):**
    *   Kisi bhi point ko plane mein locate karne ke liye, humein do perpendicular lines (axes) ki zaroorat hoti hai.
    *   Jaise ek paper par dot ki position batane ke liye, humein left edge se doori (distance) aur bottom edge se doori, dono batani padti hai. (Fig 3.2)

    ![Dot on Paper](https://www.learncbse.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/NCERT-Solutions-for-Class-9-Maths-Chapter-3-Coordinate-Geometry-Ex-3.1-Q1-1.png) *(Image similar to Fig 3.2b)*

2.  **Understanding Axes and Origin (Akshon aur Moolbindu ko Samajhna):**
    *   x-axis (horizontal) aur y-axis (vertical) ek doosre ko Origin (O) par perpendicularly intersect karte hain. (Fig 3.8)
    *   OX aur OY positive directions hain. OX' aur OY' negative directions hain.

    ![Coordinate Axes](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Cartesian_coordinates_2D.svg/300px-Cartesian_coordinates_2D.svg.png) *(Image similar to Fig 3.8/3.9)*

3.  **Coordinates and Quadrants (Nirdeshank aur Chaturthansh):**
    *   **Abscissa (x-coordinate):** y-axis se perpendicular doori.
    *   **Ordinate (y-coordinate):** x-axis se perpendicular doori.
    *   Ek point P(x, y) ke liye:
        *   x = Abscissa
        *   y = Ordinate
    *   **Signs in Quadrants (Chaturthanshon mein Chinh):** (Fig 3.13)
        *   Quadrant I: (+, +) (x positive, y positive)
        *   Quadrant II: (–, +) (x negative, y positive)
        *   Quadrant III: (–, –) (x negative, y negative)
        *   Quadrant IV: (+, –) (x positive, y negative)

    ![Quadrants and Signs](https://cdn1.byjus.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Quadrants-of-a-Coordinate-Plane.png) *(Image similar to Fig 3.13)*

4.  **Points on Axes (Akshon par Bindu):**
    *   **On x-axis:** Koi bhi point x-axis par (x, 0) ke form mein hota hai (uska y-coordinate hamesha 0 hota hai). Example: (4, 0), (-5, 0). (Fig 3.12 - Points A, C, E)
    *   **On y-axis:** Koi bhi point y-axis par (0, y) ke form mein hota hai (uska x-coordinate hamesha 0 hota hai). Example: (0, 3), (0, -4). (Fig 3.12 - Points B, D)
    *   **Origin (Moolbindu):** Coordinates are (0, 0).

    ![Points on Axes](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/vAPMAPfs4uI/maxresdefault.jpg) *(Image similar to Fig 3.12)*

5.  **Uniqueness of Coordinates (Nirdeshanko ki Advitiyata):**
    *   Plane mein har point ke liye ek unique pair of coordinates hota hai.
    *   Order matters: (x, y) ≠ (y, x) agar x ≠ y. For example, (4, 3) is different from (3, 4).
