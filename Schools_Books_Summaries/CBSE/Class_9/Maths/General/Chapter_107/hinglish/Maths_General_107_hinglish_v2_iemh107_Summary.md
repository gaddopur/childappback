# [Class 9] General - Chapter 7: Tribhuj (Triangles)

## 🌟 Core Concepts

Yeh chapter triangles (त्रिभुज) aur unki properties, khaas kar congruence (सर्वांगसमता) ke baare mein hai. Mukhya concepts hain:

1.  **Triangle Basics:**
    *   Definition: Teen intersecting lines se bani band आकृति (closed figure).
    *   Parts: 3 sides (भुजाएँ), 3 angles (कोण), 3 vertices (शीर्ष).

2.  **Congruence (Sarvangsamta - सर्वांगसमता):**
    *   Meaning: Figures jinka shape (आकार) aur size (माप) dono bilkul same ho. Ek doosre ko poora cover kar lein.
    *   Notation: `≅` symbol ka use hota hai (e.g., ∆ABC ≅ ∆PQR).
    *   **CPCT:** Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles (सर्वांगसम त्रिभुजों के संगत भाग). Agar do triangles congruent hain, toh unke corresponding sides aur angles barabar hote hain. Yeh proofs mein bahut important hai. Sahi correspondence likhna zaroori hai (A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R).

3.  **Criteria for Congruence of Triangles (त्रिभुजों की सर्वांगसमता के लिए कसौटियाँ):**
    *   **SAS (Side-Angle-Side):** Agar ek triangle ki do sides aur unke beech ka angle (included angle) doosre triangle ki corresponding sides aur included angle ke barabar ho. (Yeh ek Axiom hai).
    *   **ASA (Angle-Side-Angle):** Agar ek triangle ke do angles aur unke beech ki side (included side) doosre triangle ke corresponding angles aur included side ke barabar ho. (Yeh ek Theorem hai).
    *   **AAS (Angle-Angle-Side):** Agar ek triangle ke koi do angles aur ek non-included side doosre triangle ke corresponding angles aur corresponding side ke barabar ho. (ASA se derive hota hai).
    *   **SSS (Side-Side-Side):** Agar ek triangle ki teeno sides doosre triangle ki corresponding teeno sides ke barabar ho. (Yeh ek Theorem hai).
    *   **RHS (Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side):** Do right-angled triangles mein, agar hypotenuse aur ek side barabar ho. (Yeh ek Theorem hai).
    *   *Important Note:* AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle) aur SSA/ASS (Side-Side-Angle) congruence ke liye sufficient criteria **NAHI** hain.

4.  **Properties of Triangles (त्रिभुज के गुण):**
    *   **Isosceles Triangle (समद्विबाहु त्रिभुज):**
        *   Theorem 7.2: Agar kisi triangle ki do sides equal hain, toh unke opposite angles bhi equal honge. (Angles opposite to equal sides are equal).
        *   Theorem 7.3: Agar kisi triangle ke do angles equal hain, toh unke opposite sides bhi equal honge. (Sides opposite to equal angles are equal - Converse of Theorem 7.2).
    *   **Equilateral Triangle (समबाहु त्रिभुज):** Sabhi sides equal hoti hain aur sabhi angles 60° ke hote hain.

## 📘 Key Learnings

Is chapter mein humne seekha ki triangles ko congruent kaise prove karte hain aur iske kya fayde hain.

1.  **Congruence ka Matlab:** Do cheezein congruent tab hoti hain jab woh har tarah se identical hon - shape mein bhi aur size mein bhi. Jaise ek hi saal bane do 1 rupaye ke sikke (coins) 🪙, ya ek hi bank ke do ATM cards 💳. Geometry mein, do line segments congruent hote hain agar unki length barabar ho, do angles congruent hote hain agar unka measure barabar ho, aur do triangles congruent hote hain agar unke corresponding sides aur corresponding angles barabar hon.

2.  **Congruence Rules Samajhna:**
    *   **SAS 📐:** Imagine do triangles hain, ∆ABC aur ∆PQR. Agar AB=PQ, BC=QR aur inke beech ka angle ∠B = ∠Q hai, toh hum keh sakte hain ∆ABC ≅ ∆PQR. Angle ka sides ke *beech* mein hona zaroori hai.
    *   **ASA 📐:** Agar ∠B = ∠E, BC = EF, aur ∠C = ∠F hai (do angles aur unke beech ki side), toh ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF.
    *   **AAS 📐:** Agar ∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠E, aur side BC = EF hai (do angles aur koi ek corresponding side), toh bhi ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF. Kyunki agar do angles equal hain, toh teesra angle (angle sum property se) apne aap equal ho jaata hai, aur yeh ASA jaisa ban jaata hai.
    *   **SSS 📏:** Agar AB=DE, BC=EF, aur AC=DF hai (teeno corresponding sides barabar), toh ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF.
    *   **RHS (Right-angled triangles ke liye) 📐 hypotenuse:** Agar ∆ABC aur ∆DEF dono right-angled triangles hain (∠B = ∠E = 90°), unka hypotenuse barabar hai (AC = DF), aur koi ek corresponding side barabar hai (jaise AB = DE), toh ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF.

3.  **CPCT ka Istemaal:** Jab hum congruence rules (SAS, ASA, etc.) ka use karke do triangles ko congruent prove kar dete hain, toh hum CPCT ka use karke unke bache hue corresponding parts ko bhi equal bata sakte hain. Jaise, agar ∆ABC ≅ ∆PQR by SAS rule, toh hum keh sakte hain ki AC = PR, ∠A = ∠P, aur ∠C = ∠R (by CPCT). Yeh proofs mein bahut kaam aata hai.

4.  **Isosceles Triangle ki Properties:**
    *   Agar ek triangle mein AB = AC hai, toh unke saamne wale angles, ∠C aur ∠B, bhi barabar honge. 📈 (Diagram: Isosceles triangle ABC with AB=AC, showing ∠B = ∠C).
    *   Iska ulta bhi sach hai: Agar ek triangle mein ∠B = ∠C hai, toh unke saamne wali sides, AC aur AB, bhi barabar hongi. 📉 (Diagram: Triangle ABC with ∠B = ∠C, showing AB=AC).

Isse construction tez, cost-effective aur quality-controlled hota hai. Congruence ensures ki sab parts perfectly fit hon.
