# [Class 9] Maths - Chapter 7: Triangles (त्रिभुज)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ)

Yeh chapter triangles (त्रिभुज) aur unki properties, khaas kar **congruence** (सर्वांगसमता) par focus karta hai.

1.  **Triangle Basics (त्रिभुज की मूल बातें)**
    *   Teen intersecting lines se bani closed figure.
    *   Components: 3 sides (भुजाएँ), 3 angles (कोण), 3 vertices (शीर्ष).
2.  **Congruence (सर्वांगसमता)**
    *   Definition: Figures jinka shape (आकार) aur size (माप) dono same ho. Bilkul identical. ('Congruent' ka matlab 'sabhi tarah se barabar').
    *   Examples: Same size ke do photos, ek hi saal bane do 1 rupee coins, same radius wale do circles, same side length wale do squares.
    *   Congruent Triangles: Ek triangle ke sides aur angles doosre triangle ke *corresponding* (संगत) sides aur angles ke equal hote hain.
    *   Notation: ∆ ABC ≅ ∆ PQR (Iska matlab A corresponds to P, B to Q, C to R). Correspondence order bahut important hai.
    *   **CPCT**: Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles (सर्वांगसम त्रिभुजों के संगत भाग). Agar do triangles congruent hain, toh unke corresponding parts (sides and angles) equal honge.
3.  **Criteria for Congruence of Triangles (त्रिभुजों की सर्वांगसमता के लिए कसौटियाँ)**
    *   **SAS (Side-Angle-Side / भुजा-कोण-भुजा)**: Do sides aur unke beech ka included angle equal ho. (Axiom 7.1)
    *   **ASA (Angle-Side-Angle / कोण-भुजा-कोण)**: Do angles aur unke beech ki included side equal ho. (Theorem 7.1)
    *   **AAS (Angle-Angle-Side / कोण-कोण-भुजा)**: Koi bhi do angles aur ek corresponding side equal ho. (Derived from ASA)
    *   **SSS (Side-Side-Side / भुजा-भुजा-भुजा)**: Teeno sides equal ho. (Theorem 7.4)
    *   **RHS (Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side / समकोण-कर्ण-भुजा)**: Right-angled triangles mein, hypotenuse aur koi ek side equal ho. (Theorem 7.5)
    *   *Important Note:* Sirf teeno angles equal hona (AAA) congruence ke liye kaafi nahi hai. Sirf do sides aur ek non-included angle equal hona (SSA ya ASS) bhi congruence guarantee nahi karta.
4.  **Properties of Isosceles Triangles (समद्विबाहु त्रिभुज के गुण)**
    *   Isosceles Triangle: Ek triangle jiski do sides equal hon.
    *   Theorem 7.2: Equal sides ke opposite angles bhi equal hote hain. (Angles opposite to equal sides are equal).
    *   Theorem 7.3 (Converse of 7.2): Equal angles ke opposite sides bhi equal hote hain. (Sides opposite to equal angles are equal).
5.  **Properties of Equilateral Triangles (समबाहु त्रिभुज के गुण)**
    *   Teeno sides equal hoti hain.
    *   Har angle 60° ka hota hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य बातें)

**1. Congruence ka Matlab Samjho (Understanding Congruence)**

Imagine karo tumhare paas do bilkul same design aur size ki tiles hain. Agar ek tile ko doosre ke upar rakho toh woh perfectly cover kar legi. Yehi congruence hai. Triangles ke case mein, congruence ka matlab hai ki agar hum ek triangle ko kaat kar doosre par rakhein (zaroorat padne par ghuma kar), toh woh poora cover ho jaana chahiye.

**Example:** Agar ∆ ABC ≅ ∆ PQR, toh:
*   AB = PQ, BC = QR, AC = PR (Corresponding sides equal)
*   ∠ A = ∠ P, ∠ B = ∠ Q, ∠ C = ∠ R (Corresponding angles equal)
Isi ko hum short mein **CPCT** kehte hain.

**2. Congruence Rules (सर्वांगसमता के नियम)**

Yeh rules shortcut hain yeh check karne ke liye ki do triangles congruent hain ya nahi, bina saare 6 parts (3 sides, 3 angles) ko measure kiye.

*   **SAS (Side-Angle-Side):**
    *   Agar ek triangle ki do sides aur *unke beech ka angle* (included angle) doosre triangle ki corresponding do sides aur *unke beech ke angle* ke barabar hai, toh triangles congruent hain.
    *   Diagram Idea:
        ```
          A             P
         / \           / \
        /   \         /   \
       AB = PQ     ∠B = ∠Q     BC = QR
      /_____\       /_____\
     B       C     Q       R
        ```
    *   *Galti se Bacho:* Angle ka sides ke beech mein hona zaroori hai (ASS ya SSA rule nahi hota).

*   **ASA (Angle-Side-Angle):**
    *   Agar ek triangle ke do angles aur *unke beech ki side* (included side) doosre triangle ke corresponding do angles aur *unke beech ki side* ke barabar hai, toh triangles congruent hain.
    *   Diagram Idea:
        ```
          A             P
         / \           / \
        /   \         /   \
       ∠B = ∠Q     BC = EF     ∠C = ∠F
      /_____\       /_____\
     B       C     E       F
        ```

*   **AAS (Angle-Angle-Side):**
    *   Agar ek triangle ke koi do angles aur *koi ek corresponding side* (included hona zaroori nahi) doosre triangle ke corresponding do angles aur corresponding side ke barabar hai, toh triangles congruent hain.
    *   *Reason:* Agar do angles equal hain, toh teesra angle automatically equal hoga (kyunki sum 180° hota hai). Toh AAS effectively ASA hi ban jaata hai.

*   **SSS (Side-Side-Side):**
    *   Agar ek triangle ki teeno sides doosre triangle ki corresponding teeno sides ke barabar hain, toh triangles congruent hain.
    *   Diagram Idea:
        ```
          A             P
         / \           / \
        /   \         /   \
       AB = PQ     BC = QR     AC = PR
      /_____\       /_____\
     B       C     Q       R
        ```

*   **RHS (Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side):**
    *   Yeh rule *sirf right-angled triangles* ke liye hai.
    *   Agar do right triangles mein, hypotenuse (90° ke opposite wali side) aur koi bhi ek corresponding side equal hai, toh triangles congruent hain.
    *   Diagram Idea:
        ```
          A             P
          |\            |\
          | \           | \
        AC=PR(Hyp)   AB=PQ(Side)  ∠B = ∠Q = 90°
          |__\          |__\
         B    C        Q    R
        ```

**3. Isosceles Triangle Properties (समद्विबाहु त्रिभुज के गुण)**

*   Agar AB = AC hai (sides equal), toh ∠C = ∠B hoga (opposite angles equal).
    ```
       A
      / \
     /   \  AB = AC
    /_____\
   B       C  => ∠B = ∠C
    ```
*   Agar ∠B = ∠C hai (angles equal), toh AC = AB hoga (opposite sides equal). (Yeh Theorem 7.2 ka ulta/converse hai).

**4. Proofs mein Congruence ka Use (Using Congruence in Proofs)**

Bahut saare geometry problems mein humein kuch prove karna hota hai (jaise do lines parallel hain, ya do segments equal hain). Iske liye hum aksar:
1.  Do triangles identify karte hain jinmein woh parts aate hain.
2.  Un triangles ko kisi congruence rule (SAS, ASA, SSS, RHS, AAS) ka use karke congruent prove karte hain.
3.  Phir **CPCT** ka use karke kehte hain ki corresponding parts equal hain.

**Example:** Jaise Example 1 (Fig 7.8) mein, ∆AOD ≅ ∆BOC prove karne ke liye SAS use kiya (OA=OB, OD=OC, ∠AOD=∠BOC). Phir CPCT se ∠OAD = ∠OBC mila, jisse AD || BC prove hua (alternate angles).
