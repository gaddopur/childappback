# [Class 9] Maths - Chapter 7: Triangles (त्रिभुज)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (मुख्य अवधारणाएँ) 📊

1.  **Congruence (सर्वांगसमता)**
    *   Meaning: Figures jinka shape (आकार) aur size (माप) dono same ho. Ek figure doosre ko poori tarah cover kar le. (एक आकृति दूसरी को पूरी तरह ढक ले).
    *   Notation: ≅ (Jaise ∆ABC ≅ ∆PQR)
    *   Correspondence (संगतता): Vertices, sides, aur angles ka correct matching zaroori hai. (P ↔ A, Q ↔ B, R ↔ C). Galat correspondence likhna sahi nahi hai (∆QRP ≅ ∆ABC likhna galat ho sakta hai agar correspondence match na kare).
    *   **CPCT**: Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles (सर्वांगसम त्रिभुजों के संगत भाग). Agar do triangles congruent hain, toh unke corresponding sides aur angles equal honge.

2.  **Criteria for Congruence of Triangles (त्रिभुजों की सर्वांगसमता के लिए कसौटियाँ)**
    *   **SAS (Side-Angle-Side / भुजा-कोण-भुजा)**: Do sides aur unke beech ka included angle equal ho. (Axiom 7.1)
    *   **ASA (Angle-Side-Angle / कोण-भुजा-कोण)**: Do angles aur unke beech ki included side equal ho. (Theorem 7.1)
    *   **AAS (Angle-Angle-Side / कोण-कोण-भुजा)**: Do angles aur ek corresponding side equal ho (non-included side). (Derived from ASA)
    *   **SSS (Side-Side-Side / भुजा-भुजा-भुजा)**: Teeno corresponding sides equal ho. (Theorem 7.4)
    *   **RHS (Right Angle-Hypotenuse-Side / समकोण-कर्ण-भुजा)**: Right-angled triangles mein hypotenuse aur ek side equal ho. (Theorem 7.5)
    *   **Important Note**: AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle) aur SSA (Side-Side-Angle) congruence ke liye sufficient nahi hain.

3.  **Properties of Isosceles Triangles (समद्विबाहु त्रिभुज के गुण)**
    *   **Isosceles Triangle (समद्विबाहु त्रिभुज)**: Aisa triangle jiski do sides equal hon.
    *   Theorem 7.2: Equal sides ke opposite angles equal hote hain. (यदि AB = AC, तो ∠C = ∠B).
    *   Theorem 7.3 (Converse of 7.2): Equal angles ke opposite sides equal hote hain. (यदि ∠B = ∠C, तो AC = AB).

4.  **Properties of Equilateral Triangles (समबाहु त्रिभुज के गुण)**
    *   Sabhi sides equal hoti hain.
    *   Sabhi angles 60° ke hote hain.

**2. Congruence Rules Samajhna (सर्वांगसमता नियम समझना):**

*   **SAS (भुजा-कोण-भुजा):** Agar ek triangle ki do sides aur unke *beech* ka angle, doosre triangle ki corresponding do sides aur unke *beech* ke angle ke barabar hai, toh triangles congruent honge. Angle ka sides ke beech mein hona (included) bahut zaroori hai.
    *   *Example (उदाहरण)*: Jaise Example 1 (Fig 7.8) mein, OA=OB, OD=OC (Sides) aur ∠AOD = ∠BOC (Included Angle, vertically opposite). Isliye, ∆AOD ≅ ∆BOC by SAS rule.
    `[Diagram showing two triangles with two sides and the included angle marked equal]`

*   **ASA (कोण-भुजा-कोण):** Agar ek triangle ke do angles aur unke *beech* ki side, doosre triangle ke corresponding do angles aur unke *beech* ki side ke barabar hai, toh triangles congruent honge. Side ka angles ke beech mein hona (included) zaroori hai.
    *   *Example*: Agar ∆ABC aur ∆DEF mein, ∠B = ∠E, ∠C = ∠F aur side BC = EF (included side), toh ∆ABC ≅ ∆DEF by ASA rule.
    `[Diagram showing two triangles with two angles and the included side marked equal]`

*   **AAS (कोण-कोण-भुजा):** Agar ek triangle ke koi bhi do angles aur ek corresponding side (jo included na ho), doosre triangle ke corresponding angles aur side ke barabar hai, toh bhi triangles congruent honge. Aisa isliye kyunki agar do angles equal hain, toh teesra angle bhi automatically equal hoga (angle sum property).
    *   *Example (उदाहरण)*: Jaise Example 3 (Fig 7.15) mein, ∆AOB aur ∆DOC mein, ∠ABO = ∠DCO (Alternate angles), ∠AOB = ∠DOC (Vertically opposite angles), aur OA = OD (Given side, not included between these angles). Isliye, ∆AOB ≅ ∆DOC by AAS rule.
    `[Diagram showing two triangles with two angles and a non-included side marked equal]`

*   **SSS (भुजा-भुजा-भुजा):** Agar ek triangle ki teeno sides, doosre triangle ki corresponding teeno sides ke barabar hain, toh triangles congruent honge.
    *   *Example (उदाहरण)*: Jaise Example 7 (Fig 7.37) mein, ∆PAQ aur ∆PBQ mein, PA=PB, QA=QB, aur PQ=PQ (common). Isliye, ∆PAQ ≅ ∆PBQ by SSS rule.
    `[Diagram showing two triangles with all three corresponding sides marked equal]`

*   **RHS (समकोण-कर्ण-भुजा):** Yeh rule sirf right-angled triangles (समकोण त्रिभुज) ke liye hai. Agar do right triangles mein, hypotenuse (कर्ण) aur koi bhi ek corresponding side equal hai, toh triangles congruent honge.
    *   *Example (उदाहरण)*: Jaise Example 8 (Fig 7.38) mein, ∆PAB aur ∆PAC mein, ∠PBA = ∠PCA = 90°, Hypotenuse PA common hai, aur side PB = PC (given). Isliye, ∆PAB ≅ ∆PAC by RHS rule.
    `[Diagram showing two right-angled triangles with hypotenuse and one side marked equal]`

**3. Isosceles Triangle Properties (समद्विबाहु त्रिभुज के गुण):**
Ek triangle jisme do sides equal ho (jaise AB = AC), usse isosceles kehte hain.
*   **Equal Sides ke Opposite Angles Equal:** Jo sides equal hain (AB aur AC), unke saamne wale angles (∠C aur ∠B) bhi equal honge. (Theorem 7.2)
    `[Diagram of an isosceles triangle ABC with AB=AC, showing ∠B = ∠C]`
*   **Equal Angles ke Opposite Sides Equal:** Agar kisi triangle mein do angles equal hain (jaise ∠B = ∠C), toh unke saamne wali sides (AC aur AB) bhi equal hongi. Yeh Theorem 7.2 ka ulta (converse) hai. (Theorem 7.3)
    `[Diagram of a triangle ABC with ∠B = ∠C, showing AB = AC]`

**4. CPCT ka Use:**
Jab hum prove kar dete hain ki do triangles congruent hain (kisi bhi rule se - SAS, ASA, AAS, SSS, RHS), tab hum CPCT ka use karke unke baaki corresponding parts ko bhi equal bata sakte hain. Jaise agar ∆ABC ≅ ∆PQR, toh hum keh sakte hain ki AC = PR, ∠A = ∠P, etc. (by CPCT). Yeh proofs mein bahut kaam aata hai.

Jaise Taj Mahal ki minarets ya Jaipur ke Hawa Mahal ki windows. In patterns mein aksar congruent shapes ka istemal hota hai jo structure ko sundarta aur balance dete hain. Rangoli patterns mein bhi aksar congruent designs banaye jaate hain.
