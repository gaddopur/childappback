# [Class 9] General - Chapter 109 (Circles)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter *Circles* (Vritt) aur unse judi properties ke baare mein hai. Neeche diye gaye concepts important hain:

1.  **Circle Basics (Vritt ki Mool Baatein)**
    *   Circle (Vritt): Ek plane mein fixed point (centre) se equal distance par sabhi points ka collection.
    *   Radius (Trijya): Centre se circle par kisi bhi point tak ka distance.
    *   Chord (Jeeva): Circle par do points ko join karne wala line segment.
    *   Diameter (Vyas): Centre se pass hone wali chord (sabse lambi chord, radius ka double).
    *   Arc (Chaap): Circle ka ek hissa (minor arc - chhota, major arc - bada).
    *   Segment (Vritt Khand): Chord aur uske corresponding arc ke beech ka region (minor segment, major segment).
    *   Sector (Trijya Khand): Do radii aur unke beech ke arc se bana region.
    *   Centre (Kendra): Vritt ka fixed central point.

2.  **Angles Subtended (Banaaye Gaye Kon)**
    *   Angle subtended by a Chord/Arc at the Centre: Chord/Arc ke endpoints ko centre se join karne par bana angle (∠POQ).
    *   Angle subtended by a Chord/Arc at a Point on the Circle: Chord/Arc ke endpoints ko circle ke kisi aur point se join karne par bana angle (∠PRQ).

3.  **Properties of Chords (Jeeva ke Gun)**
    *   **Theorem 9.1:** Equal chords circle ke centre par equal angles banati hain. (Barabar jeevayein kendra par barabar kon banati hain).
    *   **Theorem 9.2 (Converse of 9.1):** Agar chords centre par equal angles banati hain, toh chords equal hoti hain. (Agar jeevayein kendra par barabar kon banayein, toh ve barabar hoti hain).
    *   **Theorem 9.3:** Centre se chord par daala gaya perpendicular chord ko bisect karta hai (do barabar hisson mein baant'ta hai). (Kendra se jeeva par dala gaya lamb use samadvibhajit karta hai).
    *   **Theorem 9.4 (Converse of 9.3):** Centre se chord ke midpoint ko join karne wali line chord par perpendicular hoti hai. (Kendra ko jeeva ke madhya bindu se milane wali rekha jeeva par lamb hoti hai).
    *   **Theorem 9.5:** Equal chords centre se equidistant (barabar doori par) hoti hain. (Barabar jeevayein kendra se samdoorasth hoti hain).
    *   **Theorem 9.6 (Converse of 9.5):** Jo chords centre se equidistant hoti hain, woh length mein equal hoti hain. (Jo jeevayein kendra se samdoorasth hoti hain, ve lambai mein barabar hoti hain).

4.  **Properties of Arcs (Chaap ke Gun)**
    *   Congruent arcs (Sarvangsam chaap) equal chords banati hain and vice-versa.
    *   Congruent arcs centre par equal angles banati hain.
    *   **Theorem 9.7:** Ek arc dwara centre par banaya gaya angle, circle ke remaining part ke kisi bhi point par banaye gaye angle ka double hota hai. (Kisi chaap dwara kendra par banaya gaya kon, vritt ke shesh bhaag ke kisi bindu par banaye gaye kon ka duguna hota hai).
    *   **Theorem 9.8:** Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal. (Ek hi vritt khand ke kon barabar hote hain).
    *   Angle in a semicircle is a right angle (90°). (Ardhavritt mein bana kon samkon hota hai).
    *   **Theorem 9.9 (Converse of 9.8):** Agar ek line segment do points ko join karta hai aur woh line ke same side par sthit do aur points par equal angles banata hai, toh chaaron points ek circle par hote hain (concyclic).

5.  **Cyclic Quadrilaterals (Chakriya Chaturbhuj)**
    *   Definition: Ek quadrilateral jiske chaaron vertices circle par hote hain.
    *   **Theorem 9.10:** Cyclic quadrilateral ke opposite angles ka sum 180° hota hai. (Chakriya chaturbhuj ke sammukh kono ka yog 180° hota hai).
    *   **Theorem 9.11 (Converse of 9.10):** Agar kisi quadrilateral ke opposite angles ke pair ka sum 180° ho, toh woh cyclic hota hai.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

Yahan hum kuch important theorems ko detail mein samjhenge:

1.  **Equal Chords & Angles at Centre (Theorem 9.1 & 9.2):**
    *   Agar do chords AB aur CD equal hain (AB = CD), toh centre O par unke dwara banaye gaye angles bhi equal honge (∠AOB = ∠COD). [Diagram: Circle with centre O, equal chords AB and CD, showing triangles AOB and COD].
    *   Iska ulta bhi sach hai: Agar ∠AOB = ∠COD, toh AB = CD. Yeh prove karne ke liye hum triangles AOB aur COD ko SSS (Th 9.1) ya SAS (Th 9.2, using OA=OC, OB=OD, ∠AOB=∠COD) congruence se congruent dikha sakte hain.

2.  **Perpendicular from Centre to Chord (Theorem 9.3 & 9.4):**
    *   Agar centre O se chord AB par perpendicular OM daala jaaye, toh M chord AB ka midpoint hoga (AM = MB). [Diagram: Circle with centre O, chord AB, perpendicular OM from O to AB].
    *   Iska ulta: Agar M chord AB ka midpoint hai, toh OM line AB par perpendicular hogi (OM ⊥ AB). Yeh prove karne ke liye triangles OAM aur OBM ko SSS congruence (OA=OB radii, AM=BM given, OM=OM common) se congruent dikha kar ∠OMA = ∠OMB = 90° la sakte hain.

3.  **Equal Chords & Distance from Centre (Theorem 9.5 & 9.6):**
    *   Agar do chords AB aur CD equal hain, toh unki centre O se perpendicular distance (OM aur ON) bhi equal hogi (OM = ON). [Diagram: Circle with centre O, equal chords AB and CD, perpendiculars OM and ON from O to AB and CD].
    *   Iska ulta: Agar chords centre se equidistant hain (OM = ON), toh chords equal hongi (AB = CD).

4.  **Angle at Centre vs Angle at Circumference (Theorem 9.7):**
    *   Arc PQ dwara centre O par banaya gaya angle (∠POQ), circle ke remaining part par kisi point A par banaye gaye angle (∠PAQ) ka double hota hai. Yani, ∠POQ = 2 * ∠PAQ. [Diagram: Circle with centre O, arc PQ, point A on the major arc. Show angles POQ and PAQ]. Yeh minor arc, major arc aur semicircle teeno cases mein true hai.

5.  **Angles in the Same Segment (Theorem 9.8):**
    *   Ek hi segment (minor ya major) mein banne wale sabhi angles equal hote hain. Agar A aur C ek hi segment mein hain jo chord PQ se bana hai, toh ∠PAQ = ∠PCQ. [Diagram: Circle with chord PQ, points A and C on the major arc, showing angles PAQ and PCQ].
    *   Special Case: Semicircle mein bana angle hamesha 90° hota hai, kyunki centre par angle 180° hota hai (diameter banata hai), toh circumference par uska आधा (half) 90° hoga.

6.  **Cyclic Quadrilaterals (Theorem 9.10 & 9.11):**
    *   Agar ABCD ek cyclic quadrilateral hai, toh opposite angles supplementary hote hain: ∠A + ∠C = 180° aur ∠B + ∠D = 180°. [Diagram: Circle with inscribed quadrilateral ABCD].
    *   Iska ulta: Agar kisi quadrilateral ABCD mein ∠A + ∠C = 180° ya ∠B + ∠D = 180° hai, toh woh quadrilateral cyclic hoga.

    *   **Ashoka Chakra:** Humare national flag ke centre mein Ashoka Chakra hai, jisme 24 spokes hain jo centre par equal angles (360°/24 = 15° each) banati hain. Yeh Theorem 9.2 ka example hai (equal angles -> implies equal spacing/arcs between spokes).
    *   **Temple Architecture:** Kai ancient Indian temples ka plan circular ya concentric circles par based hota hai, jo cosmic harmony ko represent karta hai.
    *   **Kolam/Rangoli:** South India mein banaye jaane wale Kolam patterns aksar dots (points) ke grid par based hote hain aur unmein circles aur arcs ka sundar geometric use hota hai.
