# [Class 9] Maths - Chapter 9: Circles (Vritt)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

1.  **Circle (Vritt) ki Basic Terms:**
    *   **Circle:** Ek plane mein fixed point (kendra/centre) se equal distance par sthit sabhi points ka collection.
    *   **Radius (Trijya):** Centre se circle par kisi bhi point tak ka distance.
    *   **Chord (Jeeva):** Circle par do points ko join karne wala line segment.
    *   **Diameter (Vyas):** Centre se pass hone wali chord (sabse lambi chord, radius ka double).
    *   **Arc (Chaap):** Circle ka ek continuous part (Minor arc - chhota hissa, Major arc - bada hissa).
    *   **Segment (Vritt Khand):** Chord aur uske corresponding arc ke beech ka region.
    *   **Sector (Trijya Khand):** Do radii aur unke beech ke arc se ghira region.

2.  **Chord Properties (Jeeva ke Gun):**
    *   **Angle Subtended by Chord:** Ek chord circle ke points par angle banati hai.
        *   Angle at Centre (Kendra par kon)
        *   Angle on remaining part of circle (Vritt ke shesh bhaag par kon)
    *   **Equal Chords & Angles:** Barabar jeevayein centre par barabar kon banati hain (Theorem 9.1) aur iska converse (Theorem 9.2).
    *   **Perpendicular from Centre:** Centre se chord par dala gaya perpendicular chord ko bisect karta hai (samdvibhajit karta hai) (Theorem 9.3) aur iska converse (Theorem 9.4).
    *   **Equal Chords & Distance from Centre:** Barabar jeevayein centre se equidistant (samdoorasth) hoti hain (Theorem 9.5) aur iska converse (Theorem 9.6).

3.  **Arc Properties (Chaap ke Gun):**
    *   **Congruent Arcs:** Agar do arcs congruent hain, toh unki corresponding chords barabar hongi, aur agar chords barabar hain, toh corresponding arcs congruent hongi.
    *   **Angle Subtended by Arc:**
        *   Congruent arcs centre par equal angles banati hain.
        *   Centre par bana angle, circle ke remaining part par bane angle ka double hota hai (Theorem 9.7).
    *   **Angles in Same Segment:** Ek hi vritt khand mein bane angles barabar hote hain (Theorem 9.8).
    *   **Angle in a Semicircle:** Ardhavritt mein bana kon hamesha right angle (90°) hota hai.

4.  **Cyclic Quadrilaterals (Chakriya Chaturbhuj):**
    *   **Definition:** Aisa quadrilateral jiske chaaron vertices (sheersh) circle par sthit hon.
    *   **Property:** Opposite angles ka sum 180° hota hai (Theorem 9.10).
    *   **Converse:** Agar kisi quadrilateral ke opposite angles ka sum 180° hai, toh woh cyclic hota hai (Theorem 9.11).
    *   **Concyclic Points:** Agar ek line segment uske ek hi taraf sthit do points par equal angles banata hai, toh chaaron points concyclic hote hain (Theorem 9.9).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

**1. Jeeva dwara Kendra par aur Vritt par bane Kon (Angle Subtended by Chord at Centre and on Circle):**

*   Ek chord PQ, centre O par ∠POQ banati hai. Vritt ke major arc par R point par ∠PRQ aur minor arc par S point par ∠PSQ banati hai. (Dekho Fig. 9.2)
*   **Theorem 9.1:** *Equal chords of a circle subtend equal angles at the centre.* (Barabar jeevayein kendra par barabar kon banati hain).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar AB = CD, toh ∆AOB aur ∆COD mein OA=OC, OB=OD (radii), AB=CD (given). SSS rule se triangles congruent (sarvangsam), isliye ∠AOB = ∠COD (CPCT). (Dekho Fig. 9.4)
*   **Theorem 9.2 (Converse of 9.1):** *If the angles subtended by the chords of a circle at the centre are equal, then the chords are equal.* (Agar jeevaon dwara kendra par bane kon barabar hain, toh jeevayein barabar hoti hain).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar ∠AOB = ∠COD, toh ∆AOB aur ∆COD mein OA=OC, OB=OD (radii), ∠AOB = ∠COD (given). SAS rule se triangles congruent, isliye AB = CD (CPCT).

**2. Kendra se Jeeva par Lamb (Perpendicular from Centre to Chord):**

*   **Theorem 9.3:** *The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord.* (Kendra se jeeva par dala gaya lamb jeeva ko samdvibhajit karta hai).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar OM ⊥ AB, toh ∆OAM aur ∆OBM mein OA=OB (radii), OM=OM (common), ∠OMA = ∠OMB = 90°. RHS rule se triangles congruent, isliye AM = BM (CPCT). (Dekho Fig. 9.6 & 9.7 for converse proof structure)
*   **Theorem 9.4 (Converse of 9.3):** *The line drawn through the centre of a circle to bisect a chord is perpendicular to the chord.* (Kendra se jeeva ko samdvibhajit karne ke liye kheenchi gayi rekha jeeva par lamb hoti hai).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar AM = BM, toh ∆OAM aur ∆OBM mein OA=OB (radii), AM=BM (given), OM=OM (common). SSS rule se triangles congruent, isliye ∠OMA = ∠OMB. Choonki ∠OMA + ∠OMB = 180° (linear pair), isliye ∠OMA = ∠OMB = 90°.

**3. Barabar Jeevayein aur Kendra se Unki Doori (Equal Chords and their Distances from the Centre):**

*   **Distance Definition:** Ek point se line ki doori us point se line par dale gaye perpendicular ki length hoti hai. (Dekho Fig. 9.8)
*   **Theorem 9.5:** *Equal chords of a circle (or of congruent circles) are equidistant from the centre (or centres).* (Barabar jeevayein kendra se samdoorasth hoti hain).
    *   *Activity Idea:* Equal chords AB, CD banao. Perpendiculars OM, ON dalo. Paper fold karke dekho, OM = ON milega. (Dekho Fig. 9.9)
*   **Theorem 9.6 (Converse of 9.5):** *Chords equidistant from the centre of a circle are equal in length.* (Kendra se samdoorasth jeevayein lambai mein barabar hoti hain).
    *   *Activity Idea:* Centre O se equal length ke perpendiculars OL, OM banao. Inke endpoints par perpendicular chords PQ, RS banao. Measure karne par PQ = RS milega. (Dekho Fig. 9.10)

**4. Chaap dwara Banaye gaye Kon (Angle Subtended by an Arc):**

*   **Congruent Arcs & Chords:** Equal chords ke corresponding arcs congruent hote hain, aur congruent arcs ki corresponding chords equal hoti hain. (Dekho Fig. 9.13)
*   **Congruent Arcs & Angle at Centre:** Congruent arcs kendra par barabar kon banate hain.
*   **Theorem 9.7:** *The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle.* (Kisi chaap dwara kendra par banaya gaya kon, vritt ke shesh bhaag ke kisi bhi bindu par banaye gaye kon ka duguna hota hai).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Point A ko centre O se join karke extend karo. Exterior angle property use karke ∆OAQ mein ∠BOQ = 2∠OAQ aur ∆OAP mein ∠BOP = 2∠OAP. Add karne par ∠POQ = 2∠PAQ milta hai. (Teeno cases Fig. 9.15 mein dekho).
*   **Theorem 9.8:** *Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.* (Ek hi vritt khand ke kon barabar hote hain).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Theorem 9.7 se, ∠POQ = 2∠PAQ aur ∠POQ = 2∠PCQ. Isliye 2∠PAQ = 2∠PCQ, yaani ∠PAQ = ∠PCQ. (Dekho Fig. 9.16)
*   **Angle in a Semicircle:** *Angle in a semicircle is a right angle.* (Ardhavritt ka kon samkon hota hai). Yeh Theorem 9.7 ka special case hai jab arc ek semicircle ho (centre par angle 180°).

**5. Concyclic Points & Cyclic Quadrilaterals (Samchakriya Bindu aur Chakriya Chaturbhuj):**

*   **Theorem 9.9:** *If a line segment joining two points subtends equal angles at two other points lying on the same side of the line containing the line segment, the four points lie on a circle (i.e. they are concyclic).* (Yadi ek rekha khand do binduon ko milane par, us rekha ke ek hi taraf sthit do anya binduon par saman kon banaye, toh chaaron bindu ek vritt par sthit hote hain). (Dekho Fig. 9.17)
*   **Cyclic Quadrilateral:** Jiske sabhi vertices circle par hon. (Dekho Fig. 9.18)
*   **Theorem 9.10:** *The sum of either pair of opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral is 180º.* (Chakriya chaturbhuj ke sammukh konon ke kisi bhi yugm ka yog 180° hota hai).
*   **Theorem 9.11 (Converse of 9.10):** *If the sum of a pair of opposite angles of a quadrilateral is 180º, the quadrilateral is cyclic.* (Yadi kisi chaturbhuj ke sammukh konon ke ek yugm ka yog 180° ho, toh chaturbhuj chakriya hota hai).
