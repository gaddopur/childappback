# [Class 9] General - Chapter 9: Circles (Vritt)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter circles (vritt) aur unse judi properties ke baare mein hai. Hum explore karenge chords (jeevayein), arcs (chaap), angles jo woh banate hain, aur special quadrilaterals jo circle ke andar fit hote hain.

1.  **Circle Basics (Vritt ki Mool Baatein)**
    *   Definition: Ek plane mein fixed point (centre/kendr) se equal distance par sabhi points ka collection.
    *   Parts: Radius (trijya), Diameter (vyas), Chord (jeeva), Arc (chaap), Segment (vritt khand), Sector (trijyakhand).

2.  **Chords aur Centre se Unka Relation**
    *   **Angle Subtended (Kendr par Bana Kon):**
        *   Equal chords centre par equal angles banati hain (Theorem 9.1). ↔️ Agar angles equal hain, toh chords bhi equal hongi (Theorem 9.2 - Converse).
    *   **Perpendicular from Centre (Kendr se Jeeva par Lamb):**
        *   Centre se chord par perpendicular, chord ko bisect karta hai (Theorem 9.3). ↔️ Centre se chord ke midpoint ko join karne wali line chord par perpendicular hoti hai (Theorem 9.4 - Converse).
    *   **Distance from Centre (Kendr se Doori):**
        *   Equal chords centre se equidistant (barabar doori par) hoti hain (Theorem 9.5). ↔️ Agar chords equidistant hain, toh woh equal length ki hongi (Theorem 9.6 - Converse).

3.  **Arcs aur Unke Angles**
    *   **Congruent Arcs (Sarvangsam Chaap):** Equal chords congruent corresponding arcs banati hain, aur vice-versa. Congruent arcs centre par equal angles banate hain.
    *   **Angle Subtended by Arc (Chaap dwara Antarit Kon):**
        *   Centre par bana angle, circle ke remaining part ke kisi bhi point par bane angle ka double hota hai (Theorem 9.7). (Angle at Centre = 2 × Angle at Circumference).
        *   **Angles in the Same Segment (Ek hi Vritt Khand ke Kon):** Ek hi segment mein bane angles equal hote hain (Theorem 9.8).
        *   **Angle in a Semicircle (Ardhvritt ka Kon):** Semicircle mein bana angle hamesha right angle (90°) hota hai.
    *   **Concyclic Points (Ekvrittiya Bindu):** Agar ek line segment joining two points, us line ke same side par lie karne wale do aur points par equal angles banata hai, toh chaaron points concyclic hote hain (Theorem 9.9).

4.  **Cyclic Quadrilaterals (Chakriya Chaturbhuj)**
    *   Definition: Aisa quadrilateral jiske chaaron vertices circle par lie karte hain.
    *   **Property:** Opposite angles ka sum 180° hota hai (Theorem 9.10). ↔️ Agar kisi quadrilateral ke opposite angles ka sum 180° hai, toh woh cyclic hota hai (Theorem 9.11 - Converse).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

Yahan hum kuch important theorems ko detail mein samjhenge:

1.  **Theorem 9.1: Equal chords of a circle subtend equal angles at the centre.**
    *   *Samjho Kyun:* Agar do chords AB aur CD equal hain (AB = CD) ek circle mein jiska centre O hai. Toh triangles ∆AOB aur ∆COD consider karo.
        *   OA = OC (Radii)
        *   OB = OD (Radii)
        *   AB = CD (Given)
        *   Isliye, SSS congruence rule se, ∆AOB ≅ ∆COD.
        *   Toh CPCT (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles) se, ∠AOB = ∠COD. Matlab equal chords ne centre par equal angles banaye! ✅
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek circle banao, usmein do equal chords AB aur CD mark karo. Centre O se A, B, C, D ko join karke triangles dikhao.

2.  **Theorem 9.3: The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord.**
    *   *Samjho Kyun:* Ek circle mein centre O se chord AB par perpendicular OM draw karo (∠OMA = 90°). Ab ∆OAM aur ∆OBM dekho.
        *   OA = OB (Radii)
        *   OM = OM (Common side)
        *   ∠OMA = ∠OMB = 90° (Given)
        *   Isliye, RHS congruence rule se, ∆OAM ≅ ∆OBM.
        *   Toh CPCT se, AM = BM. Matlab perpendicular ne chord ko bisect kar diya! ✅
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Circle mein chord AB banao, centre O se perpendicular OM draw karo jo AB ko M par mile. Radii OA aur OB join karke right-angled triangles dikhao.

3.  **Theorem 9.7: The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle.**
    *   *Samjho Kyun:* Maan lo arc PQ centre O par ∠POQ aur remaining part ke point A par ∠PAQ banata hai. AO ko join karke B tak extend karo.
        *   Triangle ∆OAQ mein, exterior angle ∠BOQ = ∠OAQ + ∠AQO.
        *   Also, OA = OQ (Radii), toh ∠OAQ = ∠OQA.
        *   Isliye, ∠BOQ = 2∠OAQ. (1)
        *   Similarly, ∠BOP = 2∠OAP. (2)
        *   Adding (1) and (2) (for minor arc case): ∠BOP + ∠BOQ = 2(∠OAP + ∠OAQ) => ∠POQ = 2∠PAQ. ✅
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Fig 9.15 ke teeno cases (minor arc, semicircle, major arc) ko dhyan mein rakho. Point A ko arc PQ ke bahar circle par kahin bhi le sakte hain.

4.  **Theorem 9.10: The sum of either pair of opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral is 180°.**
    *   *Samjho Kyun (Intuitive Idea):* Ek cyclic quadrilateral ABCD imagine karo. Arc BCD centre par jo angle banata hai (reflex ∠BOD), woh ∠BAD ka double hota hai. Aur arc DAB centre par jo angle banata hai (∠BOD), woh ∠BCD ka double hota hai.
        *   Reflex ∠BOD = 2∠BAD
        *   ∠BOD = 2∠BCD
        *   In dono angles ka sum 360° hai (complete angle at centre).
        *   Toh, 2∠BAD + 2∠BCD = 360° => ∠BAD + ∠BCD = 180°. Similarly for the other pair. ✅
    *   *Diagram Idea:* Ek circle banao, us par 4 points A, B, C, D mark karke cyclic quadrilateral banao. Opposite angles ko highlight karo.
