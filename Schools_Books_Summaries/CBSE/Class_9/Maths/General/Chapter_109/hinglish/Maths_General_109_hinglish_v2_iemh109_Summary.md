# [Class 9] General - Chapter 109 (Circles)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter **Circles (Vritt)** aur unse judi properties ke baare mein hai. Neeche diye gaye concepts is chapter ka base hain:

1.  **Circle Basics (Vritt ki Mool Baatein)**
    *   Circle (Vritt): Ek plane mein fixed point (centre/kendra) se equal distance (radius/trijya) par sabhi points ka collection.
    *   Chord (Jeeva): Circle par kisi bhi do points ko join karne wala line segment.
    *   Diameter (Vyas): Centre se pass hone wali chord (sabse lambi chord).
    *   Arc (Chaap): Circle ka ek continuous part (Minor Arc - laghu chaap, Major Arc - deergh chaap).
    *   Segment (Vritt Khand): Chord aur uske corresponding arc ke beech ka region (Minor Segment, Major Segment).
    *   Sector (Trijya Khand): Do radii aur unke corresponding arc ke beech ka region.

2.  **Angles Subtended (Banaaye Gaye Kon)**
    *   Angle subtended by a Chord/Arc at the Centre (Jeeva/Chaap dwara Kendra par banaya gaya kon).
    *   Angle subtended by a Chord/Arc at any point on the Circle (Jeeva/Chaap dwara Vritt par kisi bindu par banaya gaya kon).

3.  **Chord Properties (Jeeva ke Gun)**
    *   Relation between Chord length and Angle at Centre (Jeeva ki lambai aur Kendra par kon ka sambandh).
    *   Perpendicular from Centre to Chord (Kendra se Jeeva par lamb).
    *   Distance of Chords from Centre (Kendra se Jeevaon ki doori).

4.  **Arc Properties (Chaap ke Gun)**
    *   Relation between Arc length and Angle at Centre (Chaap ki lambai aur Kendra par kon ka sambandh).
    *   Congruent Arcs (Sarvangsam Chaap).

5.  **Angles in Segments (Vritt Khand mein Kon)**
    *   Angles in the same segment (Ek hi Vritt Khand ke kon).
    *   Angle in a Semicircle (Ardhvritt mein kon).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

Is section mein hum important theorems aur unke proofs ko samjhenge. Diagrams se concepts aur clear honge.

**1. Chord aur Centre Angle ka Rishta (Theorems 9.1 & 9.2)**

*   **Theorem 9.1:** Ek circle ki **equal chords (barabar jeevayein)** centre par **equal angles (barabar kon)** banati hain.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar AB aur CD do equal chords hain, toh triangles AOB aur COD mein OA=OC (radii), OB=OD (radii), aur AB=CD (given). SSS congruence se, ∆AOB ≅ ∆COD. Isliye, ∠AOB = ∠COD (CPCT).
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.4 jaisa. Do equal chords AB aur CD hain, centre O hai. Angles ∠AOB aur ∠COD barabar honge.

*   **Theorem 9.2 (Converse of 9.1):** Agar ek circle ki chords centre par **equal angles** banati hain, toh woh **chords equal (barabar)** hoti hain.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar ∠AOB = ∠COD hai, toh triangles AOB aur COD mein OA=OC (radii), OB=OD (radii), aur ∠AOB = ∠COD (given). SAS congruence se, ∆AOB ≅ ∆COD. Isliye, AB = CD (CPCT).
    *   *Diagram:* Fig 9.4 use kar sakte hain.

**2. Centre se Chord par Perpendicular (Theorems 9.3 & 9.4)**

*   **Theorem 9.3:** Circle ke **centre se chord par daala gaya perpendicular (lamb)** chord ko **bisect (samdvibhajit)** karta hai.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar OM ⊥ AB hai, toh right triangles OMA aur OMB mein OA=OB (radii), OM=OM (common), ∠OMA = ∠OMB = 90°. RHS congruence se, ∆OMA ≅ ∆OMB. Isliye, AM = BM (CPCT).
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.6 jaisa. OM ⊥ AB hai, toh M, AB ka midpoint hoga.

*   **Theorem 9.4 (Converse of 9.3):** Circle ke **centre ko chord ke midpoint se join karne wali line** chord par **perpendicular (lamb)** hoti hai.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar M, AB ka midpoint hai, toh triangles OAM aur OBM mein OA=OB (radii), AM=BM (given), OM=OM (common). SSS congruence se, ∆OAM ≅ ∆OBM. Isliye, ∠OMA = ∠OMB (CPCT). Since ∠OMA + ∠OMB = 180° (linear pair), toh ∠OMA = ∠OMB = 90°.
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.7 jaisa. M agar AB ka midpoint hai, toh OM ⊥ AB hoga.

**3. Equal Chords aur Centre se Doori (Theorems 9.5 & 9.6)**

*   **Theorem 9.5:** Ek circle ki **equal chords (barabar jeevayein)** centre se **equidistant (samdoorasth)** hoti hain. (Congruent circles ke liye bhi true hai).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar AB = CD, aur OM ⊥ AB, ON ⊥ CD. Toh AM = CN (kyunki perpendicular bisects chord, aur equal chords ke halves equal hote hain). Right triangles OAM aur OCN mein OA=OC (radii), AM=CN. RHS congruence se, ∆OAM ≅ ∆OCN. Isliye, OM = ON (CPCT).
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.9 jaisa. Agar AB=CD, toh OM=ON hoga.

*   **Theorem 9.6 (Converse of 9.5):** Ek circle mein jo chords centre se **equidistant (samdoorasth)** hoti hain, woh **length mein equal (barabar)** hoti hain.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Agar OM = ON (distances), OM ⊥ AB, ON ⊥ CD. Right triangles OAM aur OCN mein OA=OC (radii), OM=ON (given). RHS congruence se, ∆OAM ≅ ∆OCN. Isliye, AM = CN (CPCT). Since perpendicular bisects the chord, AB = 2AM aur CD = 2CN. Toh AB = CD.
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.10 jaisa. Agar OM=ON, toh PQ=RS hoga.

**4. Arc aur Angles ka Rishta (Theorems 9.7, 9.8, 9.9)**

*   **Congruent Arcs:** Agar do chords equal hain, toh unke corresponding arcs (minor aur major dono) congruent hote hain. Aur agar do arcs congruent hain, toh unki corresponding chords equal hoti hain. Congruent arcs centre par equal angles banate hain.

*   **Theorem 9.7:** Ek arc dwara **centre par banaya gaya angle (kon)**, circle ke **remaining part ke kisi bhi point par banaye gaye angle ka double (duguna)** hota hai.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Point A ko centre O se join karke extend karein. Exterior angle property use karke prove kiya ja sakta hai ki ∠POQ = 2∠PAQ. (Teen cases consider karein: minor arc, semicircle, major arc).
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.15 ke teeno cases.

*   **Theorem 9.8:** **Angles in the same segment (ek hi vritt khand ke kon)** of a circle are **equal (barabar)**.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Yeh Theorem 9.7 ka direct result hai. Agar A aur C same segment mein do points hain, toh ∠POQ = 2∠PAQ aur ∠POQ = 2∠PCQ. Isliye, 2∠PAQ = 2∠PCQ, yaani ∠PAQ = ∠PCQ.
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.16 jaisa. ∠PAQ = ∠PCQ.

*   **Angle in a Semicircle:** Semicircle (ardhvritt) mein bana angle **right angle (90°)** hota hai.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Yeh Theorem 9.7 ka special case hai jab arc ek semicircle ho. Centre par angle 180° hota hai, toh circle par angle 180°/2 = 90° hoga.

*   **Theorem 9.9 (Converse of 9.8):** Agar ek line segment joining two points, us line ke same side par lie karne wale do other points par equal angles banata hai, toh **charon points ek circle par lie karte hain (concyclic hote hain)**.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Contradiction method se prove karte hain. Maan lo D circle par nahi hai. Circle AD ko E par intersect karta hai. Toh ∠ACB = ∠AEB (angles in same segment). Lekin given hai ∠ACB = ∠ADB. Isliye ∠AEB = ∠ADB, jo possible nahi unless D aur E coincide karein.
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.17 jaisa. Agar ∠ACB = ∠ADB, toh A, B, C, D concyclic hain.

**5. Cyclic Quadrilaterals (Chakriya Chaturbhuj) (Theorems 9.10 & 9.11)**

*   **Definition:** Aisa quadrilateral jiske **charon vertices (sheersh)** ek circle par lie karte hain.
    *   *Diagram Dekhein:* Fig 9.18.

*   **Theorem 9.10:** Ek **cyclic quadrilateral ke opposite angles ka sum 180°** hota hai.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Consider karo arc BCD. Centre par angle (reflex ∠BOD) = 2∠BAD. Consider karo arc DAB. Centre par angle (∠BOD) = 2∠BCD. Dono angles ka sum 360° hai. Toh 2∠BAD + 2∠BCD = 360°, yaani ∠BAD + ∠BCD = 180°. Similarly for other pair.

*   **Theorem 9.11 (Converse of 9.10):** Agar kisi quadrilateral ke **opposite angles ke ek pair ka sum 180°** hai, toh woh quadrilateral **cyclic** hota hai.
    *   *Proof Idea:* Theorem 9.9 ki tarah contradiction se prove kar sakte hain.
