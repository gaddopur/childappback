## 📘 Key Learnings (मुख्य सीख)

**1. Number Types Ka Parichay (संख्या प्रकारों का परिचय)**

*   **Natural Numbers (N):** Counting numbers: 1, 2, 3,... Inn numbers ka collection N se denote hota hai. Yeh infinitely many hote hain.
*   **Whole Numbers (W):** Natural numbers mein zero (0) add karne par milte hain: 0, 1, 2, 3,... Collection W se denote hota hai. *Kya har natural number ek whole number hai? Haan. Kya har whole number ek natural number hai? Nahi, kyunki 0 whole hai par natural nahi.*
*   **Integers (Z):** Whole numbers aur negative numbers (-1, -2, -3,...) milakar bante hain: ..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2,... Collection Z se denote hota hai. *Kya har integer ek whole number hai? Nahi, kyunki negative integers whole numbers nahi hain.*
*   **Rational Numbers (Q):** Woh numbers jinhe *p/q* form mein likha ja sakta hai, jahan p aur q integers hain aur q ≠ 0. Examples: 1/2, -3/4, 5 (kyunki 5 = 5/1), 0 (kyunki 0 = 0/1). Collection Q se denote hota hai. 'Rational' 'ratio' se aaya hai. *Kya har integer ek rational number hai? Haan, kyunki har integer m ko m/1 likh sakte hain.* *Kya har rational number ek integer hai? Nahi, jaise 3/5 rational hai par integer nahi.*
    *   **Equivalent Rational Numbers:** Ek hi rational number ko alag-alag fractions se represent kar sakte hain, jaise 1/2 = 2/4 = 10/20. Standard form mein hum *p* aur *q* ko co-prime (जिनका common factor sirf 1 ho) lete hain.
    *   **Finding Rationals Between Two Numbers:** Do rational numbers *r* aur *s* ke beech mein ek rational number (r+s)/2 hota hai. Aur bhi methods hain, jaise denominator same karke beech ke numbers likhna. *Kinhin bhi do rational numbers ke beech infinitely many rational numbers hote hain.*

**2. Irrational Numbers (अपरिमेय संख्याएँ)**

*   Woh real numbers jo rational nahi hain, yaani jinhe *p/q* form mein nahi likha ja sakta.
*   Examples: √2, √3, π, 0.121121112...
*   Inka decimal expansion **non-terminating non-recurring** (अनवसानी अनावर्ती) hota hai. Matlab decimal ke baad digits na kabhi khatm hote hain, na hi repeat hote hain.
*   Pythagoras ke followers ne pehle discover kiya tha (around 400 BC). √2 pehla famous example tha.
*   π (Pi) bhi ek irrational number hai. Hum calculation ke liye often iski approximate value 22/7 ya 3.14 use karte hain, par π inke equal *nahi* hai. π ≈ 3.14159265...

**3. Real Numbers (वास्तविक संख्याएँ) (R)**

*   Sabhi rational aur irrational numbers milakar Real Numbers ka collection banate hain.
*   Har real number number line par ek unique point ko correspond karta hai, aur number line ka har point ek unique real number ko. Isliye number line ko **Real Number Line** kehte hain.

📈 **Number Line Representation (संख्या रेखा पर निरूपण)**

*   Rational numbers ko locate karna aasan hai.
*   Irrational numbers jaise √2, √3 ko locate karne ke liye Pythagoras Theorem ka use karte hain.
    *   √2: Ek square banayein jiski side 1 unit ho. Uska diagonal √2 hoga. Compass se yeh length number line par transfer karein (origin se).
    *   √3: √2 wali length par 1 unit ka perpendicular banayein. Naye triangle ka hypotenuse √3 hoga. Ise number line par transfer karein.
    *   √n: Isi tarah √n ko locate kar sakte hain agar √n-1 locate kar liya hai (Square Root Spiral activity).
    *   √x (for any positive real x): Geometrically construct karne ka method bhi hai (Fig 1.11, 1.12). AB = x units, BC = 1 unit. AC ka midpoint O lekar semicircle banayein. B par perpendicular draw karein jo semicircle ko D par mile. BD = √x.

**4. Decimal Expansions (दशमलव प्रसार)**

*   **Rational Numbers:**
    *   **Terminating (सांत):** Decimal expansion kuch steps ke baad end ho jata hai. Example: 1/4 = 0.25, 5/8 = 0.625. Yeh tab hota hai jab denominator *q* ke prime factors sirf 2 aur/ya 5 hote hain.
    *   **Non-terminating Recurring (अनवसानी आवर्ती):** Decimal expansion kabhi end nahi hota, par digits ka ek block repeat hota hai. Example: 1/3 = 0.333... (0.3̄), 2/11 = 0.181818... (0.18̄).
*   **Irrational Numbers:**
    *   **Non-terminating Non-recurring (अनवसानी अनावर्ती):** Decimal expansion na end hota hai, na repeat hota hai. Example: √2 = 1.41421356..., π = 3.14159265...

*   **Conversion:**
    *   Terminating decimal ko p/q mein badalna easy hai (e.g., 0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4).
    *   Non-terminating recurring decimal ko p/q mein badalne ke liye algebra use karte hain (Examples 7, 8, 9). Jaise x = 0.3̄, toh 10x = 3.3̄ = 3 + x, isse 9x = 3, ya x = 1/3.

**5. Operations on Real Numbers (वास्तविक संख्याओं पर संक्रियाएँ)**

*   Rational +,-,×,/ Rational (except division by 0) = Rational.
*   Rational +,- Irrational = Irrational.
*   (Non-zero Rational) ×,/ Irrational = Irrational.
*   Irrational +,-,×,/ Irrational = Result Rational bhi ho sakta hai, Irrational bhi. (e.g., √2 + (-√2) = 0 (Rational), √2 × √2 = 2 (Rational), √2 + √3 = Irrational, √2 × √3 = √6 (Irrational)).

*   **Identities for Positive Real Numbers a, b:**
    *   √(ab) = √a √b
    *   √(a/b) = √a / √b
    *   (√a + √b)(√a - √b) = a - b
    *   (a + √b)(a - √b) = a² - b
    *   (√a + √b)² = a + 2√(ab) + b

**6. Rationalizing the Denominator (हर का परिमेयकरण)**

*   Jab kisi expression ke denominator mein square root wala term ho (jaise 1/√2, 1/(2+√3)), toh use ek equivalent expression mein convert karna jiska denominator rational ho, rationalization kehlata hai.
*   Method: Suitable identity use karke numerator aur denominator ko multiply karna.
    *   1/√a ko rationalize karne ke liye √a/√a se multiply karein → √a/a.
    *   1/(a+√b) ko rationalize karne ke liye (a-√b)/(a-√b) se multiply karein → (a-√b)/(a²-b).
    *   1/(√a+√b) ko rationalize karne ke liye (√a-√b)/(√a-√b) se multiply karein → (√a-√b)/(a-b).

**7. Laws of Exponents (घातांक के नियम)**

*   Yeh laws positive real number base 'a' aur rational exponents 'p', 'q' ke liye bhi valid hain:
    *   aᵖ ⋅ a<0xC2><0xAA> = aᵖ⁺<0xC2><0xAA>
    *   (aᵖ)<0xC2><0xAA> = aᵖ<0xC2><0xAA>
    *   aᵖ / a<0xC2><0xAA> = aᵖ⁻<0xC2><0xAA>
    *   aᵖ ⋅ bᵖ = (ab)ᵖ
*   Definitions:
    *   a⁰ = 1
    *   a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ
    *   ⁿ√a = a¹ᐟⁿ
    *   aᵐᐟⁿ = (ⁿ√a)ᵐ = ⁿ√(aᵐ) (where m, n are integers, n>0, no common factors other than 1)

    *   **Sulbasutras (शुल्बसूत्र) (Vedic Period, 800 BC - 500 BC):** Inn ancient texts mein geometrical constructions ke rules they, aur √2 ka ek remarkable approximation diya gaya tha: 1 + 1/3 + 1/(3×4) - 1/(3×4×34) ≈ 1.4142156..., jo modern value ke kaafi kareeb hai. Yeh dikhata hai ki ancient India mein irrational numbers ki understanding thi.

    *   **Population (जनसंख्या):** Bharat ki jansankhya (approx 140 Crore ya 1.4 Billion) ek bada whole number hai. Population growth rate (%) aksar decimal mein express kiya jata hai (rational number).

    *   **Poverty Line (गरीबी रेखा):** Sarkaar poverty line define karti hai (income per month, e.g., ₹1059.42 for rural areas per capita per month as per Tendulkar committee recommendations adjusted for inflation). Yeh ek specific rational number (decimal) hai jo economic status classify karne mein help karta hai. In data ko analyze karne se desh ki economic aur social progress samajhne mein madad milti hai, jismein number systems ka fundamental role hai.
