# [Class 9] Rekhayein aur Kon (Lines and Angles) - Chapter 6

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter geometry ki neev hai, jisme hum lines (rekhayein) aur angles (kon) ke basic guno (properties) ko samajhte hain.

1.  **Basic Terms (Mool Paribhashayein)**
    *   **Rekha Khand (Line Segment):** Ek line ka hissa jiske do end points hote hain (Jaise AB).
    *   **Kiran (Ray):** Ek line ka hissa jiska ek end point hota hai (Jaise $\vec{AB}$).
    *   **Rekha (Line):** Dono dishao mein anant tak badhti hai (Jaise $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$).
    *   **Sanrekhi Bindu (Collinear Points):** Teen ya adhik bindu jo ek hi rekha par sthit hon.
    *   **Asanrekhi Bindu (Non-collinear Points):** Bindu jo ek hi rekha par sthit na hon.
    *   **Kon (Angle):** Jab do kirnein ek hi end point (vertex/sheersh) se shuru hoti hain. Kirno ko kon ki 'arms' (bhujayein) kehte hain.
    *   **Kon ke Prakar (Types of Angles):**
        *   Nyoon Kon (Acute Angle): 0° se bada, 90° se chhota.
        *   Samkon (Right Angle): Theek 90°.
        *   Adhik Kon (Obtuse Angle): 90° se bada, 180° se chhota.
        *   Riju Kon (Straight Angle): Theek 180°.
        *   Prativarti Kon (Reflex Angle): 180° se bada, 360° se chhota.

2.  **Kon ke Yugm (Pairs of Angles)**
    *   **Poorak Kon (Complementary Angles):** Do kon jinka yog (sum) 90° ho.
    *   **Sampoorak Kon (Supplementary Angles):** Do kon jinka yog 180° ho.
    *   **Aasann Kon (Adjacent Angles):** Do kon jinka common vertex (ubhayanisth sheersh) ho, common arm (ubhayanisth bhuja) ho, aur unki non-common arms common arm ke alag-alag taraf hon. (Fig. 6.2)
    *   **Konon ka Raikhik Yugm (Linear Pair of Angles):** Aise adjacent angles jinki non-common arms ek seedhi rekha banati hain. Inka sum hamesha 180° hota hai. (Fig. 6.3)
    *   **Sheershabhimukh Kon (Vertically Opposite Angles):** Jab do rekhayein ek doosre ko pratichhed (intersect) karti hain, toh aamne-saamne banne wale kon. Yeh hamesha barabar hote hain. (Fig. 6.4)

3.  **Pratichhedi aur Apratichhedi Rekhayein (Intersecting and Non-intersecting Lines)**
    *   **Pratichhedi Rekhayein (Intersecting Lines):** Do rekhayein jo ek bindu par milti hain. (Fig. 6.5(i))
    *   **Samantar Rekhayein (Parallel Lines):** Do rekhayein jo kabhi nahi milti, unke beech ki doori (perpendicular distance) hamesha samaan rehti hai. (Fig. 6.5(ii))

4.  **Samantar Rekhayein aur Tiryak Rekha (Parallel Lines and Transversal)**
    *   **Tiryak Rekha (Transversal):** Ek rekha jo do ya adhik rekhayon ko alag-alag binduon par kaat'ti hai.
    *   **Isse Banne Wale Kon (Angles Formed):**
        *   Sangat Kon (Corresponding Angles)
        *   Ekantar Antah Kon (Alternate Interior Angles)
        *   Ekantar Bahya Kon (Alternate Exterior Angles)
        *   Tiryak Rekha ke Ek Hi Taraf ke Antah Kon (Consecutive Interior Angles / Co-interior Angles)

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

Is chapter mein hum kuch important axioms (abhigrahit) aur theorems (pramey) seekhte hain jo lines aur angles ke beech ke relations ko establish karte hain.

1.  **Raikhik Yugm Abhigrahit (Linear Pair Axiom):**
    *   **Axiom 6.1:** Yadi ek kiran kisi rekha par khadi ho, toh is prakar bane dono aasann kono ka yog 180° hota hai. (Agar ray OC line AB par O par milti hai, toh ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°). (Fig. 6.6)
    *   **Axiom 6.2 (Converse):** Yadi do aasann kono ka yog 180° ho, toh unki non-common arms ek rekha banati hain. (Agar ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°, toh AOB ek line hai).

2.  **Sheershabhimukh Kon Pramey (Vertically Opposite Angles Theorem):**
    *   **Theorem 6.1:** Yadi do rekhayein ek doosre ko pratichhed karti hain, toh sheershabhimukh kon barabar hote hain. (Agar lines AB aur CD O par intersect karti hain, toh ∠AOC = ∠BOD aur ∠AOD = ∠BOC). (Fig. 6.8)
    *   *Proof Idea:* Linear Pair Axiom ka use karke prove kiya jaata hai. Jaise, ∠AOC + ∠AOD = 180° aur ∠AOD + ∠BOD = 180°. Dono ko compare karke ∠AOC = ∠BOD milta hai.

3.  **Samantar Rekhaon se Sambandhit Abhigrahit aur Pramey (Axioms and Theorems related to Parallel Lines):**
    *   **Sangat Kon Abhigrahit (Corresponding Angles Axiom):**
        *   **Axiom:** Yadi ek tiryak rekha do samantar rekhayon ko pratichhed karti hai, toh sangat kon ka pratyek yugm barabar hota hai.
        *   **Converse Axiom:** Yadi ek tiryak rekha do rekhayon ko is prakar pratichhed kare ki sangat kon ka ek yugm barabar ho, toh dono rekhayein samantar hoti hain.
    *   **Ekantar Antah Kon Pramey (Alternate Interior Angles Theorem):**
        *   **Theorem:** Yadi ek tiryak rekha do samantar rekhayon ko pratichhed karti hai, toh ekantar antah kon ka pratyek yugm barabar hota hai.
        *   **Converse Theorem:** Yadi ek tiryak rekha do rekhayon ko is prakar pratichhed kare ki ekantar antah kon ka ek yugm barabar ho, toh dono rekhayein samantar hoti hain.
    *   **Kramagat Antah Kon Pramey (Consecutive Interior Angles Theorem):**
        *   **Theorem:** Yadi ek tiryak rekha do samantar rekhayon ko pratichhed karti hai, toh tiryak rekha ke ek hi taraf ke antah kono ka pratyek yugm sampoorak (supplementary) hota hai (sum 180°).
        *   **Converse Theorem:** Yadi ek tiryak rekha do rekhayon ko is prakar pratichhed kare ki tiryak rekha ke ek hi taraf ke antah kono ka ek yugm sampoorak ho, toh dono rekhayein samantar hoti hain.

4.  **Ek Hi Rekha ke Samantar Rekhayein (Lines Parallel to the Same Line):**
    *   **Theorem 6.6:** Woh rekhayein jo ek hi rekha ke samantar hon, परस्पर (mutually) samantar hoti hain. (Agar line m || line l aur line n || line l, toh line m || line n). (Fig. 6.18)

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Indian Context)

Lines aur Angles ke concepts Bharat mein kai jagah dekhe ja sakte hain:

1.  **Vastukala aur Nagar Niyojan (Architecture and Urban Planning):**
    *   Prachin mandiron (ancient temples) ke intricate designs mein symmetry aur specific angles ka use hota hai.
    *   Mughal architecture (jaise Taj Mahal) mein geometric patterns aur parallel lines ka sundar upyog hai.
    *   Modern Indian cities ki planning mein, jaise Chandigarh (Le Corbusier dwara design kiya gaya), sadakon ka grid pattern parallel aur perpendicular lines par आधारित hai. Building plans banate waqt architects inhi concepts ka istemal karte hain.

2.  **Infrastructure (Buniyadi Dhancha):**
    *   Bharat ka vishal Railway network parallel tracks par nirbhar karta hai. Tracks ke beech ki doori hamesha constant rakhni hoti hai, jiske liye parallel lines ka concept zaroori hai. Railway crossings par intersecting lines aur angles bante hain.
    *   Pul (Bridges) aur flyovers ke design mein structural integrity ke liye angles aur parallel supports ka sahi calculation bahut important hai.

3.  **Vigyan aur Technology (Science and Technology):**
    *   Physics mein light ke reflection aur refraction (prakash ka paravartan aur apvartan) ko samajhne ke liye ray diagrams banaye jaate hain, jisme angles (angle of incidence, angle of reflection/refraction) ka use hota hai. Yeh concept Bharat mein scientific research aur education ka hissa hai.
    *   Engineering mein forces ko represent karne ke liye vectors (line segments with direction) ka use hota hai, aur unke beech ke angles net force calculate karne mein madad karte hain.

Yeh chapter sirf exams ke liye nahi, balki hamare aas-paas ki duniya ko mathematically samajhne ke liye bhi important hai. Aap apne school exhibition ke liye bamboo sticks se hut model banate waqt bhi parallel aur intersecting lines ka use karenge!
