# [Class 9] General - Chapter 106 (Lines and Angles)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter geometry ke basic building blocks - lines (rekhaayein) aur angles (kon) - ke baare mein hai. Concepts ko hum aise samajh sakte hain:

1.  **Basic Terms (Mool Paribhashayein):**
    *   **Line Segment (Rekhakhand):** Ek line ka part jiske do endpoints hote hain (Jaise AB).
    *   **Ray (Kiran):** Ek line ka part jiska ek endpoint hota hai aur doosri taraf anant tak jaati hai (Jaise $\vec{AB}$).
    *   **Line (Rekha):** Dono dishao mein anant tak badhne wali (Jaise $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$).
    *   **Collinear Points (Sanrekhi Bindu):** Teen ya zyada points jo ek hi line par hote hain.
    *   **Non-collinear Points (Asanrekhi Bindu):** Points jo ek hi line par nahi hote.

2.  **Angles (Kon):**
    *   **Formation:** Jab do rays ek hi endpoint (vertex/sheersh) se shuru hoti hain. Rays ko arms (bhujayein) kehte hain.
    *   **Types (Prakar):**
        *   **Acute Angle (Nyoon Kon):** 0° se bada, 90° se chhota (0° < x < 90°).
        *   **Right Angle (Samkon):** Exactly 90° (y = 90°).
        *   **Obtuse Angle (Adhik Kon):** 90° se bada, 180° se chhota (90° < z < 180°).
        *   **Straight Angle (Riju Kon):** Exactly 180° (s = 180°). Ek seedhi line banata hai.
        *   **Reflex Angle (Brihat Kon):** 180° se bada, 360° se chhota (180° < t < 360°).

3.  **Pairs of Angles (Konon ke Yugm):**
    *   **Complementary Angles (Poorak Kon):** Do angles jinka sum 90° ho.
    *   **Supplementary Angles (Sampoorak Kon):** Do angles jinka sum 180° ho.
    *   **Adjacent Angles (Aasann Kon):** Common vertex, common arm, non-common arms common arm ke different sides par hon. (Jaise ∠ABD aur ∠DBC in Fig 6.2).
    *   **Linear Pair of Angles (Konon ka Rekhik Yugm):** Aise adjacent angles jinke non-common arms ek straight line banate hain. Inka sum hamesha 180° hota hai. (Fig 6.3).
    *   **Vertically Opposite Angles (Sheershabhimukh Kon):** Jab do lines intersect karti hain, toh opposite bane angles. Yeh equal hote hain. (Jaise ∠AOC aur ∠BOD in Fig 6.4).

4.  **Lines (Rekhaayein):**
    *   **Intersecting Lines (Pratichhedi Rekhaayein):** Lines jo ek point par milti hain. (Fig 6.5(i)).
    *   **Parallel Lines (Samaantar Rekhaayein):** Lines jo kabhi nahi milti, unke beech ki perpendicular doori hamesha same rehti hai. (Fig 6.5(ii)).

5.  **Axioms and Theorems (Abhigrahit aur Pramey):**
    *   **Axiom 6.1 (Linear Pair Axiom):** Agar ek ray ek line par khadi ho, toh bane adjacent angles ka sum 180° hota hai.
    *   **Axiom 6.2 (Converse of Linear Pair Axiom):** Agar do adjacent angles ka sum 180° ho, toh unke non-common arms ek line banate hain.
    *   **Theorem 6.1:** Agar do lines intersect karti hain, toh vertically opposite angles equal hote hain.
    *   **Transversal Related Concepts (Tiryak Rekha se Sambandhit):** (Implicit from examples/exercise 6.2)
        *   **Corresponding Angles (Sangat Kon):** Equal hote hain agar lines parallel hon.
        *   **Alternate Interior Angles (Ekaantar Antah Kon):** Equal hote hain agar lines parallel hon.
        *   **Interior Angles on the same side of transversal (Tiryak Rekha ke ek hi taraf ke Antah Kon):** Supplementary hote hain (sum 180°) agar lines parallel hon.
    *   **Theorem 6.6:** Jo lines ek hi line ke parallel hoti hain, woh aapas mein bhi parallel hoti hain.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein)

Is chapter mein hum kuch important rules (axioms aur theorems) seekhenge jo lines aur angles ke beech ke relationships ko explain karte hain.

1.  **Linear Pair Axiom (Rekhik Yugm Abhigrahit - Axiom 6.1 & 6.2):**
    *   *Kya kehta hai?* Agar ek seedhi line (straight line) par ek ray khadi ho, toh jo do adjacent angles banenge, unka jod (sum) hamesha 180° hoga.
    *   *Example:* Imagine karo ek road (line AB) hai aur usse ek choti gali (ray OC) nikal rahi hai point O se. Toh jo do angle banenge (∠AOC aur ∠BOC), unko milakar 180° hoga.
    *   `[Diagram: Ek line AB, point O us par. Ray OC O se upar ja rahi hai. Angles ∠AOC aur ∠BOC dikhaye gaye hain. ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°]`
    *   *Iska ulta (Converse - Axiom 6.2):* Agar do *adjacent* angles ka sum 180° hai, toh unki jo non-common arms hain (bahar wali sides), woh milkar ek straight line banayengi.

2.  **Vertically Opposite Angles Theorem (Sheershabhimukh Kon Pramey - Theorem 6.1):**
    *   *Kya kehta hai?* Jab do lines ek dusre ko kaatti (intersect) hain, toh aamne-saamne wale angles (vertically opposite angles) hamesha barabar (equal) hote hain.
    *   *Proof ka Idea:* Hum Linear Pair Axiom ka use karke isse prove kar sakte hain. Maan lo lines AB aur CD point O par intersect karti hain.
        *   Ray OA line CD par hai, toh ∠AOC + ∠AOD = 180° (Linear Pair). (1)
        *   Ray OD line AB par hai, toh ∠AOD + ∠BOD = 180° (Linear Pair). (2)
        *   (1) aur (2) se, ∠AOC + ∠AOD = ∠AOD + ∠BOD. Dono taraf se ∠AOD hatane par, ∠AOC = ∠BOD milta hai.
        *   Similarly, ∠AOD = ∠BOC prove kar sakte hain.
    *   `[Diagram: Do lines AB aur CD ek dusre ko O par kaat rahi hain. Angles ∠AOC, ∠BOD, ∠AOD, ∠BOC dikhaye gaye hain. ∠AOC = ∠BOD aur ∠AOD = ∠BOC]`

3.  **Parallel Lines and Transversals (Samaantar Rekhaayein aur Tiryak Rekha):**
    *   Jab ek transversal line do parallel lines ko kaatti hai, toh kuch khaas angle pairs bante hain jinke beech special relation hota hai:
        *   **Corresponding Angles (Sangat Kon):** Equal hote hain. (Jaise Fig 6.21 mein ∠ABE aur ∠BCG agar PQ || RS).
        *   **Alternate Interior Angles (Ekaantar Antah Kon):** Equal hote hain. (Jaise Fig 6.26 mein ∠APQ (50°) aur ∠PQR (x) agar AB || CD).
        *   **Interior Angles on the same side (Kramagat Antah Kon):** Inka sum 180° hota hai. (Jaise Fig 6.22 mein ∠DCE (y) aur ∠CEF (55°) agar CD || EF).
    *   *Converse bhi true hai:* Agar inmein se koi bhi condition (jaise corresponding angles equal hain) puri hoti hai, toh lines parallel hongi. (Example 5 mein use hua hai).
    *   `[Diagram: Do parallel lines m aur n, ek transversal t unhe kaat rahi hai. Corresponding angles, alternate interior angles, aur consecutive interior angles ke pairs highlight kiye gaye hain.]`

4.  **Lines Parallel to the Same Line (Ek hi Rekha ke Samaantar Rekhaayein - Theorem 6.6):**
    *   *Kya kehta hai?* Agar do lines (m aur n) ek teesri line (l) ke parallel hain, toh woh dono lines (m aur n) aapas mein bhi parallel hongi. (m || l aur n || l => m || n).
    *   *Example:* Imagine teen parallel railway tracks. Pehla track (l), doosra (m), teesra (n). Agar m, l ke parallel hai aur n bhi l ke parallel hai, toh m aur n bhi aapas mein parallel honge.
    *   `[Diagram: Teen lines l, m, n. m || l aur n || l dikhaya gaya hai. Conclusion: m || n]`

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh)

Lines aur angles sirf kitaabon mein nahi hain, yeh hamare desh ke structure aur data mein bhi dikhte hain:

1.  **Architecture and Planning (Vastukala aur Niyojan):**
    *   **Ancient India:** Indus Valley civilization ki cities (like Mohenjo-Daro) mein roads right angles par intersect karti thi, showing planning. Mandiron ke intricate carvings (jaise Khajuraho ya Konark) mein precise angles aur geometric patterns ka use hota hai. Jaipur city ka grid layout intersecting lines ka classic example hai.
    *   **Modern India:** Naye infrastructure projects jaise Metro lines (Delhi Metro, Mumbai Metro), flyovers, aur bridges (jaise Bandra-Worli Sea Link) ke design mein parallel lines (tracks, lanes) aur specific angles (supports, ramps) ka accurate calculation bahut zaroori hai structural stability aur efficiency ke liye.

2.  **Art and Culture (Kala aur Sanskriti):**
    *   **Rangoli/Kolam:** Inn traditional art forms mein intersecting lines, parallel lines, curves aur various angles ka use karke complex geometric patterns banaye jaate hain.
    *   **Textile Designs:** Indian textiles (sarees, fabrics) mein often geometric motifs hote hain jo lines aur angles par based hote hain.

3.  **Economic/Social Data Representation (Arthik/Samajik Data Prastuti) 📊:**
    *   Jab hum India ki economic growth (GDP), population change, ya literacy rates jaise data ko graphs par plot karte hain, toh lines aur angles ka use hota hai:
        *   **Line Graphs:** Alag-alag states ki performance compare karne wale line graphs mein lines intersect kar sakti hain (showing one state overtaking another), parallel chal sakti hain (showing similar trends), ya alag-alag angles par rise/fall kar sakti hain (showing different rates of change). For example, NITI Aayog ke reports mein aise graphs hote hain.
        *   **Pie Charts:** Budget allocation ya population demographics ko dikhane wale pie charts mein har sector ek angle se represent hota hai. Total angle 360° hota hai.
        *   **Bar Graphs:** Different years ke production data ko compare karte waqt bars parallel hote hain, lekin unki height data represent karti hai. Trend lines draw karke angles study kiye ja sakte hain.

Yeh concepts samajhna sirf Maths ke liye nahi, balki apne आसपास ki duniya, desh ke vikas aur data ko critically analyze karne ke liye bhi important hai.
