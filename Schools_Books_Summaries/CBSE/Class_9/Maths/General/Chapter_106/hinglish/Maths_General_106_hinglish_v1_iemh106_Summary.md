# [Class 9] General - Chapter 106 (Lines and Angles - Rekhayein aur Kon)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter geometry ka base hai, jisme hum lines (rekhayein) aur angles (kon) ke basic properties ko samajhte hain.

1.  **Basic Terms (Mool Shabd):**
    *   **Line (Rekha):** Dono directions mein indefinitely extend hoti hai. (↔️)
    *   **Line Segment (Rekhakhand):** Ek line ka part jiske do end points hote hain. (•---•)
    *   **Ray (Kiran):** Ek line ka part jiska ek end point hota hai aur dusri direction mein indefinitely extend hoti hai. (•--->)
    *   **Collinear Points (Sanrekhi Bindu):** Teen ya zyada points jo same line par hote hain.
    *   **Non-collinear Points (Asanrekhi Bindu):** Points jo same line par nahi hote.

2.  **Angles (Kon):**
    *   **Formation:** Jab do rays (arms) ek common end point (vertex) se originate hoti hain.
    *   **Types of Angles (Kon ke Prakar):**
        *   **Acute Angle (Nyun Kon):** 0° se bada, 90° se chhota (0° < x < 90°).
        *   **Right Angle (Samkon):** Exactly 90° (y = 90°).
        *   **Obtuse Angle (Adhik Kon):** 90° se bada, 180° se chhota (90° < z < 180°).
        *   **Straight Angle (Riju Kon):** Exactly 180° (s = 180°). Ek seedhi line banata hai.
        *   **Reflex Angle (Prativarti Kon):** 180° se bada, 360° se chhota (180° < t < 360°).

3.  **Pairs of Angles (Kon ke Yugm):**
    *   **Complementary Angles (Poorak Kon):** Do angles jinka sum 90° ho.
    *   **Supplementary Angles (Sampoorak Kon):** Do angles jinka sum 180° ho.
    *   **Adjacent Angles (Aasann Kon):** Common vertex, common arm, non-common arms on different sides. (Jaise ∠ABD aur ∠DBC Fig. 6.2 mein).
    *   **Linear Pair of Angles (Konon ka Raikhik Yugm):** Adjacent angles jinke non-common arms ek straight line banate hain. Inka sum hamesha 180° hota hai. (Fig. 6.3).
    *   **Vertically Opposite Angles (Sheershabhimukh Kon):** Jab do lines intersect karti hain, toh opposite angles ke pairs equal hote hain. (Fig. 6.4 mein ∠AOD = ∠BOC aur ∠AOC = ∠BOD).

4.  **Lines (Rekhayein):**
    *   **Intersecting Lines (Pratichhedi Rekhayein):** Lines jo ek point par milti hain. (Fig. 6.5(i)).
    *   **Non-intersecting / Parallel Lines (Apratichhedi / Samantar Rekhayein):** Lines jo kabhi nahi milti, unke beech ka perpendicular distance hamesha same rehta hai. (Fig. 6.5(ii)).

5.  **Transversal (Tiryak Rekha):** Ek line jo do ya zyada lines ko distinct points par intersect karti hai.

6.  **Angles made by a Transversal (Tiryak Rekha dwara banaye gaye Kon):**
    *   **Corresponding Angles (Sangat Kon):** (e.g., ∠1 & ∠5 in standard diagrams)
    *   **Alternate Interior Angles (Ekantar Antah Kon):** (e.g., ∠4 & ∠6)
    *   **Alternate Exterior Angles (Ekantar Bahya Kon):** (e.g., ∠1 & ∠7)
    *   **Interior angles on the same side of the transversal / Consecutive Interior Angles (Tiryak rekha ke ek hi taraf ke antah kon):** (e.g., ∠4 & ∠5)

7.  **Key Axioms and Theorems (Mukhya Abhigrahit aur Pramey):**
    *   **Axiom 6.1 (Linear Pair Axiom):** Agar ek ray kisi line par stand karti hai, toh aise bane do adjacent angles ka sum 180° hota hai.
    *   **Axiom 6.2 (Converse of Linear Pair Axiom):** Agar do adjacent angles ka sum 180° hai, toh unki non-common arms ek line banati hain.
    *   **Theorem 6.1:** Agar do lines intersect karti hain, toh vertically opposite angles equal hote hain.
    *   **Parallel Lines Axioms/Theorems:**
        *   Agar transversal do parallel lines ko intersect karti hai, toh corresponding angles equal hote hain (Axiom).
        *   Converse: Agar corresponding angles equal hain, toh lines parallel hain (Axiom).
        *   Agar transversal do parallel lines ko intersect karti hai, toh alternate interior angles equal hote hain (Theorem).
        *   Converse: Agar alternate interior angles equal hain, toh lines parallel hain (Theorem).
        *   Agar transversal do parallel lines ko intersect karti hai, toh interior angles on the same side supplementary hote hain (sum 180°) (Theorem).
        *   Converse: Agar interior angles on the same side supplementary hain, toh lines parallel hain (Theorem).
    *   **Theorem 6.6:** Lines jo same line ke parallel hoti hain, woh ek dusre ke bhi parallel hoti hain. (l || m aur n || m => l || n).

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

Is chapter mein humne seekha ki lines aur angles ke beech kya relations hote hain, khaas kar jab lines intersect karti hain ya parallel hoti hain.

1.  **Linear Pair Axiom (Raikhik Yugm Abhigrahit):**
    *   **Concept:** Jab ek ray (kiran) ek line (rekha) par khadi hoti hai (stands on), toh jo do adjacent angles (aasann kon) bante hain, unka jod (sum) hamesha 180° hota hai.
    *   **Diagram:** Imagine karo ek straight line AB hai aur point O par ek ray OC khadi hai. Toh ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°.
    *   **Example:** Agar ∠AOC = 60°, toh ∠BOC = 180° - 60° = 120°.
    *   **Converse:** Agar do adjacent angles ka sum 180° hai, toh unki jo non-common arms hain (jo alag-alag hain), woh milkar ek straight line banayengi.

    ```
    Diagram Description:
    Line A-O-B. Ray O-C originating from O, making angle AOC and BOC.
    A-----O-----B
          |
          C
    ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°
    ```

2.  **Vertically Opposite Angles (Sheershabhimukh Kon):**
    *   **Concept:** Jab do lines (jaise AB aur CD) ek point O par intersect (pratichhed) karti hain, toh aamne-saamne wale angles equal hote hain.
    *   **Proof Idea:** Hum Linear Pair Axiom ka use karke isko prove karte hain. Jaise, ∠AOC + ∠AOD = 180° (Linear Pair) aur ∠AOD + ∠BOD = 180° (Linear Pair). Dono equations se hum conclude karte hain ki ∠AOC = ∠BOD. Similarly, ∠AOD = ∠BOC.
    *   **Diagram:**
    ```
    Diagram Description:
    Two lines A-B and C-D intersecting at point O.
       A       C
        \     /
         \   /
          \ /
           O
          / \
         /   \
        /     \
       D       B
    ∠AOC = ∠BOD (Vertically Opposite)
    ∠AOD = ∠BOC (Vertically Opposite)
    ```
    *   **Example:** Agar ∠AOC = 50°, toh ∠BOD bhi 50° hoga. Aur ∠AOD = 180° - 50° = 130°, toh ∠BOC bhi 130° hoga.

3.  **Parallel Lines and Transversal (Samantar Rekhayein aur Tiryak Rekha):**
    *   **Concept:** Jab ek transversal do parallel lines ko kaatti hai, toh specific pairs of angles ke beech relation hota hai:
        *   **Corresponding Angles (Sangat Kon) are EQUAL.** (Jaise F-shape mein angles)
        *   **Alternate Interior Angles (Ekantar Antah Kon) are EQUAL.** (Jaise Z-shape mein angles)
        *   **Interior angles on the same side (Consecutive Interior Angles) are SUPPLEMENTARY (sum = 180°).** (Jaise C-shape mein angles)
    *   **Converse:** Agar inmein se koi bhi condition (Equal corresponding angles, Equal alternate interior angles, Supplementary consecutive interior angles) satisfy hoti hai, toh lines PARALLEL hongi.
    *   **Diagram:**
    ```
    Diagram Description:
    Two parallel lines l and m, cut by a transversal t. Angles numbered 1 to 8.
        l ------------------→
           \ 1 / 2 /
            \ / /
             X------- (Intersection point)
            / \ /
           / 3 \ 4 /
          /     \ /
    t    /       Y------- (Intersection point)
        /       / \
       / 5     / 6 \
      /------ /-------\---→ m
     /       / 7 \ 8 /
    /       /     \ /

    If l || m:
    Corresponding: ∠1=∠5, ∠2=∠6, ∠3=∠7, ∠4=∠8
    Alternate Interior: ∠3=∠5, ∠4=∠6
    Consecutive Interior: ∠3+∠6=180°, ∠4+∠5=180°
    ```
    *   **Theorem 6.6:** Agar line 'l' line 'm' ke parallel hai, aur line 'n' bhi line 'm' ke parallel hai, toh line 'l' aur line 'n' bhi aapas mein parallel hongi (l || m, n || m ⇒ l || n). Yeh property construction aur planning mein bahut useful hai.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh) 📊

Lines aur angles ka concept hamare desh mein ancient times se use hota aa raha hai, aur aaj bhi modern infrastructure mein iska important role hai.

1.  **Traditional Architecture & Art:**
    *   **Mandir Architecture:** Purane Bharatiya mandiron ke complex structures mein precise angles aur geometric patterns ka use hota tha, jo stability aur aesthetic appeal dono dete the. Shikhara (tower) ke design mein angles ka calculation important tha.
    *   **Rangoli/Kolam:** Yeh traditional art forms hain jo ghar ke angan mein banaye jate hain. Inme अक्सर lines, curves, aur angles ka systematic use karke intricate patterns banaye jate hain, jo symmetry aur geometry ka sundar example hain.

2.  **Modern Infrastructure & Planning:**
    *   **Indian Railways Network:** India ka vast railway network duniya ke sabse bade networks mein se ek hai. Tracks ka parallel hona trains ke smooth aur safe movement ke liye critical hai. Iski planning, construction, aur maintenance mein geometric principles ka use hota hai. Efficient railway network desh ki economy ke liye bahut zaroori hai (goods transport, passenger travel).
    *   **Urban Planning (Nagar Niyojan):** Jaise Chandigarh ka example liya gaya, waise hi new townships aur SEZs (Special Economic Zones) ki planning mein road networks, building layouts, aur utility lines (paani, bijli) ko design karne ke liye parallel aur intersecting lines ke concepts ka use hota hai. Achi planning se traffic congestion kam hota hai aur resources ka sahi istemal hota hai, jiska seedha economic benefit hai.
    *   **Agriculture (Krishi):** Bade farms mein irrigation channels (nahar) ko design karte waqt bhi angles aur slopes ka dhyan rakha jata hai taaki paani efficiently distribute ho sake. Isse water usage optimize hota hai aur fasal ki paidawar badhti hai, jo ki Indian economy ka ek important hissa hai.

Yeh examples dikhate hain ki geometry, specifically lines and angles, sirf textbook ka concept nahi hai, balki hamari daily life, culture, aur desh ke economic development mein bhi ek important role play karta hai.
