# [Class 9] Maths - Chapter 6: Lines and Angles (Rekhayein aur Kon)

## 🌟 Core Concepts (Mukhya Avdharnayein) 📊

Yeh chapter geometry ka base hai, jisme hum lines aur angles ke basic properties ko samajhte hain.

1.  **Basic Terms (Mool Paribhashayein):**
    *   **Line Segment (Rekhakhand):** Ek line ka part jiske do endpoints hote hain (Jaise AB).
    *   **Ray (Kiran):** Ek line ka part jiska ek endpoint hota hai aur dusri taraf anant tak jaati hai (Jaise $\vec{AB}$).
    *   **Line (Rekha):** Dono directions mein anant tak extend hoti hai (Jaise $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$).
    *   **Collinear Points (Sanrekhi Bindu):** Teen ya zyada points jo ek hi line par hote hain.
    *   **Non-collinear Points (Asanrekhi Bindu):** Points jo ek hi line par nahi hote.
    *   **Angle (Kon):** Jab do rays ek hi endpoint (vertex/शीर्ष) se shuru hoti hain. Rays ko arms (भुजाएं) kehte hain.

2.  **Types of Angles (Konon ke Prakar):**
    *   **Acute Angle (Nyoon Kon):** 0° se bada, 90° se chhota (0° < x < 90°).
    *   **Right Angle (Samkon):** Exactly 90° (y = 90°).
    *   **Obtuse Angle (Adhik Kon):** 90° se bada, 180° se chhota (90° < z < 180°).
    *   **Straight Angle (Riju Kon):** Exactly 180° (s = 180°).
    *   **Reflex Angle (Prativarti Kon):** 180° se bada, 360° se chhota (180° < t < 360°).

3.  **Pairs of Angles (Konon ke Yugm):**
    *   **Complementary Angles (Poorak Kon):** Do angles jinka sum 90° hota hai.
    *   **Supplementary Angles (Sampoorak Kon):** Do angles jinka sum 180° hota hai.
    *   **Adjacent Angles (Aasann Kon):** Do angles jinka common vertex, common arm ho, aur non-common arms common arm ke different sides par hon (Fig 6.2).
    *   **Linear Pair of Angles (Konon ka Raikhik Yugm):** Do adjacent angles jinke non-common arms ek straight line banate hain. Inka sum hamesha 180° hota hai (Fig 6.3).
    *   **Vertically Opposite Angles (Sheershabhimukh Kon):** Jab do lines intersect karti hain, toh opposite angles ke pairs bante hain jo equal hote hain (Fig 6.4).

4.  **Lines (Rekhayein):**
    *   **Intersecting Lines (Pratichhedi Rekhayein):** Lines jo ek point par milti hain (Fig 6.5(i)).
    *   **Non-intersecting/Parallel Lines (Apratichhedi/Samaantar Rekhayein):** Lines jo kabhi nahi milti. Unke beech ka perpendicular distance hamesha same rehta hai (Fig 6.5(ii)).

5.  **Transversal (Tiryak Rekha):** Ek line jo do ya do se zyada lines ko distinct points par intersect karti hai.

6.  **Angles made by a Transversal (Tiryak Rekha dwara banaye gaye Kon):**
    *   Corresponding Angles (Sangat Kon)
    *   Alternate Interior Angles (Ekaantar Antah Kon)
    *   Alternate Exterior Angles (Ekaantar Bahya Kon)
    *   Interior angles on the same side of the transversal (Tiryak rekha ke ek hi taraf ke antah kon) / Consecutive Interior Angles / Co-interior Angles.

## 📘 Key Learnings (Mukhya Baatein) 📈

Is chapter mein hum kuch important axioms (abhigrahit) aur theorems (pramey) sikhenge.

1.  **Linear Pair Axiom (Raikhik Yugm Abhigrahit):**
    *   **Axiom 6.1:** Agar ek ray kisi line par khadi ho (If a ray stands on a line), toh is tarah bane do adjacent angles ka sum 180° hota hai. (Fig 6.6: ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°)
    *   **Axiom 6.2 (Converse):** Agar do adjacent angles ka sum 180° ho, toh unki non-common arms ek line banati hain. (Fig 6.7 (iii) shows this)
    *   In dono ko milakar **Linear Pair Axiom** kehte hain.

2.  **Vertically Opposite Angles Theorem (Sheershabhimukh Kon Pramey):**
    *   **Theorem 6.1:** Agar do lines ek doosre ko intersect karti hain, toh vertically opposite angles equal hote hain. (Fig 6.8: ∠AOC = ∠BOD aur ∠AOD = ∠BOC).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Linear Pair Axiom ka use karke prove kiya jaata hai. Jaise, ∠AOC + ∠AOD = 180° aur ∠AOD + ∠BOD = 180°. Dono ko compare karke ∠AOC = ∠BOD milta hai.

3.  **Parallel Lines and Transversal (Samaantar Rekhayein aur Tiryak Rekha):**
    *   Agar ek transversal do *parallel lines* ko intersect karti hai, toh:
        *   **Corresponding Angles Axiom:** Corresponding angles ka har pair equal hota hai. (e.g., ∠1 = ∠5 in standard diagrams)
        *   **Alternate Interior Angles Theorem:** Alternate interior angles ka har pair equal hota hai. (e.g., ∠4 = ∠6)
        *   **Consecutive Interior Angles Theorem:** Interior angles on the same side of the transversal ka har pair supplementary hota hai (sum 180°). (e.g., ∠4 + ∠5 = 180°)
    *   **Converse Theorems (Vilom Pramey):** Agar ek transversal do lines ko is tarah intersect kare ki:
        *   Corresponding angles ka koi ek pair equal ho, OR
        *   Alternate interior angles ka koi ek pair equal ho, OR
        *   Interior angles on the same side of the transversal ka koi ek pair supplementary ho,
        *   Toh woh dono lines *parallel* hoti hain.

4.  **Lines Parallel to the Same Line (Ek hi Rekha ke Samaantar Rekhayein):**
    *   **Theorem 6.6:** Woh lines jo ek hi line ke parallel hoti hain, woh aapas mein bhi parallel hoti hain. (Fig 6.18: Agar m || l aur n || l, toh m || n).
    *   *Proof Idea:* Transversal draw karke corresponding angles ki equality use karte hain.

## 🌏 Bharatiya Context (Bharatiya Sandarbh) 📊

Lines aur Angles ke concepts hamare desh mein sadiyon se use hote aa rahe hain:

1.  **Ancient Town Planning (Prachin Nagar Niyojan):** Indus Valley Civilization ke sheher jaise Harappa aur Mohenjo-daro mein grid pattern (parallel aur perpendicular streets) ka istemal kiya gaya tha, jo advanced planning dikhata hai. Yeh intersecting lines aur right angles ke knowledge ko darshata hai.

3.  **Rangoli/Kolam:** Yeh traditional art forms geometric patterns par based hain. Inme lines, curves, aur angles ka sundar istemal hota hai, jo symmetry aur design ke principles ko reflect karta hai.
4.  **Indian Railway Network:** Bharat ka vishaal railway network duniya ke sabse bade networks mein se ek hai. Tracks ko parallel rakhna, junctions design karna, aur curves banana - yeh sab lines aur angles ke accurate application par depend karta hai. Yeh desh ke economic infrastructure ka ek important hissa hai.
5.  **Agriculture & Land Surveying (Krishi aur Bhumi Sarvekshan):** Zameen ki maap (measurement) aur boundaries define karne ke liye historically angles aur lines ka gyaan zaroori raha hai, jo property ownership aur taxation ke liye important tha. Aaj bhi modern surveying techniques inhi basic principles par adharit hain.
